At this Moment in time, I'm thinking on wether I should release it or not. I would consider this the most realistic Fire Department ever put into SA-MP. I will be making other FD's like this for a pack I plan on releasing, pleasing the SA-MP Community. This station has a Living Quarters, Chief(s) Offices (By this I mean the Fire Chief, Battalion Chief, and the Medical Supervisor.), Work out room (It's a bit unrealistic but there was a big space and I needed to fill it), Kitchen, Bathroom, and a Bay Area .

The First Picture is the Front of the Station, from here you can see the Main Bays,Poles,minor shots of the Living Area, and Kitchen.

This next photo shows the Side Parking area which is currently 'Beta?' I'm messing around with Gates ect. this picture also portrates the fact the map ISN'T floating, and the Baydoor that will hide what I have it set for is the Brush Truck.

This next picture is of the Living Quarters I MIGHT revisit the Living Quarters and space out the Beds more for Dressers in between the Beds.This room also has a Fire Pole built in to decrease response times. (Just run into the pole and go down, you loose health but if your on the right server were you ca heal yourself RPly your going down the pole safely so you can legitamently heal yourself if you take damage0

This next picture is of the Kitchen, this was probably the LEAST time consuming process in building this whole map in its intierty. But it's Legitament.

This Bathroom is only 3 objects not counting the walls, I spent more time deciding wether I should make it a multiple shower room or making it a single.. I obviously chose single.

[b]This is a 'State of the Art Gym' so to speak.. (Lol) it offers 6 Weight Benches, 4 Tread Mills, 4 Bikes, and one Boxing ring (If your Stationed in the Ghetto XD)[/b



These are the Offices The First Picture is for the Battalion Chief, and Medical Supervisor (Or if you want a Secretary Fire Marshall Ect.)The next Photo is of the Chief Office.
