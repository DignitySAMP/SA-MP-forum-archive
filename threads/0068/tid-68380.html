First of all: I don't know whether this has been brought up already or not.


Well, I look at the server advertisements often, and 1 thing just shows up at about 85% of the advertisements: Only a few sentences, no pictures, and only an ip/forum.
Personally I think this won't get any players. You can only advertize once a month, and people 'd sure like to see a good advertisement. Ofcourse it's their decision, but I'm sure this would attract more players.

I REALLY know it's the advertizer's decision; But what about a rule like:

- 1-2 screenshots at the very least.
- Needs to contain atleast 10 sentences of information, an ip, and [eventually] a forum.

This would definitely lure players. The advertisements like: JOIN MY SERVER ***.***.***.**:7777 won't really lure players, anyway.


So that's just MY point of view. Discuss.
