Evolution RolePlay [WIP]
Background:
About 6 months ago I was part of a community that was thriving and I was developing a fresh script for it. After some rough months, the community went down and I had no use for this gamemode because I wasn't really coding anymore. I had started learning Java and C# in school and left Pawn behind for a while.

Notes:
Nearly every single thing in this gamemode was made from scratch, aside from the includes.
I may script again but I'm still unsure. Whatever
Dutils, Dini, and a_angles were not made by me. Credit for them does not go to me.

Features:
  • Advanced Weather System: IE: Can't rain after a dust storm
  • New Player Tutorial: Go to bank and create an account and get your debit card
  • House System: Built to be temporary but it can work for permanent too. Build a house and set it for sale or rent. Multiple houses can use the same interior by way of virtual worlds
  • Vehicle System: Buy and sell cars, there is also resale depreciation
  • Pickup System: Allows admins to place pickups dynamically using menus.
  • ATM System: Use your debit card to do transactions
  • Tax System: Debit cards are charged a specific tax which admins can set.
  • Position Save: Save your last position on disconnect
  • Weapon Drops: If you die you drop your current weapon
  • Advanced Chat: OOC & IC chat with animations
  • Admin System: General commands with extra commands to create houses/pickups
  • Vehicle Lock System: Lock your car and also hotwire it. Hotwire System is incomplete atm.
  • Bank System: Deposit, withdraw, get balance, and receive a debit card
  • Menu Based Login System: Login and register with menus
  • Faction System: Incomplete: Community fell apart before I began work on this part.
  • Debit Cards: Use your debit card to make purchases at local stores, also has PIN security
  • SMS System: Chat like system but for SMSing other players during RP
  • Server Side Money: Server knows EXACTLY how much money you have so you can't cheat. Was going to be part of anticheat
  • Payday System: Get payed depending on your employment status and your house payment is automatically deducted. Jobless are sent a welfare check
  • Wound System: If you get shot or stabbed, you start bleeding and can bleed to death. Hospital can heal you
  • Medical System: Admins can set the amount per HP heal for players that want to get their wounds bandaged.
  • Gas System: All cars have gas and admins can set the gas prices.
  • Speedometer: Textdraw based speedometer and gas information in bottom right corner.
IMPORTANT: Guide to making yourself top admin
  1. Register on the server.
  2. Go to scriptfiles/Users/
  3. Find your username and open the file.
  4. Set AdminLevel to 5.
Once you do that, you can use the ingame command /setadmin to set someone an admin.

Questions? Comments?
Feel free to ask in this thread.
Enjoy the release.
