[GM] Elite Squad VS Drugs Dealers v1.1


Created by Bruno
Shantytown(Favela) Created by Luan(my brother)

Commands:

/objective: See the objective of the game mode dependent of your team.
/givecash [playerid] [amount]: send money to other player.
/info: See the information of the weapon you can buy. Name/Ammo/Cost.
/buy: Buy the weapon. Must be near of the weapon.
/r or /radio: Enable/Disable your voice on the radio. With it enabled everyone of your team see your messages.
/w or /whisper: Enable whisper mode. When you say something only very near persons can read
/s or /shout: Enable shout mode. Make your message can read by far persons or...
/n or /normal: Enable the normal messages for near persons
Objectives:

**** Elite Squad ****"
Your objective is invade the shantytown, If you reach the end of the shantytown you and the Elite Squad Won.
You can buy weapons in your base but for it you need money.
**** Drugs Dealers ****
Your objective is protect the shantytown of the Elite Squad.
You can buy weapons in your shantytown but for it you need money.

Money:
To gain money kill the shantytown residents, the Drugs Dealers. Killing an enemy you gain $1000
Remember don't kill friends or you lost money and score.
