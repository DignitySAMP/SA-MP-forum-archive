Fixed:

_________________________________________________

FILTERSCRIPT COMMANDS
Commands for players:

/killb - kill youself with bomb
/bt - to create timer on vehicle
/bd - to create detomator on vehicle
/cb - to create bomb on place
/dv - to activate detomator on vehicle
/db - to explose bomb
To deactivate: /ccb - bomb /cct - timer /ccd - detonator
/bs - teleport to the Bomb Shop

Commands for admins:

/setbt [playerid] [time] [0(no) or 1(yes)] - to create timer on player's vehicle
/setbd [playerid] [0(no) or 1(yes)] - to create detonator on player's vehicle
/explose [playerid] - explose player
/abomb - allow/disallow automatically creating bombs on vehicles
/setbombv [playerid] - to create bomb on player's vehicle
