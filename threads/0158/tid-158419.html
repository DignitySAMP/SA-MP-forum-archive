My first GM. Made with monkeyboybearcub!

This GM lets you experience life on Mount Chilliad, either as a zombie, human, soldier, or medic. You have absolutely no warning when the next zombification will strike, or any other attack.

This is a fairly simple gamemode, but it took us awhile to make it.

View /help for the commands and everything!

Bugs and stuff:
•Going up the Mountain there is a missing fence and it looks terrible. Don't go through it. It'll lead you off track!

•There is a V.I.P system! It's used for the ARMY and MEDIC as they're VIP only! If you don't want the VIP system you'll have to edit the teams too!

We would appreciate it if you left the /credits alone.
But still, you can remove it if you want.

Changelog:

All the VIP help commands were removed but not removed from the /cmds list. It still is named MCI 1.2 and not MCI 1.3! You cannot choose Army or Medic anymore. I am removing it until I fix all of it.
