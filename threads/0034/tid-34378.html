The Camera Editing Filterscript v1.1

Description:
Well this is an FS i made based on the Flying Filterscript and Zezombia camera positioner.
The difference is that u can move the camera with the keys.

Commands:
/campos - Start the camera pos editing.
/camlook - Start the camera look editing.
/resetcam - Reset the camera.
/camspeed [speed] - With this command you can change the camera movement speed.

Keys:
WASD - Camera X/Y Control
Space and Shift - Camera Z Control
Mouse 1 - Changes to Look/Pos mode while editing the camera

Updates:

Version 1.1:
*Now you can edit the camera speed

*Better Camera Streaming
