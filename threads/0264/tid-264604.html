Hello People.
I am releasing my Gamemode, because i don't want to use it on my server as it isn't a heavy RP one, and my server is.
This GameMode is A bit of freeroam with RP.... I can call it a ligh RP.

I will list some of its feactures. Feel free to edit it, if you don't remove the credits.
The credits goes to me, and for some other scripters of this comunity, that i didn't write their name and can't find it anymore....
Big thanks for Cali-Houses system, and Dybanic vehicle ownership.
I am using their systems, the others are all made by me.
Feactures:

House system, /buyhouse anywhere, and ask an administrator to accept it.
Vehicle system, i am now creating some cars, in the the usual dealerships.
Jobs system: You can easily join any job with /become(the job name), or an admin can set it for you.
Faction system: If you lead some faction/gang, you will be able to invite members there.
Payday system: Diferent jobs have diferent payments.
Bank System: /balance, /deposit, /withdraw AND /getcredit... Yes, we have a creit system, that allow you to ask for a credit in the bank, and it will take you 2k in each payday to pay it.
Admin system: /ah to see the commands and /makeadmin to make admins. It has 5 levels.... It is RAdmin(I released it at Sa-MP forums).
Register/login system: In dialogs.
Save system: It is saving using dini.
Some tips:

You can earn money alone by trucking, bus driver, and streets sweeper.

I hope that you enjoy...
Just go and see.

More updates will come later, make your suggestions.
