This is an unusual thread, it doesn't happen very often but this is an appreciation thread to Beta Tester JernejL.

JernejL has unfortunately left the beta team of SA-MP and we thank him for all of his work during the 10 years at SA-MP and we wish you well on his journey to pastures new.

To those that don't know his work he created the amazing SA-MP Map Editor, Math Plugin, Vehicle Data Extraction to enable Kalcor to implement those amazing functions we have today and much more behind the scene assistance to Kalcor.
