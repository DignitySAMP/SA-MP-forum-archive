Block Pandemonium 1.0

This is the follow-up thread to: https://forum.sa-mp.com/showthread.php?tid=448258. I've turned include into a filterscript, thought this minigame would be fun to play with your friends. You start the minigame by typing '/block', at least one of your friends needs to join in order for minigame to start.

Objective: Run around the arena to turn blocks into your color. Win by having the most blocks of your color when time runs out.

Video: Capturing the blocks, how it works?

[ame]http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gzRQiSBik1k[/ame]

Video is not about the minigame. It's about two guys running around the arena.

Images: Lets look from a bird view, how minigame works?

(1) (2) (3)

Gametext below is showing scores during the minigame. There's a time limit of 90 seconds (I've changed to 60 in the downloadable version).

1) First image shows the starting position of each player.
2) Second image shows red being the winner by having most blocks when time has ran out.
3) Third image shows a draw.

Notes: There might be bugs, since I haven't really tested properly. Filterscript (as well as include) does not use streamed objects nor pickups until further notice. If you really need that, you have the liberty to edit
