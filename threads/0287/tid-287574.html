If you got 2 monitors then it's an not so common but known bug. There are 3-4 ways to solve it:

1. Just desible your 2dr monitor when you play sa-mp. (Maybe not so smart if you like to have the monitor on.)
2. Set your Visual FX quality to low. (Always works but makes your game look a bit less impressive^^)
3. Rename your GTA Sanandreas foulder! Yes you heard right and yes it works. So just rename "C:\...\Rockstar Games\GTA San Andreas" to something else like "C:\...\Rockstar Games\GTA San Andreas2" (This works but sometimes it just stops working again so you need to rename it to something else...
4. Install sandboxie: http://www.sandboxie.com/ then run your gta inside of the sandbox and it should work. (I can't test right now but if I remember it fixes the problem. Also note this can fix many wired problems with sa-mp.)
