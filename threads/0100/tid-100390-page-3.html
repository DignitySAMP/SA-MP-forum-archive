Host: ServerFFS

IP/Port: N/A
Purchased: Around October 2009

Rate support: (1-10): 5
Rate pricing: (1-10): 6
Rate performance: (1-10): 5
Rate ordering: (1-10): 9
Rate control panel: (1-10): 10

Things that make this host amazing: The control panel is such an awesome job, awesome features and easy managment.
Things that make this host horrible: Live support, setup time, performance
Will you renew: Probably not.
Moving to another host: Bought a VPS from another company

Comments:
The guy on the live support told me to try his solution and come back If It didn't work,
and first off, the solution didn't work, second off, the guy LEFT as the ONLY guy there, leaving me with no support at all.

The setup time I remember took ~5 hours, which Is not acceptable for such company.
The prices could be better - If the support were more professional I wouldn't complain.
Ordering was easy - fast, and secure, that is a big +.

Their design is professional and looks really good, and their custom control panel is professional, advanced and filled with features - yet so easy to use.

The performance was bad, I remember getting messages about overriding a CPU usage limit, which was really stupid, I'm paying for the host, I should be able to host a big gamemode.

My players also complained about lag - often, even though their ping was average or good.

So I'd give them 6/10 overall.

+/-

- Support
- Performance
- Easy to override their CPU usage limit with big gamemodes
- Lag
- Prices (due to their performance and support issues)

+ Control panel
+ Ordering
+ Managment of your server

I hope this is helping you in your choice of server hosting.
