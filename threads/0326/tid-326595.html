That's not really necessary. Having tons and tons of lines doesn't mean your script is huge. For example, I have a 17k lines gamemode. It's a roleplay script. It has like: all player, admin, helper, vip, newbie commands. Dynamic Factions, Business, Houses & Vehicles. It has a unique Dealership idea. Has unique fishing system, few unique jobs. Of course it's not complete yet, it won't ever be but I'm pretty sure that if I reach 30k lines (if that'll ever happen), the system will be pretty much complete.

So, overall, yes, making a RP gamemode can take some time but you can always make the base of a roleplay script. Commands, a small, nice and unique vehicle system, few houses, a pretty new idea of businesses, few unique jobs and that should do it. You can start your server from there and let your players "model" your gamemode through suggestions. At least, that's in my opinion the best idea. Having a huge RP script, which it's pretty much done isn't that good idea. You should let your players come with unique ideas.

A simple DM server can take days and days to develop. Don't even think you'll be done with RP script in a week. I'm telling you right now, prepare yourself with tons of patient, spare time and determination. It'll take you weeks, at least, if not months to make a decent roleplay gamemode.

ps: Pretty much every roleplay server is based in Los Santos because people are used with the city. It has some poor zones (ghettos), which are perfect for gang roleplay and it has rich zones, for mafias and stuff.

Placing Gangs in Las Venturas, just doesn't add up. So, if you're planning to start the server in Las Venturas, don't even expect to place gangs.

And you'll have to use many tips, guides so that players will know all the important locations and stuff.
