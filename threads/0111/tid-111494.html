Right so I've been thinking of a few gamemode ideas, some minigames and some full plot gamemodes and I've decided to pass them on as I'm pretty much done with scripting (bored) but I'm sure people would enjoy playing some of these.

The different scripts would be:

Saw - seen the movie ? It would be like a big fun house, you would try and escape and do puzzles to advance through it, some puzzles would require help and some not so you have to use tactics to survive (don't just kill all other players).
We could use bots to simulate some players (trapped in cages etc) and use like the LV meat factory interiors and body parts etc, we could make it pretty sick looking hehehe. I'm sure tons of traps and nasty ways to die in Samp could be thought of and scripted.

Titanic, Psideon - this would be a huge maze of rooms (custom interiors) on a few different levels which again would be puzzle based (but mostrly mazes) so you need to work together to get out and this one would be timed obviously as the ship would be sinking (this is why we need custom interiors, so we can move the objects). If you chose the wrong path and the ship had sunk too far you wouldn't be able to get out and you have killed yourself etc (round based, gmx).

Kamakazi/Car darts - little mini game, think of the sumo rings most people use in thier sumo servers and then think about a road above this (offset to the side and raised then tilted) which we drive down and we go over a ramp (on the road) and try to land in certain spots. This would actually take skill as you would have to control your direction and speed to control where you land on the board and after a while you would get good at it.

Well that's about it for now, I haven't given them too much thought as you can see, well that and the fact that I suck at explaining

I just thought I would share the ideas as I probably won't make them myself and I'm sure some people are always trying to think of unique things for thier servers so there you go.

Please feel free to discuss these ideas, help expand them or design them etc.
