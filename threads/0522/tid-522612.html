Hello all. You know the title. If you have coded SCM/CLEO Past you know there is an Opcode :

Code:
09E1: get_car_model $IMPORT_CARS_MODEL(4@,6i) price_to $IMPORT_CAR_PRICE
Which returns the Price of Vehicle model you given. So Today i planned to write a CLEO Custom script Will loops all vehicle models (400 to 611). Detects prices. and saves them to an INI File.

Why this is useful?
Because i wanted to make a vehicle spawner But vehicles spawned are not free. Cars with different prices. So i maked this script to detect Original SA Car prices. And i want to share it with you.

Is The Car Prices True?
Yes. And i think it's good for RP Servers. As o it used in Import/Export Cars in Single-player.
For example. SuperGT Is $105000. Which Bloodring Banger is $500. Also Hydra is $45000. I Don't know why

I Just wanted to share it. It's your choice to use or not.
