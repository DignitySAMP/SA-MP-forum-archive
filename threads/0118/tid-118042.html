Hi all , this is my first release . I have a lot of my own filterscripts and one roleplay gamemod , all of this is on Croatian language . Bugs : I don't know for any bug. This filterscript use MidoStream , so make sure you have it.
This script is really fun and I decide to release it here . This script have 10 NPC's and about 100 objects . I work on it 1 hour , that is a short work time but script is very fun . I translate script to the English language, here is some pictures of Croatian version of <<Caffe Balcan>> filterscript.











Commands :
/enterc - to enter,
/exitc - to exit,
/menu - meni of the food and drinks,
/roof - to go on roof,
/beer - to drink beer,
/juice - to drink juice,
/cigarete - to smoke cigarete,
/parachute - to get parachute.


How to install :
1. Unzip the pack
2. Copy everything from filterscripts and paste to your filterscripts in your server directory2. Copy everything from filterscripts and paste to your filterscripts in your server directory
3. Everything from npcmodes copy to your npcmodes in your server directory
4. Add BalcanCaffe to your server.cfg
5. Change maxnpc 50 in server.cfg to number higher of 10
6. Make sure you don't have register and login system in your gamemod or read tutorial about if(IsPlayerNPC(playerid)) return 1;
7. Enjoy
