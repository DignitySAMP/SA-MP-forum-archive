Both the above answers are ridiculously non-technical and won't work.

If this is what I think it is, then this is a common GTA SA problem, however it's a weird one to do with DirectInput and often doesn't occur straight away or will work sometimes and not others. Going in and out of the menu tends to fix it.

The only definite answer I know involves a mod ("MouseFix" by the Japanese Ryosuke), as they have to patch DInput bugs, but something tells me I shouldn't post a link here because it might lead to the apocalypse of the SA:MP forum, so just try to see if it works on single player first and then the rest is up to you.

Alternatively, a ****** search also pulled up this rather unusual answer: http://gtaforums.com/topic/449363-gt...ntry1060372454

However I wouldn't recommend replacing the DInput8.dll file as he suggests, and instead placing it into your GTA SA installation folder (I hope that's not considered modding - ugh) and testing it. It might be a false find though - test for a while to make sure the bug doesn't come back at all, because it's known to be an occasional problem.
