How do you legally copyright something

Look at that link, and if NGG has done that, they it is legally copyrighted, if not, you can use it if you have the script (to my best knowledge)

Copyright, in the US, UK, and most other western countries is automatic as soon as it is committed to a 'tangible medium'.

The so-called "poor mans copyright" of mailing something to yourself is pure myth of NO legal validity at all.

To register a copyright - which is not legally necessary, but gives 'some' extra rights, go to www.copyright.gov in the US, www.ipo.gov.uk in the UK, or your countries equivalent body.
