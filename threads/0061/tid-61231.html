Fallout's SpeedoMeter (Updated)



Features:
- Everything is made with textdraws
- You can see the vehicle's name, a healthbar, the speed in m/h and km/h and there is a speed bar.
- The speedometer appears when you enter any vehicle, and disappears when you exit.
- When your vehicle is on fire, you'll see an exclamation sign on the speedometer
