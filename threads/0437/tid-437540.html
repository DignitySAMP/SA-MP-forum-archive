Introduction

Back in the day when I used to be working on TwD Freeroam script, I also had started working on a black market system. What my theme was is I would make the sell-able weaponry for cheap, it was quite fine until I decided to stop working on the server. This was left in middle and was not completed. Today, while organizing my computer, I found this map. I finalized and added a little touch, more like brushed the dust off and thought to release it. It is pretty basic because not much time I've given to it. This map might be good for Freeroam or Freeroam/Role-Play gaming modes. The actual black market script, beside this mapping is also stacked in my drive, once I get time, which will be next weekend or so; I will finish that off and release it.
