Anyway, my point was that you can script an amazing server with everything as it is in real life. However, you will have to find players that can work well with what you have scripted and improvise well with what you don't.

I used to play on an epic RP server with all the economy and everything fully scripted. However, without any of the global/b/s/occ bullshit. Only a few jobs that required scripting such as calculating taxes, prices and diseases spreading rate etc. Scripted stuff got added when needed, none of those million commands you can never learn!
