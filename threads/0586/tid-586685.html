There's no complaints but I would mention checking the IP addresses under their test servers it seems to be one OVH server and active-servers.com, active-servers.com has no DDoS protection so better make sure you're not under that host.

Their demo account at https://www.samp4you.com/demo doesn't work and as you can tell a lot of test servers are offline and their ******** page hasn't been updated in months, leads me to believe you can expect slow support responses since they can't even keep up with upkeep for their online services.

Also to mention that it seems they only have one server available to order servers on which means they could be overselling.

There's nothing to really tell you besides the fact some things seem a bit sketchy other than that you would need to try them yourself. I personally could never go with such a company that doesn't take care of their responsibilities of keeping their website maintained. A good example as I explained was the fact that the test servers some are offline and the demo account doesn't work but also the English translation has not been completed for over a year as you can see from a website archive from July 2014 https://web.archive.org/web/20140707....samp4you.com/ The site clearly has the English logo on the top right side yet it is still in beta and has never been fully completed.
