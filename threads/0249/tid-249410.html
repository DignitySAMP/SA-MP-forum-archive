.: ^^ Zombie FilterScript | All things | ^^:.


.: About that :.
It’s zombie filterscript, where people can be zombie. You can make some object zombie too . That object zombie is make by zeruel_angel . Its littlebit edited by me. Other things are maked by me . Have a fun and download this . There is only 5 commands, but 19 dialogs . There is some random messages too, but you can delete that . If someone buy or someone make someonje to zombie, then come gametext and a lot of things !

.: Commands :.
/Zombie --> It’s for ZombieAdmin . You can be ZombieAdmin . Its show you dialog (pic 1)
/ZombieShop --> You can choose what zombie you wan’t to be and after that you can choose the time .
/ZombieRegister --> First you need to use that to register.
/ZombieLogin --> Every time you need to login by using that .
/SickertCmd --> You can first time get admin by that and then you can delete that command.

.: Zombie Shop :.
You can buy zombies for time and price :
5 MIN --> It is $750.000
10 MIN --> It is $800.000
15 MIN --> It is $850.000
20 MIN --> It is $900.000
25 MIN --> It is $950.000
30 MIN --> It is $1.000.000
If your time is over, you get message, like your time is now ended !(Pic 2)

.: Zombie Styles :.
You can choose zombies :
Chainsaw Zombie --> Its give you chainsaw .
Knife Zombie --> It give you knife
Dildo Zombie --> It give you dildo
Shovel Zombie --> It give you dildo .

YEa there is : If you buy zombie 30 min, there is GivePlayerMoney(playerid, -10000000); , but its need to be GivePlayerMoney(playerid, -1000000); I fix it on v1.1 if that comes .

.: Download :.
V 1.0
PWN and AMX : SolidFiles
PasteBin(But that FS have scriptfiles too)

.: Install :.
If you want to complite it, you need that :
Code:
#include <zcmd>
#include <dini>
#include <dudb>
#include <colors>
#include <sscanf2>
That you get there, where is writed "PWN and AMX" and you need to add that .

You need to copy all things to the folders, what there are . When you have copyed, you need to add in server.cfg :
Code:
filterscripts ZombieFS
And then in server.cfg for homehost :
Code:
plugins sscanf
Or when you have linux server, then use :
Code:
plugins sscanf.so
Then press "save" and thats work .
