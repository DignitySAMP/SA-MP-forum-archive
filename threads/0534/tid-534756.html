Here is a few:

Last Man Standing: Players stand on the wings of a playne (preferbly stunt or dodo) and the pilot has to shake them off over time. Last one remaining on the wing wins.

Dodge The Shamal: Players stand on a tall flat skyscraper and attempt to dodge the pilot who flys at them. Experience pilots required. A good area for this is the skyscraper near flint county bridge.

Hide n Seek: Server admin hunts players inside a big interior (maddog, crack house ect.) and the last one alive wins. Server admin must turn off his/her radar though.

Races / Triathlon


Just a few I did when I was admin of a certain pilot server.


You could also search the filterscript section for various other events such as a spelling one or moneybag. There are plenty of great scripts there,
