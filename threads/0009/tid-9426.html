TOW TRUCK MINIMISSION!
(las venturas EDITION)

Like in single player game, if you enter a special vehicle (in this case Tow Truck MODEL ID=525) You get a message
saying that you can start special Minimission just by pressing "buttom 2". You can cancell it anytime by just by
pressing "buttom 2" again. If you get off the vehicle for more than 10 sec during a MiniMission, you will cancell
it. For every 5 times you complete this minimission, you will get a $PECIAL BONU$. All just like in Single Player
Game.

Just follow instructions in "readme.txt" to install this MiniMission to your server.
It includes PWN and AMX, Readme.txt with instructions.
TowTruck.txt Has 25 locations of wrecked vehicles in Las Venturas (you can change them if you like)

Details:
Wrecked vehicles are set randomly beetwen all the locations in TowTruck.txt, Wrecked Vehicle can randomly be a
Wrecked car or a wrecked truck. Vehicles color are random too. Wrecked Vehicles are created when a mission start,
and destroyed when a mission ends, be sure to have enought place for new cars to be created (700 max vehilces and 70
max different model with 0.2.1 server). Wrecked cars are LOCKED,(a timer makes sure that is locked for everyone
everytime). A raceCheckpoint near the wrecked car FIXS the SYNC vehicle bug in the server. (when you see a car in
some place but the server "thinks" is in other place)

BUGS: If the Towed car explodes, you will need to cancel the mission and start it over again... (THANKS EvilWind )

Now tell me, whats the problem with car respawn? There is a server bug about car respawning, the cars spawn else where, but not where they are sopouse to spawn, thats why I used a racecheckpoint to attach the car. As you can see in he code, the Racecheckpoint is created IN FRONT of the car, but you will see that sometimes the race checkpoint is far away from the car, this is because for the SERVER the car is somewhere and for you (the CLIENT) the car is elsewhere. Its a SYNC bug not a CODE BUG.
Its the same when you are playing and you see a player enter an invisible vehicle and then the vehicle spawn there, thats because for you the car was far away but for the other player the car was just there... Again a SYNC bug.

If the problem is different, please let me know so I can fix it.
