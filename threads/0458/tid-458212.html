Car Customization

With Wheel Arch Angels 2.0

By EvanA(Evan Abagail)


So when people kept saying "Godmode is all you can come up with?" I knew I had to do something unique. So I came up with this. Its actually pretty simple. Its a car customizer for admins. It uses pAdmin so it should work quite fine with your admin system.

Plug-Ins:
ZCMD[Click here for the thread]

Commands:
/gr /cr /goldrims /cutterrims /rimshine /mega /shadow /grove /import /dollar /trance /hyd /convertable /stereo /pro /win

/gr || /goldrims || Adds gold rims to your vehicle.
/cr || /cutterrims|| Adds cutter rims to your vehicle.
/mega || Adds "mega" wheels to your vehicle.
/shadow || Adds "shadow" wheels to your vehicle
/grove || Adds "grove" wheels to your vehicle.
/import || Adds "import" wheels to your vehicle.
/dollar || Adds "dollar" wheels to your vehicle.
/trance || Adds "trance" wheels to your vehicle.
/hyd || Adds hydraulics to your vehicle.
/convertable(V2)|| Places a roof on your convertable. Not tested with not compatible vehicle. Only use on convertables to avoid errors/bugging up the server.
/stereo(V2) || Install a stereo system in your vehicle.
/pro || Add a pro spoiler to your vehicle. Only use on compatible vehicles. Not tested with vehicles that aren't compatible.
/win || Add a win spoiler to your vehicle. Only use on compatible vehicles. Not tested with vehicles that aren't compatible.
