You cant learn scripting by watching videos or listening to "teachers".
There are things that you can only learn by trying. This includes many hours of fails and "wasted" time, but thats absolutely normal and neccessary.
The first few gamemode you create will always be a big mess, because you need to gain that practical experience from them, and thats why trying to create a real gamemode for a real server first, without that previous painful experience, always is a bad idea.

You want a teacher? Heres probably the best lesson any teacher could give you:
1. Try
2. Fail
3. Learn from the fails
4. Repeat 1-3 until the amount of fails gets acceptable
5. Create a gamemode
