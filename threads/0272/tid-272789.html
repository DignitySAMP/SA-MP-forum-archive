VBMail - Emails system
__________________

By: ViniBorn


Description :

With this FS you can:

- Send messages to offline players
- Keep e-mails received in your inbox
- Delete e-mails


To begin the exchange of messages, both players must have a computer.
It can be purchased by /buypc

After purchasing, simply use the command /email.

The FS works with dialogs, which increases the interaction and facilitates understanding.

- Indication of e-mails (Read) or (not read)
