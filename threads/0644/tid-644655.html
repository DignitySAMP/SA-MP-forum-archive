Hey,

since SA:MP introduced custom models, I started working on a project which allows anyone to upload their own custom models to share it with the SA:MP community. We only accept models, which comply with the SA:MP rules.

Website: https://www.samp-models.net

Features:
* Unlimited Projects (the term "Project" means the representation of a model or a collection of models)
* Enough download servers and space
* Project Comments (will be activated by one of our staff members - except for members which are registered since at least 4 weeks)
* Project Categories
* Project Stars (like on GitHub)
* All projects will be reviewed by our staff members, before they go online (to ensure no viruses are uploaded or other bullshit)
* Hosting of your dff and txd files for your SA:MP server. 50 MB per user. (Possibility of using the pawn native RedirectDownload to our services, so that your users don't consume your traffic; increases download speed) (Click here for details)

Planned Features/Changes:
* Report System (Project or Comment report)
* Searching via categories
* User notifications (for project/comment activations etc.)

If you would like to see a new feature, you can also leave a comment in this thread or leave me a PM.
