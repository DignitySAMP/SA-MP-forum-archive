These are such stupid questions and the answers people give are way too short.

The best scripter?
That's so vague.
  • I'm sure there are scripters that are not active on this forum (I for example registered in 2009, but only started posting frequently the last few months).
  • What does define 'best'?
    • Programming in a certain style?
    • Writing very clean code?
    • Using certain techniques?
    • Optimizing for memory usage?
    • Optimizing for performance?
    • Knowing how to write plugins?
    • Being active on the forums?
    • Writing advanced tutorials?
    • Helping lots of people in the Scripting Help forums?
    • Making lots of big releases?
    • Creating a plugin that thousands use?
    • ... do I need to continue?
The right question to ask would be:
Who do you think has contributed the most to this community?
And still, that question is vague as well.
You would have to give lots of arguments why you consider someone the 'best scripter'. Don't just say a name. Give some examples.

The best script?
Again, this is vague.
That's like asking: What do you consider to be the best game? And saying the name of a game without explaining why.
I'm sure there are people that just play one 1 server. Say for example an RP server.
Is it because one day they visited the server, saw something they liked and never sought another one? There might be a server with similar features, that has more players.
Does it mean the first server is worse than the second? Maybe, maybe not. Probably not for that player, because he enjoys playing on his regular server.
Does that mean there aren't more exciting scripts with lots of action? Yeah there definitely are. But that doesn't mean that player will like that play style.

So to end, the best script? I'm just going to say mine, even though it isn't even live.
The best scripter? I'm going to point at myself, since I think I have learned a lot in the past few years.
(The stupid answers are trying to prove my point.)
