Hello Mr.Bob

Filterscript name: Hello Mr.Bob
Filterscript lines: 231
Author: Tr1viUm
Function: This filterscript allows you to enter the cargobob's back.
Commands: /cargo /cargoout
Other enlightenment: When you enter a cargobob it sets your time to 00:00 so the lights will turn on. It will be turned back when you leave the cargobob. When the cargobob gets destroyed the players in it will die to. When it isn't working, get in the cargobob as driver and try it again(Desync).
