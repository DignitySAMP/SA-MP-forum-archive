Hi !

Here are two maps : one of the outside of the building, and one of the interior.

The exterior is no big deal. I mainly added objects in the parking and I changed the trees in front of the faзade.

I changed nearly all the fournitures inside the building, in order to create new rooms. As the mapping was created following some goals, here are informations about what you see in the video :
  • Gates you see in the door openings should be movable.
  • From 01:35 to 01:43, you can see a cell the might be use for custody. Just to keep your suspects in a cell during investigation, or when you have a lot of work. So it's not the main cell/jail.
  • From 02:27 to 2:40, the room is the jail office. In the server I used to manage a few years ago, this room was used to search the prisoners, check their records, manage the jail, etc.
  • At 2:40, you see two gates. They should be used as a security lock in order to reach the jail, made of the 3 cells and the corridor. So the whole you see at the video is the jail.
