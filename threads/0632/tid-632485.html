Hi all,

So long story short SAMP is a hobby of mine that I recently picked up again after a year or so. SAMP offers shit that I can't find in other games. For more than 1.5 year I have an (in my eyes) amazing idea for a gamemode that's - as far as I know - unique in SAMP. Hell, I even wrote down major parts of it and I have like 20 pages of handwritten notes of ideas. In addition to that I have an idea to target players outside SAMP as I think there's some potential there.

Now I can't find reliable sources on whether or not the playerbase grows or declines and what geographical areas and language groups are most popular.

Money to rent reliable servers isn't a problem, scripting isn't a problem either (although I will have to relearn some stuff after 2 years), the next few months I will only work part-time so there's some time to spend. On the other hand I lost most contacts in SAMP so I have to start from scratch when it comes to finding people to work with (key position players, staff), it'd be a huge time commitment and I don't know if it's all worth it.

What is your opinion? Would SAMP still work for a fresh idea?
