What does it do?

Allows you to control another players movements through the use of animations and key press checks. The player you control will be unable to do anything except move there head and attempt to enter vehicles.

Commands

/control [ID]

/ceasecontrol

Controls

Up, left, right, key_sprint, key_walk

Bugs

- In some cases the player you control may still be able to move for a short time until the animations lock in properly.
- If your gamemode or another filterscript has animation commands, it may conflict with this filterscript.
