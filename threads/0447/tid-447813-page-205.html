What is Polarian Gaming?
Polarian Gaming is an upcoming San Andreas Multiplayer Roleplay server that will be based in the city of Los Santos. Our goal is to bring back the middle ground of roleplay. We have learned that in 2018, there seems to be two spectrums of roleplay and Medium Roleplay is never really explored anymore. We want to bring that back. Using the NGG script as our base, we are working on creating an interesting and unique experience for players by giving them a comfortable place to roleplay.

WHAT DO WE NEED
Currently, Polarian Gaming is looking for the following:

STAFF
The staff team of Polarian Gaming could use a few more inclusions. The following is what we need:
  • Two admins, preferably from the OC or EU timezones
  • Two or three people to serve as Community Helpers
  • Potentially a scripter (we will not be paying for scripting services)
FACTIONS
In addition to staff, we could also use the following for factions:
  • A person to serve as the leader of the Fire Department
  • A person to serve in the Government. We are considering having our own Government, not one based under the United States. This will give those in the server true freedom of how they want things ran.
HOW TO CONTACT US
While you can certainly PM this account, we highly recommend you contact us by one of these methods:
  • Joining our Discord and pming either Shplink or Wilson
  • Emailing either shplink@pg-rp.net or wilson@pg-rp.net
  • Joining our forums at pg-rp.net/forums/forum.php and PMing Shplink or Wilson
