Location Information - Beta v1
Hello all!
This is an application we made (see credits) for some information on interior places. With this, you will see:

1. The name of the place.
2. A screen shot of the place.
3. The location of the place
4. The interior ID of the place.

With the pictures, I took the outside of the place if it was possible to get out from the coordinates. Else, I just took what it looked like from the inside. You DO need to be connected to the internet for the pictures to load.

Do you really think you have traveled all of San Andreas? WRONG. During the small time I had making this, I have probably discovered more about San Andreas and how truly powerful of a mod SA-MP is then some of you during your whole time playing games by Rockstar.

Some interesting think I've found is that SA-MP hasn't completely taken out all of the weapon pickups default in San Andreas. Two pistols, and two bats. I've also found what the Andromeda looks like from above, look for it in the application. I've been to the map of the very last level, and I've been to the floorless place were CJ was filmed taking the call that his mother had died at the very beginning. And this is only the beginning. I'm not gonna go into it. Try it out for yourself for more information on that.
