Firework sphere
Where I got the idea

Some days ago I was looking for cool firework system, but i didn’t found nothing, so I thought to make some by my own. And when I finished making, I thought I will share it with you guys.

System details

Player can place firework box on the ground by command (/placefirework). And then player have to enter command (/startfirework) for starting firework.

Commands

1) /placefirework [Height] [Radius] [Amount]
2) /startfirework [ID]
3) /changetime [Time] (Between 1-6 effects comes out best way)

Download

http://pastebin.com/2sJMJQdy

If you like this system then you can +rep
