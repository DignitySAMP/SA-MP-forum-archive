hello, i made a nice GM for creating movies/clips
31 menus
25 commands
25 animations
12 teleports

its a nice & simple game mode
Commands
/anims or /animations
/veh or /vehicles
/setskin
/setscore (score)
/weather (weather ID)
/CE or /CreateExplosion (create explosion at /se)
/givehealth (health)
/givearmour (armour)
getcamera - saves the player position for /lookat
/getcameralook - same thing..
/lookat - look from the saved position to other saved position
/res - restore the camera behind the player
/clearchat or /cc - clear chat for you..
/savediepos - spawn at the place you died
/SE or /SaveExp - /CE..
/SetTime
/SetInterior or /SetInt or /SI
/teleports or /tp
/giveweap[0-3]
/SetVW or /SetVirtualWorld
