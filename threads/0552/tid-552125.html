pawn Code:

BigBrother.isWatching(You) //What's my big brother doing? o.O

Power=a=means=it=an=end=0
Power is not (a, means, it is (an, end)) //"Power is not a means; it is an end." -George Orwell

war=peace=freedom=slavery=ignorance=strength=0
war is peace|freedom is slavery|ignorance is strength //"War is Peace; Freedom is Slavery; Ignorance is Strength;" -INGSOC (also George Orwell)

dont = []; y = 1
dont.count + y or ('chickens'.before('they hatch')) //Don't count your chickens before they hatch! (another idiom)

$all = array('not well', 'not well', 'not well', 'well'); //All is well! (Just a saying)
$end = array_pop($all);
$all = ($end == 'well') ? $end : 'not well';
return true;

assert(life != fair); //Life is not fair. (An opinion of some)

return (Do || !Do) || (there == !try); //"Do. Or do not. There is no try." -Yoda (SW)
