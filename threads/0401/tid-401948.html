Pizza Boy FS

1.0.010
Introduction

This script is not a copy of any gamemode job's systems. This is unique scripted Filterscript for Well Stacked Pizza Co. near Ganton Gym.

Futures

- Checks if you are a pizza boy before entering a pizza boy 'Intruder'
- Checks if you are already delivering pizzas so you can't change direction when already delivering
- Mapped place just for the 'job' on the left side of Well Stacked Pizza Co. near Ganton Gym
- 3D Text Lables to see where is the job join point and where is the point from where you get the pizzas
- Message system(Just the normal SendClientMessage) to notify you whenever you do something in-game.
- 3 commands(/join,/acceptjob,/deliverpizza)
