Just a teleport menu I created and added some teleports in for you, feel free to change the teleports to your desired locations.

Description:
This simple teleport menu works by typing /teleport to bring up the menu. The menu has 4 categories Los Santos, San Fierro, Las Venturas and Other. Each category has a number of different teleport locations in it.

Pictures:











Instructions and Downloads:
Place the tele.pwn and tele.amx into your filterscripts folder.
Open server.cfg and add tele to the filterscripts line.

tele.rar (.amx and .pwn) Solidfiles
