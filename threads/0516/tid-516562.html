Okay guys so I couldn't actually find anything to with a pay n toll so I decided to make my own!

- This doesn't include anything dynamic as I'm literally brand new to mapping.

Four images added into a folder for ease of vision.

*Click me for images*

Features.

+ Added a barrier across the road to prevent players entering LV via the main highway. (This stops them reaching the prison I'm making out at Area 51!)
+ Added a Generator by the side of the control room so players don't go down the sides with their cars.
+ Arrows to direct specific users correctly. - I found people go down the wrong side of the roads some times so I added arrows for the side they need to go through.
