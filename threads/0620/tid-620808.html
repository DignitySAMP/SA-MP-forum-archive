Well done, I like your concept as well as the wildlife AI.

In my opinion this is one of the most original concepts I've seen in a while. I see you're doing well with attracting new possible players and fans, which is great, however, to maintain a good chunk of those players I think it's a necessity to not allow players getting bored after they have explored all the great features you have decided to add. I would love to see some kind of shift more towards an RPG or any softer Roleplay based gamemode, with for example fun (weekly/daily) missions (maybe unlocked by talking to NPC's?), or other fun activities which can be played as if it were singleplayer.

This allows for a nice mixture between roleplaying and personal goals/achievements to work on when the server is relatively quite, or when you don't feel like hardcore roleplaying. This will keep the game just a little more interesting in the long run for me personally.

Just felt like I'd leave my thoughts on this matter. Overall, looks like a great team бnd concept! Good luck!
