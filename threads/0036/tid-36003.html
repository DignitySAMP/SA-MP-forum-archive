Hello everyone. This is my first release that i'm releasing, altho i had many others never came out.
I hope everyone will like. It's a headquater system. I was bored in adding them, but it's quite simple. Each headquater requires 6 coordinates. (4 for vehicles, 1 for outside, 1 for inside)
Commands:
Rcon Admin commands:
/hqout [Sets the position of hq outside]
/hqname [sets the name of hq, for example /hqname lol]
/hqcar[1-4] (without space) [ saves the positon of u and uses it for hq car 1-4 (you must be in a vehicle)
/hqcartype (hqid) (carmodel) [sets the model of headquater cars]
/hqcars (hqid) (maxcars[1-4]) [ sets the amount of cars that spawn near a hq. Requires restart. max is 4.]
/hqcost (hqid) (cost) [ sets the price of (hqid) to (cost) ]
/hqlevel (hqid) (level) [sets the (hqid) to a certain (level) ]
/hqowner (hqid) (teamid) [ Sets the team that owns the hq. Using GetPlayerTeam and SetPlayerTeam ]

Player Commands:
/hqbuy [explains itself]
/hqbuycar [adds 1 more car to your hq. Costs 50k]
/hqdoor [enters/exits the hq. Depends on your pos. IF inside exits, etc. ]
