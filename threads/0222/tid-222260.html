• Insanity Caps •

- Description

It allows you to wear caps with any of the skins game(skins thats no have caps/hat/etc), you can use the caps typing the command /cap [1 to 9] and to remove type /remove.

- Author

Allan Jader (CyNiC)
Zeex(with the zcmd include)

- Notes

You can change how much you want the filterscript, leaving the credit to the
author.
*Some skins already have a hat/cap so they are invalid for this.



Download

http://www.4shared.com/file/dLzDfc3z/Insanity_Caps.html
