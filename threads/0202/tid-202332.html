Ambition
I can remember a few years ago, I started playing SA:MP, joining communities and generally just trying to help about. I've created a few scripts in my time, but I thought I should go further and create something which could be helpful for everyone - some sort of website. After a lot of thinking and planning, I finally came to a decision that if I created a website which could control a server, it could greatly benefit people. So, I started to learn the basics of website creation, and advanced onto more harder things, until I finally came to the stage where I could make my ideas possible. So here I am, developing SA:MP - Content Management System, which will hopefully be successful and help out a lot of communities which are in need of something of this sort.
