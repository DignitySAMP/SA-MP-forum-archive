I read plenty of books last year, but I really enjoyed reading this one:
Blindness

The author won a Nobel Prize with it.

It's a story about how a disease spreads over a city, turning all citizens to behave like animals. Underneath the story, it is actually about how modern society is full of apathy.

I was surprised by how accessible the book was; a previous Nobel Prize book I read was hard to read and very boring.
