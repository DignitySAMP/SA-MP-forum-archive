ProjectX RPG,
Was ofcourse an RPG.
It was scripted by myself and soeren.
It is kind of old, but does come with some good idea's to people wishing to make RPG's or RP servers.
The Sever is very unique with many diffrent things

It was overall a light RP.
But you can just change that quite simply.

- A unique job system,
For instance the PD, you join as a community support officer and have acess to limited vehicles,
Once you have enough of the server currency you can pay to promote yourself,
With this there then goes on to multiple stages, You have choices in your path of work, And can choice for instance to be a prision guard or become an Air Support Unit or a regular officer.
There's endless things you can do and learn from this.

-The Pcash system,
Soeren is one of the original people from WestSide RPG witch invented the Rcash and so we have our own server currency witch is rather nice becuase it's got a centemental value too it and is alot more enjoyable than your avarage doller's witch gets rather boring.
The Pcash system is rather good.

-Leveling System.
So on as GF style,
"BUT"
We have a banking system there is 3 banks located in LV LS and SF, you have to be in the bank at every hour to receive your bank check.
Witch is 30 Pcash,
With this also comes a upgrade point, once you have the required ammount you go to the LV chappel and you can level up, tutorials and guidence is given ingame.

Multiple gang's

Note this is no GF edit so admins will have to set gang leader's.
There is endless things.

I cant hounestly be botherd to go into detail but i just got a notification on hotmail from someone private messaging me so i decided to come on and check out the Forums and decided to realise our old work, hopfully wishing that someone learns from it or takes it further.

Just try it out for yourself's.
