I don't understand these useless features which only make playing harder (having to pee in the middle of a shootout or you will lose health and die, wtf?), but the hunger system is actually important if you have private-owned restaurants, because it will force players to use those restaurants and put money back into circulation. Same with cars whose tires wear out if you have private-owned mechanic garages. It's pretty important to make these features that force players to spend their money, otherwise half of the businesses in the server end up useless and complete waste of money. I even made a script that simulated customers visiting people's businesses when the server didn't have many actual players online just to keep the businesses profiting their owners.
