I just have a few questions to ask any RP SAMP players who would like to answer any or all of these questions. I am creating a Roleplay server and want some public opinion from others who have played other Roleplay servers:

1) Do you prefer commands or keybinds?

2) If you had the choice, would you prefer to be able to change your skin each time you logged in, keeping any weapons, money and vehicles owned from previous sessions?

3) Based on the above question, instead of being given a default skin based on your age, would you prefer to be able to select between Gender, then Race, And finally your own skin selection from that category?

I know there's keybind tools out there, but I would rather them be built-in if it were player-preferred. Second, I'm writing the code to have the player select their own skin by category, and wasn't sure if this should be once per account, or at the beginning of each session, because most servers stick you with one skin that you don't even get to choose.

I was given a heads-up from an RP server scripter, and after I told him I'd find a premade Gamemode, he said "you should write your own because most servers already use the same script, only modified" or something along those lines. I had a lot of doubt, realizing my biggest project I never even finished, and it was much smaller than a full server. I've taken on this challenge to be able to give back to the masses and be able to bring something new to the table, but I don't want it to be "too new" meaning I would like people to enjoy it and not hate it for its differences.

As stated before, answer these questions to your best ability, and I will take each post into consideration.
