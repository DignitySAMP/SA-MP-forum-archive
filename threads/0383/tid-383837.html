Los Santos turf war v1.2.5a


Description: Los santos turf war is the war between Grove, Ballas and Vagos, Aztecs, swats(with zones). It also includes turf war system as well as a built-in admin system. If player kills 5(z) rivals on rival zone(defenders) than rival zone will start flashing to red color, in a meanwhile time when zone is captured, the zone color will turn to attacker zone color, or if 5 allies are on Rival zone then Zone(defenders) then it will also start turf war, it also include etc dms if in case there are not much players are online then you can use /dm 1,2,3,4,5 etc to play with 1 or 2 players, same for /derby 1,2,3 etc. Each skin contain different class.

Swats work: swat work is to neutralize the areas and keep it free of Gangwars(Gangs). swats has there own territory at Airport and at LSPD.

Lines: over 3,500.

Commands

! for team chat
/acmds for admins
/spyhelp
/cmds to view commands in game.

I am Planning to FULLY UPDATE current features for better efficiency.. and will add lots of features for players as well as VIPS.

Plans:

HQ(nice interiors + inbuilt features)

More to go...




ETC: You can also change the turf systems by that how many members or death are require to star a turf war. TO do that, open Zone.pwn then search for MIN_MEMBERS_TO_START_WAR 5 and MIN_DEATH_TO_START_WAR 5, change it to whatever quantity you want(1 = 1 member), just don't set it to 0 or zones will randomly flash.
