JobFilterScript
[FS]Career V1.4
UPDATE 25-2-08

I Made this Filterscript for my RPG but i didnt end up getting a server to host it with, so i decided to Release this script I hope everyone likes it, and please feel free to give me any feedback, i will try and help you as much as i can.

When you enter this game the skins that look like cops will be locked unless you are a registered Cop, To Register you must type /searchjob if you dont have a job (if you do you must quit that job first) then follow the prompts and do what it says

Jobs Available
Cop
FireFighter
Agent
Medic
Taxi Driver
Pilot

Commands
/searchjob
/joblist
/apply(job)
/quit(job)
/find(id) -only for taxi-
/jobhelp
/heal(id) -for medic-
/000 [reason] -emergency call-
