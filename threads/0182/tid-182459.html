Fishing System


Description

So, hi everyone. I made this fishing system something like a month ago, i was to lazy to release this. So in this fishing system you need to buy fishing tools at lighthouse doors with command /buyfishing.(You need some worms and a fishing rod and there is a pickup). Then you need to go to that Big wheel fishing place, then write command /fish . You will see a gametext "Fishing" from 4 to 14 seconds(its random and you will be freezed). After that time you will have a random catch of fishes or some wallet or also nothing. Every time you fish your worms will dissapear by one. You can have 15 fishes in one time, if you have 15 you can't fish and you need to go to docks and sell your fishes. You will get some money(depends how much is your all fishes weight). After selling your fishes you can't fish for 10 minutes. Also you can throw you all fishes back with /releaseallfishes and you can see what is in your inventory with command /inventory. All is with dialogs. This script have 535 lines.
