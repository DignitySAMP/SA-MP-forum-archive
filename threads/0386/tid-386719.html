I'm currently making a call of duty game, It's still under development.
Apologies for no screen shots, though i do promise a good game once you join.
Also, you will need to update it to 0.3x.

Features

*Teams*

-United States of America
-Taliban Cell
-Soviet Union
-European Alliance
-Germany

*Other...*

-Welcome Messages
-Death Messages
-Spawn's with Armor
-Anti Spam
-Register/Login System Dialogs hashed passwords
-Team Radios - # to use team chat
-Anti Spawn Kill
-Anti Weapon Hack

*Rank System*
A lot of ranks, some vehicles like hydra require rank for you to enter them. You can view your current rank using /Myrank.

*Admin System*
-Comes with a LOT of commands. 1-7 Levels... simply type "/Level [1-7] in the chatbox and the commands for that level will appear. These commands include:...


/kick
/jail
/ujail
/mute
/unmute
/report
* Eminem 2ka9 slaps around a bit with a large trout.
/freeze
/unfreeze
/clearchat
/givecash
/givescore
/lspec
/lspecoff
/goto
/gethere
/explode
/stats

*Team Bases/Zones*
-Each bases are green, red or orange etc, this indicates the group that owns it. Just a gang territory.[Can be seen on the photos below inside your map]

*Connection Info*
-Info is given in the chatbox once you connect. Also once you spawn you are greeted with a small dialog box telling you what you shall do first. (/Cmds)

*VIP System*
-VIP System comes with 3 levels: (Silver, Gold & Platinum)

*Weapon Buying*
-You can buy weapons In-Game using "/w" (They cost, available for all players)

*Anti-Cheat*
-A lot of anti-cheat systems, made to fight off hackers.

*General Player Commands*
A lot of them, Including /switch(Switch Team) - /Kill - /Help - Rules - /Admins - /Myteam - /R - /Myrank and More!

*Login and Register*
-Appears the first time you join, type /register [password] - After that you login automatically.

*Anti-Spawn Kill*
-You in invulnerable for the first 10 seconds after you spawn.

*Bank System*
-You can Withdraw, Deposit, Balance and Transfer your Cash! Access bank using /Banktele - Once in bank use /Bank to access your bank account.

*Neon Shop*
-Access the Neon Shop using /Neonshop - Once in the garage use /Neon to access the neon colours selection dialog(Currently available for all players, modify to VIP if you wish)
