Description
New Santos, a totally different style to Los Santos. The map includes a lot of good locations, including a hospital, police station and many restaurants. I made sure all roads do lead somewhere, there hopefully won't be any huge bugs (holes in the floor, bumpy objects).
Download New Santos

AdrenalineX - New Santos
Future New Santos
I will still add and improve this map and keep updating this post. If any mappers want to add to this map, be my guest. A few roads could do with expanding, so if anyone wants to help, just reply or visit us at our www.AdrenalineX.co.uk website. Please reply if you like or dislike what I've released, I'd like to hear everyones opinions.

Update
  • *Fixed unlevel ground (curbs mainly)
  • *Added 2 new roads to enter New Santos
  • *Added Pay&Spray near the Lighthouse.
  • *Improved Race Route
  • *Improved ******* Video
Update 2
  • *Replaced floating bridge joining LS Aiport tunnel with a more realistic bridge.
  • *Change oil refinery to mountain side, including changes to pizza shack.
  • *Found and replaced many duplicated objects (mainly lighting).
  • *Improved gold course surrounding.
