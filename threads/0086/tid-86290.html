Do I agree to the rules? Yes I agree but before anyone else signs up I'd like to make a few suggestions.

a) Are we using the vanilla L4D setup? This has alot of inherent flaws and I think we should use the competitive standard cevo setup. It basically rebalances things a little bit by having a tank EVERY round, a with 75% of the time, and healthkits (most of them) are replaced with pills. This balances things much more effectively and makes for a much more interesting, strategic game. Its what the newegg wanfest is using, as well as cevo, etc. I have a server with it setup, if you want to message me on steam we can go over it. I think its a must.

The cevo setup also comes standard with the readyup plugin which helps alot in competitive play.

b)some specific rules might need adding. as an example from the newegg wanfest:

The following is a list of illegal spots during match play:

* The vents behind the elevator on the 28th floor of No Mercy 4 are illegal.
* Camping vents while a tank is active is illegal.
* On No Mercy 3, if a tank is active, going in the bathroom in the restauraunt and camping on top of the stalls is illegal.

The following is not allowed during live match play:

* Skipping any crescendo event without triggering it is illegal.
* Blocking the path of the survivors with a car or a tree or any other moveable object which prevents them from advancing is illegal.
* Any method of circumventing the infected spawn timer is illegal.
* Being in a position that is not reachable by regular zombies as a survivor is illegal.
* Camping anywhere the infected cannot attack you is illegal.
* Bunny hopping with the hunter to increase damage pounces is illegal

but yeah. we're in. Try to pimp this tournament out a bit more so we can get more teams to play with please ^_^
