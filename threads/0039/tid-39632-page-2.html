First of all, You gotta have a game-mode in your /gamemode folder, You can find some nice ones over @ the GameMode release board.

Add the .AMX file to your game mode folder, then open up "server.cfg" in your root and add the name of that amx file,

For example, i upload "mygamemode.amx" i should put this line 1 space after "gamemodes" in server.cfg "gamemode0 my_gamemoede 1"

For teleports, they come with SOME game modes.

I won't help you anymore, Please refer to the scripting section on how-to do this stuff, It may be all confusing at first but you'll get the hang of it.

Good luck,
