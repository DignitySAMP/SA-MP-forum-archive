Introducing
This is my first FS so please comment it
well i create this is to someone who have DM/RP.etc server(not stunt) can go stunt too..
it's like u are at stunt server, but ur not.this FS can works for all gamemode.

COMMANDS:


TELE:
/stunt(tele to SFAIR)
/lsa(tele to Los Santos Airport)
/waterjump (tele to waterjump)
/sp(tele to super pipe)
/back (to get back to your DM/RP.etc)
/savep(save postion)
/loadp(LOAD POSTION)
-----------------------------------
Vehicle :
/v [carnumberid] (to spawn vehicle)
/lights (to turn on and turn off the lamp)
/nitro (add nitro to your car)
/speedboost (enable or disable speedboost)
/vrepair (to fix and repair ur vehicle)
/vflip(to flip ur vehicle)
--------------------------------------
Other :
/jp (to spawn a jetpack)
/afon (enable antifall)
/afoff (disable antifall)
-------------------------------------this command also avalible at /shelp--------------------------------
