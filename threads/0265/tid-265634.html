Here is a few tips for owning a Roleplay server. ( or if your thinking of starting one. )
  • Don't give admin to anyone who asks. Make sure the Co-Owner will not abuse.
  • Make Sure he/she can RP well
  • Get 50 player slots or more
  • Make rules ASAP
  • Get forums
  • Get a script and edit the sh*t out of it ( If your new to opening a server ) or make a script from scratch
  • Get 1 developer who can script and map, Who won't screw you over.
  • Get forums, Try and get a PAID vBulletin. If you can't you can use freeforums or a SMF host.
  • Be strict on DMers,Hackers and Non RPers, Teach them how to RP
  • Don't refund. The players will leave because they got what they wanted. Hard to understand I know.
  • Get a HOST! I have seen TONS of servers with home host. It will kill your computer and it will never really attract players.
  • Act Mature
