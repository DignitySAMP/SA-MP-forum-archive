well, before i made a teleport menu, this is a whole menu with lots of new features.
Features:
Teleporting
Car spawning
Skin choosing
Player choices
Car tuning
Guns
Weather
Time

Teleporting: i am using the same teleporting script as before

Car spawning: lots of cars, theres a choice between sports cars, 4 door cars, bikes, boats, helicopters, planes and other cars

Skin choosing: every gang skin is included

Player choices: Players money, player virtual world, health and armour

Car tuning: change your cars wheels, cars colour, give your car nos and hydraulics

Guns: Shotguns, smgs, rifles, mele, thrown and heavy weapons

Weather: change the servers weather

Time: change your time

at the moment i have it set so any 1 can type /wmenu but u can change the command to suit your server/admin script

special thanks to : [LTP]RacerX and [alr]Dabomber

there are a few warnings but the script still works
