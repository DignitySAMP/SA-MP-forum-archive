What Is It?

This filterscript helps you to make families in game, create gangzon in game and also create Hq for families
families can attack together's turf(gangzone) and win the war with killing together when a family wins the money(profit) of turf will pay to the family and family with winning the wars and winning the profits can buy a Hq for its self. its so amazing!
most this is good for Rp or Rpg Servers But its a nice idea to use it in Dm Servers!



Commands:

RCON Commands:
/fhelp [All The Commands Will Show to Rcon Admin]
Family CMD:
/fcreate [you can create a family]
/fdelete [you can delete a family]
/fedit [you can edit a family]
/fstrike [you can give strike to a family]
/fstrikes [ you can set a family strikes to]

Hq CMD:
/qedit [you can edit a HQ]
/gotohq [You can teleport to a HQ]
/createhq [You can create a HQ]
/deletehq [You can Delete a HQ]

Gangzone CMD:
/creategzone [you can create gangzone(your minimumX and minimumY is The Place that you stand and the maxX and maxY is for example minX+100.00 and minY is for example minY+150 {the 100 and 150 will put in cmd like /creategzone 1000000 100 150 . that 1000000 is the profit})]
/deletegzone [you can delete gangzone]
/tedit [you can edit a gangzone(turf)]
/tused [can see which turf is in use]

Player CMD:

Family CMD:
/park [you can park family vehicles and after every vehicle respawn will get there]
/next [you can choose next clothes]
/acceptfamily [you can accept to join a family]
/fclothes [you can start choosing closthes.]
/done [you can set your skin to a clothes that you like]
/safe [you can withdraw or deposit money in your family safe(usage for buying HQ)]
/f [you can speak with your family members]
/timeofwar [you can see how many time you have to win the war and see your scores]
/attack [you can attack to a gangzone(turf)]
/families [will show you all families name and ...]
/famedit [leader can edit his family something like family name,rank name,skins,hq text,family color and set family safe place]
/finvite [to invite someone]
/funinvite [to kick someone from family]
/slot [to see the family vehicle slot]

HQ CMD:
/exithq [to exit from hq]
/enterhq [to enter to hq]
/buyhq [to buy a hq (should have enough money in your family safe)]
/sellhqtomarket [[to sell your family hq and give money back]

GangZone CMD:
/turf [to see id of turf you are standing on it]
/zones [to show or hide gangzones(turfs)]
