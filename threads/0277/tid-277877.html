If you wanna make your own transfender and pay 'n' spray, I advise you to use MTA map editor to find the transfender and pay n spray garage. When the player's are in range, just use a dialog or something to show up and let players choose stuff to add for transfender. Use AddVehicleComponent:

https://wiki.sa-mp.com/wiki/Function:AddVehicleComponent
https://wiki.sa-mp.com/wiki/Car_Component_ID

For pay n spray, all you need is to use SetVehicleHealth, and take money away from the player..

Not that hard.
