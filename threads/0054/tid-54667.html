hej guys,

its been a while since my first release, (this is my second....) so i thought maybe i'll release something new.
well, i am kinda in RP so i made an AirportRadio. While that one was done, i thought this is to fast. I need to add something else. You know every flight has its own number. So i made something that you wont get permission to takeoff unless you got a flight planned. So this one gives you flight a 'lable' where after you can leave.

The commands are:
All AP's
/sosland
/requesttakeoff
LVAP:
/requestlandLV
LSAP:
/requestlandLS
SFAP:
/requestlandSF
FlightPlanning:
/planflight

Radio chat:
/Aradio_on
/Aradio_off

the lable gets together useing the following: [DESTINATION][PLAYERID][VEHICLEID]

This is version 0.2
I'll try to update if possible

I am asking people to leave everything in the script with my name as it was or just delete it if u want but please don't replace it with your name, ofcourse I know I cant check or report it, but it is fair and it doesn't make u a liar.

I am trying to put the following in the next version: Automatic reconization on wich airport you are so you only have to do /requesttakeoffLV
The /requestland is going to be the same.
Imma add the abandoned airport 2. And the possabilitie to let people earn money with flights planned by the server. Also you can cancle a flight (in air or on the ground)
