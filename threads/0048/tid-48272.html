pawn Code:
enum cInfo
{
    cModel,
    Float:cLocationx,
    Float:cLocationy,
    Float:cLocationz,
    Float:cAngle,
    cColorOne,
    cColorTwo,
    cOwner[MAX_PLAYER_NAME],
    cDescription[MAX_PLAYER_NAME],
    cValue,
    cLicense,
    cRegistration,
    cOwned,
    cLock,
    ownedvehicle,
};
new CarInfo[12][cInfo];
Change the "CarInfo[12][cInfo]". E.g. if you want 30 Cars change 12 to 30 ("CarInfo[30][cInfo]").
