Image2Label Converter 6/04/2014 [UPDATED]

Evening SA-MP members,
I wanted to download the Image2Label converter by Pablo Borsellino.
His mirror was outdated, atleast he posted his source code into a pastebin file.
I've updated, editted and re-compiled his script.
All credits of the main source goes to him.
What did I do?
I've editted the Color Picker a bit.
Deleted the header, and made it converting 3x faster.

All sizes can be CONVERTED now.

UPDATING THE SCRIPT!
- Only 32x32 pixels can be converted now, I'll AUTO-resize all chosen pictures to 32x32.
- Improving the lay-out.
- Allowing you to choose pictures from the internet. (You must have internet connection though)
- Fixing the ''x'' on the color picker. (Not picking correct location)
- You must close it on the taskbar now, I'll add a ''Close'' button in the lay-out.
- Adding a option to convert images to textdraws.
