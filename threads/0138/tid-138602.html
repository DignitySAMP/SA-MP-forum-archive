I don't know and I ain't interested about the real owner of the Cops and Robbers script, what matter is the script that I got from (weed).

Credits done? Great. I won't put any screenshots, and i don't care about videos neither.

At first I was intended to make the script and buy a host, but then I opened a server of another game and SA-MP died to me, so I decide to release it.

Bugs? One of the reason I haven't finished it. There might be some bugs, but I couldn't even test it, 'cause I couldn't find anyone to test it with me
