Originally Posted by GilOG

Make sure not to ban hackers with aimbot, kick them with a warning. If it's something like airbreak then you can make it a ban but make that ban appealable.
Please, please, please -- do not give misleading advice. That is absolutely not the correct way to go.


Firstly, if anything, that should be directly the other way around.

An airbraker (if they're solely airbraking) is not going to really harm the gameplay except make the server look really silly. If you really want to hand warnings FOR HACKING/CHEATING out like they're candy (as opposed to having a firm no hacker policy), then this is the time to give one.

Someone using an aimbot in a competitive server like cops and robbers is wrong on many levels. It is the purest form of cheating and why would anyone give anyone a warning for that? If you're going to give warnings, give it for the minor forms of hacking.

Same with a racing server, if they're airbraking in the background screwing around, then not the biggest crime in the world. If they're subtly speed hacking then that is cheating and shouldn't be tolerated.

Also, players who are hacking and deliberately attempt to ruin people's gameplay should also be banned without warning.

Anyway, this isn't really meant to be a hacking discussion.

Just try to be unique, have some interesting features, don't have a million typos and colours in the textdraws, messages etc. Don't do anything stupid and take advice and suggestions from your players.
