Depends on the make and model of the laptop. I have an old HP here that has the battery in the same compartment as the HDD, which requires unscrewing one screw. But on my Sony Vaio I had to pop off the entire back cover. Not that difficult though. Unscrew all screws that you can find (keep track of which one goes where because some are different sizes) and the cover should come clean off.
