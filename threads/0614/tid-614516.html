Information

GTA Wiki's description of the event:

Quote:

Once begun, a briefcase will be marked on the radar. Players must hold the briefcase for the longest amount of time, even if it means killing over players to obtain it. If the player is killed, the timer, which is counting how long the player is holding it for, will stop. If the player regains the case, it will carry on from where it left off.

The top three winners will be listed in the usual "Gold, Silver, Bronze" system which is counted and displayed in the bottom right corner of the HUD.
Well, there are some things you should know about:

* Briefcase/briefcase carrier will be shown with icon because we don't have the fancy icons of GTA Online.
* It displays on the right of the map in this script because bottom right corner didn't look as good as I thought.

Briefcase Spawns

You can add/edit/remove briefcase spawn locations from BriefcaseLocations array. Make sure it's in this format:

Code:
{location name, x, y, z}
