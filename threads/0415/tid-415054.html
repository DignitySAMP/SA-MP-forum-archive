What if it was possible to create an RPG GM - Based on Far Cry? (If you don't know it, watch the video below) Would it be practical; and would it be attractive. Perhaps, once players connect to the server, they have the option to choose how they landed on the wild, dangerous island, for example, they could have crashed by plane, or have been swept ashore by the tides. The idea of tribal warfare and such can be interesting as well, perhaps there can be a spiced up variety of how to initiate the server. Maybe if the island was say..Dominated by two tribes, each controlling one side of each island, so the players could stumble upon one, and join it. Also, there would of course be a survival mechanic scripted in it, with the /inventory and such. The island would have been hospitable in the past, which is why there's some houses here and there, maybe a small town in the middle and such, but it could have changed, and players would need to scavenge what they can get.

Good luck, but I think you missed few points,.

It all is similar to DM, TDM, yea you can enforce strict admin watch but everyone will want to kill and fight, indeed that is the point of the concept.

As in game industry so it is in server ownership, you need to make a "design document", who is the target audience? ((medium/light roleplayer's)) how will I make player's like the server and how will I prevent them leaving, what is the story? what factions will be present in the game, how to prevent TDM/DM? and so on.

You gotta look at those points because they are crucial for the design of the script and server.
