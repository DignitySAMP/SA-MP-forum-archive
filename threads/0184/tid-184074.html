» MAP: Mount Chilliad Helipad
_______________________________________________


» Description

A realistic-style helipad located on top of Mount Chilliad. Has a small gas station, realistic lights, fences, supportive structures on the mountainside, random crates and a small shed.

It's built on the small viewpoint with the memorial-plate.

I originally made this for another server, but I have decided to release it. There are 87 objects in total.
