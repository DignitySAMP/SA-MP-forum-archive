We had discussed this on and off for weeks leading up to this decision (as far as who would run the community as Executive Director), but ultimately, we were backed into a corner when the person we had in place decided to back out less than 24 hours after the interim tag was removed from his name. The community wasn't going to support a 4th Executive Director in the span of 30 days. Unfortunately, with the way our staff team is structured, while it isn't going to take one person to get the job done, there's a lot of responsibility that comes with the position that takes a lot of time out of your day. I would say the stress of the position is what led me to only last 2 and a half months in it, despite how long I've been on the team.

At the end of the day, there simply was no heir apparent to the position by the time things were said and done. The trouble with looking beyond the Admin team itself is you're really taking a leap of faith on someone, especially if you don't know them all that well. Even beyond the position, though, the decision was also based on where we were as a community and the demographics that made it up, not to mention the public image of the community has certainly been frayed over the years (hell, I've seen plenty of threads on these forums of people absolutely shitting on it).

Also, with regards to your last point, Devin did not make any money on transferring the accounts. We considered several options, including either shutting the community down all together and that's it, or doing that but at least giving everyone an out moving forward. We felt it was in the interest and, I suppose, a matter of gratitude to at least allow them to continue within another community.
