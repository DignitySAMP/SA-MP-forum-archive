INTRODUCTION
Hello Guys,
Today i Release my Script called San Andreas Ultimate Fighting Championship
It took me 2 hours for map and 2 hours for script
I Used RNPC for two Fighter called Fighter A and B
Actors for Bartender, MC and DJ
Features
Time Start Round:
-Every hours will have one fight match
-Notice for all player online while the remaining 60 seconds
BAR and BETTING:
-You can buy Beer,vodka,water When standing near a bartender
-You can Bet for Fighter When standing near a bartender
-If you win will get X3 Money Bet.
FIGHTING SYSTEM
-More 15 Fighting styles NPC can do.
-When Attacked will lost random 10 HP.
-COMBO when action will give random 30 HP
-Random Bleeding.
COMMAND:
-/ufc [bar,bet]
-/ufcwave
-/ufccheer
-COMMAND TEST: /test (for cut time to remaining 60 seconds)
