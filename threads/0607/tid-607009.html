About Us
____________________________________________

The server got published at Nov 13rd, 2015 and is a Freeroam/DM/Stunt/Derby/TDM server. More gamemodes are

under developement (such as race and hide&seek (?)). We have nearly 30k registered players in-game and a quite active forum

with a lot of friendly players and administrators. Get used to know new people and have fun!

*Duels

*Group/Gang Wars

*Stunt Hotspots

*A lot of DMs (Sawnoff, Minigun, Sniper, more to come)

*Team Deathmatches and WWII (Grove VS Ballas, Red vs Blue Team)

*Object attachments to body
