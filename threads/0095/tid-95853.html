Hidden Interior Explorer

A long time ago I made a part of this FS, but yesterday I've finished it. All interiors in a menu, no one forgot (I hope). This FS makes also floors of unsolid interiors solid so you can explore it better.

Interiors: 150.
Menu's: 17.
Objects: 3.
Command: /hilist



^ An example of the solid maded floors.


^ Use my other map to drive here in LC.

^ An another example of the solid maded floors.

PWN Download.
AMX Download.

Please don't edit it, if it has bugs you can tell that in this topic.
Thanks to Wanted, Vice and Rullez for testing it and thanks to Wanted for his server.

It works fine on 0.3, but also works on 0.2X.
Enjoy the FS.
