Neon System
Hello guys, today i came back with another filterscript called 'Neon System BETA Version'
Why 'BETA'?, because it is too simple....
There are only 6 available colors, Blue, Red, Green, White, Pink, Yellow

Installation Guide:

1. To use this fs u need to use zcmd include.
2. Download NeonSystemBETAbyahmad95.rar from here
3. Unrar NeonSystemBETAbyahmad95.rar and put the contain into your filterscripts folder.
4. Write your server.cfg on filterscripts line. (eg: filterscripts NeonSystemBeta )
5. Start your server. enjoy!
