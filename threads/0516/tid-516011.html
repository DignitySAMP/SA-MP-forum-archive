GAMEMODE BACK STORY
2 years ago I decided to develop a custom freeroam script that I personally gave out to a couple of people in SA-MP. I have no Idea what they have done with it and I really do not care, but none the less, I have tonight released the first Alpha version of the code I am currently using, Because my code is in version 1.6.8 I am now releasing the first Alpha stage version. Althought this Alpha version of the script is very simple, over the next few weeks I will be updating this code personally and completely different from my code I have now, but more updates that you can use either to implement to your server or just use, the choice is up to you.

P.S To people who will post negative comments in the section below: I am Ignoring you, so do not expect me to respond.

WHY DID YOU NAME IT LEVELS?
Do you all remember the server called MINI MISSIONS that has over like 100 Mini Events? Yes? Okay

Well this is sort of like that, but instead its a freeroam script, you can join and leave the levels event whenever you want and you can also reset your level stats just to play side by side with friends.

However, the Levels system is completely custom and coded from scratch.

To Add your own Maps and Levels:

1. Map out an area you want to use
2. Grab the start / finish cords and the Min Z height you want the player to be at when he respawns
3. Add them into the code, very simple copy and paste.

GAMEMODE FEATURES
* Yini saving / loading
* Permanent Dynamics Vehicles
* Mini-Game: Levels
* Mini-Game: More coming soon
* Secure logining / registering
* UB Hashing passwords (Updating to Whirpool for this version for you all)
* General Freeroam commands to use.
