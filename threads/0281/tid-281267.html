I've seen over 20 Threads by people, begging and spamming for Reputation points, here's how it goes, and Read this before you ask for Reputation points.

How to give a Reputation point:
  • To give a reputation point you have to click on the star under the member's avatar
The first way to get Reputation points:
  • You should be able to help people all over the forums, Especially in Scripting Discussion, once you help someone, that someone will give you a +Rep, which will Increase your Points by 1 reputation or more.
Second method:
  • Post a map/filterscript/gamemode/include, or ANY kind of scripts YOU'VE done, and not scripted by someone else, and claiming its yours, this would be STEALING, and taking credit of someone else's work.
  • After you have posted some of your works, people will find it helpful, or worth of using on their server(s), They'll give you a +rep.
Requirements:
  • The person should have AT LEAST 50 posts to be able to give a reputation point, else It'll show as GREY on your UCP, and it won't show under your avatar.
  • Reputation points are mostly used on Server Advertisement, and you only have 1 post/month, so don't spam, else it will end with a forum ban, or your reputation points will be set to zero, as stated by Dugi.
If i missed something please post it and I'll add it.

This thread was basically made, because I'm sick of people PMing me, and other members for +Reps, And if we're nice and we give a +rep to someone, we get spammed for it... This has to end.
