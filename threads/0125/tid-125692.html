VERSION 1.0
?Features:
*Easy car spawner.(Just type /v carname/modelid, example: /jester, or if you download the other one is /v carname)
*Car list by typing /cars.
*When you spawn a vehicle, you automaticly get in !
*Car random colors
*No more messes in the server!!! Only 1 car per person, and when player disconnects car gets destroyed
*Respawner commnad included!! Just log in into RCON and type /respawncars and whoala!

?How to use:
*Download the *.pwn, compile it, add the *.amx and the *.pwn on yourserver/filterscript folder. Open your server.cgf and on filterscript line add s&r, and you are done

UPDATE: VERSION 2.0 IS OUT!!
Features:
*Totally re-scripted(Less CPU ussage )
*/cars is gone
*NEW SPAWN SYSTEM, ADDED SPAWN BY MODEL ID!! Now you can do /v infernus or /v 411 , it will spawn the same car :P
*COLOR OPTION ADDED!!! Just type /v [carname/modelid] [color1] [color2] and whoala !!
*If you dont type a color, dont worry, you will get a random color too!!
*1 car per person!! No more messes!!
*Automatic get in vehicle.
*Sound Added!!
*GameText now shows the carname, aswell the model id, also color 1 and color 2.
*DCMD commands.

*Download the *.pwn, compile it, add the *.amx and the *.pwn on yourserver/filterscript folder. Open your server.cgf and on filterscript line add s&r, and you are done
