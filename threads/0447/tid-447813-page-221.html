- Specific Chats - For different entities.
- Comprehensive Administration System - Most important features are arranged to be edited in-game, several tools to aid admins in their part.
- Compelling Vehicle Modifications - Possibility to modify compatible vehicles anywhere.

- Skills - Specific skills required to a specific action, raising your difference between you and another player ( Ex: Vehicle Parts being crafted in order to an individual vehicle modification )
