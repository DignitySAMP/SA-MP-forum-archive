I remmebered that before 2 months i made 1 not bad mod, I called it World War III . Now im going to share it
whith you coz, I dont have any intrest in sa-mp now. All I ask is to leave the mode name , and credits.

I warn you that there are few bugs.

Nations:

China
USA
Russia
Royal navy army
Lithuania

Commands:
Only few of them, usally most of them are based on pickups

! - radio.
@-admin chat
/login /register /logout.
/acmd - admin commands.
/stats - to see other stats.
/info - to see your statistics.
/ranks - to see your current rank.
/base - to set spawn back to base.
Lithuanians: /o3 /c3 /enterbase

Admin commands and levels:

Level 1:/nrg /warn /kick /healall /armourall /goto
Level 2: /ban /force /explode /gethere /getallhere /ann
Level 3: /mission1-5 /makeadmin1-3 /general /pimp

Ranks of soldiers:

1st grade : Prv - Private
2nd grade : Sgt - Sergeant
3nd grade : Leu - Lieutenant
4nd grade : Mjr - Major
5nd grade : Cpt - Captain
6nd grade : Col - Colonel
7nd grade : Gnr - General
Bigger rank you have ,more health and armor you get.


Simple mission system, 5 missions automaticly are set to launch in periods of time.
1.USA Paradrop
2.Nuke
3.Delivering the goods
4.Defusing the weapon
5.China Paradrop

Simple weather and time system, mission changes, weather and time changes.

Warzone system same as gangwars, can fight for them and everything else.

Bunker system: each nation got bunker located in desert . Bunker means that if you pickup an save icon ,after next spawn you will spawn at bunker not at base, but if you will whant to spawn back to base you will have to type /base .

Class system. You can select your proffesion from:
Infantry:M4 + Deagle + Night Vision goggles
Pilot: Deagle + Parachute + /f command in bomber China plane + able to pilot Shamal
Sniper: Sniper + Silenced pistol + Infra Red goggles
Medic: 9 mm pistol + /heal command + Night Vision goggles
Anti-Vehicle: Bazoka + He grenades + Shotgun? + Plant command + Night Vision goggles

Anti plane bunkers , there are 3 of them you can find them on high mountains of desert. Once you enter them you get an minigun , then its easy to hit those Hunter pilot bastards.

If you host it , throw me an ip ,coz I would like to remember old times.



Prieview of map

AMX

PWN




Forgotten to say ,that you can save chat log too, by making txt file into scriptfiles direction. Txt file should be named : chato-logas.txt you can rename it to whatever you whant, by editing the source

Known Bugs:
Admins: dont tune nrg or sanchez !! Server ownage..
