Download SA-MP map editor here: https://forum.sa-mp.com/showthread.php?tid=282801
Check the interiors here: http://weedarr.wikidot.com/interior
Place the coordinates of the interior you want at "camera view" at the top right corner. Click the objects you want to remove and press DELETE key on your keyboard(click the insert button if you want to add any object).
After you're done, click the "code" button, copy the codes and paste under your OnPlayerConnect code on your fs/gm.
