Weapon Shop System v1.0

Hello, i want share my first FilterScript in here. This is called Weapon Shop System. You can add, delete, change, and many more you can do. And sorry if any mistakes word because i can't speak English well

Picture:





Command:
  • /buygun: Open a dialog and buying a weapon with a weapon list
  • /buyweap: Buy a weapon without a weapon list (You can lookup at /weaponlist
  • /weaponlist: Open a weapon list dialog
Credits:
  • Kye - SA:MP
  • Y_Less - sscanf
  • zeex - zcmd
  • Me (hack4ever a.k.a. sheenidgs) - Creator
Donwload:
  • PWN Only (Pastebin)
  • PWN + AMX + Include Files (Solidfiles)
Known Bugs:
  • None of Bug.
Note:
  • You need sscanf and zcmd includes for using this FilterScript.
  • I recommend to you to download all package (PWN+AMX+INC).
  • Don't remove the credits!
