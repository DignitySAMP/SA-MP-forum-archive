Information

Hello, I want to introduce you to my work vehicle speedometers, it shows km/h and the machinery Everything is made with textdraws

_________________________________________________
How to install?

Put this filterscript in this folder server\filterscripts\<here>
Add VehicleSpeedometer to your filterscripts line in server.cfg
