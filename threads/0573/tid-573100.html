You don't have to remove your skin mods. If you are going to change the original cop skins, you also need to mod the new skins. Modding isn't really "supported" in SA-MP, but I'll leave you with this:

Code:
// game
280, lapd1, lapd1
281, sfpd1, sfpd1
282, lvpd1, lvpd1
// sa-mp
300, lapdna, lapd1
301, sfpdna, sfpd1
302, lvpdna, lvpd1
Take a copy of your cop skin mod files: lapd1.dff, sfpd1.dff, lvpd1.dff. Rename them to lapdna.dff, sfpdna.dff, lvpdna.dff. Add the new files to the SAMP/custom.img.
