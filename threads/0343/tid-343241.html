Hello This is my first release(filterscript).


I hope you like it !

I know this is easy but somebody it\'s still learning or is a newbie and it will help this i think...

Features:
  • It\'s Dialog Based
  • No no include/plugins needed(A_samp.inc of course)
Instructions:
How to install?

1.Download it.

2.Copy the GunFsByEmil.pwn and GunFsByEmil.amx and put it in the yourservername/filterscripts folder

3.Open Server.cfg and at the line Filterscripts add Filterscripts GunFsByEmil

4.Restart your server and enjoy

Usage:

Type /guns to show the dialog.
