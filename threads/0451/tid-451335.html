Unlimited Gaming DeathMatch: Midnight Terrors (v2)

Features:
- MySQL Saving System, using BlueG r7 MySQL Plugin
- Admin & Helper System
- VIP/Donator System
- Faction System
- Dynamic Gang System
- Dynamic Street System
- Dynamic Vehicle System
- Random Spawn System
- Cops & Criminals System
- Tow System
- Helicopter Rope System
- GTAIV Granade System
- SilverBullets (Explosive) System
- And much more...

Admin & Helper System:
- There is 5 admin levels + Owner is lvl 10
- There is 5 helper levels + Helper manager is lvl 10





Helicopter Rope:
- If you are a cop/soldier you can rope down from helicopter by pressing 2

Granade Throw from car:
- If you have granades you can throw it from car as a driver by pressing Y

SilverBullets System:
- Use /sb if you have silverbullets (Can be bought in 24/7 or VIP store)
- Aim someone and shoot and he will explode and die
- It's easy to switch from regular to silver bullets and backside

VIP/Donator System:
- There is 3 VIP/Donator ranks/levels
- There is DP (Donator Points) that can be used in donator shop
- There is VIP/Donator base (Including VIP house and Drug Factory)
- Donators can spawn vehicles (Golden Infernus)
- Donators can make drugs in drug factory
- Donators spawn in VIP house






Street System:
- There is over 60 streets already added all over Las Venturas
- System is tottaly dynamic (/makestreet | /deletestreet)




Stats System:
- All your statistics at one place (/stats)
- To be sure stats can be saved with cmd and on disconnect (/savestats)




Faction System:
- There is 2 factions (Police and Army)
- There is 5 ranks in faction
- There is radio chat with faction and department






Criminal/Police/Army System:
- Police can taze players by aiming at them and pressing MMB or using command /taze
- When you taze someone your tazer needs to recharge to taze again
- Admins can recharge your taze or you can wait until it recharges by itself
- Recharge is used to prevent spam
- When you kill someone you get yellow color if it's your first kill
- If you kill 3 or more players you get orange color
- If you kill over 5 player or you kill a cop you get red color
- Police take care of yellow and orange wanteds
- Army take care of red wanteds
- To see wanted criminals police/army use /laptop




Tow System:
- Cops can tow your car with a TowCar [Press ALT near car]
