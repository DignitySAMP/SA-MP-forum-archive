You won't be able to access the file. The best thing you can do is go in Singleplayer and drive through the whole of Los Santos (or maybe it's all of San Andreas) near every single house and see if a red marker appears at the door (you've got to do it at night time). If the red marker appears then write down the house, visit the same house in SA:MP debug and /save at the doorstep. Repeat that process for every single burglary house.

You can, in fact, access the file you need but you'll need to filter out the buildings you don't need. In the data\maps directory and its subdirectories you'll find IPL files. You can open those with notepad. Scroll down to the header 'enex'. The entries have rather cryptic names, but I think it's safe to assume that something like "SFHSS1" means "San Fiero House 1".
