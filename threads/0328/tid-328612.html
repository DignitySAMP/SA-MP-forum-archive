[FilterScript] ChemTrails

Another little FS by me

Features
Uses particle 'smoke_flare' ( MODEL ID: 18728 )
Supported Planes:
  • Skimmer
  • Rustler
  • Beagle
  • Cropduster
  • Stuntplane
  • Shamal
  • Hydra
  • Nevada
  • Dodo
Why aren't the Andromada and AT-400 supported? -See known bugs below.

Commands
/ctrails - enabled/disables the effects.

Video
Not very good visible in the video, but ingame it's just fine.
[ame]http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r0MTiZ9xppU&feature=player_embedded[/ame]

Known Bugs
You may not see the particles when the camera is too far away from the particle object (Which is attached to the end of the wings of the plane).
That's the reason why I didn't support andromada and AT-400, the particle object would be too far away that you wouldn't see the particles.
(This also happens in the video @1:11)
