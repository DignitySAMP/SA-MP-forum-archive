• What is it?
The Ultimate Las Venturas RolePlay NPC Pack comes with 6 Bots including: 2 Limo's (Black and White), Dodo, Airport Terminal Taxi, Freeway Elegy and an Airport Tug. The File includes the .rec files, the NPCModes and the .pwn and .amx of 2 FilterScripts. The 2 FilterScripts do different things. "LVNPCPack" Comes with the same features as stated and as the other FS "LVNPCPack_Icons" Which includes the Coloured Map Icons while the other has NO Map Icons. All cars/plane's drive round a loop of the area around the LV Casino's and the Airport, The Dodo Pilot flys around nearly the whole of LV which lands and takes off from the airport.

• How to use
Each folder has a either a .pwn and .amx or a .rec file which need to be placed into the correct folders where your SA-MP 0.3 Server is located. IT WILL NOT WORK IF NOT DONE CORRECTLY!
Once completed this, "LVNPCPack" or "LVNPCPack_Icons" (without quotes) needs to be placed into the server.cfg filterscripts line.
Join your server and watch the Limo's Stop and pick up Passengers, Tug drive around the airport, Dodo fly around, The taxi pick up Airport passengers and the Elegy which Drives a loop around the big freeway.
