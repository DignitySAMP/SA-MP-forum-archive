Portal Gun


Hey all, I'm releasing this becouse I have it here on my PC doing nothing, so I wanna share it

What is it?

It's a Portal Gun, just like the game Portal.

How to install?

Extract the .rar file, compile portal.pwn, and add it to your filterscripts directory, add the plugin to your plugins directory, and add the includes to your /pawno/includes directory too.

Open up server.cfg and add to filterscripts line portal, and add to your plugins line streamer.

Pictures or a video?

[ame]www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4XHRhgxEY8[/ame]

Usage:

Get a Rocket Laucher(the one that hasn't the auto aiming), and aim where you want and then shot.
To reset the portals, use the command /resetp.

Credits:

Well, I did it, so credits to me only ^_^

Bugs:

None actually, if you found one, please report.

Link:

Pastebin *

* You will need to download foreach, zcmd and Incognito streamer plugin.

Enjoy!
