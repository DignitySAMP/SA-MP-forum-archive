OleKristian95's V.I.P Place
- What is this?
This is my second release here on the SA-MP Forums, its 3 mansions located close to "The Big Ear".
I'm calling it V.I.P Place because its like where the important people should live. ^^

- How does it look?
All mansions are 3 different houses with different looks around. One of the houses is still empty but I'll think about updatig that one in the future.

The left house got some sport activities and a nice place to sit down and just drink some wine or something.

and the second house got a bar where you can either buy some alcohol or take a glass of water. I've also added some lights on the right house because it was a little bit dark.

The whole place got fences around the house and a open gate entrance.
(If you want a gate that opens and closes I have added the gate co-ords below.)
