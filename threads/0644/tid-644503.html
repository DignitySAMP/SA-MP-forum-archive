Introduction

Origin Generation Gaming is a SA:MP Gaming community, primarily revolving around the roleplay aspect of the modification on Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas.

We make sure to run a community that's always fair and fun for everyone. In other words, community first and game server second. The server is originally started back in 2014 (December) and recently re-launched in 2017 (October). The script is worked on daily to give all the players a smooth gameplay and make your stay worth.

/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\

What are we offering? Let us pin down some of our unique features;

• Revolutionary Gang Crate System
We do have lot of great systems added for official families. Gang Crate is one of those. As a part of a gang/family, you can steal high grade weapon crates off military base and put those weapons into your gang lockers. Trust me, it's fun yet hard to steal those crates.

• Bank robbery system
We have one of the unique and interesting robbery system. Pick your friends up, go to bank and with usage of our implemented commands, create awesome roleplay scenario of bank robbery that will bring you decent amount of money and make you rich! More information can be found here.

• Weapon dropping system
You can drop your weapons on the floor and they will be visible to other players. They can simply approach them and pick them up. It is more realistic and increases the level of roleplay on our server.

• Advanced execute system
We have one of the coolest systems scripted for our Hitman Agency. During their contracts, hitman agency members can use the command /execute and finish their victim with just a single bullet.
