Shooting Range

Information
This filterscript was created by me, however, i got the idea from DeatheR's Triathlon, and i just took the part where it shoots the fishes, however i didn't want to change the objects (the fishes) into some targets or so, or some balls or whatever, i thought fishes would look funny and awesome to kill! The major part of the script credits goes to DeatheR, some of the functions for a very random Fish spawn!


The mini-game has:
  • 20 Fishes to shoot at.
  • 4 Weapons to choose from ( Ak47, M4, Shotgun, Sniper Rifle).
  • 6 Points from where to shoot.
  • 7 Pickups.
  • Dialog based.
  • Commands: /re (to QUIT) /range(to JOIN)
