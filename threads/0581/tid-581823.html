Why is everyone using 'hire' to describe the acceptance of a new member to the staff team lately? It seems improper. Administrators aren't slaves that do all the work for you, they are members as well and you must respect that.

The answer to your doubt is quite simple: Do not accept low qualified members to join the staff team - even if that slightly hurts their feelings. The community's image relies on the staff team, and if the community has an 'owner'/'leader' that has anger issues, bad grammar, heaps of stacks of lack of knowledge and/or the common lazy-to-provide support and updates mood, then it isn't going to work out either.

Grammar, grammar, grammar, it's all about grammar and their knowledge overall when it comes to accepting members into the staff team. I personally wouldn't accept someone that lack at both, even if they gave me a hundred dollars. Without these two aspects, they will repeatedly fail to do their job correctly, over and over.

I also noticed that administrators on servers tend to insult the players they ban from the server, if I was the leader of such community that had members like that, they wouldn't last long there and I'd try to compensate the one being insulted/banned (if it was done incorrectly); otherwise, I would just change the reason and apologize to them.

Also, spy on the administrators of the server once and a while. And test them with series of fake accounts to see how they react upon different situations - don't let them get by anything, I wouldn't let them.
