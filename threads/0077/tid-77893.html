Hey hey!... [SAP]Sidhu here. I made a trailer (for a truck) interior some time ago... I don't really know if it's good, so everyone who downloads this can be the judges of that...

_________________________________________________

Command:

/gototrlr - takes you to the Trailer interior (for rcon admin use only!)

_________________________________________________

How to use:

Go to the trailer (vehicle ID 435 which is Artict1 (Semi Trailer)), and go to the back of it. When behind the trailer, press G and you will be teleported inside. To leave the interior, simply go close to the end of the trailer and press enter.
