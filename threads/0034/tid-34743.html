My register & login filterscript i made credits to DracoBlue for dudb and someone for /stats cmd,

Dudb link - http://forum.sa-mp.com/index.php?topic=684.0

This filterscript saves a players:-

Money
Kills
Deaths
Wantedlevel
Level - for commands etc
In an editable file in scriptfiles, also uses register & login variable's to pevent players from spawning without logging if they are already registered, also this can be altered to make people have to register before they can spawn.

Downloads

PWN - http://www.mediafire.com/?bklexxidxjx
AMX - http://www.mediafire.com/?zzdirvmw3mm

RegServ + XAdmin cmds FS

PWN - http://www.mediafire.com/?slxtysx0oeh
AMX - http://www.mediafire.com/?rix0niccxda

To edit your level open the (yourname).txt file in scriptfiles and change "Level" to 5,
