You have to fix the colors, you're using orange derivates for everything and it is not very appealing. For instance, use light and dark colors not too intense, following a designer's thumb rule -never use more than 3 colors-. The page functions are good, but the design and eye-candy leave me to wish.

Overall, the page looks very 1990'ish. The header gradient, the flat fonts (in the buttons, in the banner logo and in the page body), the bad spacing between lines of words, and flat, bare, or unappealing title page would make me think you're offering servers with specs from 10 years ago.

Instead of having a large wall of text talking about your services, make it look modern with a slideshow or a smooth picture aligning it correctly with other pieces of brief text, generally pictures are more attractive to sight than a boring wall-o' text.
