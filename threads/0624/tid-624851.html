Vortex Deathmatch: Free-for-all

Vortex Deathmatch: Free-for-all - Version: 1.3.7
Features:
• Fun and simple free-for-all gamemode
• Random spawns, based in Fort Carson and surrounding areas
• Player stats presented in textdraws on-screen
• Bonus cash rewards with the built in headshot and killstreak systems!
• Player spawn protection to prevent the chances of camping
• Shop to spend your hard earned cash
• Unique suicide system, for the hell of it

Installation:
1. Download the lastest version from below.
2. Drag the files into your gamemodes folder, and change the server.cfg gamemode to "dm"
3. Change the rcon password to something of your choice.
4. Start it up and login!

Additional Notes:
• This is my first gamemode, so I would greatly appreciate some feedback on how to improve it!
• At the top of the script are the cash amounts given and taken from players, adjust these as you see fit.
• Please don't remove the credits as I've spent time making this for your enjoyment, thanks!

Images and Videos:



Upcoming Features:
• Vehicle spawns for roaming around the greater Las Venturas
• Improved shop with weapons and possibly an ammo system
• Multiple classes to choose from on login
Feel free to suggest some additions for this gamemode, I'd be happy to take some ideas for the next update!

Bugs:
• No known bugs.
I've spent a remarkably long time fixing bugs, but if you do find any please let me know so I can fixed them!

Download:
Version 1.3.7: https://dl.dropboxusercontent.com/u/...h%20v1.3.7.zip (dropbox)
Version 1.3.8: https://dl.dropboxusercontent.com/u/...h%20v1.3.8.zip (dropbox)

If any part of the post breaks please let me know immediately, otherwise I can't do anything!

As always, thanks for taking a look, if you've any suggestions or anything feel free to contact me!
