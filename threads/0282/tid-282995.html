Idiots (the people above me that is).

You don't necessarily need money to run a SA-MP server. There are countless free web/server hosts out there at your disposal!

However, it is recommended to pay money for your web/server hosting (and the hosted tab if you wish). I would recommend using Volt Host as your web/server host. You are instantly given free web-hosting with the purchase of a SA-MP server. Although the web-hosting is limited to low bandwidth and disk space, it should be good enough for you.

You asked "Do you need cash to start a server?": Your answer is no, you do not need cash to begin a server.
