The lobby is currently...well...a lobby. I'm not sure how else to describe it. It's a room where players gather during games. We have a shop in the lobby, which we are trying to find the ideas for.

Current shop contents:
-Backpacks $200 to $600
-Skin Change $200

"Toys" might be cool, but it's not exactly end-game content. What we are aiming for is stuff that would be worth more than $1000+, that may give an advantage, but still be realistic. Cars and Houses are out of the question, as this isn't an RP server.

Ideas that were thrown up during brainstorms (and some I thought of just now):
-Purchasing VIP with in-game money
-Purchasing access to special lobby activities (for instance, if we lock a map to the general public, you could buy your way in)
-That's all I can think of...

I'm not one for original ideas.
