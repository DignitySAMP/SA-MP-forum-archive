About It
This is mainly a interior for the dodo plane. When go to that plane and press g you will be taken into the interior. When you want to get out you simply press the "f" or the "Enter" key.

Features
  • Bathrooms
  • Piolet Cockpit
  • Chairs
  • TV
  • Moving Doors
  • And More!
