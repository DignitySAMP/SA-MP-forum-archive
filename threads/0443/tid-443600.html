How to be color blind?

It's genetic,usually the father carries the gene and if the mother also has the gene usually the children are color blinded. Color Blindness at Females is very rare,so the gene they carry usually will not affect them.

Abit more info from biology,the colorblindness gene is on the X chromosome,so females willl carry it to the male toddlers,It will not appear in Y chromosome so the males will cause every children they have to get color blinded,only for male toddlers tho,females will still carry,in extreme conditions you can get color blinded as a female
