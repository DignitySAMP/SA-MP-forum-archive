DISCLAIMERL: Please read before posting about the location!

This map is built at the abandoned airport for no special reason. I build all my exteriors at the abandoned airport and then move them using Adri1's map mover V1 or texture studio.

The map is user-friendly (flat bottom) , highly customizable and is supposed to act as a 'Custom object' within your own custom map(s).

Map mover by Adri1: https://forum.sa-mp.com/showthread.php?tid=506610

Simple (ghetto) apartment exterior built using modular (wall) objects.

I'm not using it, feel free to redistribute, claim it as your own or anything else. I don't care.
Map only contains basic texturing. I'll leave the rest up to your imagination.


Textured: Minimal -- Just the door frames.
Enter-able: No
Object Count: ~340

*Map contains a total of 9 doors. (8 Doors at the frontside, 1 backside door)
*Map contains bars in front of windows at the first floor to prevent burglary.
*Backside of the apartment is relatively empty (Stairs to roof & Back entrance)

Location: Abandoned airport. (Use map mover tool to move map to desired location)
Known Bug: Stairs collision file somtimes interferes with player just in front of the apartment.
