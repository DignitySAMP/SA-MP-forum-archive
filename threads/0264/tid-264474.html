What is this?
This is a simple race system/type where you can challenge
a player with a bet to have a Lead,Follow like race.
There are no boundaries or maps for this race, it can
happen anywhere. Watch the video below to get a clear idea.

Video:
[ame]http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8Vajl_5wFBY[/ame]

Command:
/challenege [Playerid] [Bet Amount]
/outrunhelp

Requirements:
Zeex's ZCMD
******'s sscanf2

How does it work?
When a player challeneges you, a var is set on your id with his.
A timer checks for the existence of any challenge and detects
the player & rival's postions to set the lead player.

Bugs:
Currently, i haven't noticed any bugs. If you find them, please report them here, Thanks.
