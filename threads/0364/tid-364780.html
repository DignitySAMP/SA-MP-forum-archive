Alfa BAR
Petrol station BAR, Los Santos
Few words about map.
I have always found this gas station boring and empty. And what else can make things more interesting then a bar. So at the back, next to car wash, there is a bar. You can enjoy it, by playing a pool downstairs, or simply buy a drink up stairs. You can also hang out on a terrace.
Map is made out of 173 objects. It also includes a car park, but no cars.
I would appriciate, if you send me a notification about using this map in your server. I would like to see, how it looks like in game. Please, enjoy it.
