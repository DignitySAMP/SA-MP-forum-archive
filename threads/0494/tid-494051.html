Rocket To The Moon By AdHaM612


Credits:

AdHaM612(Made The Script And Mapped The Moon)
JagjeetIndian(Mapped The Rocket)
Zeex(ZCMD)

What is this ?:

This is a FS which has a rocket, you must enter the rocket then type /launch then it will fly until it arrives at the moon. On the moon, you will find mountains, cars and volcans. When you feel bored from the Moon, you can go back to the earth by entering the Rocket then type /earth and it will drop down until it arrives at the rocket's base.

Commands:

/rocketcmds
/tprocket
/launch
/earth
