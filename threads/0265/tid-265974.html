Description: one day i was scripting and thought of using OPSP Include ( OnPlayerShootPlayer , wups) include then i thought of Call of Duty Games with the mode Hardcore i thought of making maps and every gun ingame will do 100 health damage every bullet so here i came up with Hardcore Team Deathmatch SA-MP heres a quick note not all maps are made by me some are from samp forums and MTA.

Features:

-1 Shot 1 Kill
-Weapon Anticheat
-15+ Maps
-Two Teams ( Team Blue , Team Red )
-3D Text Label saying what team they are
-Buy Dialog (/buy)
-On Spawn buy Dialog
-Few Textdraws
-Timeleft Textdraw for each map (3 min)
-Italy Most popular map from MTA
-Killing Sprees
-Score Saving
-Money Saving
-Weapon Skills droped down to 1
-Weapon Drop

Video:

[ame]http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m7ZHouJTUGA[/ame]
