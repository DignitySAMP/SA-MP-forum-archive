If you want to run something professional, at least 1 GB.
If you are going with a VPS, make sure it's guaranteed RAM because often, dedicated servers where your VPS is are overloaded and you won't get any burstable RAM.
Also, unmetered bandwidth is recommended. Most of VPS providers tell you upload speed. If not, ask them.

Regarding setting up servers for your customers. Most of companies has auto activation these days. I recommend you learn HTML/PHP before running into such business.
