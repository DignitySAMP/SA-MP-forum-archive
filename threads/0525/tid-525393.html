So I thought I'd make my first map release in around five years. Here's something I finished this morning.

Palomino Creek Sheriff's Department:

It's only small and simple, but there again, a sheriff's department based in a small town like Palomino Creek probably would be!

I mapped it where the library was. There's two separate car parks (or parking lots, whatever you prefer to call them); one for visitors and another fenced off one for the sheriffs. I added a barrier but for the life of me I couldn't get it to rotate properly, so I'll leave that to you.
