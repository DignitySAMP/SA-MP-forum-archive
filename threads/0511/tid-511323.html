To begin, I'd just like to say I am only creating this topic to get idea's and to know what players enjoy doing in a Cops and Robbers gamemode.

So my partner and I have been developing a "Apocalyptic" themed Cops and Robbers gamemode for the past 2 weeks. We've got a lot done and we're currently working on vehicles and adding more robbable shops. The thing is, I've never created one before so this is a completely new experience to me.

So I'm here to ask you guys - what do you want to see in a Cops and Robbers server? What type of features, etc. I appreciate any and all feedback you can provide as it will help the overall development of this Cops and Robbers gamemode.

Here's a few pictures showing the current few elements of our gamemode.

This is a picture of the Idlewood Area. I'd like to note that we don't usually have this much grass and we're going to be making adjustments to it to avoid lag. The yellow bar on the bottom isn't actually developed yet - but we plan on integrating it with our "Karma" system. Think of Fallout 3 here. Karma will be the players score. We're still thinking of ideas, so I can't really give you more information as of yet.


This is a picture of the freeway, displaying some of the mappings.


And finally - this is a picture showing the "Most Wanted" vehicle. We're most likely going to change the model. The most wanted vehicle will randomly spawn after 15 minutes at a random location. If a player delivers it to the drop off point they earn cash. This is just a small feature we added.

We're not too sure how to develop the karma system, as of yet. Currently when you do a bad act such as rob a store your karma will go down.
