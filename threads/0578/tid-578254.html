Personally I hate roleplay servers.

Dislikes:
-Personally the first time I visited a roleplay server, I got bored pretty quickly because I didnt know what to do,
-I couldnt get help because there was no chat.
-I tried to get on another server that also roleplay and it had a test which asked what was meta-gaming,power gaming, occ and other stuff like that, it had the answers to that on a list, but not all of them, so I left, the idea seemed interesting though.
-The term roleplay in itself doesnt really seem attractive to me, cause I see no appeal in being someone else (like acting you were a cop, robber, pizza delivery boy or whatver) I would rather be myself.
- There are too many terms to remember and to many rules.( meta gaming, power gaming, ninja something, OCC,MG)
-The slowness of it all, because when I first came on a roleplay server I was really dejected because I couldnt get a car that WASNT RENTED (it was locked), this made me look for a car for atleast 30 minutes, and that was so annoying because I only got to see the regular scenery of the gta san andreas map.
-This is more concerning the players, but I hate those guys on the forum that comment "Great for roleplay" like it would be good on any other server like CnR or a Racing server...
-Everyone is invisible on the map, so its hard to locate your friend.

Likes:
-Money has value in roleplay servers. (you can buy stuff like houses, cars, properties) but they are expensive.
-It offers many jobs, meaning the player can choose which way he wants to earn it, and the jobs are different so its exciting for new roleplay players (or so I think).
-Due to money having value in roleplay servers, and the slownes off it, there are a lot of players spending a lot of time on roleplay, which is good because its a good method to keep players online, its addicting and you are actually happy when you earn money on the server.
-Hunger/thirst systems which also prolong players time on the server.
-Roleplay servers can be addicting because of this.
