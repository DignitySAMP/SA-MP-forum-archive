Steam Downgrader
Hello and welcome to the guide that instructs you to both downgrade your Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas and download / use the programs provided.
NOTE: This guide only applies to users who have installed GTA:SA through Steam.

A lot of users have in the past have struggled to find certified downgraders to downgrade their GTA:SA, if they have purchased their game through Steam. Finally, I have decided to share my friend's (certified) downgrader file for those who have purchased GTA:SA by Steam. Here, I have written basic steps to guide you through to download, downgrade, and .. more download and upgrading stuff. Basically, this is what you will accomplish after following through these steps:
  • Downgrade GTA:SA
  • Install SA-MP properly

Step 1:
Download the downgrader file from this link:

Step 2:
Open the file from where you have saved it. Once you have opened it, you will recieve a window that looks like this.

Click Start to Begin.

Step 3:
After you have clicked Next, you will go to another page. If you wish, you may click back to read the previous instructions or exit the installation.

Find where your GTA:SA game folder has been placed and locate it. Check if your computer has enough space required for the installation (downgrader). Once you are done, press Next to continue.

Step 4:
After you have clicked Next, you will go to another page. If you wish, you may click back to read the previous instructions or exit the installation.

To continue, you must agree to the terms of agreement. If you do, click on the " I agree with the above terms and conditions" check box. Then click next.


Step 5
You have now reached the 2nd last page of your installation proccess. To continue, press Next.



Step 6
This is last instruction page to complete your installation. It is also the last chance to properly quit the program without possibly harming your GTA:SA Files. If you feel you are ready to dowgrade your game to get SA-MP, click Next!



Step 7
Now you have downgraded your Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas so it is compatible for SA-MP installation. If you wish to download SA-MP, go to www.sa-mp.com/download.php. Download the SA-MP client installer from 1 of the 2 mirrors and install the program into your GTA San Andreas Folder.
