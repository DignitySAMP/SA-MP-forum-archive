Hey this is my seconds filterscript im releasing. i made this system for a DM server a few weeks ago and now its ready to be released.
Hope you enjoy it.

Script Info:
Command:
/duel [id] [weapon] [price]
weapons: deagle, mp5, chainsaw, silpist, combatsg, shotgun.(you can always add more if your in the mood for it).
/acceptduel
/declineduel
/watchduels
/stopwatch

Info:
When you duel someone with /duel the price u enter will be the fee for both players, when the other player accepts the inventation the amount will be removed from the player's money and put in a price pool, the winner of the duel wins the price pool.
When your inside the the duel room you cant look true the walls. when your outside(inside the watching area) you CAN look into the duel rooms.
