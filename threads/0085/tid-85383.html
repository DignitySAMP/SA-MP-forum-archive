Introduction
Till now, it`s been not possible to shoot from a Maverick, neither from the normal one than from the Police one.
But everyone want that !
Who don`t want to aim in a hot chase on escaping suspects from a flying Maverick ?
And who cant imagine all the possibilities if it were just possible
We all would love the cinematic Scenes of chases, where the hunted Players are driving thru the explosions around themself and dancing with the action !
And now you can do so !
Now the wishes from players, who liked it, can stop !
From the Creators of the GTA 4 Wantedsystem, Nicksoft now comes a complete innovative new Script, to make your gaming experience a whole much better !
Because NOW its possible to shoot rockets (Bullets are complete impossible ) from a flying Police Maverick directly on everything !
As this weren`t enough, i`ve added the most importants settings at the top of the script with an explaining, what they do , to make it evereyone possible to change them.

How did you do this ?
In simple : I used a checkpoint to aim In SAMP there is no custom way to aim, so i changed it to a checkpoint It follows the chopter in a relative way, so if the chopter moves, the aiming spot moves with it. The Camera always look at the Checkpoint.. With the control, you increase/decrease the realative Position of the Checkpoint, so its added like x (from chopter) + relative x-added (from control). So its realy easy to aim, even you need some minutes to get it ! To dont make the chopter be in the cam, them cam is put at the ground of the maverick (can look a bit bad, if your standing on the ground). If you shoot, the rocket-model teleports to your actual position, moves to the aimed position at the moment you pressed fire, and if its there, it explodes

Control
Notice:Buttons can be hold down
Up, Down, Left, Right ---> Arrows
De-/Increase height ---> The keys you wuld use for an hydra to start/land (in LV for example the height is the most of the time right set)
Left Mousebutton ---> Shoot rocket
(If there comes a problem with "Launcher not ready", use /reload)

Easy Settings:

If you cannot script, but want to use this script, just edit this settings, which you can find on top of the Script:
Code:
//#################### Server Settings ##############################

// ---------------- Section Performance -----------------------------
new camtime = 100; //the time for refreshing the camera of the gunner
new rockettime = 200; //the time to check if the rocket is at the aiming spot
new keytime = 100; //the time to lookup pressed keys for gunners

// ---------------- Section Testing ---------------------------------
new testing = 0; //0 = off, 1 = on || testmodus meaning teleporting to an existing chopter + getting police skin to test this script (type /testing ingame)

// --------------- Section General Options --------------------------
new explosiontype = 6; //type of explosion, see wiki
new Float:explosionradius = 10.0; //radius of explosion, see wiki
new Float:rocketspeed = 56.0; //speed of the rocket to move

/* ---------------- Some Informations -------------------------------
1.Look at line 79/80 to change the Model of your raket (to see what model it is, use ****** or read the commentary at their back)
2.I used a lot of variables, i dont know anymore exactly, for what they are used ! You can delete unused Variables if you want to !
3.If your not good in scripting, just edit the values above (!!!)
4.If the message appears too often, that the Launcher is not ready, use /reload
5.Feel free to edit, but never miss out my credits in script & console-printout
*/
//#################### End of Settings ##############################
