» MAP: Military Executive Headquarters
_______________________________________________


» Description

This is another interior of mine - an executive headquarters for military factions, such as the army or national guard.

It's built underground with a security-entrance at "ground level" with metal detectors. A few stories underneath that, we find a briefing room, a large office/intelligence center, a smaller office for some kind of leader, a surveillance room, yet another office and an armory.

This was made to fit the bunker southwest of Las Payasadas in Bone County, so I mapped a helipad and a SAM-site on top of that as well - you can see that in the beginning of the video.

There are 250 objects in total.

This is mapped in Interior 1!
