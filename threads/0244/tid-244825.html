Code:
1) Convert your maps to CreateObject if you haven't already

2) Open pawno -> click new

3) Put your CreateObject code under "OnFilterScriptInit"

4) Hit F5 or Compile

5) Put in server.cfg "filterscripts NAME" (Replace NAME with the name of the script)
Is that what you mean?
