A /me Is Used for Your Character Doing Something (Example: /me Takes off his shoes)
A /do Is Used for Other Terms Like (Example: /me Attempts to take your dollar note - /do S/F (Succeed/Fail - Answer in a /do also). Also You can Use /do for Thinking, and what your character sees, smells, hears ect...
