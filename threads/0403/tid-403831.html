I've been on many servers, in different locations and in different languages. Small servers, big servers, All sorts... What I mean is I have experience, and I have a good understanding about Role Play, and what a Role Play server needs to be like, So I decided to actually make one myself.

But looks like people still make these crappy refund servers and call them HARD RP, when they can't Role Play at all. In my opinion there is no such thing as Hardcore RP, medium RP and etc... RolePlay is RolePlay, like a person said. And all of the servers being in Los Santos, it's just getting kind of boring...

I want to make something unique, I already got a unique script, never seen on an english community, and a scripter. So I thought since I was making this so other people could enjoy it, not just me, I should ask everyone else what they would like.

First thing. Map? Where would you like the server to be based in. All servers do big cities, and it gets pretty boring, so i think I am going to do the Country Area.

Red County,
Bone County,
Fort Carson,
Bayside,
Angel Pine.

I really got attached to making a server in Bone County, but there was a problem - THE NAME, I don't want this to look like another server with a crappy name, I mean I have to start somewhere, but I need a good name, or else people will think it's another crappy server and just ignore it.

Well, that's just about all I need for now, so please give me a few tips/ideas. Anything you've been dreaming to to see in the RolePlay Community? Well share it here, this could be turned into what you have been waiting for.


P.S. Ignore the last post, I am not a noob, Just wanted to start a little discussion
