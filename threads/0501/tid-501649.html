You have thousands of datacenters around the world.
You just need to select the most suitable ones for the kind of services you want to offer.

Hosting the services at your house will give you some problems.
For example: the electricity bill will increase like sh*t.

You need to have a good network connection[preferable 100mb upstream / 100mb downstream], just for the computer.[You should not use it, since it can cause lag spikes, and packet loss for some people connected to the computer]

And one of the most important things, you need to have a computer which supports to be online 24 / 7, with the right maintenance.


Having a datacenter? There's no problem at all!
You rent a server, put things in it, and let the people connect to it.
