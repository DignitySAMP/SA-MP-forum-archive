After many hours of research I have come to the conclusion of asking you guys.

So I've been working on a script which I found online, and have broken it down into a cleaner and fresher script for the 0.3z launcher.
I was curious as to hear your guys thoughts on the situation, and if you think this could work.

I was going to re-launch a new genre which has seemily died out from the SA:MP community; The uh-.. Prison Roleplay genre.

You're probably just tihnking; "Fuck sake, another attempt which is going to die out". I have no intentions on keeping it up for months on end, but I feel like it may have potential.

If you could please vote on the poll above, that'd be excellent.
You don't even have to comment, you can just post a poll response and that'd be awesome.

If you'd also like to help code or become a staff member for the whole project, let me know, and I'll certainly come into terms with you.

Danny. (Cookland)
