During the Watts riots in 1965,
scientists developed a new strand of vaccine for
vaccine research and tested it upon an un-fortunate
victim. This victim developed inhuman body systems
and completely overrode the bodys internal organs and
brainfunctionality, the brain lusted for blood,
the actions that were displayed were researched
and the body of the victim was research.

During this research, an event took place, a
couple convicts from the San Andreas state
penitentiary whom were serving on death row
were subjected to take participation in research
for this vaccine and virus methods of spread, unfortunately all the convicts died.

Now they have attempted the new vaccine and they have picked out a wide list of convicts from corrupt cops to gangnbangers.
The aim of the game is to survive and be the last person standing to be infected, will you survive?

Features
• Very simply engineered with basic general functions.
• Various random spawning.
• Brand new skin selection system
• Reporting system for normal users
• Once all players are infected, resets the game.
• Selects one random player to start off as the alpha-infected(first infected).
• Once a player who is infected hits another player it infects them.
• Effective admin commands but not many only for rcon.
• Spawning system, when they game starts it takes five minutes until spawning is disabled, if a player tries connecting and spawning into the game while spawning is disabled he will go into spectation until next round.
• Color coding system, for infected, uninfected and spectators.
• Every 4 minutes a random message is broadcasted, eg. "Weapon unlocks are available! The more kills you get the more weapons your able to gain, get killing!"
• Administrators are now distinguished by a lazer attached onto there weapon.
• Weaponry is now added, you now spawn with a knife and a colt 45.
• Scoring system that assigns you with new weapon unlocks, such as when you reach a score of 5 you unlock and spawn with a desert eagle, instead of the glock.
• Weapon skills set, so you cannot akimbo with weapons.
• Brand new headshot system, if you shoot an infected player in the head, more health is declined than a regular shot.

Commands
Code:
Normal user - /nextplayer /playertypes /admins /infected /spectators /uninfected /report
Administration(RCON admin) - /restartround /infect /kill /uninfect /disablespawning /clearchat /respawn
