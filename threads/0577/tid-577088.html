Well, i noticed there really werent any Fire Department maps within Red County. I was searching for one for my Server and just couldnt find a decent one. I decided to take it upon myself and create this Station. This is the first map i created in about a year. Within this fire station, you will find 4 bays, which can hold 5 vehicles and 2 smaller ones (On my server i have an Engine, Ladder, Yosemite, 2 Ambulances, a quad and golf cart), there is room for parking out of the front door. When you walk in, youll be greeted by an entertainment area (A couch 2 arm chairs and a flat screen tv), to the left would be the bathroom, straight ahead is the kitchen, and to the left of the kitchen is the bunk room. There is a door that goes from the Day room, to the bays. Hope yall enjoy it, if you do please comment! If you dont, suggest what i could do to make it better!
