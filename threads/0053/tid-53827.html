Private Parking

Major updates been done on the Filterscript.

1) Now you have to /buyticket or /buyticket2 for entering the carpark. (cost 2000$, can be changed in script)
2) After /buyticket the gate will open, and closes automatic after 5 sec.
3) After /buyticket2 the gate will open and the elevator will take you to the second floor. (be sure u stand on the white arrow)
4) For exiting the carpark, do /exitpark or /exitpark2.
5) It also has a pickup next to the ticket-machine with commands.

I fixed the bug, So now you can only do /buyticket and /exitpark at the pickup's ...

Have fun with it ...

Special thanks to Kapil's Map Designer
