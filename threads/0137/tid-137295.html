Karts

What is it?

Detailed map with 4 Karts and a pretty nice race track for you to race others round. It also features the command /gokarts to teleport you to the Map, 3 icons you can buy food from and depending on how much health you have is how much it restores, no more then 4 Karts can go round this track unless more are spawned so I made it when you exit one of the Karts that come with the script it destroys and respawns you at the teleport and the Kart.
There is also 2 ramps to stop Karts going round the track the wrong way and getting out of the arena.
