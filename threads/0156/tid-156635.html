Hi MrDzx,

There is a SA:MP function to do this:
Code:
Attach3DTextLabelToVehicle( vehicle3Dtext, vehicle_id, 0.0, 0.0, 2.0);
Take a look at the wiki for further help: https://wiki.sa-mp.com/wiki/Attach3DTextLabelToVehicle
