The problem with this is you're hosting a live online service on your machine, if you do not know how to properly secure it which when it comes to PHP / Apache / MySQL etc it requires a decent amount of configuring and knowledge. These are not things you're going to learn in the matter of a few days or weeks, you need to spend at least a good month or so with a test server testing the methods and tutorials and documents you read online that help you secure your online service properly.
