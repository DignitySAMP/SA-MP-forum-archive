Weponz Drift

What Is It?

My 1st drift map,Its a small drift track over the stadium near grove street,It has tight bends,short straights,up and down bends and you are sure to be swinging side to side most of the track,It has barriers in the most critical parts of falling off the track,Ive tested it its nice and the barriers will stop and accidental falling.Theres around 40 objects so a streamer is not needed,Theres also 8 elegys

Teleport?

/wepzdrift

Code:
CMD:wepzdrift(playerid, params[])
{
   	SetPlayerInterior(playerid, 0);
    SetPlayerPos(playerid, 2737.6038,-1758.1714,44.2911);
    ResetPlayerWeapons(playerid);
    new str[100];
    GetPlayerName(playerid, str, sizeof(str));
    format(str, sizeof(str), "%s Has Joined 'WepzDrift'! Type /wepzdrift To Join Him!", str, params[4]);
    SendClientMessageToAll(0x0, str);
    return true;
}
