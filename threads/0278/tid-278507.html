What's this?
Hello.
This is a filterscript, that doesn't just record your peak online players.
It has many other functions, which are listed below, and it's pretty easy to use!


Features
  • Saving the records, the date and the time of it for every day.
  • Possilibity of enabling / disabling textdraw that shows the current players and today's record (pics below)
  • Possibility of viewing last days record by just inputting the date in the Record Control Panel
  • Possibility of resetting the records of the date specified(RCON Admin Only!!)
  • More!

How to use?
It's pretty easy.
If you would like to toggle the textdraw showing the info (Current players and today's record) just open the script, go to line 8, and either define
pawn Code:
#define UseTD true
(Default) if you would like to see the textdraw, or
pawn Code:
#define UseTD false
if you want it hidden.

To access the Records Control panel, use the command /recordspanel.
Have fun!
