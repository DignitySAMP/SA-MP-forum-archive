So I got inspired by both Mafia II and The Godfather II (both are videogames - If you didn't knew them yet).
I quit the Assassin's Creed SAMP project ages ago, because it didn't quite give the feeling I wanted, and since I really love to script, I wanted someting else and unique. That's why I came up with this: Mafia III: San Andreas.

Quote:
Originally Posted by Promotion/Slogan
Power and money. Those are the only ways to succes. The Mafia knows this.
Los Santos, San Fierro and Las Vegas. Every city's got 2 Mafia families fighting for it.
Do you choose to fight along with them for power and money? Or would you rather not stick into it and start your own business, or perhaps even join the police or SWAT to fight the crimes of the Mafia?
Join us!
The idea of this gamemode goes as following:

Every city (LS, SF and LV) got two Mafia families. They all try to get as much money and power, and unfortunately it doesn't go without a price, since there are more families.
You gain money by simple things like robbing small stores (which can be owned by players - more of that later), up to robbing the bank, a jewelry etc. Power is gained by taking over certain spots, like airports, local police stations, businesses etcetera (and no, not by just staying in a certain area for a while - you've gotta do some missions for that).

What I also thought of, is the 'tutorial' you get in some roleplay servers. You get the same explaination always. You either have to drive through the whole town, you barely watch what they're telling you and after that you're on your own, barely knowing what to do. It's always way too hard at the beginning. That's why I came up with a storyline.
When you register and spawn for the first time, you'll have a story (probably a mainstream story, "just came back from the army, we don't have alot money" blablabla). Before you'll be able to go out all alone, you'll have to do some missions, complete some achievements etc.

When you register, you have the choice between "Civillian" and "Mafia". I'll explain it a bit;

Civillian
As a civillian, you'll just roleplay like any other roleplay server (except for the fact that it's totally unique, and it won't be so strict and mainstream like other servers). I want everyone to have some "power", so you won't get small jobs like a courier or anything like that. I'm not gonna tell about this storyline when you start, but basically, as a civillian you'll get these options:
* Join the police (and eventually the S.W.A.T.)
* Start your own business or shop (or buy one) [This option will get maaaany suboptions - Keeping it secret though]

Mafia
When you choose this, you'll get some kinda of mainstream shitty story like "We've always been poor, coming from a poor country, trying to get a new life here" and you'll have to do some missions, get involved with the Mafia and then join them and try to get as high as possible etc. May sound boring but I have many ideas with it, but keeping alot secret yet.

So the gamemode will kinda be a mix between:
- Roleplay
- Reallife (yes, roleplay and reallife are different! - Alot of reallife servers call theirselves 'roleplay' while they are really not that)
- Team deathmatch

The only aspects I like from Godfather II (and what I'm gonna put in this gamemode) are:
* The war between families (but not a constant deathmatch/bloodbath!)
* (This I did not mention before); you can choose an ability when you join the Mafia and improve this ability, and your family can use this in certain missions. (Like special ability to start fire, or to work with explosives etc).

I'm not sure if I shared everything I wanted to share, but this is atleast a bit of what I'm working on.
What's your opinion on this?.
I don't care if nobody likes it. I just like to script, and I'd really like to know if other people like this, because I'm kinda excited myself. If this seems to be succesfull I'll set up a server eventually!!!.

I'm open for ideas. If you have any, please PM them to me. I'll ignore messages like "Can I be co-owner or admin? I have alot of experience!" - It's not sure yet if I'll setup a server. Or messages like "Do you need help with scripting?" - No thank you, I'm experienced enough myself and really can handle it. I'll keep you somewhere in my mind though if something doesn't work out (no I will not).

Kind Regards,
Kevin

FYI;

Roleplay
Roleplay (or Roleplaying-Game) means nothing else than "Act like the character you are". If you choose to be a cop, you have to act like one. If you choose to be a fireman, you act like one. If you choose to be a criminal, you steal shit and smoke dope and that shit. A real RP(G) server lets you choose from the very start what you want, and you'll always be that and act like that (and sometimes you can change it at anytime). So rules like "don't drive to fast" really have nothing to do with roleplaying.

Reallife
So well, what most servers are or claim to be; you have a 'life' and try to make the best of it, with many aspects of reallife. Has really not much to do with roleplaying.


And my gamemode tends to be roleplay; you choose to be mafia or not (when you register you'll have to choose that); you should act like the choise you make. And from that point you'll have to roleplay with some reallife aspects (I'd like to have some reallife aspects in it, like a police that patrols and fines people who are speeding - and if a store is robbed, actually set up patrols there for a while (Like in Mafia II), and eventually start an investigation etc).
