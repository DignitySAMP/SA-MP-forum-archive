Okay let's put all jokes aside and actually help the topic starter. Basically there's a formula that gives you the exact playerbase.

Players = (U / L)^2 * (C + F) * 1.7524

U = amount of unique server features
L = level of roleplay rated between 1 (worst) and 10 (super duper strict hardcore rp)
C = amount of characters (letters) in the server name
F = amount of government factions

For a server called Angle Pine Roleplay with 10 unique features, rp level 8 and 3 government factions your playerbase will be 46 players.
