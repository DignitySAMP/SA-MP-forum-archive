You shouldn't begin with RP, a role playing environment which consists of many concepts familiar with real life is hardly achievable, you just don't learn how to RP today, start making a RP script tomorrow, things need time, if you want to make a RP script you firstly need to learn how to RP (if you don't already) and trust me it's not easy to perform what you learn from the first time, it's not a programming language. Testing RP scripts requires testers, if not a complete team of them, you just don't stay 3 days in front of the computer testing and scripting your RP game mode, that's pretty much committing suicide. I don't think 'starting' should be that much hard, right? Begin with something easy, DM is considered easy because it's ALL based on killing unless you would add complex features which would also not differ from the complexity of RP scripts. So, my suggestion is beginning with something easy for yourself, we ain't saying what would be easy, what's easy for me would be hard for you and normal for someone else, everyone has his knowledge, skills and ways of managing stuff. Just begin with what you wish to, what you can do, what you can edit/modify or create not what you just download and start server off with, without any basic idea what you are running, how the fuck are you to fix bugs then? If crash issues happen, if there's a loophole, many things can make your server with zero players even with hosted tab, it's all instability, players won't play where server crash due to scripting errors and such.

Summary is, decide what you want to make basing on your knowledge, even if basic knowledge, ONLY choose what you can develop or even if you modify a released script, you should know how to fix bugs, debug code and understand what you are actually having in your script, such a shame if you don't even know what you are having in your server and yet you own it, how would you manage something you, yourself don't know anything off? That's why usually beginners aren't recommended to open a server with a released script. So you should start off learning basics (if you didn't already) and then advance into choosing your own game mode after you are satisfied with your knowledge, whether basic or advanced, it's up to you.
