----
----Features:
  • When entering a car it shows the name of it.
  • When talking in chat,what you'll have said will be shown on your nametag (only other players can see it not u).
  • Basic Commands (afk,back,teles,help,rules,f,pm,kill).
  • Change your name's color! (cmenu).
  • Stunt Maps (on lsa,sfa,lva).
  • 53 Drift Teleports and 1 deathmatch map.
  • 7 Derbies and 32 basejumps.
  • Extra: Race Map.
  • Infinite Nitro.
  • Repair your car by pressing 2!
  • More things you can find by playing it.
  • Changes made to 1.5 (pastebin).
  • Changes made to 2.0 (pastebin).
    and believe me there were a lot of changes to 2.0!
----Pictures:
  • Album (imgur.com)
----Recommended:
  • a custom version of ZeroAdmin.
  • adding new things to the gamemode (like maps,cmds etc.)
----Requirements 1.0:
  • Incognito's Streamer (Plugin + Include)
  • Zeex's ZCMD (Include)
  • maddinat0r's sscanf v.2.8.2 (Plugin + Include)
  • Dracoblue's Dini (Include)
----Requirements 1.5:
  • Incognito's Streamer (Plugin + Include)
  • Yasha's iZCMD (Include)
  • maddinat0r's sscanf v.2.8.2 (Plugin + Include)
  • Gammix's Dini2 (Include)
----Requirements 2.0:
  • Incognito's Streamer (Plugin + Include)
  • YourShadow's Pawn.CMD 3.1.3 (Plugin + Include)
  • maddinat0r's sscanf v.2.8.2 (Plugin + Include)
  • Gammix's Dini2 (Include)
