how to can i connect the npc with my gm because im new on npcs. thanks!!!!! again!
When you download it you will see a npcmodes folder,a recordings folder,and a filterscripts folder.
open the filterscripts folder and put the 2 files in your Server Folder in "filterscripts".
Then put the items in the npcmodes folder in your Server npcmodes.
After that when you have your npcmodes folder open you will see recordings....
put the contents of the downloaded files in recordings in your server's recordings..
Last but not least in your server.cfg add Kriminal and HS like this: filterscripts Krimson
(Krimson is the LV edition) (LS editions are like this): filterscripts HS Kriminal Krimson
