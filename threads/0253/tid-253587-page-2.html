Well, it works sometimes, but I'd still suggest making advertisements without lying. Telling people that they'll get admin is just lame, as you need to know the player your making an admin (To prevent your server from being fucked up.), you need to be able to trust the person and also he needs to be active.

Just tell them what the good sides of your server is, for example, unique script, good mapping, awesome roleplay, good admins etc. Dont highlight the bad sides of your server, you dont even need to mention them.
If your patient enough, you might end up with actual roleplayers, instead of these powerhungry guys that come to your server just to become admin.

My tip to you is: Do not ever lie in an advertisement, dont mention the bad sides of the server, try to highlight the good sides, maybe scripting, mapping etc. Good luck.

Advertisements does actually give you a few players, not always, but sometimes if your patient enough, you might end up with a popular server.
