Herro SAMP community!
I'm here to gather the communities opinion on a gamemode I am actively developing. Feedback is welcome as always.

Quote from Wikipedia:
Quote:

A dystopia is a community or society, usually fictional, that is in some important way undesirable or frightening. It is the opposite of a utopia. Such societies appear in many works of fiction, particularly in stories set in a speculative future. Dystopias are often characterized by dehumanization,[1] totalitarian governments, environmental disaster,[2] or other characteristics associated with a cataclysmic decline in society. Elements of dystopias may vary from environmental to political and social issues.
The gamemode is based around heavy player interaction along with a medium to heavy RP level. It will revolve around the city of Los Santos and it's surrounding parts although it will have many custom mappings. It will be a "Dystopian" roleplay gamemode. In Los Santos, it will be controlled by a government that is corrupt. Not in a DM'ing way, but like paying off gangs, executions of political enemies, etc. There will be a few factions including:
  • The City Guard(Basically a dystopian version of the police)
  • The Army
  • The Insuregency(Rebel like faction)
  • Medical Department
  • Government News(In charge of propoganda and such)
We're trying to make Los Santos a place where players won't spend the majority of the time in. So when players reach level 2, they gain access into the wastelands.
