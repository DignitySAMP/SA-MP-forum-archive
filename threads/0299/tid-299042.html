Secret Operation - GameMode: Hitlars Hideout


i started playing sa-mp (5 years ago)

Protect The Prime Minister by Sandra18[NL] is my favourite gamemode,

thanks to that i downloaded her gamemode and edited it

so this is my new gamemode full credits go to Sandra18[NL]


Teams:

Team 1:
Name: Hitlar
Color: Yellow
Duty: Survive for (default) 10 minutes
Spawnlocation: Outside a secret building in the country
Spawnweapons: cane & minigun unlitmited ammo
Enemies: British Army & SAS

Team 2:
Name: Hitlars Bodyguard - You Can Jail Any British sas or soldier for 1 min if captured alive or anyone else of that matter
Color: Light-Blue
Duty: Protect Hitlar & Nazi Germany
Spawnlocation: Outside a secret building in the country
Spawnweapons: Silenced 9mm, Micro SMG (Uzi), AK47 unlitmited ammo
Enemies: British Army & SAS

Team 3:
Name: SAS Special Forces (British)
Color: Dark-Blue
Duty: Hunt for The Nazi Germany Leader & Kill Him (Hitlar)
Spawnlocation:On a plane above hitlars loaction - you need to parachute down and kill him
Spawnweapons: 9mm, Shotgun, Country Rifle unlitmited ammo (also a rocket launcher x 5) you get another rocket launcher when you respawn
Enemies: Hitlar & His Bodygaurds

Team 4:
Name: American Armed Forces
Color:red
Duty: Kill Hitlar
Spawnlocation:Country West - Head east Towards a yellow dot on the map & help the british army kill hitlar
Spawnweapons: Flamethrower, Shotgun unlitmited ammo
Enemies: Hitlar & His Bodygaurds

Team 5:
Name: Britsh Armed Forces
Color: Green
Duty: Kill Hitlar
Spawnlocation:You Just Parachuted on to a beach and across the water is hitlar outside his hideout - go kill him
Spawnweapons: Shovel, Micro SMG (Uzi) unlitmited ammo
Enemies: Hitlar & His Bodygaurds


Gameplay:
There are 5 teams, each with a duty. It's bases on a simple TDM.
The game can end in 2 ways:
Hitlar survives for a given time (default 10 minutes)
Hitlar gets killed

In case The Nazi Leader (Hitlar) survives, Hitlar & nazi germany will gain 1 point, when Hitlar Gets killed, the British & Army will gain 1 point and the gamemode will restart.
The score will get saved in a file (scores.txt).

If the player who is Hitlar leaves the server, the mode will not end, but the timer will stop and the Hitlar-skin will be available again. (Use F4 and /kill to choose a new skin). When a new player starts as Hitlar, the timer will get set back to (default) 10 minutes.


Commands:

Normal commands:
/help, /kill, /score, /duty
