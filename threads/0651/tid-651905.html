In our server (Breaking Desert Roleplay) there are events that kind of look like fortnite. It's possible to script the actual Fortnite server, so good luck with it! I can explain how our event system works. We spawn on a flat platform ( or it's a sandy beach, which isn't flat) and we build walls for three minutes, but we're limited to 1000 points. Every kind of wall costs another amount of points and is really a tactical game. After those three minutes you get one minute to pick your weapons (1. MP5 + Shotgun, 2. Pistol + M4, 3. Pistol + Sniper). The walls can be destroyed by shooting at them, last player standing wins!
