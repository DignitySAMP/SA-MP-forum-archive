EPISODE 1
Yo Yo Yo Yo! This is Nikobaxton, and i present to you my first Samp Series - WAR.

WAR is a story about how the war between the san andreas government and the gangsters affect the lives of four guys:-

Niko- Ex gangster
Kate- Niko's Wife
Billy- Part of the San Andreas Police Department
Jean- Gangster and Billy's best pal
Mayor- Mayor of the City

Most of the episodes will focus on these characters and their stories! While some other may or may not explore different entities in San Andreas.

Synopsis: This episode shows how the War originates and just introduces you to the characters.
That's the main purpose of the episode! Tho i think the last bit of sequence of scenes were amazing Expect some really good action and drama from the series! And the upcoming episodes will be much cooler than this one, as you guys would already know about the characters and the setting, that gives me the space to put in some cool set-pieces and show my moves

That's it! Watch Episode 1! Feel free to comment and criticize my shortcomings
