He is not talking about forums...but the actuall script.
I can help you with TeamViewer, but i suggest you do this yourself, its very easy
I assume you allready have all your files saved in your PC
1. Make a FTP account under FTP Accounts on Volt-Host Dashboard
2. Download and install the FileZilla client version from herehttp://filezilla-project.org/
3. Login to your server with the ftp credentials
4. Simply upload all your files onto the server
5. Modify your server.cfg with the ip given by Volt-Host
6. Start your server.

If you think its too hard, then here is another way arround
Go to your dashboard on Volt-Host, then to the FileManager, and choose upload directory, in the box that pops-up, choose your saved game directory from your PC.
I dont recommend the second solution, since it takes more time for the upload to be made.

I hope i helped you with these, if you still having problems, contact me here or on MSN
