Introduction


Yes, wheels do change the vehicles handling skill.

These are all from personal experiences and opinions, however the handling stats have agreed with them in the handling configurations.

Information about testing

These wheels were all tested on the same vehicle; Premier.

Wheel Statistics - Opinionated

Cutter - 4/10
Less grip. It has a fantastic design, but a low grip, it can take corners. My team and I find this one used best for straight drag racing.

Rimshine - 5/10
Same as the standard vehicle wheels. No change, which what we found it as.

Offroad - 10/10
Handling, speed and aggression. We have looked into them, they slightly increase the speed of going up hills and the turning whilst turning on and off roads.

Mega - 5/10
Same as the standard vehicle wheels. No change, which what we found it as.

Access - 9/10
Very good grip and speed, mixed with a combination. Not so good at off-road, but great when it comes to turning and accelerating.

Grove - 3/10
Useless, terrible at handling and bad at offroading. However, they are stylish! I give 3 points.

The rest, we have not tested and/or they are the same as Mega.
