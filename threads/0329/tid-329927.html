Is it possible? If so, how?
The default login for routers is generally admin/admin. Try accessing your router.

And, without router access, you cannot port forward.

Quote:
Originally Posted by T0pAz

And also how will I know if I have a router or not?
Normally, all internet connections are routed through switches & routers. So, if you are in a home/office network, there are high chances of you being connected to a network router.

In the command prompt, use 'ipconfig/all' to know about your LAN. Try connecting to your 'default gateway' through HTTP (80) & Telnet (23). When you are asked for the login details, the router/modem model name/version is generally seen on the login box/prompt (telnet).
