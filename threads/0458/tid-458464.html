Information:
Hey guys, so this is a filter-script I just made tonight, mainly my second..
Well let me tell you what this does:
If you're on the console, and there's a money, speed or weapon hacker, this will inform you on the console about the hack, with a bit of details about it(Not spamming).

I know this might not sound good for some, but can be really useful for many more people!

So this just sends messages to the console in case there is someone hacking on your server, with money, speed, or weapon hacks.

I will be adding more on request of course.

Download Link:

http://www.mediafire.com/?uimzia9tha5hyd1

Credits:

-Me.
-Bravo6 (( GhosT )) For Beta-Testing with me

Installation:

Simply compile the .pwn file you downloaded, add the .amx file to your filterscripts folder.
Then, add JCHW in your server.cfg near filterscripts.
Done!

Notes:

-Again, not sure if you'll find this interesting and useful or not, anyway here it is!
-The system for weapons hack checks for miniguns, edit it for more weapons.
-Oh, don't forget! You can change the MAX_MONEY and MAX_SPEED just on the defines, to your needs!
