Installation:

* Download the file!
* Unzip the Files! (Recommended use Winrar)
* Copy all files 'filterscripts'
* Paste in the filterscripts folder.
* Then!
* Open 'Server.cfg'
* Go to in line 'filterscripts'
* Add 'H_Speedo' (filterscripts H_Speedo)
