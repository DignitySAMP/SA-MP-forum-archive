Yes, it's a game. Wanted to share here and maybe compare scores

Game: http://gabrielecirulli.github.io/2048/
Info: Use your arrow keys to move the tiles. When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into one!
Objective: Have 1 tile with value 2048
Caution: It's addicting !

Best score: 6584 => 7260
