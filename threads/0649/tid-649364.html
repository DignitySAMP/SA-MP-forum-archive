I started alone. Mainly because at the time our previous community had just died off and me plus about 15 to 20 others needed a place to play.

I did it alone because of a previous experience in scripting with a partner. People love to release you're stuff or try to sell it when they get mad at you. It was just better to do it myself and ensure the community property stayed the community property.

In the beginning we started off with about 20 people. In the 'good times' we rolled around with about 45 to 50 on consistently and at all times which made doing server updates and maintenance a pain in the rear lol.
