Realistic Office [Interior]
What is it:
  • It's an interior you can use for any job. An office with many desks, computers, a bar and a place to change clothes
  • I made it in 1 hour, it has extacly 56 objects

Description
  • It's an interior with 5 big rooms
  • 1. Room 1 is a room where you can store clothes. There are also 6 suits, 6 jeans and a sweeping broom
  • 2. Room 2 has 4 office tables, 4 seats, 2 couches and 2 closets
  • 3. Room 3 has 6 tables with 6 computers. 3 are turned into one direction, 3 into another. There are also two book closets
  • 4. Room 4 is a room for the boss or who ever runs it. It has 2 shelves, one TV, a table with a computer and map closets
  • 5. Room 5 is a Bar. It has a small bar with few bottles. There are also 2 blue tables and 1 red table. All have seats
How to put this map into your server:
  • Copy all of the 'CreateObject(id,x,y,z);'
  • You might need to convert this so it fits your streamer. Use this website: http://maps.serverhex.com/
  • Open your GameMode/Filterscript and find 'OnGameModeInit' or 'OnFiltersriptInit' if you're putting in a FS
  • Simply paste it under and compile. There should be no errors, since it's nothing but objects

- If you want to see how this looks in game, go to 90.157.146.25:7777 and type /office
- This is an example of the command to go in the office - Put it under 'OnPlayerCommandText'

Code:
	if(strcmp(cmdtext, "/office",true) == 0)
	{   
        SetPlayerPos(playerid, 2826.0137,-1679.5593,664.4708);
        SendClientMessage(playerid,COLOR_YELLOW,"Welcome to the office. Don't be too loud!");
        return 1;
	}
