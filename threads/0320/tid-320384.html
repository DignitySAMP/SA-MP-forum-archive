hey everyone,

this evening i(and i had some help with a small issue from toreno and some others THNX!) have made a small simple car wash filterscript in LS

Features:
when you are at the entrance of the you can see a 3d textdraw which says: type /washcar to wash your car
and then the entrance is opening and you have to drive into a red marker.
when you are in the marker you get freezed and the carwash is washing your car(you see REAL WATER!!)
then after about 10 sec the carwash is clear and the water stops, the marker dissapears and the exit is opening
