This even occurs due to unstable internet. Check your PC and VPS internet connection speeds separately via speedtest.net and post them here. Run the test at least 5 times in each machine and post your end results. Meanwhile during each speed test, check how the pings, download and upload numbers change. If the ping, download and upload numbers have huge differences than the last result, then there is something wrong with the internet connection of your PC or the remote host.
