- Private Facility -
This is an island. Its basically a secure facility. You cannot enter it on foot, unless you know the gate commands. This is my first released script, so i would appreciate positive feedback on this.

Contains:
  • A large area, plenty open space
  • Hangar, with 2 shamal planes ready for takeoff!
  • Space for you to add your own things. Edit what you want!
  • A large satellite dish, and a rather nice fountain
  • Small military section, with a tank and a hydra on a helipad.
  • Vehicles ( Hydra, Rhino, Shamal, Hotring racer,Quad bikes, Caddy and a couple NRG bikes. )
  • A long bridge, with props such as a minigun (object, not the weapon)
  • Security cage. Stops unwanted people getting onto the bridge.
  • A rather high wall that reaches around the whole facility
  • Other usefull objects such as a tower, and some huts.

Commands:
  • /facilityhelp
  • /gate1o
  • /gate1c
  • /gate2o
  • /gate2c
  • /maingateo
  • /maingatec
  • /gotofacility
