*Island Hotel!*
This is an island, made a couple of days ago. It has a hotel with 10 empty rooms in it, 8 regular rooms and 2 big rooms. The idea with this is to let players make their own rooms. You can either convert it to an MTA map code, or make some sort of script where you buy furniture and rooms.

*Notes*
A streamer is recommended, i use Incognito's streamer.

*Video*
[ame]http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gaJQUm96ZxQ&hd=1[/ame]

Feedback & Rep please.
Enjoy.
