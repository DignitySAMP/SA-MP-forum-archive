Hello, this is my next map that I have made and made public. This is a Country Airport and is located on a farm nearby San Fierro Airport. The airport is a small one, has a large terminal made out of two barns, on the top floor we have toilets and check-in desks, on the level below there is Departures and Arrivals hall, each having two gates. In the Departures hall, there are vending machines, a duty-free shop with a Burger Shot restaurant, benches and a phone booth. The runway is lit up for flights at night and has a windsock at the side of it, and also to make it look realistic, there is a Tower, Radar, Refuel point and a Static Shamal from VC on the planestand. I hope you will enjoy this, I have added some screenshots
