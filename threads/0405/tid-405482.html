Ok, this is a car system made by me, I think that you can enjoy from this system, its very easy to use, very usefull..

Commands ():


Players:
/ Car - vehicle information
/ Cars - server vehicle information
/ Buycar - buy a car
/ Sellcar - Sell Car
/ Callcar - send the vehicle to you
/ Outside - take your car people
/ Outsideall - take all the people in your vehicle
/ Watchcar - watch your car
/ Watchoff - stop watching your car
/ Lockcar - lock your vehicle
/ Unlockcar - open your vehicle
/ Resetcar - respawn your car
Special Car:
Note: you can see the neon only at night or when the weather 2 (rcon weather 2 /)
/ Buynitro - buy Nitro
/ Sellnitro - Sell Nitro
/ Buyneon - Buy Neon
/ Sellneon - Sell Neon
/ Gotocar - telleport to your car
buyhyd) / buyhydraulics /) - buy car hydraulics
/ Carplate - change license plate
/ Delplate - Lmhhok license plate
/ Fix - vehicle repair
/ Flip - flip vehicle
/ Carcolor - Buying a car color
/ Carneon - turn off and turn on the neon
/ CarWheels - Buying Car Wheels constant
/ SellWheels - constant wheels sale

Admins:
Note: To use the commands as an admin Login Rcon
/ Canbuy - making the car for sale
/ Cantbuy - making public vehicles
/ Canbuyall - Making All vehicles for sale
/ Cantbuyall - turning all server vehicle to public
/ Scar - Making special vehicles
/ Admincar - making Administrators car rental
/ Respawncar - return all vehicles to their starting point
/ Lockallcars - lock all vehicles on the server (open only Admin locked)
/ Unlockallcars - unlock all vehicles on the server
/ Carplate - change license plate of a car
/ Delplate - delete the composition of the license plate, put the plate of the car ID
/ Carcolor - Change the color of a particular vehicle
/ Carneon - turn off and turn on the neon per car
/ Giveneon - Neon Not always gives the player
/ Cartext - Edit the text of a public service vehicle
/ Carmanager - an easy way to control the traffic on the server
/ Resetcarcolor or / delcarcolor or / deletecarcolor - returns to its initial color composition
/ Smcm - show the types of vehicles on the map by name you enrolled
/ CarWheels - Wheel permanent installation free car

Prices:

PHP Code:
#define rCar 200000  //Regular car price
#define sCar 500000 //Special car price
#define NeonPrice 8000000 //Neon price
#define NitroPrice 5000000 //Nitro price
#define HydPrice 2500000 //Hydraulics price
#define ColorPrice 500000 //Color Price
//Wheels Price (down.....) 
Install:

-Pastebin: Link
^ Put this on "FilterScript"
-On "ScriptFile" Open "Users", and in "Users" Open "Cars"

Enjoy !!
