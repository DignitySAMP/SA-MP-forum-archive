More information about the server (new):

All of the state's economy rests on three "elephants":


* Metal ore mines.
* Refineries for oil (fuel).
* Plant products.


1) Mine - specializes in the extraction of iron ore from which then, in the forge, get the metal. It is widely used by other companies, so a regular ore mining is very important to the state's economy. Here players can work as a miner.
Received at the mine metal is widely used by different organizations, but the main consumer - is plant food. For more details on the appointment of the Company described below.
For normal operation of the plant are constantly required to metal, which is engaged in transportation of its delivery service (on this work explain below).
In stock Mines truck buying metal for the company. Also, buy the metal Police and players working gunsmith, for weapons and ammunition. Buy metal for weapons can be right in the mine and metal for plant products - in the warehouse, which is located next door to mine.

Usefulness: If there is no work at the mine, the entire state will be without a weapon, as well as, "stall" plant products (its usefulness explain below).

2) Refinery - The Refinery can buy fuel for the plant for the production of products or for distribution to its filling stations. There are 2 lines, respectively, for the plant and for gas stations, will help you find the right pointers.
Warehouse stocks replenished as refinery production and oil refining.
The work truck fuel explain below.

Usefulness: If there is no fuel truck, the residents of the state will be walking, because gasoline at the gas station will not be, as well as "stall" plant products.


3) Plant products - a company which makes products for businesses of the State. Factory workers, using metal and fuel products obtained. Of the performance of this enterprise depends, in principle, the entire U.S. economy. Since the products are delivered by private businesses that make up, in turn, the economic system of the State.
At this point, you can apply for one of two majors - the worker manufacturing plant or delivery service
So, tell you more about the service delivery (work truck).
This work is available only to those who have a driver's license. You can get it there, on the plant. Metal can deliver (from the mine), fuel (oil refinery) and, accordingly, the products (factory products). Get a job, you have to choose what you want to deliver. When you decide to sit down in specially designed for this type of cargo car. The parking lot is 3 types of cars.

To make it easier to understand and comprehend what you what, I write:

* The Mines are taking metal on plant products
* The refineries are carrying fuel or gas stations, or on plant products
* The plant for the production of products we carry products to businesses

Purchase prices are different everywhere, and the selling price of raw materials (oil, metals, food) sets the owner of the business / enterprise.

Usefulness: If there are no products, "tormaznetsya" the whole business system, and eventually the entire U.S. economy!

Therefore, the state's economy is completely dependent on the players. Think, decide for themselves whom to work with! But if you get up one company, all the rest also stop! I hope for your seriousness and responsibility.

2) Station State:

Now, each dressing - this is business. Every player has a certain amount of money can buy a gas station and set their own price for the fuel, and the price that will pay for the import of fuel for its gas stations. Important! Every owner of a gas station should be interested in determining the price of petrol and the establishment of the purchase price of fuel. If the fuel is going to cost too much - just residents of the state will not be refueled at this gas station. If the fuel is too cheap, then the business owner will not be profitable. Similarly, with purchase prices: if too high a price - it is not the most beneficial owner. If the price is too low - fuel truck will pass by a gas station, and most owner will carry gasoline, which again is not beneficial (Purchase fuel refineries - free). Number of fuel at the gas station is shown as a 3D text around dispenser.
In order to fill their vehicles, use / fill at the gas station. Transportation will be filled up to a full tank. The price will depend on the set price for a liter of petrol stations and on this count tucked liters of gasoline.
