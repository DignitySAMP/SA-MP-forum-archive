All you need to run a rather 'successful' server shall include the following;

- You should plan the whole gamemode (main idea, features, GUI blabla) before starting it. Make a really large note on what you're scripting, or else the end wouldn't be so good.

- Get support. I assure 99.9999% that you will leave your script halfway there or even release it if you are not getting any support from the back from players.

- Be organized: Be organized with your scripting. You should have the basic structure of your script, as well as what you are/are not going to add. Believe me, it helps.

- Have faith, patience, and a good knowledge of scripting: Scripting a server isn't a one-day job, if you are just thinking of copy/pasting otherwise. You should have patience, as well as the knowledge of what you are scripting.

That's all the main points I do before scripting something. So yea', here ya' go!

You need to have knowledge in what you are doing.

You do not need a custom made script, there are scripts there for a reason, however, you should credit the author.

You will need people. At first, your server will just be made up of who you are working with, they will invite people, who will invite people etc.

The first thing that I do, is take my gamemode, which is scratch, and upload it to a vps which I rent, right off the bat. It makes testing a lot easier. Then, I get a website to keep everything organized, and a skype chat for people involved. Then, without hiring any staff, people are willing to help. After it takes off, you need to assign staff. I wait because I like to know how people are willing to contribute to this particular server, and their maturity etc.
