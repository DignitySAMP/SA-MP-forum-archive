» Description

I have mapped three different city hall interiors, intended to be in Angel Pine, Fort Carson and Palomino Creek, you can of course use them where ever you would like to.

They would probably be suitable for libraries, offices, DMV's, or anything like that. Watch the video below to see what they look like.

Note that we're talking about 3 independent maps, so you'll have to convert/add them individually.
