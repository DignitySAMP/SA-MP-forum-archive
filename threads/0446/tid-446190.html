Hello.
This is one of some my old gamemode that I decided to release.
The gamemode was developed in July 2012 to become the server of training LSMD for an italian RP server. Unfortunately, the project was also abandoned because it could not guarantee the same reality of any roleplay server (as the wounded in this GM have been made with the object of a corpse, by choice of scripting and savings slot).
It 's very simple and not pretentious. You will need <sscanf2> plugin.



Features:

- Three selectable classes to spawn, password-protected (to prevent unwanted intrusions): THE MANAGER - MEDICAL - PARAMEDIC.
- 64 possible rescue scenarios, fully editable and updateable.
- Dragging wounded quite rudimentary (very buggy) for travel by ambulance or helicopter rescue.
- Commands for the Roleplay.


Other commands:

- /randomhelp (simulates a call to the switchboard).
- /service [fake id of the patient] (to go to the rescue and get the checkpoint).
- /r.
- /d (chat departmental to roleplay conversations with the PD).
- /LSPD (fictitious chat to roleplay an agent of the LSPD).
- /Lspd2 (ictitious chat to roleplay an agent of the LSPD, another officer's name ).
- /Sadoc (ictitious chat to roleplay an agent of San Andreas Department of Correction (prison)).


Known bugs:

- None.


Credits:

The credits goes to Ruben-for creating this simple GM. You can keep them or remove them at your leisure.
A special thanks goes to Stanford for editing the GM, making /drag work properly.
