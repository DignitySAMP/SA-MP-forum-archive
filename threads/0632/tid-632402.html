Did you ever get tested for color blindness? Serious question. That shade of yellowish-green is so ugly. I can recommend color.adobe.com to get a nice color set. #FF0000 Red 
#00FF00 Green
#0000FF Blue 
#FFFF00 Yellow 
#FF00FF Magenta 
#00FFFF Cyan
