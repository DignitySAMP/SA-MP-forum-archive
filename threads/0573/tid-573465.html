Hi,

I own a dedicated server from soyoustart.com (a OVH Reseller), they do have a good migration system its not perfect but if you know what you are doing with firewalls and extras security you will have no problems.

I have tried the gaming ones, But because I run multiple servers (sa-mp, minecraft), I decided to buy a dedicated one for about Ј40.80 a month (I also have 24 IP Address).

If for some chance you are under attack I would advise talking to the support team or if you have the money buy a one off price firewall. I believe the name is ASA Firewall (Never used it myself).

OVH offer decent servers, its really what you use them for.

I have no issues with 0.3.7 at all, if you are being attacked there is a forum thread for a plugin:

https://forum.sa-mp.com/showthread.php?tid=551128 (Not sure if it works or does anything with 0.3.7)

that should help, if players are unable to join. I would suggest checking your firewall.
