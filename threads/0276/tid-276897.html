iVE: Vehicle Death Physics

*Just wanted to share something with you guys..
*The script is simple but it gives a nice effect as i see.

What it does?
When the vehicles die (it's health reaches to zero)
It will gain a random velocity, either fly in air, spin, or whatever..
Atleast it replaces the boring vehicle bounce-on-death..
