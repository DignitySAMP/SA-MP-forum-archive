Features
Security - The server has a encryption, so it doesnt show the password blah blah

Anticheat - A serverside weapon hack! Every weapon is stored in the player's file, and everytime a player's file doesnt match the weapon they get banned (( sorry, it's hard to explain ))

Saving - Weapon saving from all weapon slots are saved, So is money etc...

Housing - 350 houses spread across San Fierro. A dynamic system, you can move around houses also.

Businesses - 30 businesses around San Fierro, you can create more if needed!

Faction System - Hold up to 30 factions, this can be easily changed ingame

Public Places - You can make up to 30 public places. (( Example: Police, Fire ))

Tazer - The tazer shot is dynamic, you can taze a player with a SD Pistol

Sniper Shot - Good for mafiaa, You can snipe people, one shot kill.

Bombs - Yes there is a bomb system, You can plant bombs, make bombs, and give bombs.

Admin System - About 60 Admin commands, There are two groups, Moderator / Admin.

Drug System - You can buy drugs at a place in San Fierro, you have to plant them and watch the plant grow (( Made with objects )). The drugs step is like this:
  • Buy drug seeds
  • Plant drug seeds
  • Grow them
  • Use them
Police / FBI - Plenty of FBI commands and police commands, commands listed below:

MOTD - Have a MOTD system that can be set by admins and moderators

EMT - EMTS can heal people who are dead. Players who die, reset in there position in a animation.
They can accept death, or just wait for a EMT. Accepting death makes a player in heaven for 2 minutes.

Taxi - Taxi faction is also there. You can set your fare and then people can call you to get picked up

GPS System - You will know your location via GPS, a message above your health bar will tell you your location

Jobs - Most of the jobs are faction jobs. (( Police, FBI, MEDIC, Taxi, News Reporter )). Other jobs are: Trucker, Mechanic, Product Deliver, Hotdog Dealer.

Sidejobs - Sidejobs include: Sweeper, Paperboy, Bus Driver.

License System - You can have a drivers license, fishing license, and a weapon license. They can all be bought except for the drivers. The drivers license is gotten by a driving test.

Animations - Animations are provided. About 30 of them.

Dynamic Registration System - Register your age, your religon

Dynamic Customization Character System - When registering you can customize your characters: walk, clothes, sex and fighting style.

Stores - Up to 12 types of stores you can make.

Dynamic Infobox system - This is a function you can use to show a small black box with text in it.

Faction Leadership - Faction leaders can invite, kickout, and set ranks for there faction.

Bugs
I will guarantee there will be bugs found. But not more than 2. The vehicle system is really buggy. So expect to see bugs from there.

Bads
