I've never used Bandicam. I can't answer that. I just know for sure that Dxtory has fantastic quality. & indeed you will need a faster read/write speed, but that shouldn't be a problem if you're serious on creating videos. I would be sure to get quality hardware in order to do so. It also doesn't require a very fast HDD either, nor do you need an SSD. I'm using a Seagate Barracuda 2TB @ 7200RPM. It reads/writes at ~92MB/s. Of course it's not enough for 1080p quality but with the right codec, it can.

What people don't realize is that you don't have to use the default Dxtory codec. I'm using one called Lagarith Loseless Codec, and it records 1080p at stunning quality and no choppy recordings at all. Usually you would need a HDD that can read/write at ~145Mb/s+ in order to get pure 1080p quality without choppy footage.

Loving the codec, the software, and the recordings!
