[FS] Weapon Menu
I initially made this as part of a DM game mode but since I don’t use it anymore, I thought of releasing it. It is a simple menu that is shown when you spawn. It gives you the ability to select a new set of weapons, your previous weapons, or random weapons. When you select new weapons you choose a weapon for each of your three slots. If you want to select your weapons again after you have chosen ‘Same Weapons Every Time’ simply type /weapons. Also if you exit the menu without completely selecting all 3 of your weapons you will be given a set of random weapons. Feel free to post comments, edit the code, and use it to have fun!
