Akira's Tire and Siren Filterscript


The main thing about this script, is it's simple,

The commands are,

Code:
/goldrims ( add'd Gold Rims to your vehicle )
/cutterrims ( Add's Cutter Rims to your vehicle. )
/siren1 ( Add'd siren from 0.3d into the filterscript )
/siren2 ( Add'd siren from 0.3e into the filterscript. )
Nothing much just something to play around with.

I Hope you enjoy, any suggestions for updates or upgrades?
