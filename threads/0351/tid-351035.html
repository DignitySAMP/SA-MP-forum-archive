Okay, that may have been slightly fabricated but anyway.. I haven't made anything in Photoshop in a while and I'm running out of ideas so I guess I'm getting a bit rusty. So yeah, all jokes aside.


Not doing anymore requests

Rules
  • No server logos, banners. No company or advertisement stuff. Only signatures that may or may not be somewhat related to you etc.
  • One request per user.
  • YOU MUST HAVE 100+ posts. Why? Because I don't want some newbie just join today and walk out with a free signature.
  • Reputation isn't a big deal, but I may deny some requests for people with 0 reputation and join date that is this month/week etc.
  • No banners, wallpapers, large art.
  • You must follow the request format.
  • Last but not least, show some respect and credits would be very much appreciated.
Request Format
PHP Code:
[b]Text:[/b]
[b]Size(pixels):[/b]
[b]Colors:[/b]
[b]Render/Stock:[/b]
[b]Additional Info[/b] 
Example
Text: Jansish
Size(pixels): 400x150
Colors: Black and blue
Render/Stock: Render
Additional Info Make it glossy
