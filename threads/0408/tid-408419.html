Kind day!

Probably every looked a cartoon 'SpongeBob SquarePants'? And probably everyone remembers the place where worked himself Bob?
This script adds to your server this restaurant 'Krusty Krab'.

Description:

Go to a restaurant to boat, where usually sits Squidward, and press key 'Alt'.
You will see the first menu - krabsburger's.
Once selected, opens menu with French fries (which, as the following menu, you can skip).
The third menu - drinks.
After make the order, open the information window.
Then you will get a tray of food.
Order food to eat go to the table and write the command /eat (you can change the team in #define cmd).
That's all.

Sorry for poor knowledge of English (:
