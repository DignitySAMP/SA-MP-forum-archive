[Palomino Creek's Mansion]
By Erwin.

Hello, today i'm glad to announce, that my map is finally done. Well it took only 1 week, for me to create it. The new map is a big two-storey mansion. On the 1st floor there is 2 rooms, GYM,Office, some corridors and the toilet, on the 2nd floor is kitchen and bar/club. I don't know what inspired me to map something like this, well i have one map something similar to this tho. But that one wasn't really good, so i've made a 100% better custom interior and exterior house,even with 2 floors. It looks really awsome, you should check it out, by seeing in screens and download the map itself. Download link is down below. The map has over 1000+ objects at one place.
