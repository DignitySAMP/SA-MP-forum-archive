Hi guys, as title says tell us your story.

Did you start programming/scripting/etc because of SA-MP? Or did you apply/test your knowledge from school?

Were you already a professional when you came here? Or did you take on a job afterwards?

I'm curious to find out if SA-MP motivated you!



My story:

I started playing SA-MP on 2007 (In and Out). I was in school (before high school) and together with few we were friends enjoying public servers and hosting some scripts for fun.

Me and another one had a project about recycling and we edited a script where everybody would join, read few things and do some missions about it.

Later on we joined a popular community and made our way to the server administration in about a year or so. We were there for three years until it closed down and we moved on.

I never had classes about programming and I never joined any courses about it. (I do not have much knowledge...)

Still SA-MP gives me this "rush/feeling" to script/edit something and feel acomplished at the end. It's like a hobby of mine.

At the momment I finished High school and took some time off before joining university to work.
