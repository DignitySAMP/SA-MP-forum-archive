Some of you might be familiar with Pokйmon, some won't. However, it's been a huge part of my childhood - we had a lot of fun with the series, the cards, the gameboy.

But, Pokйmon isn't just all about stuffed animals whom fight each other for fun. It has been, since its release, been a thread of haunted games, creepy stories and whatsoever. All of them are fake, but pretty enjoyable to read. I will tell it as if it is actually true - to increase reading pleasure, of course

I will explain you them in a serie of tales, consisting of 4 parts.

Part I: Unit 731

Unit 731 is the sickest thing you'll ever hear in your entire life. Unit 731 is a war unit of the Japanese army during World War II. Its purpose was to test out new kinds of warfare: chemical and biological. Of course, they need living creatures to test their stuff on. You know what they used? Real, living people. Not only men, but also women and children.

The Japanese army performed operations, slaughterings and medical experiments on living people like you and me. Without any form of painkilling. They did weapon tests on them: what will happen if I throw a grenade 5m away of that guy? They performed vivisections, where they cut humans open to see what's inside. Alive. They cut out their limbs to see how their blood system works. They froze people's limbs to see how gangrene works. They cut out stomachs and other organs to see what the effects are on the body. They injected people's blood with other animal's blood, with air (lethal) and even sea water. They buried people alive. Or burnt them alive.

All the above is, sadly, true. Source: http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unit_731

Now, you might ask yourself, how does this have anything to do with Pokйmon?

Well, as you might know, Japan had a separate Green version (EU and USA had Red and Blue, they had Green and Red). If you went into a certain bush in one of the areas, you could run into a "glitch" Pokйmon. Name? "731". What happened? Watch the following video:
