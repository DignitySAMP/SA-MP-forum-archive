Hello lads, I do have questions to some of you, well to people that like roleplay, I am planning to do some new type of roleplay... Historic roleplay, placed in September 1939, world war 2, The year when Nazi Germany invaded Poland, and some of you will think "Ohh Poland they lost badly" Well of course we did (I am Polish) however we fought to a last man, like Battle of Wizna, we had 720 soldiers lost 40 and Germany had 44,000 and lost 400 or so, (check wiki if you dont believe me) However back to the topic.

Do you think it would be interesting to roleplay in that type? IT would not be DM, Germany city and HQ would be San Fierro and Poland city and HQ would be Los Santos and of course lets not forget about the rural land, farms and so on. Now each faction would have a General (Non admins, we would choose the finest people for the role) the attack MUST be planned, Germany nor Poland cant go and attack each others places without Planning it and orders from High Command aka Admin approval, If High Admin approves it, The country gets permission and other one does not know about it until the system tells them their owned place, if one faction takes it over it is their which would be marked by "faction zones" on the map. When a player registers first he chooses which country he wants to serve, and here is the thing you do have to have a roleplay name, which you will need to first get before registering so if you want to fight for Germany get a german name, if for Poland get a polish name. When it is done he spawns in a barracks and there is where roleplay begins, High Command Officers give them what type of soldier he is (command) and what division he belongs (players can ask to be in one like: Air Force, Armour Division, Infantry) there as well will be ranks as you proceed in the battles.

EDIT: Forgotten to add, in here we change the history, in here Poland can win or lose, Germany can win or lose. As well in a spare time soldiers and officers can spend their time driving around, have parties and so on, everything that a soldier could do when he is not in a war, well everything that is proper RP in these times.

Do you think it would be interesting? Would you try that out?
(Modding will be hard, getting some skins for roleplay.)
