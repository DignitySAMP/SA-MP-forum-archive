Introduction

That Gamemode is Simple but good enough to be used , i've made a Dialog Lobby Cause i don't had time to make a new one with textdraws like i do in some of my multi gamemodes (not released) , btw i hope you will like it .
Features:

-Two Modes: DM & Stunt
-Registration/Login System [Same Used in my Other GMs]
-Two Custom Maps for Every Mode
-Admin System with 4 ranks and Some Cmds.
-Global Cmds: /info /afk /report /admins /givemoney /savestats /rules /kill /credits /changemode
-DM Cmds: /shop /dmobjective
-Stunt Cmds: /nos /stobjective /anims

VIP Features:
-VIP Turismo Unlocked in Stunt
-Double Ammo on DM
-Less Damage Taken From Other Players
-Extra +30 Damage in Every Weapon
-Items From /shop got unlimited Ammo only for VIPs
-/nos is Unlimited for VIPs

Admin Cmds & Ranks:

PHP Code:
Moderator: /clearchat(/cc) /kick /warn /slap /spec(off)
Admin: /aduty /explode /getinfo /ban /get /goto /acar /giveallmoney /giveallscore
Manager: /resetaccount /setscore /setvip /ann /ann2 /music /givegun /setlevel /banip /unbanip /apanel
Owner: /cuff /uncuff /sethealth /luckyass /setkills /setdeaths 
Help Command (About DM)

PHP Code:
-You Must Kill To Get Score And Money
-You Can Buy Guns With Your Money Using /shop
-To Change Your Skin u Must Pay For VIP , then You'll be able to use /vipskin
-You Can Get VIP Features By Donating on Our Website 
Help Command (About Stunts)

PHP Code:
-You Must Do Stunts And Drifts and Sometimes Kill People with Your Vehicle to Get Score
-You Can Select Five Vehicles And a VIP Vehicle in The Car Class Selection During Spawn Time
-To Unlock the VIP Turismo You Must Pay for the VIP Package that can be Purchased in Our Website
-You Can Add Nitro in Your Vehicles Using /nos
