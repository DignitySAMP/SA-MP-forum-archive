Perfect for role-play server !
What's Vuki_TrashCar?
It's a filterscript that make a trashed car where a car explode. People have to trail this trashed car with the DFT-30.
Usefull for activity in role play

Commands
  • /enlevertc : (only admin) delete a trashcar in front of you.
  • /alltc: (only admin) Delete all trashcar created.
  • /maketc: (only admin) Make a trashcar in your place (that slap you to)
  • /remorquertc: Trail the trashcar near you if you are in a DFT-30
  • /broyertc: For the version 1.2, the trashcar will go in a mill that turn the wrecked car into scrap.
Bug
