Verona Beach Stuntpark
Hey, I made this map not a long time ago. I hope you like it!

About the map

It's a nice stuntpark with a lot of stunt places and nature objects which makes the map pretty. Around 25 ramps, 125 objects and it doesn't take too much place. There is a place where helicopters can land too... Plus, I added a wall, now you can't dive and get under the map! This is my ~40th map (much of them no one knows and they aren't used anywhere!), so now it doesn't take much time for me. It looks much awesome when you play it, not looking on screenshots. I needed only 1-2 hours to make it... This looks really nice at night with lighting lamps and other things! I think it's not needed to tell where is this map located!
