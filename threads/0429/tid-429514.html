Hello.

Many Of You Didnt Have A Host, And They Want To Play With Friends

There Is 1 solution (Hamachi Server)

First You Must Download Hamachi from Here :

Choose "Unmanaged"

Click Here

When Download Complete Install Hamachi And Create A NetWork :

Click Network



Choose Create a new network

Create Your NetWork And Click "Create"



Give The NetWork (Name & Pass) To Your Friend, And Give Your Friend (Your Hamachi IP)

Open Samp-server.exe And Enjoy !
