You must download the BETA 0.3 client to play on 0.3 servers, you need the 0.2X client to play on 0.2X servers. 0.3 is only compatible for 0.3 servers, 0.2 is only compatible for 0.2 servers.

0.3 BETA isn't fully released and has a tiny limit of maximum players (16), wait until 0.3 is officially out then you'll want to upgrade your server.
