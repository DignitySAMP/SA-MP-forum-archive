My Map: Volcano Island


About it:

I used to play on this pilot server, so i set a mpping limit to my self. I thought of making a map with an island and airport. It was a bit in a mess so they didnt accept it. But since my skills in mapping grew i edited it and made it better and bigger

~*~FEATURES~*~
* Mini Airport For it
* No roads
* Beaches
* Volcano with smoke
* Fire Station
* 2 Mansions
* About 6-10 Other houses. ( I made a miscount so i said 6-10)
* 2 Parking lots
* Gas Station
* Heli Pad
* 239 Objects
* 0 Vehicles
