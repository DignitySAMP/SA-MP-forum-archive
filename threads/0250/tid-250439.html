Boss Fights - Simple Teamdeathmatch Script
(Including Register System, and a lot of Admin commands)
Version 1.0.0



* What is the GameMode about?

There are 5 Teams:
  • Boss of the Red Team
    Just the Boss, he must try to survive.
  • Protectors & Fighters of the Red Team
    There are protectors and fighters. The protectors are supposed to stay with the Boss and help him - the fighters must try to kill the boss of the other team.
  • Boss of the Blue Team
    (same as above)
  • Protectors & Fighters of the Blue Team
    (same as above)
  • Civilians
    Civilians are neutral and can support each sides.

Most of the GameMode was described now already. But just again a short tutorial about the gamemode:
There are 2 Bosses, one for each team. Those bosses must try to survive. If a boss dies, the team who killed him scores. You can get - score and + score. That means: If your score is 3, you win, but if your score is -3 for example you lose. The server restarts after one team won / lost.

* Why do I release this?

Well I have to admit, the GameMode may be a lot but perfect. It has bugs, but I think the commands or the register system may help some people.

* Features
  • Full Register System
  • Admin Commands and Admin System
  • Simple Teamdeathmatch Script
  • And more
