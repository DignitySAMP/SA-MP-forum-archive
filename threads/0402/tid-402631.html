(*Xcoper Roleplay's / Beta v1.0*)
________________________Roleplay's Beta v1.0__________________________________




INTRO:

Xcoper Roleplay's is set in 2008 at the small town Los Santos.
Los Santos had many residents and as it became more available more dangerous stuff happened. As the Los Santos Police took in his SWAT force and the military to catch all the criminals, some stores in Los Santos became more well kown and richer.
So these stores build two new cities called San Fierro and Las Venturas. Those famous stores moved to San Fierro and Las Venturas to become even more famous. One of them bought up a casino and became even richer.
People can now even afford to buy cars.
New Jobs are coming to the city
And this is where it begins ............. Thank you for you reading the intro. This was the Xcoper Roleplay Beta v1.0 intro.


Commands:
Code:
Normal player commands:
/open [to open a door]
/close [to close a door]
/Break door ~Need to be high thief level~

/Lock /unlock [Lock or unlock a door [Only if you are owner or have keys]
/Give keys ~Both to houses and cars~

/Eat [If you have food]
/Buy ~You need to be in red points~
/Give food ~Give food to player~

/Light on ~Turn on the light on the car~
/Light off ~Turn off the light on the car~

/refuel ~Refuel your car at gas station~
                  ~More commands coming soon to normal players~

Police commands:
Number 2 [Pull over]
/Nails [Set out Nails on the road]
/Undo nail [Delete the nail on road]
/Road block [Set out road blocks]

/Handcuff or Number 2 [If they are orange]
/Handcuff
/Jail or number 2 [If he is orange or red]
/Unjail ~Need to be in redpoint and pay to take him out~
/Ticket or number 2 [If he is yellow] 

/speed meter ~Take up a speed meter~
/set sp ~setting out a speed meter on side of the road / need access from admin to use this command~

/broach ~If a player is orange or red. So can police use /broach to broken up the door~ ONLY FOR POLICE)
