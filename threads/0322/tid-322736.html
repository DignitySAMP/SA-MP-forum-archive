Introduction

I am now gonna release this gamemode so called ~Los Santos War*Chaos~

Features

A scoreboard and a timer which automatically restarts the server after one of the teams has reached 50 points.
A few admin commands to change the scoreboard
A few cool player commands
A "car" system. Every team has a black car with a marker. The other team can steal it.
Gang Chat

Objective
It got like mission thingy
One team Has to plant Bomb /bomb
other team got to defend their base from being pooped by ze C4 bomb

-

Car mission
One team steals the car
other team has to defend the opposite team from stealing the car and taking it to their base

Simple Explanation:-
Plant a Bomb
BOOM
Make your team reach 50 score first

/help /rules /commands /bomb /kill /info
