As one of the servers admins I feel I need to apologize to those that enjoyed the server. Quote:

Well the decision has been made. CnB has been closed down for now. This includes the server and this website.

As much as we would like to continue to operate, we are out of development resources and the current set-up of the community, what happens on a day-to-day basis in-game and on this website, has become unacceptable and not worth managing.

In other words, nobody is interested enough to keep it going. A massive, humongous thanks to John for his insane dedication and the amount of time he put into management. But, it's time for the one-man show to end.

Hello. I'm Tomo, one of the admins on CnB. We tried, over the summer, to get things into a working state, but we were less than successful. With people getting unbanned only to get banned again minutes later, we figured it would be best until we got some more free time that it wasn't worth keeping the server open. We've done similar things in the past but never quite to this extreme.

We've been working on this since late 2007, and until a couple of months ago had only a single code developer. I love working on the server (I wouldn't be lying if I said I once spent about two hours attempting to escape a single map just to prove a point), but free time over the last few months hasn't been a luxury for any of us. I'm in a bit more stable position at the moment, and am hoping to get back to work soon. I'm not promising anything, but we'll do our best.
