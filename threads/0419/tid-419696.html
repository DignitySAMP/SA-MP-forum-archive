So, I posted on the scripting help thread requesting someone to help me make an SQLite house system, and I was contacted by FusionzzRoleplay, who said he'd be willing to do it for cash. I agreed and he began "making the codes" and after a few days, sent me a picture of what the house system looks like:

Quote:
Originally Posted by FusionzzRoleplay
Going good so far, shouldn't take to long I'll pm you when I got it working.
Quote:
Originally Posted by FusionzzRoleplay
Here it is was pretty easy to do to be honest, tell me if you like it if so how much are you willing to pay?
/imageshack/img268/8053/samp003up.png
I of course asked him if he would let me come in-game and test it, but he denied and said that he didn't have a server even though he posted an advertisement nearly a week ago here: https://forum.sa-mp.com/showthread.php?tid=419198

After this, I noticed that the SA-MP version in the screen shot was 0.2x and I asked him if it was up to date with the SA-MP version and he replied, "It worked for me". Then, in further investigation, I searched up the image link on the SA-MP forums and noticed that a thread in the archives from 09' about a house system using that image shack link: (Top of the thread) http://forum.sa-mp.com/archive/index.php/t-98767.html

Over skype he unadded me and replied saying, "Wow, wrong images copied wtf".
I made this thread to warn members of the SA-MP community of this person with his schemes and tricks and to always do research before agreeing to pay someone for scripting, mapping, etc.
