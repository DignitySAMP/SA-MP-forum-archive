We've had numerous problems with Volt, which results in us moving to another server. Since USRP is one of the popular servers on samp, we get attacked on a daily basis. If its not Data Center rangebans from one host, or just unreliable support, we have always gone back to Volt.

After renewing BOTH of our servers thru volt for 100+ days. The day after we move back to them, Volt is hit with a huge DDoS which in return null routes the ips. So pretty much we were told either to buy a dedicated, or they'd shut our server down.

Well there goes $100+ USD down the drain. I wont use Volt anymore. They don't offer DDoS protection thru their VPS's, only ip tables. We had a problem were our server was down for DAYS because of a file read error... we went thru a month + of our server crashing numerous times a day because of an FTP problem....


After the messages we recieved today concerning this DDoS issue, i highly doubt i will ever use Volt Host again, and i recommend you don't either.
