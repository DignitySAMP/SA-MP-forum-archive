What I notice is that on connect, they get disconnected right away with reason 2 (Kick/Ban). A thing to keep in mind is that if the script kicks the player for whatever reason, any message won't be sent unless it is delayed.

So delay any kick in case you get a reason of a kick and if nothing happens, perhaps posting OnPlayerConnect callback?
