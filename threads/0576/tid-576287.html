Quote:
Originally Posted by Luigi Tattaglia
This has got nothing to do with any mod because regardless of how far your draw is, the shot distance is always default. Leave that aside.

Players get sniper bugged because there is a hit compensation that often fails.

Sometimes you can't damage one player, but you can damage all the others; sometimes 50% of the playerbase can't damage you, but you can damage them, while the other 50% can damage you; sometimes you don't take damage by one person, but you take damage by another.


Its all very random and happens once a shot doesn't successfuly register on you while you've indeed hit the player. If the player has his nametag on, you can see him lose HP/armor and then the HP/armor reinstates itself back to what it was before you've hit. This is when the player gets sniper bugged. Players with higher packetloss/latency are more prone to getting this and so are players with higher FPS.
