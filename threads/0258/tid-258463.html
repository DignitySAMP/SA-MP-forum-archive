Originally Posted by Ironboy

Hello!
What is FileZilla? Can anyone explain me ? and how to use it?
Filezilla is a FTP (File Transfer Protocol) application, which allows you to connect to your server files from a distance.

You can use it to upload files to your server with ease.

EDIT:
To use it

Host: [your IP here]
Username: [your RDP username here, the name you use to login to your VPS- or dedicated-server]
Password: [The password your using to get on your VPS- or dedicated-server]
Port: stock it's put on port 55555

Then click the button quickconnect

Youll now see 2 browsers, the left one is the one of your current desktop.
The right one is the one from your server (VPS or dedicated server).

You can now easilly drag files from your desktop to your server, to transfer files from your PC to your Server.

I hope this helped you a little, if I had time I would make a manual with screens for ya =P
