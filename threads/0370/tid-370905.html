Current Setup:

Processor: AMD Phenom II X4 955 Black Edition (4x3.2GHZ)

Proposed Project:
Case: Enermax Fulmo (Full ATX + USB 3.0)

Processor: AMD FX-8150 Zambezi (8x3.6GHZ)
Motherboard: ASUS M5A99FX Pro R2.0
Video Card: EVGA NVidia GeForce GTX 680 FTW (4GB mem) with Stock Cooler
Memory: Corsair 16GB (4 GB x 4) DDR3-1866
Storage:
SSD: Patriot Pyro 120GB SATA III
(Going to use 1.5TB 7,500 RPM HDD for storage)

The proposed project will cost $1603.93 before taxes and shipping, which is a lot less than I paid for my current rig.

What do you guys think? Anything I should add or drop?
