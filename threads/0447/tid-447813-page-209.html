Mapping Team
Application Status: Open
We are looking for accomplished mappers to help us in our goal of bringing the streets of San Fierro into the 21st century.

We're looking to give San Fierro the facelift it deserves in order to provide a great environment for a wide variety of characters and roleplayers, including creating areas and neighbourhoods with their own unique style, repurposing existing buildings and empty spots to provide roleplay opportunities, and other bits of set dressing.

Our ideal candidate would ideally...
  • ...be an established or active member of the Ultimate Roleplay community, both in-game and on the forums.
  • ...be able to map realistically, often taking inspiration from real life subjects
  • ...work well in a team as well as alone (to project specifications)
  • ...be at least somewhat experienced with JernelJ's SA-MP Map Editor, as well as custom textures
Any questions or concerns can be directed to the Head of Mapping, Alto in our Discord.

Be sure to link any screenshots or threads of your latest mapping in your message as that will serve as the main assessment of your application.

---

Helper Team
Application Status: Open
Our staff team consists of versatile, talented and motivated individuals who put their skills to the community's best, helping the players and ensuring a good environment within the community. If you wish to be part of our ever-growing staff and help on the behind the scenes of Ultimate Roleplay, you will have to apply. To know more about the process, read below. Helpers are a vital part of the well-being of Ultimate Roleplay. It is their duty to help community member's with their queries and assure that they provide help in the various community elements.

Candidates to Ultimate Roleplay's staff team should meet these criteria:
  • Be an active member of the community;
  • Write and understand English to a high degree;
  • Be keen on helping the community grow;
  • Use Discord actively.
Playing a vital role in Ultimate Roleplay comes with several responsabilities:
  • Offer support to community members;
  • Assist in development (bug testing, bug reporting);
  • Forum moderation.
Any questions or concerns can be directed to the Head of Mapping, Carlos in our Discord.
