How To Use?

It's very simple and really awesome to use, It helps you to navigate to the pointed location by the commands, It set's an ICON on minimap as shown at the bottom pictures. When you enter's the setted/pointed checkpoint you will get a GameTextForPlayer as shown in photos. Its bug free and 99.9% Easy to use if you know the basics of PAWNO.
