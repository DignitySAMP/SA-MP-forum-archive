Project: FreeRoam v1.1

Features in this script:
- Anti-cheat
- Over 20 custom maps and teleports
- Admin system with more than 15 commands
- VIP system
- Golden Coins System
- And many more!

Required plugins
mTime
MySQL Plugin (Strickenkid)
sscanf
Streamer
Whirlpool

How do i become an administrator or VIP?
To become an administrator: login with RCON and then do (/setlevel <name/id/partofname> 5)
To become VIP: do (/setvip <name/id/partofname> <viplevel>)
