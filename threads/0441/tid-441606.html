Hello guys, this is my 1st FS and I hope you like it
This is a simple rob casino pickup filterscript.

If you press /robcasino you will be teleported to the casino, than go in and go on the yellow info icon and it gives you 20.000$(you can change the amount of money it gives you!) and it respawns after your next death!

Some screenshots:
http://imageshack.us/a/img607/6474/gallery4mu.jpg
http://imageshack.us/a/img818/1176/gallery3uf.jpg
http://imageshack.us/a/img401/647/gallery2d.jpg

If you want to change something and you don't know how go to: https://wiki.sa-mp.com/wiki/CreatePickup

HOW TO USE
1. Extract the .rar file
2. Put the .pwn and .amx file in the folder "filterscripts" of your server
3. Put "robcasino[FS]" in the filterscripts line in server.cfg
4. Enjoy your FS!

Links(old version!)
sendspace: http://www.sendspace.com/file/2psnoh
pastebin: http://pastebin.com/9QcpMgyu

Released New Version!
FEATURES:
*It takes longer to rob the casino!
*Your wanted level is increased when you rob the casino(+1)
*The pickup is pickable after your next death(when you die your wanted level will be set to 0!)
*You can cancel the robbery by pressing /cancelrob
