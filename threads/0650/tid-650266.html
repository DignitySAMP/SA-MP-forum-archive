Lets say we have an interior, ID 5 which we want to use 2 times, to make it usable 2 times and not have the problem u encountered, you set the world when they enter and check it when they exit,

so once a player enters the first interior we do
Code:
SetPlayerVirtualWorld(playerid, 10);//10 can be changed
and when he exits we do
Code:
if(GetPlayerVirtualWorld(playerid) ==10) //he's exiting the first interior 
//code here
Then at the second interior when he enters (same id #5) we do
Code:
SetPlayerVirtualWorld(playerid, 15); //different number
Then when he exits same check
Code:
if(GetPlayerVirtualWorld(playerid) == 15)//he's exiting the second interior
//code here
