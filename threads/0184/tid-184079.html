» MAP: Los Santos Tennis-Mansion Improvements
_______________________________________________


» Description

Lots of improvements to make the mansion in western Los Santos seem even more enjoyable.

I've added lots of trees and shrubs, some roadblocks for style, a couple of small garages, added tables and a staircase to the balcony, a helipad, some statues, benches, trashcans, etc.

Perfect for any Los Santos-script that needs a little extra beauty.

220 objects in total.
