Bank Account System.
This filterscript is sleek, professional and looks great, no bugs and uses three main features which are
streamer, y.ini, ZCMD. This script is great for roleplay servers for the obvious reasons, it's easy to use too.

There are ten Cash machines planted around Los Santos for you to withdraw money and it also saves your bank account money.

If you have any suggestions, new locations covered and more please comment and i'll get back to you.

To give yourself money just log into the rcon and type /givebankmoney and you'll be on your way!
