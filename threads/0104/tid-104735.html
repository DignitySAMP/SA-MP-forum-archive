[MAP] Car Crash on Main Street [SA-MP]
What is it?:
It's a Car Crash on Main Street, the road of Ammunation in Los Santos. It helps Roleplay servers with their roleplay and makes them more unique.

Who made it?:
Me, Rampage.

What can I do with it?:
You can use it on your server, as long as credits are given. Credits either must link to this page, or my user page on the sa-mp forum. I don't like credits being stolen.

How long did it take?:
