(Yes, I love Nintendo's games, Ok?)

Nintendo's new console, the Wii U, is on it's way to face the world. And even though people are afraid it's going to become a Wii-like situatin: with hardly no good 3rd paty games, I think it will change. Why?
Yes, I do think the Wii U will be weaker then the next PS and XBOX, but I doubt the difference will be as big as this generation. Also, according to some articles from long ago, the problem wasn't sheer power, but the hardware of the ol' Wii didn't support some features which made porting a pain in the *ss.

Why am I excited for the Wii U?

First of all, there's the controller.


Even though some may find it gimmicky, I find it a nice approach at something new. Downside: uptill now, the system is only know to be able to support up to 2 of these tablets, and 4 'other controllers'. (This includes Wii-motes, or their new pro controller)

I also like the idea of asymmetrical multiplayer.


Then, there's games. Wii's line-up was, apart from most 1st party games, a bit weak. But with the Wii U, quite afew 3rd parties (especially Ubisoft) are excited to work with the console.
And I must say, this is the first time that I've been excited for as much as 4(maybe more) games in the launch window.
