OVH seems to have some routing issues lately, but they work fine generally.

OVH gives you a VPS based on where you're from. If you live in Europe, you'll get one from their French data
center. If you live in North America, you'll get one in their Canadian data center. I'm not sure about other areas of the world, but you'll likely be put into the closest data center. At this time, OVH offers no way to pick the data center you're in.

I have one and they're fine for the price. The VPS I have has a 3.1 GHz CPU (AMD Operon). Their DDoS protection is fine, especially for the price.

However, the visualization platform OVH uses isn't ideal for running game servers. They use OpenVZ which uses shared resources among clients, which allows them to oversell (they probably do, but within reason), and it is a cause for random hiccups which can cause random noticeable lag in-game. Xen or KVM is more ideal for game server hosting.

I use NFO servers (nfoservers.com) for my game server. It uses Xen as the visualization platform, they give you dedicated hyper-threaded Intel Xeon CPU cores which makes for really great performance. They've been around for a decade, and really know what they're doing. They use Internap data centers at all of their locations which have great links, and offer decent DDoS protection. NFO has a lot of experience with game server hosting, so they have a lot of experience with mitigating the attacks that come with game servers. They automatically filter common/obvious attacks and send you emails with the details when they do filter attacks. They offer intuitive firewall control to block attacks before they even reach your VDS (VPS). Their prices are pretty great for what they offer, too. Their support is unmatched, IMO.
