Here's my edited version of HESK. I've been editing it to work as a LSPD Database to keep track of crimes and criminals. The idea was to have officers post whenever they're looking for someone or have arrested a criminal. There might be easier ways today such as UCPs etc. to do this, but it's working good and doesn't require any other coding work. I've been using it for nearly a year and it made things really easy. You can search the database for criminals and determine whether someone is wanted, has been arrested before or not.

Here's some pictures:


Ticket submission:


Moderator overview:


Selected Ticket:


You can create accounts for each department, a general account for normal officers or just one account for each officer. I've been using one account per department plus admin accounts for HC members.

Just upload the contents to your server and go to www.domain.com/install, follow the instructions and you're done.

Fields such as Time, Witness, District etc. are optional and not included, you need to create them through the admin panel.

If you need any help setting it up or understanding the way it works, feel free to ask.
