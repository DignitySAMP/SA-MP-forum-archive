Hello community, I would like to present you with BattleField San Andreas, a little custom gamemode that I made for myself.This was my first script I have ever made, so please go easy on me .

What the script includes:

(1). 2 teams, USA and China.
(2).Class system
(3).Team chat.
(4).Some vehicles only driveable to a certain class such as Hunters and Hydras.
(5).Ability to change classes on your team with the type of a command.
(6). YSI Database. [WARNING]: For some reason when you register the password does not save, but it works on my other scripts im working on, Just letting you know.
(7).A few custom mappings, some made by me, a the AA one is made by a fellow SAMP mapper.
(.The score is able to be saved and the kills and deaths.
(9). Anti Team killing system.

What you need to have:
(1).SSCANF plugin for the team chat.
(2).Streamer for the objects
(3).Users files in your scriptfiles for the player account saving.
