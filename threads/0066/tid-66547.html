Colored health bar



It is a simple textdraw that over-lays the health bar. There are two versions: blue and green. It works in increments of ten. So if you have 90 or above health, it is full. 80 or above, it has a chunk missing. I might could add some more colors if requested. Please report any bugs and enjoy.
