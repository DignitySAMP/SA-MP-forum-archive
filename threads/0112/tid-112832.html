This is a simple filterscript, what brings back the Click PM function from the beta testing. This is a kind of different but the same.

Usage:
v1.0:
Press TAB, choose sb from the list double click him, write and send ur message.
v2.0:
Press TAB, choose sb from the list double click him, write and send ur message.
Use the /togpm command to enable /disable coming private messages.
