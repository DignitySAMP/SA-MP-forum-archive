TRAIN BOT-RIDE ON THE CARRIAGE!

What is it?
It's a Bot Train that stops at every train station in San Andreas (except Market station it goes slowly there).

[size=10pt]How to install?
To install simply put the train.pwn and train.amx from the "filterscripts" folder you downloaded into the one in your Server directory,
do the same with "npcmodes", then put the folder "recordings" into Server Directory\npcmodes\.
