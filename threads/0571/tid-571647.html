Do you think sa-mp is additive?
Yes and no. It's addictive when you found a good server and things are going well for you on that server. If you dont have a good server I'm easily able not to spend time on SA:MP at all. At the moment I can think of a few good servers although I am not interested in them since I kinda want to start my own server that fits my own roleplaying preferences and level.

Why do you think sa-mp is so additive?
It's user friendly and has a lot of variety in gamemodes. On top of that it has the most hardcore roleplay servers (that are realistic and in a moden time scenario) I've ever seen in a game. When purely looking at the roleplay-aspect the only thing that has better roleplaying servers is Minecraft but those are mostly medieval and not modern.

What's most additive to you?
Strategic roleplaying mixed with regular roleplay - doing strategic things on a roleplaying server in order to gain more in character power to the point where you have control over a rebel army that could heavily damage the server's government and replace certain parts of SA with your own rebel government. In the mean time when nothing is happening having regular roleplay with an in character family, and sometimes combining both of these scenarios, when your family is in danger because of the power you have.
