That's right!
Fly to the moon with a cheap spaceshuttle (that cheap it's made out of wood)
I was really bored, so I decided to make this little FS, which contains:
  • A /up and /down command! (for everybody free to use, so this can be abused, actually)
  • A /help command!
  • A mapped moon, out of 70 objects!
It goes up pretty slow, so it might take a while to get to the moon!
The place where the flight takes off: On some farm, close to a tunnel (From the mission you gotta steal a combine harvester for the Truth)


Please comment.
Yes, I know it ain't that good (just like my other "scripts" )
Maybe this could be some inspiration for a better scripter, who can improve this?
You're free to edit it, as long you give credits for MY part. Also you can make your own version.
