Bascially, use a streamer and change the CreateObject(); lines to whatever streamer you decide to use. We use CreateDynamicObject, a superb streamer by Incognito. Or, just select 1 map and add it manually to OnGameModeInit(), then try find the spawn pos (usually it's within the first object line params.
CreateObject(28272, 883.348, 2823.8383, 8.282, 0.000, 0.000, 90.000);
