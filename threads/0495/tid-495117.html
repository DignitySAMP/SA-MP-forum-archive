THIS WILL NOT BE DEATHMATCH SERVER

I want to start a very limited RP server. I know nothing about scripting, I have seen many videos on starting a SAMP server but I can't seem to do it myself, I don't have the patience for it and I would prefer to find the people that are willing to do this with me. I have played in RP servers including Ls-RP, IG-RP, PR-RP, etc and I would like to create something different without the need of much Roleplaying.

The server will mostly be for Organized Crime Roleplayers and Law Enforcement. Cartels, Mafias, Gangs, Police, S.W.A.T, and possibly D.E.A and FBI. Since this will most likely be a heavily violent environment of constant confrontations/shootings I have decided to change some RPing rules to make this a more long lived entertaining virtual environment. In other words RPing will not be strict when it comes to shooting but the server will be far from being a DeathMatch server.FOR EXAMPLE: A confrontation between 2 gangs breaks out and a player gets shot, the player will not be forced to RP the injury until the health of the player reaches critical point.

Why? I believe this will make things a bit more entertaining since it will give players the opportunity to stay involved in the confrontation a bit longer rather than being forced to stop the action in order to unnecessarily RP their minor injuries. Of course after dying players would be forbidden to return to the battle field. This is an example of what I mean by changing some of the RP rules. I have many other ideas for a server including many unique job script ideas among other things.

I don't have the knowledge to make this happen on my own so I would need help of many people for this. This would most likely interest Cartel/Organized Crime RPers but normal civilian RPing will be thought of as well. Rules and ingame Laws would be needed to make this happen and I can only do so much myself. Anyone interested in cooperating for this, HMU.

I want this to be as realistic as possible without the need of heavy RPing. I want to make the money struggle, drug trafficking, viloence, the fight for power, control and crime in general a lot more realistic and entertaining.

Imo, if you want to run a server, you should take the time to do the essentials first.. (Learning PAWN, creating forums, having a plan for hosting, what DJ_SHOCKER told you, etc.)

I don't want to be an asshole, but the hard truth here is, if you can't bother to try on your own, you shouldn't bother on running a server. Else, you can take the easy road - downloading a gamemode, editing simple text stuff or adding more filterscripts, blah blah blah, then calling yourself server management. Which is prolly not gonna bring much players..

Instead, perhaps download gamemodes, carefully examine them, try to understand them w/ tutorials on top, and slowly try learning to script similar things, but with new twists. Before you know it, you'll be better on having your creativity take over and make it possible to make the server you want. Or you can also look for scripters in the other section of these forums..

I've seen a-lot of roleplay servers, and all of them failed. Why? They lacked the resources ( capable leadership, a patient staff team ). Also, simple mistakes in grammar are often a sign of failure.
