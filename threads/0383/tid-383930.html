San Andreas Truck Simulator


Hi,unfortunately i didn`t finished this mod,I wanted to finish it and post it here,but I have more important things to do.

Let me tell you something about it.

*I wanted to do something like 'Truck Simulator' so i did a main menu for game:


*That`s the main menu,when you enter in game is showing up and you can acces it from game with /menu.
*When you click New Game you need to select your skin,city and the truck:






*Statistics from main menu:
http://imageshack.us/photo/my-images/577/samp018sa.png/

*And upgrades points
http://imageshack.us/photo/my-images/51/samp019bk.png/
(I can`t add more pictures)

*The upgrade points is Km`s with the truck,when you have 500 km-s you can upgrade your truck.
*When you upgrade it,the truck will go a little bit faster.

About systems
*All data is saving in mysql.
*You can save/load the game in 10 slots.
*It has 2 languange,English and Romanian(you can add more or change one of them)
*I started with zcmd and i recomand it.
Things to do
*You need to recreate the locations from 'New Game' when you select the city,and the truck.
*You need to find a way to skip OnPlayerRequestClass after first die,if i remember corectly i have bug here.
*You need to add comands,i have just for testing the mod.
*You need to add missions.
Some ideas
*Dealership with trucks.
*Make it just for driving,not and free roam(if you need help ask here)
*Level system make it like 10 missions = 1 level.
*Rank`s for level with names,like Little Trucker,Big Trucker,Maniac Trucker..etc.
I will come with more ideas,i don`t have right now.

I hope you appreciate this,if you need help and i will have time..post here and i will help you.
Sorry for my bad English,if it is.

Please dont remove credits,all ideas and work is mine.
