The Murderer


1 - Presentation

This gamemode is incomplete, you'll be able to play but a lot of things are missing. I allow you to modify it and release your own version if you want, I'm only exploiting an idea based on a Garry's Mod gamemode.
This is the rules:

As a murderer: You should escape the "Deagle" and kill everybody without being spotted
As a Deagle: You should find the murderer without killing the wrong person, else you're dead
As a civillian: You should escape the murderer and cooperate with the Deagle if possible


1.1 - Content

You can create a "clone" by pressing the key N) when you have 8 points and when you're a Deagle. The clone allows you "trap" the murderer who will me attracted by this motionless character & try to kill him. You'll just have to wait and get him !


1.2 - Developpment
It tooks me 1/2 day to do this, that's quiet light but I'll try to get a full functional gamemode as soon as I can
