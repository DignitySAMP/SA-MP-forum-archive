Hello.

Some of you might be familiar with Pokйmon, some won't. However, it's been a huge part of my childhood - we had a lot of fun with the series, the cards, the gameboy.

But, Pokйmon isn't just all about stuffed animals whom fight each other for fun. It has been, since its release, been a thread of haunted games, creepy stories and whatsoever. All of them are fake, but pretty enjoyable to read. I will tell it as if it is actually true - to increase reading pleasure, of course

I will explain you them in a serie of tales, consisting of 4 parts.

Part V: Haunted Blue

This one is my real favourite. I liked the Lavender Town, as it had some very scary footage, but this is even better...Video links (the story ones - those with actual footage are in the main topic) are under Extra's

It all starts off with someone like you and me, who enjoyed playing Pokйmon a lot in their youth. However, something weird happened to his game. He thinks he has a unique cartridge, which was actually possible back then. If you now have a bug in your game, they'll just upload a patch to the internet, but back then, internet was quite rare. Anyways, it all started when he lend his game to a cousin of him...

His cousin found out about the MissingNo glitch. This is a very infamous glitch which could possible fuck up your game. It appears when it tries loading unavailable data. You had to go through some highly specific steps in order to get this one. Then you'll suddenly go in a fight with a weird, pixilized Pokйmon, and if you beat it, the 6th (something like that) item in your inventory would suddenly be *99. And if you caught it, your game would entirely mess up and you'd have to start all over. Risky, but a lot of people used it to get more masterballs. Sadly enough no scary stories about this one.

Anyways, his cousin caught some MissingNo's several times without entirely bugging the game. And put it in the PC Box (Bill's PC) - where you can save all your Pokйmon you're not carrying. When getting his game back, one of the MissingNo's acted very weirdly...

It transformed in a Ditto. A white Ditto - on GBC it'd appear as grey ("pink"). Entirely white, which is non existant in any other Pokйmon game. After a while, the Ditto transformed into an Eevee. But not just transforming like the move Ditto is able to do. Really entirely transforming - the game stats, Pokйmon index, ... everything. The same happened again and the Eevee transformed into an Arbok. And eventually it changed back to the white Ditto. This seems a very rare bug. But if you look closer to the Pokйmons...if you spell all initials of those you get: Ditto, Eevee, Arbok, Ditto...DEAD.

While making a video about these events, everything suddenly started to act weird. At the end of the story I posted above, his mic suddenly broke. He got several BSOD's. A lot of electronics just randomly broke. However, he managed to make yet another video.

In that video, he just says that creepy stuff started to happen etc. You can easily make that up. But then, at 4:40, a very weird thing happens.

[ame]http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xGuVyrbKEaA[/ame]

You suddenly hear a noise in the background. He goes checking out what happened. And randomly, all by itself, his own video started playing on another computer. The one with the original story. Creepy.

In his next video, he resumes the story of his Blue cartridge. So after not touching his game for some months, he decides to play it again, and looks in the PC Box whether the white Ditto is still there. He can't find it immediately and it appeears to just have vanished. Though, when scrolling through his list, he found a Pokйmon with a custom name. A name he never added himself. It was the white Ditto. Its name was...Whitehand.

You might or might not be familiar with Whitehand. It's yet another myth (though not interesting enough to make an entire topic about) and again related to Lavender Town. Whitehand would be the ghost Pokйmon at the top floor of the Lavender Ghost Tower - aka the boss Pokйmon of Lavender Town. However Nintendo removed it because it would be too disturbing for children - nobody knows why though. This myth is based on the conversation you have just before entering the tower. A girl asks you "Do you believe in Ghosts?". When replying "no", she will tell you: "Hahaha, I guess not. That white hand on your shoulder, it's not real."

This is where he brings up the Blue theory. Everything which broke so far, was related to blue. A Blue Screen of Death. A blue SD card crashed. A drawer opened itself with blue stuff in it. His blue microphone started acting weird. His mouse, which supposedly started the video in his last one, has a blue LED.

Recording himself sleep, something creepy happens. Don't watch this if you get easily scared.

[ame]http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kVHGzFeelhk&feature=plcp[/ame]

A blue bowl falls. Randomly. And yet again his cam breaks. His blue camera. He decides leaving his house and live with a friend for some time.

At his friends, he uploads what he had recorded earlier but wasn't able to upload before. A screen capture of his glitched (haunted?) game. You can read the story here. Basically his entire game glitches in a very creepy way.

[ame]http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TnLkI5ggVGo&feature=plcp[/ame]

At the Professor Oak picture (which appears in a normal game just at the beginning), he resets his Gameboy. Oak would say "Hello", but is it just coincidence it resets RIGHT AT THE TIME only "Hell" appears?

The guy now resumes everything which happened. He already noticed the colour blue, obviously. But then there's also something with 5. Ditto, Eevee, Arbok...all three consist of 5 characters. Whitehand: white is 5 letters. A hand has 5 fingers. Lavender Town is the 5th town you can visit. His game glitched (previous video) after his 5th fireblast. Fireblast has 5 arms as animation. Though he also notices everything with 5 is only IN the game. The color blue appears to have effects outside the game (blue cam, ...)

He decides to investigate this theory and makes yet again another screen recording.

[ame]http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xYhtyV3gGLc&feature=plcp[/ame]

The white Ditto has magically disappeared in his Pokйmon. Which leaves him...5 Pokйmon. He goes, with these 5 Pokйmon, to the 5th town (Lavender), to the 5th floor of the Tower. On the first floor, he sees the weird man-sprite flashing for a second. The last known form of whitehand. He continues ascending to the 5th floor. Where he talks to the 5th NPC. With a creepy message. Asking it again and again. The 5th time he talks to her, his game crashes almost the same as last time.

He calls his cousing about the stuff in his next video. Trust me, nothing is going to pop-up, so it's safe to turn up your volume Though notice that all weird, paranormal stuff started to happen after playing with a Ouija board. A game where you summon spirits.

[ame]http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3pBC9bng7Lg&feature=plcp[/ame]

This really creeps me out lol.

And then when eventually the other Gameboy arrives, this happens:

[ame]http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lT9H220u9CU&feature=plcp[/ame]

I shat myself on that blue broom falling.

If you watch some more videos of strange noises etc. You'll notice a lot of them occur at 1:18 in the night. And in the 3rd capture video, Professor Oak says: R1:18. This definitely has a meaning. In the Bible, it states "-- I am the living one. I died, but look -- I am alive forever and ever! And I hold the keys of death and the grave. --". Some time later, a weird text file was made on his computer. On 11/8. The name of the document? 1134. Put that in your calculator and read it upside down...HELL.

Anyways, he actually went to that hospital/town. He found out that during World War I, a disease was spreading itself. People got blue lips, hands. And died. The disease was called...the blue death.

THE END
