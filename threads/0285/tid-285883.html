We have a excellent administration team and recently got a vBulletin license from the donations we've been receiving. We've been getting alot more players each and everyday we've been opened and when we got on the hosted tab we've gotten a larger playerbase. The theme of the server is based on you being a survivor in a large apocalypse, there are many refuges to start at, and weapons are easy to find with our /search system inside of shops and the police station. ►Story
The year is 2033, Corporations have fully gained contol of the world government due the U.S. economic collapse the world was thrown into a economic depression. It was looking for a savior. One organization profited from the world collapse, Evolution Inc or otherwise known as EVO. By using sythesized chemicals EVO created a drug that dampened human anger, frustration, and depression deeming one extremely happy no matter the circumstance, The drug was known as "Euphoria". Due to the fact that EVO had maintained its own currency by having a large stock of tradable gold and silver they were unaffected by the world economic collapse making it easy for them to slip this drug into ever country, But why would a country deny a drug that would make its citizens happy?. It was a good drug the world though it removed the sadness and sent you into a euphoric state. Weed and Cocain became obsolete. The only issue with this drug is it seemed due to constant use it slowly evolved within the humans genetic structure. It became a necessity to live, Its addiction rate sky rocketed by the year 2045. Evo was extremley please they're drug that that had produced had "Fixed" the world and didn't bother to study or watch its genetic traits. By this time the drug was gobalized every broken country was using it and it was actually for the better or so we thought. As the drug continuously evolved people craved it more and more... Until in 2050 EVO's stockpile of the drug was being exausted they couldn't produce as much as they sell. EVO's stockpile had run dry by the year 2050.


It is now 2063 the world has changed humanity is no longer the dominate species as creatures that had once been human have gone berserk attacking ANYTHING and eating ANYTHING in sight. What caused this was due to the addiction of the "Happy Drug" or as the scientist who designed it called it Euphoria. The innability to feed the addiction that Euphoria had cause had drivin people to lose they're humanity. The genetic imbalance caused by the drug drove cravings so high that humans that were on the drug now search for anything that will sooth they're addiction. It seems its not only mental insanity though as these once been human beings no longer resemble humans, They're skin flakes and limbs seem to rot. it is not fully know why Euphoria evolved humans so much over the past 30 years but it is for the worst. It was openly known to the public of those few who refused to take the drug that EVO was the maker. Though EVO had no intentions of it becoming a viral crisis they only wished to help the world they were cast down by the survivors as evil traitors of humanity. The drug was so widespread in the world nobody could stop it, What makes it worse is it seems the saliva of those who used the drug is a highly potent form of Euphoria, So those who are bitten by these crazed creatures innevitably turn into one of them.


Though there is hope surviving organizations from all over the world came to the city of which was completley ran by Evolution Inc. Los Santos. They hide within the city trying to find survivors that aren't infected, and combat the once proud Evolution Inc, As they are the threat to humanity! Or are they humanities only hope?
From here on how the story goes its up to those proud few who wish to save humanity... Or watch it get completley wiped from the planet.

Welcome to hell.

►Factions

Evolution Inc. - They're the people behind the infection, they started the whole outbreak. They currently have the anti-virus but are holding it for themselves and testing purposes. They work with other viruses they have created and are very scientific.

SOF203 - They're a military group protecting survivors in their base in LV where there's a low quantity of zombies. They serve to protect the rest of the surviving humans and they have a vendetta against Evolution Inc.

The Society - The Society is known for their secrecy in their small group, they don't see the point of helping nor making it worse. They're there to survive and to take what they need for themselves, if you stand in their way prepare for the worse. They're selfish and and ruthless.

Vipers - They were a large group of individuals who were motorcycle riders before the outbreak. They were at the pier when the city was under attack, they built up around their surroundings and made a small base at the pier. They're mean and tough, they also attached chainsaws to their motorcycles. You don't want to get near them.

United Nation Reformed - Focused on turning the city back in to its former state and bringing political correctness back. The U.N.R is the remnants of a government which collapsed after the 'Biological Disaster' ravaged the land, they use brutal tactics to apply the laws once lost, but focus on helping U.N Citizens with supplies of weapons and medicine.

Crimson Caravan - They're a group of people working with the survivors to build up and re-build society. They work well with everyone and have a very safe base built up in the middle of it all. They have a large supply of items and weapons to help protect against the infection, they're ready for anything.
