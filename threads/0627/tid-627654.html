These attempts should be alarming. They are not generated from a player on the server who is failing to login, but from an automated software that is used to crack passwords.
If you do not use this feature (https://wiki.sa-mp.com/wiki/Remote_Console) I personally recommend that you disable it and you never use the RCON functionalities (even on the server) i.e kick everyone who attempts to login.

To disable the (remote) feature: add to your server.cfg file the line "rcon 0". More info: https://wiki.sa-mp.com/wiki/Server.cfg
