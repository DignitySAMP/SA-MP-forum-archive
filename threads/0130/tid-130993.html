SixtyNine's Island.
This map is my first island/map i have made public. This map was made in early 2009. I tryed to make this map with a half decent effort because i was going to use it for myself but now i have no good use for it. It consists of a small airstrip, a helipad, a nice house and alot of other small things that you will find if you look around. The main way of travel to the other places in this island is via boardwalk. I have spent id say about 20~ hours into this island and finished it off to an acceptable degree for you guys.
