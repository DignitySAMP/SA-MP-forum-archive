[FS] Info
I was just bored today so I spent 2 hours making a script that shows information about a online player.

What is it?
It's a script that shows the info of a online player through textdraws. It shows the following information about a player of your choice:
  • Players Name and ID
  • Players Money
  • Players Score
  • Players Ping
  • Players Money
  • Players Armour
  • Players Health
  • Players Vehicle(Says "None" if they are not in one)
  • Players Money
  • Players Skin
  • Players Location
  • Players Distance Travelled
[color=red]Scripting
Info: It took me 2 hours to script the 1k lines but most of it was pre-made. Thanks to Dracoblue for DCMD and Dini, Whoever made the zone script, and whoever made the list of vehicle names.

Commands: There is only 2 commands for now which is /info[ID] and /information [ID] (They both do the same thing ).
