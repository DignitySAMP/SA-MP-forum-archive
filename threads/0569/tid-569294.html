About the map:
Hello, "Hospital Interior" is my third map, I wasn't planning to do a textured map for a long time but my local friend from samp helped me to texture this map. OK this is an interrior for Medical Service Agency. I've been working on this map for 45 minutes plus texturing, which took more than few hours because I have never used textures before.This work contains unique interior.This is the biggest and best map I've made since I started mapping and I think it's awesome. Feel free to Comment and +REP me

What does this work contain ?:
1. Enter Hall (Awesome)
2. Hospital Room
3. Doctor Office
4. Mortuary (Also Awesome)
