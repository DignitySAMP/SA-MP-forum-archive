Hello people today I will release my 1st map:A LV Business Neighborhood good for LVCNR servers,RP servers,Trucking etc.It is running good with PPC_Business which I will give credits to as a little addition to my screens below .So,let's start.

DESCRIPTION

-I repeat this is my 1st map I replaced the little hospital from LV and that blue ugly building and added a business neighborhood.It's elements are:

-Vehicles:Turismo,Banshee,Cheetah all stuff
-Buildings:24/7,Ammunation,a nice hotel,some shops
-Structures:Some fancy fountains which I love and a Big Smoke Crack Fortress statue
-Nature:Palms and Bushes from The Strip <3
