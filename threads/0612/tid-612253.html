The most effective solution is to reinstall Windows after getting the files you want to keep.

If you can't transfer the files normally or in safe mode, insert the Windows installation disk or boot it from a pen drive, and there is or are ways to prompt up the file explorer. You can then retrieve the files you want (takes longer than normal, but you should be patient if you want your files).

After you get your files, create two partitions, one for Windows and one for all the files (program files goes in the biggest partition). If you have a similar problem in the future (highly likely), you could just format the Windows' partition and reinstall Windows. And everything will still be there (all your files) on the other partition.
