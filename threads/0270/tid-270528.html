The AddStaticVehicleEx function adds the "respawn_delay" parameter. You will need to add the amount of time you want to wait for the vehicle to respawn after the player exits the vehicle:
pawn Code:
// Example
AddStaticVehicleEx(487,2533.3914,-1675.9926,20.1076,269.9375,86,86,100000);
More imformation on the function and it's arguments, look on the wiki.
