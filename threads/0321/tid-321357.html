Southern Life Roleplay
UPDATE: I have update ALL of the files including streamer and sscanf plugins, I have also removed the old ban system and added a /ban command! Also fixed the sscanf errors and /help command.
(Download is below).

Southern Life Roleplay is an edit of Vortex Roleplay. The edit is a HUGE edit, with many different things added/changed.

There are:
  • New jobs & Side-jobs.
  • Driving test.
  • Added things to make Roleplay more improved.
  • Added a time system.
  • Added a new payday system, where you are required to sign a check using '/signcheck'.
  • Added Factions.
  • There are a few new Admin Commands.
  • Houses & Businesses now have a name.
  • Businesses have a Payout and an entrance fee. (Both of which can be collected through a command).
  • The vehicle ownership system has been re-created.
  • There is a vehicle drop-off point. (Scrapyard) to get materials for weapons.
  • The whole script is based on Red County and Bone County.
  • There are two new pay-toll's on the bridges.
  • There is plenty more new features, just check them out yourself!
