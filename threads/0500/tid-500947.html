Random Money Bag Event

This filterscript is intended to give your gamemode a bit of a competitive nudge. When an administrator use the command /MoneyBag a random moneybag will be created within mainland San Andreas.

There are over 100 possible locations where the Money Bag can be spawned however, deers have an apitite for money and cant resist the urge to munch on someone's well earned lost cash. So hurry and get there before someone else does!

Every 3 minutes after the event has started a clue will be given on where the money bag is. The clues are as follows:

-The Money Bag Is Closest To Los Santos (Which state out of LS / SF / LV The money bag is closest too. Blueberry is Los Santos in my opinion and will be said so in the game)

-The Money Bag Is In Rodeo (Which Area/town the moneybag is closest too)

-Player(69) Is Closest to the moneybag (Self explanitory)

-Nobody Collected The MoneyBag. It Has Been Eaten By A Deer (Damn you deers! The Event is over D


[How To Add More Locations]

There is a guide in the script which tells you how to but I will also say it here for the uh not so smart people.

The Enumorator has 7 Slots
Which are for : The X Coords,The Y Coords, The Z coords, The Money You Win ,The Region Such As LS/SF/LV","The Area"

Add your coords and clues into the brackets and viola you have another location!
