on our server we have admins look at a player after a complaint, and test what it is that the player is accused of. we've seen just about every kind of cheat availible, from heath cheats where the player takes double, triple, or even 10 times the amount of hp to die, or the ability to fake a death to a minigun from some innocent other player. invincible cars are popular as well, which usually comes with the flying car cheat. it's a major issue almost daily, but one that is dealt with quickly with a good admin presence.

the main thing is that you have to know what the limits of SA:MP are, and what are normal game issues seperate from cheating.
