Intro

My bro somehow managed to ban me from my own server and I can't play on it anymore, so I'm releasing this gamemode as you've probably guessed yes it is a modified gf script but in my opinion better than those other edited pieces of crap.

The Families/Factions/Gangs

-Civiliians - These are average everyday people who have jobs like paper rounds and fixing cars.

-LSPD - Now led by Frank Tenpenny

-C.I.A - These replaced the FBI and have new skins and a /hack command to get into the Morelli computers and find out who their next hit is.

-Armed Forces - I think a better name than National Guard they have a new army base by the east coast of Los Santos hopefully these can track down the Al Qaeda.

-Los Corona Aztecas - A mexican gang that replaced the Yakuza their base is out the back of LSPD.

-Los Santos P.I.M.Ps - These replaced the La Cosa Nostra and are based in Vinewood in a house shaped like a U.F.O.

-The Government - The President of San Andreas and the Mayor of Bayside work together to make San -Andreas a pleasant place to live.

-The Yakuza - Replace hitmen and their home is Bayside.

-News Team - Report local events to the public.

-LS Taxi - Offer people lifts when they need it.

-License Faction - Instruct people so they can get licenses.

-Bayside License Faction - Same as above but in Bayside.

-Al Qaeda - A terrorist organisation based where the hitmen once where and have a dodo for terrorist attacks.

-BSPF - Bayside Police Force the Bayside equivelant to the LSPD led by Hernandez.

-The Vercetti Family - A family I recently added that lives in Bayside.

Btw I have added some new skins and spawns to most of these factions/families/gangs.

New Commands

-/hack - Lets the C.I.A hack into the Yakuza/hitmens laptop.
-/changename - changes your current name to a new one that you choose.
-/changepass - changes your current pass to a new one that you choose.
-/changecolor - If you don't like the current color you are you can change it with this.
-/changeskin - Gives you ids for skins
-/changeskin2 - A second list of skin ids
-/gotoar - takes level 2 and higher admins to the special room.
-/allowcitizen - A command for the Mayor of Bayside, it gives the ID of the player permission to enter Bayside
-/blockcitizen - This does the opposite to /allowcitizen it removes the player IDs license and if the cops find them they will throw them out of Bayside.
-/animhelp - displays a list of anims. (Thx to Woozie)
-/animhelp2 - displays a second list of anims. (Thx to Woozie)
-/gotobs - Teleports admins to Bayside.
-/IWANT - Gives an admin everything he deserves.
-A couple of /goto commands have been added aswell like /gotowuzi and more.


New

-Bayside airport.
-Shamal with interior in LS airport so people can fly from LS to Bayside. (Thx to BeckzyBoi)
-Everyone starts with a drivers license but can have it removed by a cop if neccessary.
-Alot of things have been shifted around San Andreas to try and improve the server.
-Bayside Pass gives you permission to enter Bayside the Mayor of Bayside is the only person who can give these out.
-New colors.
-Sea Mansion for the Mayor of Bayside only him and the President can enter it.

Bugs
