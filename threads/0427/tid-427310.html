Simple Police Siren Filterscript

Created by Zack Larson and Josh Greening

Usage Command: 'codetwo' ( Has scripted in ELM )

This filter-script was created mainly for the benefit of those who use slick-top vehicle law enforcement mods on their clients gta3.img. Most other siren filter-scripts have the siren either inside on the dashboard, Which in an actual CRUISER, In real life, You would never find it on the dash. Nor on the roof of a marked - slicktop cruiser, As they almost all the time have strobe lights, And LED's in the front & back of the car, Which is what this FS makes it resemble, Having the lights in the front and back of the cruiser.[/code]

Download(If file is not .Pwn reply in comments):http://www.mediafire.com/?kzn1peld2fv1aph
