When you uninstall, you have to delete EVERYTHING related to SAN ANDREAS and SAMP.

This includes MODS, HACKS, anything that is saved into your registry, because some files get left behind and cause this error.

Delete the San Andreas user files folder and delete anything left behind your version of san andreas. After you are COMPLETELY sure that you deleted every trace from GTA San Andreas and SAMP reinstall, and try again.

Sometimes when you install specially those "Cracked" versions and you uninstall later it leaves some data and files behind.

Mods can also leave data behind. Good Luck.
