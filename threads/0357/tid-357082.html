Seat Belt System "Ekano" [V.1]


Introduction:
Hello guys, this is the Ekano system i like to call. It is a filterscrip, init you will see some functions and commands. With this system you will need to put your seatbelt on, everytime you get in a car. Otherwise if you crash the car and you are without seatbelt you will lose health.
- Im accepting mirrors.
- I allow edits but with proper credits inside.

Future(s)
V.1 [0.3e]
- Auto Roleplay for taking your seatbelt on and off
- When you exit vehicle you seatbelt is automaticly off
- You can check your seatbelt with /checkmybelt
- And the way you put your belt/helmet on is with /seatbelt

Credit(s)
