Let's get right into this, the cancellation of 0.3.8 was very disappointing as we were all excited to see the outcome of future RC's especially the textdraw system revamp. This post isn't a plea to get 0.3.8, it's a post of appreciation and to cast my opinions on your thoughts Kalcor.


Objects and Skins discussion

Quote:
Originally Posted by Kalcor

The sad truth about the 0.3.8 version is that there would have been more models produced if it were just me and Matite working on models.
Let's put it this way, many people didn't know how to model and since you announced the ability to download server sided models A LOT of people started to learn how to model taking baby steps, some even hiring modellers. It takes time for people to produce decent models, it wasn't going to be done in a matter of months from the RC being announced, but I can guarantee a lot of models will be produced. People had already started producing and posting their models publicly and creating websites for sharing models such as Southclaws' website https://samp-objects.com/ and mschnitzer website https://samp-models.net/. Myself personally I already knew how to model, I was starting to learn how to use animated textures with RWAnimate and 2DFX lighting to go above and beyond with models for sa-mp. Big servers were gathering the best modders they could find to create skins and mods for their servers such as LS-RP, RC-RP and my server BA-RP has a modelling team of 12.

Ofcourse if you and matite would have produced more models because you two are used to this, you both slammed hundreds of models in one single update; but if this project would have continued many others would of mastered modelling for SA and would of produced models non stop. And I can only say 60% of people don't show their work on sa-mp forums they show their work within their own communities.

Slighly larger and more professional servers

I know for a fine fact LS-RP, RC-RP, niCe's server and BA-RP will all be using the 0.3.7-DL update rather than the main update you will release; which will be a little awkward right forgive me if I'm wrong and don't completely understand how all this will work but wouldn't it be preferred most servers using the main update? especially the "official" servers.


LS-RP had produced over 40 skins I believe, not seen what models they were working on or releasing onto the server.
RC-RP had produced over 60 skins by 2 men, and objects being created for drug systems and all.
We, at BA-RP had produced over 50 skins and had another 25 in development. We also had 30+ objects planned for the future to go with drug systems, police lighting, exterior + interior objects, and I could go on with the amount of ideas we had.
Italy Mafia had a lot in the works as I've been told and apparently so did Horizon Gaming

and the countless efforts by the smaller servers need to be recognised to, I'm not going to include them in this topic as I don't follow the development of many servers but feel free to discuss it yourselfs in the topic.

I'm just worried that this will bring down the average player count across the board, as the players are torn between 0.3.7 & -DL

Some servers will no doubt jump to get the downloads, but their players might not realise that doing so will cause them to be unable to join the other servers not wanting to jump until its a main release.

This change punishes everyone, and will i suspect be bad for the smaller servers, especially if all the big ones change for the downloads. I'm genuinely concerned for the player counts on all servers across the master lists, compared to before this was announced.

Also worth noting, numbers are universal. You don't translate them. Letters and words require translation, and often those translations are not clearly understood. 0.3.8 would be immediatley obvious to even non native English speakers. However -DL, and the attached documentation and downloads for it, may confuse people as to why X or Y doesn't work.

Personally I was looking forward to the Vice City & Liberty City stuff personally, but will have to see which way the servers, and thus the players, go before i can make any decisions on my own server to reduce the loss of players as much as possible.
