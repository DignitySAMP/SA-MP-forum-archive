Well, I learned by looking at SeifAdmin for some time, whenever I needed to do something I always took a look at some code that looked liked it in the SeifAdmin script. During the time, I learnt a bit of how I should create variables, making commands. With time, newer SA-MP versions got released and so I got into different types of functions a bit. Till that part, I did not understand a damn of what I was doing, even thought it worked 60% of the time.

I can't exactly remember how I fully got in the idea of 'how it works', however when I did I could finally understand around 10% of ******'s topics lol. I still learn more each day, so to the point: Continue learning and don't give up. I had times that I didn't script for 8 months or so when I was a beginner scripter. I actually never quite thought of the idea of becoming an advanced scripter, or at least not the level of skill I now possess.
