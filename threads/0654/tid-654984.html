Code:
	RemoveBuildingForPlayer(playerid, 673, 244.0313, -1387.2109, 51.7969, 0.25);
	RemoveBuildingForPlayer(playerid, 673, 258.2969, -1391.3984, 51.6641, 0.25);

	RemoveBuildingForPlayer(playerid, 669, 217.7031, -1381.6094, 52.1719, 0.25);
These objects are in very close proximity and could be condensed to just two lines of code by increasing the range. You just need to be careful that range does not intersect objects that you do not want deleted.
