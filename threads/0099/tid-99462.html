I felt the same like you. I didn't understand a shit of it and everybody mocked me. I do NOT mock people for getting it, but here's my serious advice.

The Wiki won't teach you the syntax and basics of PAWN. You need to learn that yourself.

It's hard to teach people it, you just need to "get it". You have to try. I got pissed off but when I learnt it I understood.

Once you do understand the syntax and basics, the wiki is GREAT. It documents EVERYTHING about it, but you simply don't understand how to use it until you know the basics & syntax.

https://wiki.sa-mp.com/wiki/Category:Scripting_Functions
https://wiki.sa-mp.com/wiki/Category:Scripting_Callbacks

That is the key part of what you will use when you know it.
