Hey guys...

Yesterday and today I have found a very crazy bug (dunno if it's a bug ?!)

If you blow your self up or get exploded like 100 times (VERY OFTEN!), some bots of the singleplayer start to appear.
I saw this 5 times yet, it appears in 0.3c, 0.3d rc1 and 0.3d rc2. Maybe in older versions, too.

There are always three things: 1 or more vehicles that will explode after 5 seconds (it's NOT server-side, it exists only at the client that has the bug!), 1 Bot (CJ Skin) shooting at you with Pistol/Uzi and one running bot, running away from you. I think he runs the singleplayer paths, what else?

If you throw a grenade (or do something the singleplayer bots run from) they will run too.
I have kicked myself from my server after seeing the bug to test if it's serverside... you're still able to see them when you're kicked. And they are still moving & shooting. At the area seen below there are more places they appear.
The vehicles still get spawned when kicked / server closed.

After a while, my client crashed.

There were NO other players on the server! The proof for that is, that they 1. dropped money AFTER I closed the server, 2. lost the head when shooting on the head with M4.

I don't know if anyone else has seen this too, but for me it happened 5 times yet in the past 2 days...
I'm testing a new gamemode where you get killed by explosions, so I think this could be a reason for this (Like the "bug" crashing your client on death while having much objects ?!)
