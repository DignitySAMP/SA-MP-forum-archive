About the map

Information:
This is my 2nd map. Which is basically a water race/challenge/minigame in which you have a vortex or any preferred good boat and you have to reach the end first.
The starting of the race was very hard to think for me, so I made 5 fenced ways, and in each way there will be a boat under the tower.
This was done to avoid players ramming in start of the race and give the players a good straight speedy start. Please note that I haven't taken pictures of everything,
because they would have reached 25+ then.
I just took the pictures of few routes to give you a little idea about how it looks like.
So try it, don't just look from the pictures and then comment.
Objects converted: 256
Vehicles: 0
Vehicle models: 0
Map Type: Stunt/Minigame/Racing

Features

1) No escaping points
2) Several traps, ramps and hidden barrels.
3) Fully tested map
4) Mapped like an interesting water racing track
5) Preferred vehicle, any sea vehicle, but I prefer Vortex for perfect gaming.
