An issue I have with the recent survival zombie games is that guns are way too rare. I mean it's important to make the game not easy, but it's also important to allow the player to actually play the game instead of being bored dodging zombies all day.
If you want San Andreas to seem a lot bigger, then remove vehicles almost entirely.
Objectives would be an important factor in the game, to keep players from sitting in one area. Something like courier missions or raids would make for good gameplay and allow methods of obtaining resources other than just finding it on the ground.
