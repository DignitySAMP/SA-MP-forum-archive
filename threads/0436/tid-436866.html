There is another parameter after the color1 and color2: respawn_delay.
Set (and change) the respawn_delay to -1! This might help you out (note that the vehicle won't be respawned untill you use SetVehicleToRespawn() or when the vehicle dies)

...CarInfo[h][cColorOne],CarInfo[h][cColorTwo],60000);
This 60000); is the respawn_delay. This would mean the vehicle gets respawned after not being used in a minute. If you don't want this change it to -1.
The other vehicles respawn when you get out because the respawn_delay is not set so it is 0. Put -1 after the colors.

Please note that AddStaticVehicle doesn't have the respawn_delay parameters. Only CreateVehicle and AddStaticVehicleEx have this parameter!
