Coins System - Gold Coins, Silver Coins, Bronze

v1.0



Instructions:
--> Copy the script to pawno and copile it
--> Open your server.cfg and write the what you atribuite at FS at copilation

Commands in v1.0
--> /givegcoins: to give to a player gold coins
--> /givescoins: to give to a player silver coins
--> /givebcoins: to give to a player bronze coins
--> /setgcoins: to set a player gold coins
--> /setscoins: to set a player silver coins
--> /setbcoins: to set a player bronze coins
--> /mycoins to watch your amount of: gold,silver,bronze coins

What include do you need for this FS:
--> ZCMD you can download it from here: https://forum.sa-mp.com/showthread.php?tid=91354
--> SSCANF you can download it from here: https://forum.sa-mp.com/showthread.php?tid=120356
--> DINI you can download it from here: http://dracoblue.net/download/dini-16/35/
