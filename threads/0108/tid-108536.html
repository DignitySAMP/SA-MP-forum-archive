To be honest, it's about what you have in your RP scripts & not so much what city you use. For example I'm creating a RP that uses the entire map, but with great limitations which most of my code is focusing on LS. LS is over-used, yes, but offers more possibilities for my RP than in any other major city. Take the airport for example: It has ALOT of terminals and even 3 levels to use. 28 of those front entrance doors to be exact while the other 2 airports have but only a few...

But some of those previous suggestions are correct with the gangs, mafia & whatnot. If you're planning something small, then another city site might be more suitable for your RP scripts. So it's really up to the scripters which city best fits thier planned style of gameplay.
