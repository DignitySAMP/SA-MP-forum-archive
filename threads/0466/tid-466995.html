1. Story not long enough and did not utilize much of the map in the missions (Yes, I know they are using it for GTA Online)
2. It takes too long to lose wanted stars.
3. Cops spawn idiotically and in huge numbers.
4. You get wanted even when there are no witnesses. You can be in the middle of a forest, shoot a gun or kill a hiker, and you magically get two wanted stars.
5. The police have godlike accuracy.
6. The body armour (even the super heavy) is used up wayy too fast. It was better in GTA IV.
7. It's annoying how sometimes you die so easily from crashing through your windscreen.
8. The tank is not strong enough.
9. Spike strips work on a tank?
10. Still not enough side-missions and fun activities. Better than GTA IV but not nearly as good as San Andreas.
11. You can buy a 1 million dollar car and if it gets stuck, you lose it forever. It should respawn or be able to buy vehicle insurance like you can in GTA Online.
12. The iFruit app is only on iOS. Never understood why companies only develop on iOS and not Android.
13. Texture pop-ins and floppy frame-rate due to shitty 8 year old console hardware. Why haven't they announced next-gen and PC version yet?
14. Business management sucks. You get payed too seldom and your buildings aren't enterable. Every business should have it's own side activity (besides the random events when the manager asks for help).

Overall, it was a amazing game. Loved every second of it and very close to 100% completion. Really just wish it was longer. It only took me like 20-25 hours to complete where GTA IV took near 70 hours.
