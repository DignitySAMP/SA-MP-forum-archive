Introduction
This Filterscript and map is made 100% by me , I made this for role play games server because i never saw this idea made somewhere else before ! Please don't remove credits and don't forge your comments.



Features
► A teleport command that teleport you to swimming pool stadium .

► Lobby and waiting room for buying ticket and going in to swimming pool.

► Sauna room with chair and wall made of wood.

► Cloakrooms, bathrooms and Toilet.

► A bar for eating and drinking.

► Big jump with auto animation of jumping on it .

► 156 Objects .

► Door enter and exit by Pickups .



Commands
/SP for teleport near the door of Stadium.



Installation

Place the .pwn and the .amx into your filterscript folder
place SP near filterscript on your server.cfg



Bugs
No bugs
