I think it's great: a relatively small area where players bump into eachother even if they don't want to, and roleplaying should be much easier and more interasting.

There are however several problems that follow the vast majority of prision roleplay servers, at least those on which i played:
-All roleplay servers are identical to a point. There is no way to successfuly prevent that completely, but most on which i played don't even do their best to make it unique.
-They have begginer scripters and/or owners who learn while managing a server. I for one think that if you want to be a scripter, you should have a basic idea what you want to make and how.
Many servers are also managed by people who don't know how to or are deliberately biased when dealing with matters. That's the thing that kills most of the roleplay servers.

Another, but separate problem is that vast majority of servers with big potential close fast. Either because of funds, or because the owner/scripter doesn't have time for it anymore.
In general, the best roleplay servers i ever played on haven't lasted for more than three months.
