timers are not needed. Unix timestamps are great for this. The following is an example only you can use/modify it to your needs.

Example:

pawn Code:
new PlayerVIP[MAX_PLAYERS]; //to store the VIP variable in.

//inside of your VIP command:
PlayerVIP[playerid]=gettime()+days*86400;

//example:
PlayerVIP[playerid]=gettime()+30*86400; //VIP for 30 days.

//checking if its active:
if(PlayerVIP[playerid]>gettime())
{
    //they're active VIP
}
else
{
    //they arent.
}
Small advice:

Use sscanf2, it'll make your commands easier to look at and possibly faster.
