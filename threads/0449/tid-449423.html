Alien Ship Remix

Was a pretty inspiring map but the original lacked poorly if any in the visual aesthetics department. I've completely re-textured the entire ship from top to bottom with a very very simple texture theme of solid black and green I think the simplistic style makes a rock solid theme.

The ship has been relocated to above Grove Street I think it looks way cooler seeing the ship above the city. You can also change the alien ships color from green just look at the top of the script and remember only ARBG is acceptable.

Some of the map was removed in the center because the objects could not be textured but doesn't really affect the map over all.
