Just a simple question but I think some background information is required firstly. I'm in my late twenties and I've been involved with SA:MP since 2007, administrating and managing various servers and forums but I'm at a stage now in which I've encountered before. My new server is going well, usually quiet at times but I know it's going to get busier and I believe the foundations for success are in position but I have a question for the more mature members here, it's this:

How did you find, cultivate and keep good quality moderators/administrators on your server?

I know it's important to test and observe potential recruits in various situations before considering their promotion but do any of you have any advice or suggestions? The situation I have at the moment is that it's very difficult to find and recruit mature, fair and calm people who can be trusted with power and responsibility - they usually want the administration status without doing the work or to impress their friends or once given privileges, they end up with inflated egos and cease to relate well to others. I know and manage good moderators and administrators now but I know I will need to look further for more but where or how did you find yours? I think this is probably an issue for all serious individuals who just want to maintain a good, stable project with a dedicated team so surely some of you can relate to this discussion. Perhaps you can share some of your good and bad experiences?

Selecting the wrong people can bring complaints and accusations of corruption. Yet players expect a large administration presence without understanding that people in positions of responsibility and power need to be selected carefully for their abilities and capacity to treat their role seriously without resorting to misuse of their powers - it's a difficult balance to maintain. So to return to the question; how did you find, cultivate and keep good quality moderators/administrators on your server? Thanks.

Intelligence isn't a natural thing. Intelligence is something you're born into by your surroundings and experience. Simply selecting intelligence? That's like asking for the best in a job offer when you're opening a small business compared to a corporation.

Intelligence isn't a requirement. You only need those for 1 or 2 leaders. You, yourself, is more than sufficient. Imagine yourself as the manager. The manager doesn't do work. The manager tells those he hired what to do, they follow specifically, or be fired.

Complaints? A good manager can negotiate and show leniency for the first few mistakes. If the same mistakes are constant, let down the guillotine.

The most effective methods is to start off with the select best candidates. Later on you find players whom are interested in joining your team. What do you do? Tell them to 1. Be active. 2. Be helpful toward new players. and 3. If they assist you in recruitment of new players and the player-base is larger, promotions will be granted. Giving a trial or level 1 position isn't a major loss compared to the players that one person is now raking in, correct?

You aren't trying to get perfection right off. The end justifies the mean.

I had to play an empty server for 3 months, first week or two with mainly hackers to discover this. Take it to heart. Learn to trust. Hope for the best.

You'll also be expected to do your time and attend the server regularly, while using good English to communicate.

How can I get a trial? | Following years of experience I've personally found that the best approach is to recruit those that show themselves to be good players, showing a wide variety of qualities that reflect what the server needs. You should try to behave like a moderator, keep calm and if you're new; try to make plenty of friends. You have to develop and maintain a good reputation for guiding players and supporting the server in your own way. Speak good English, don't swear or get angry, make us notice you for what you do on the server. Let us observe you for some time and if you can develop and sustain a great reputation amongst our players with a mature personality you'll probably get a trial with us.

The server team at this time is obviously small to reflect the average number of players online but current members are from across the world. Bad behaviour, inappropriate conduct or corruption are taken seriously and I'll personally do all I can to address any concerns or reports of such instances as mentioned.
