[FS] Simple Driving School Test Area

As the title says, this is a simple Driving School test area, which i used in one of my previous servers, and since its of no use anymore, I decided to share it.
It's as simple as it can be, just a parking lot, surrounded by fences and one timed gate which closes automatically and can be opened with /opends.

If you need any help, you can contact me through the pm.

Simple installing, just put the pwn and amx in your filterscripts folder, and add the line "filterscripts drivingschooltrack" in your server.cfg
