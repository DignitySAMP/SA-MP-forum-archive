I'd expect that you're an idiot if you start a GSP specifically for SA:MP. There's no market or money in it.

Seriously, maybe 5 years ago I could've forgave you for such a sketchy business plan, but now, the numbers are clear, even if at least 60% of people hosting servers of any size(that you'd be able to charge more than $5/m for) didn't use VPS's/dedicated servers, you'd still be have problems making any money.

If you're still not convinced, lets break down the numbers here.
Lets say you host 5% of all SA:MP servers online, right now there are 4,280 servers online, 5% of that is 214. Lets say of those 214 servers, you make a PROFIT(including taxes, PayPal fees, etc.) of $5/m. Being reasonable, no one that knows anything about the GSP/hosting market is going to pay more than $5/m for the convenience of having you manage the back-end of their server, simply because SA:MP is so easy to host and requires so few resources.
214*5 = $1,070 USD/m. You can't make a living off of that, much less pay for the servers to host said servers.
Even if 5% of all SA:MP server owners paid you $15/m, and you pocketed 100% of that, it'd only be $3,120.

If this post hasn't convinced you that you'd be out of your mind to try and start a host specifically marketed towards SA:MP is a outright stupid idea, then I don't know what will.

For reference here, for $10/m, you can buy a solid VPS that can host SA:MP without issues, along with a TS server or even in some cases a web server.

I'm going to be nice about this...starting a web hosting or any form of hosting company is an extremely risky market and business idea as there are so many established companies already.

You have to invest a lot of money into a unique website, client management system such as WHMCS, and then the servers.

You could never make a profit unless you invest a fair bit of money, as we do not want to buy of someone that resells as that's pointless, and if you were to resell then you would have to make it more expensive to cover your costs.

So overall you need a large amount of money with the risk of loosing it all just like gambling to stand a chance.
