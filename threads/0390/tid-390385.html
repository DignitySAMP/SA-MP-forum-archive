Hey guys,

Today, I'm releasing my Radio Filterscript. This is also my first filterscript I ever posted on the SA-MP forums.

Its a radio dialog that i did make and it contains 20 stations currently. I spent like 4 hours making it. Some stations might not work is cause they switch links or whatever but i will make sure to keep it updated with new stations or new links. There is only 1 command that controls everything and thats /radio You can also stop the radio by selecting stop radio from /radio dialog. My personal favorite station is the 1st one :P and if you need some help with making your own feel free to ask.

Commands:
/radio
