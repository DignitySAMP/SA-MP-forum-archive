Hey!


The key to keeping a server active and flowing with players is indeed the uniqueness of the script, you have to be creative, and open to new ideas/suggestions. Proper language is a big thing with SA-MP servers, the more professional you seem, the more friendly environment you provide for your players, but not too strict, your SA-MP server should be fun, not restricted to prison like conditions. Another thing that attracts players is gamemode flare, the flashier, and colorful your gamemode appears, the more people it attracts, I do not fully understand this concept, I guess it brightens the mood and promotes good behavior/fun environments. I've seen almost every server turn up very successful by following the guidelines I've stated above.
