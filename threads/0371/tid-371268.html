Hello. I have made many maps, but this is the first one I am releasing to the public. It is the second map I made(if I remember correctly) and it was just sitting there gathering electronic dust because I never intended to use it. It was a test map, but I was bored and decided to improve and release it today. It is an island with a wrecked nuclear power plant on it and a couple of ships that got destroyed by straying too close to the island, which is a restricted area. It can be used as a deathmatch arena or just as some kind of place for players to hang out and explore. I suggest that it be used as a DM arena though because it already has fencing and is fully enclosed and hard to get out of without a helicopter or other aerial vehicle. Finally, it is made up of 101 objects and is located SSE of Los Santos Airport.

SCREENS:







I left a small open area for anyone who wants to add something to it on their server or personalize it or whatever. However, do not re-release any version of this map, edited or otherwise, without my permission!!!
