Welcome to the World War III Forest Team Death-Match.
The WW3 Forest TDM formerly known as just the World War III server based in the desert back in 2009 and early 2010. Has now been updated as of late 2010 (and still updated constantly!) to The World War III Forest TDM. The game mode is a mixture out of Team Deatchmatch (TDM) and Roleplay (RP) and the battle ground is located mainly in the forest (Flint County area) of the GTA SA map. High detailed mapping and a powerful and amazing script is awaiting you with hundreds of features! Some of the greatest codding and scripting known to SA-MP!



The main point of the TDM is of course fighting side by side with your team against enemies, but also to conquer (capture) the forests' main territories for your team.
They are:
☠ Fort Chilliad: (FC) Is one of the biggest and also the farthest territory. The location is the top of Mt. Chiliad, surrounded by electric fences. FC is the only territory which has commands to open and close doors and gates. The little vehicle park inside the territory allows you to evade or defend the territory or just to leave it faster than with cars. FC isn't that easy to take, therefore you may use a special feature after having taken over the territory.



FC Special feature: SCUD missile - A fast moving missile, fired from the SCUD station located in FC, this missile is fired at your desired target. (Any enemy player) Your screen is placed on the rocket to witness how far and where the rocket travels. Once a connection is made with the target, they are hit and blown to bits.

☠ Power Plant: (PP) It's exactly what it's called, a power plant, Located near the fight area and is one of the most famous territories in the server. It's highly detailed and also allows you to use the territory as sniper location. It isn't easy to take over this territory because there's only one entrance (besides invading it with air vehicles). The vehicle park consists of some caddies, a truck, sanchez and a maverick. PP is the only territory with a different feature regarding the other territories.



PP Special fatures: Jet Pack spawner (Jet pack can only be used when the territory is owned by your team.) As well as a Surface-To-Air Missile truck is located here within a garage. It can be used to take down enemy aircraft simply by the press of a button.

☠ Border River: (BR) Is a little bay and was one of the first territories in the server. Taking over BR is hard because it gets attacked more than FC, PP and Armoury but there are some nice hiding spots in the little "forest" which is part of the bay. Besides a dinghy and a sea sparrow, there aren't any other vehicles, so it's hard to leave BR without getting shot or exploded. BR might lag near trees and uses a lot of objects which don't appear immediately.



BR Special feature: ICBM (Intercontinental Ballistic Missile) One of the servers most powerful super weapons. Choose your target, stand and watch the rocket fly into air and make it's mark. Not only will it destroy the person the ICBM was fired at, it will kill everyone around that person as well. (Yes, you do receive each kill!) As well as the Anti-Ground boat. This boat is a powerful sea weapon used to take down any approaching ground vehicles, simply by the press of a button.

☠ Armoury: Armoury is the better mapped territory of the server. It's a big area and highly detailed. Therefore pay attention that you don't get lost in this huge building. Armoury is a fictitious and abandoned weapon factory. The vehicle part consists of a few sanchez and one sea sparrow. Two entrances allow you to take over the territory, one of them is only accessible on foot. There is one special location to hide in this territory, find out yourself.



Armoury Special feature: Artillery - Located near the edge of the facility, the Artillery area is a small building. It is what it is 'Artillery' choose who you want to destroy, and the huge cannon will take its job and fire VARIOUS explosions at your target. This will surely burn their pants off.

☠ Old Ruins: (OR) Is a territory in the main area of the battle ground. It's located in the middle of the team bases and therefore it always changes the ownership. People like to place mines and artillery here because hunters and hydras "like" to attack this territory, same is valid for rhinos. OR spreads a feeling of guerrilla war due to its old and decayed buildings which allow hiding and seeking. The vehicle park consists of some quads and sanchez'.



OR Special feature: IC (Ion Cannon) The OR Ion Cannon is great for taking out those pesky snipers shooting down at OR from the hills around it. Good to strike at any one, choose your target carefully and a beautiful light show of lazers will go off right on your enemies head. Denigrating them in a beautiful explosion.

☠ Angel Pine: (AP) The beautiful and nature filled town of Angel Pine has now been brought in as a menacing territory. With various vehicles that include from land and sea, it borders it's brother territory of Fort. Chilliad. A once blooming town, now a part of a scary disaster for all aircraft controlling pilots.



AP Special feature: The Electromagnetic Impulse (EMP) - This powerful weapon located in AP is used to disable all vehicles that are currently in use temporarily. It's a great tool to take down any attacking aircraft that are starting to annoy you.

☠ Xoomer Fuel: Located across from the Old Ruins and also the center of the war zone is a 'not-so-abandon' fuel center, loaded with a tank inside and a neat interior to explore. It is one of the newest territories to the server that also includes a powerful super weapon.



Xoomer Special feature: SCUD Storm - This weapon is fired at nearly everyone, blasting them with a huge explosion. It has a semi-long cool down timer, but surely gets the job done!

You also have the ability to spawn in any territory owned by your team.



☠ China: Is one of the normal teams of World War III server. The colour of this team is yellow and they are supported by the Intimidators gang.



This team owns a base near Wesley's Airport, and it's connected to Old Ruins territory with a bridge. This base is equipped with quad, sanchez, bikes, rhinos, patriots and a barracks as land vehicles, and with an hydra, a SeaSparrow and two hunters. The base also have a savezone.

☠ Europe: Is one of the normal teams of World War III sever. The colour of this team is the light green and they are supported by the Force Operation X gang.



This team owns a base near the Power Plant territory. This base is equipped with quad, sanchez, bikes, rhinos, patriots and a barracks as land vehicles, and with an hydra, a SeaSparrow and two hunters. The base also have a savezone.

☠ United States: Is one of the normal teams of World War III sever. The colour of this team is the blue and they are supported by the Special Airforce Services gang.



This team owns a base near the Armoury territory. This base is equipped with quad, sanchez, bikes, rhinos, patriots and a barracks as land vehicles, and with an hydra, a SeaSparrow and two hunters. The base also have a savezone.

☠ Russia: Is one of the normal teams of World War III sever. The colour of this team is the red and they are supported by the Nuclear gang.



This team owns a base near the Power Plant territory. This base is equipped with quad, sanchez, bikes, rhinos, patriots and a barracks as land vehicles, and with an hydra, a SeaSparrow and two hunters. The base also have a savezone.

☠ Necromonger: Is one of the special teams of the World War III server. This team is not supported by gangs.



This team owns a base above all the battlefield, in the sky. This base is equipped with hydras and vortex'.
This team is available when you have 1000 kills or more.

☠ Red Cross (Medics): Is one of the special teams of the World War III server. This team is not supported by gangs.
This team owns a base in San Fierro, but they can spawn near the battlefield.
This team is not a fighting team, but they heal people in the battlefield. For this reason, they can't be attacked too.

You have the ability to choose any team to fight for. If you play long enough and receive enough experience. (1,000 kills) You can join the special Necromonger team, that owns a unique base and is not united with the other countries!



Each team has a variety of classes to choose from with unique attributes.

☠ Assault: This class is loaded with a Knife, Desert Eagle, an AK-47, and Grenades. As well as the special ability to place /c4 and detonate them with /det.

☠ Sniper: This class is loaded with a Katana, Silence Pistol, Sniper Rifle, and Smoke Grenades. As well as the special ability to head-shot people and place /mines.

☠ Support: This class is loaded with a Katana, Desert Eagle, Combat Shotgun, an MP5, and Grenades. As well as the special ability to /resupply players of their weapons and place a /scanner that allows them to scan & spot hidden players near them.

☠ Anti-Air: This class is loaded with a Katana, Desert Eagle, MP5, Smoke Grenades, and a 'SYNCED' auto-aim Heat-Seaking RPG - to take down all enemy aircraft.

☠ Engineer: This classes is loaded with a Katana, Silence Pistol, MP5, an RPG, and Grenades. This class has a variety of special abilities that include placing /mine, /sam (A SYNCED- Sirface-to-air missile!) /repair vehicles, /fuel vehicles and /trap a vehicle.

☠ Pilot: This class is loaded with a Knife, Silence Pistol, an MP5, and Smoke Grenades. As well as the ability to drive -ANY-aircraft without a specific rank or amount of score.

☠ Combat Medic: This class is loaded with a Knife, Desert Eagle, Pump-Action Shotgun, an MP5, and Smoke Grenades. This class has a variety of special abilities that include, /heal (Ability to heal team members) /hp (drop health packages for your team members to pickup) /poison (Poison enemies and watch them slowly die, and /antidote (Cure any poison from team members to avoid them from dying)

Feel as if those classes do not have enough weapons? Just run up to the skull icon at your base to buy almost any weapon you want to use in combat, and you do not lose them once you die! Be as strong as you can be.
There is many ranks you can reach from getting score and kills.
As well as ranks for medics, if you're not too good at the killing part!

The WW3 Forest TDM has hundreds of features. Too many that cannot only be described here, but where you must join yourself to discover them.

☠ "Ghost Device" system is a neat product that can be purchased at the 'Pill' icon next to the skull weapons icon. The Ghost device is used to hide your name and player icon from the map or anyones sight. Except from people using scanners to find you. This is a near feature that will assist players in sneaking around and fighting without being spotted.

☠ An amazing feature that gives the server a 'RolePlay' feel to it is the ability to own a house. You can choose from over 100 hundred pre-added/mapped homes in the server. Not only can you choose from some of the un-owned ones, but can also request to own a house. You can choose which building you want to own, or maybe even map it yourself! You may also request almost any car you wish to have at your home, as well as the ability to spawn at your home!

☠ The server also has a very unique business system. You have the ability to purchase 3 (max) businesses' out of over 65 (and more to come) to make your own income and profit. These businesses are located all around the warzone and around San Andreas. Someone already own a business you want? Wait 15 minutes, and buy it off of them! This is a great way to make money and purchase anything you need.
A banking system is also on the server, where you have the ability to access an ATM your base to withdraw money or deposit money.

☠ Gold Package system, a hint is given to all players on the server about a gold package. This package when found gives a large amount of money to the winner. (/hint)

☠ Event Admins can also start awesome events for players. These events take place in different custom mapped areas. Winners receive fun prizes!
