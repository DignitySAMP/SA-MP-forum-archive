~DMP v1.0~
Deathmatch Practice & TCW's

Features
- Saves Stats & Settings (SQLite, "BUD")
- Automatic Duels (Step into the area)
- Userfriendly TCW's
- Userfriendly & Basic Overall.
- Anti Weapon-Cheat (Basic)
- 8 Fighting zones
- "Taken Damage" Armour & HP Icons
- "Given Damage" Sound (Toggle/Untoggle with '/Dmgsound')

Commands
General > /Pm /Report /Dmgsound /Help /Commands /Ramp
Fighting > /Z /Tdm /Leave

Moderator: /Kill (others) /Heal /Kick /Mute /Unmute /Goto /F /Jump
Admin: /Heal (id) /Healall /Spawn /Ban /IPBan /V /Freeze /Unfreeze /Get /Setlevel /Boost /Spycmds
Manager: /Rban /Muteall /Unmuteall /Freezeall /Unfreezeall /Getall
