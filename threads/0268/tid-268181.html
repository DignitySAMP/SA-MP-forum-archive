Welcome to HayZatic Team Deathmatch! Over the Past Few Days iv been working on this script adding more and more to it to make it better. And now i think i got it Really Good And Very Effective with Fantastic Script.The script has many things, like Medic Fields Team Scores, Choose Class's, Change Teams It is all there. And im releasing it to you. So here it is. Make sure you read about the updates before you download one!

This Gamemode was made from Scratch.



So far ive only spent one day working on this, but dont worry. Ill be updateing this alot.
As you see on the screen shot the 2 Textdraws with the scores are exactly what you think they are. like in the Call Of Duty Series when the scores get to 50 the server restarts. The Teams are Russia Vs. USA army. The teams both have a team color so its easier to identify.

In every base there is a Ammunation weapon shop and when you enter the buildings door you type /buy you have an option to buy some of the weapons that are there.
