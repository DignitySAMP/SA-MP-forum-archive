=============Info=============
Original script is by Eazy E aka Eric_, but i edited it a little bit, there are some new commands added.

=============Old Features=============
Security - The server has a encryption, so it doesnt show the password blah blah

Anticheat - A serverside weapon hack! Every weapon is stored in the player's file, and everytime a player's file doesnt match the weapon they get banned (( sorry, it's hard to explain ))

Saving - Weapon saving from all weapon slots are saved, So is money etc...

Housing - 350 houses spread across San Fierro. A dynamic system, you can move around houses also.

Businesses - 30 businesses around San Fierro, you can create more if needed!

Faction System - Hold up to 30 factions, this can be easily changed ingame

Public Places - You can make up to 30 public places. (( Example: Police, Fire ))

Tazer - The tazer shot is dynamic, you can taze a player with a SD Pistol

Sniper Shot - Good for mafiaa, You can snipe people, one shot kill.

Bombs - Yes there is a bomb system, You can plant bombs, make bombs, and give bombs.

Admin System - About 60 Admin commands, There are two groups, Moderator / Admin.

Drug System - You can buy drugs at a place in San Fierro, you have to plant them and watch the plant grow (( Made with objects )). The drugs step is like this:

Buy drug seeds
Plant drug seeds
Grow them
Use them

=============New Features=============

Vehicle Ownership - You can buy vehicle from dealership or from some other player, also you can sell your vehicle.

Driving School - Added timer to see how much time you have to finish your drive.

Engine system - Whole new engine system, /engine is command.

Lock / Unlock - Lock and Unlock new system, if you are near rented vehicle or near your vehicle you can lock it and unlock it.

Fuel system - Whole new fuel system when you fill your vehicle it doesn't take you 700$ it count how much fuel you need and then take for every 1 liter 5$

Truck job - Truck job is still under construction, but it give you higher paycheck.

ADMIN COMMANDS:
-/addvehicle,
-/giveweapon

Death system - changed time, you need to wait 1min not 2.

Factions - When you are police officer, and when you do /duty it put cop skin to you, if you are rank 1 then it put skin from cop that drive motorbike, if you are rank 2 it put skin from normal cop, if you are rank 3 it put Sheriff skin, if you are rank 4 it put swat skin, and for rank 5 and higher it put special agent skin.
....... and lot more
