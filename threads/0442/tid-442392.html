make 3 islands, one residential, one commercial, one industrial.

place the islands like this : R C I
have residential be farthest away from industrial, and havee commercial in the middle

on the residential island, have small apt. buildings close to commercial area and as you move farther away, increase the sizes of your houses to, townhouses, small houses medium houses, large houses and mansions.

on the commercial island have the tallest buildings in the center, have some apartment buildings near the residential island but make them larger than the ones on the residential island >10 floors. have a large park in the upper middle part of the city and have smaller parks scattered throughout. make office buildings in the middle and right side near the industrial area.

the industrial area should have factories, oil refineries, power plants, junkyards...etc.
this should be the dirtiest area of the map.

also make it so you can go inside of most buildings.
