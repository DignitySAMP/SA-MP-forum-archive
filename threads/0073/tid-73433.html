- Cars
- Obviously AWESOME Interior

Commands
/ClubCmds - Shows the commands below
/Club - Admins only (to tele to the club)
/Waiter - To waiter (use by the room by the entrance)
/Waiterexit - To exit waitering (go to the room door)
/ExitClub - Exit the club (by the entrance doors)
