Go to your firewall.
Then Exceptions and in that Allow sa-mp server.exe to send the data from your computer and receive data from out. Though it doesn't block your ports or what ever.

In server.cfg

Change
announce to 1
query to 1
lanmode to 0

Then launch sa-mp server.exe.
