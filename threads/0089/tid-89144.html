[b]Xtreme Clothing System
This is my first FS
What is it:
  • It's a filterscript where you can change your skin by simply typing the skin's name
  • You can find/choose your skin in few seconds instead of trying to find it in the Player Skin Choosing on login
  • To test it, you can come to my server ''Xtreme'' IP: 90.157.146.196:6666

Features:
  • To get a list of all the skins all you have to do is type /clothes
  • There are 1. Main characters - 2. Gangmembers - 3. Publicservices - 4. Cj Girlfriends - 5. Random skins - 6. Strippers - 7. Funny skins
  • 1. Main characters has all the Main Characters in SP, except CJ(bugged the FS) and the ones that are not for Sa:Mp
  • 2. Gang Members havs Grove, Ballas, Vagos, Mafia, Bikers, Triads, Aztecas and Rifas
  • 3. Cj Girlfriends has Denise, Barbara, Katie, Millie, Helena, Michelle
  • 4. Public Services has Police Skins, Fbi, Guards, Army, Medics, Firefighters and Swat.
  • 5. Random skins has 31 random popular skins
  • 6. Strippers has all 15 stripper skins
  • 7. Funny Skins has 14 funny skins
  • When you change a skin, there is a welcome message. Example: You change to a Scientist and you get ''I don't think we really landed on the moon..''
How to change the skin:
  • To get a list of all clothes, type /clothes
  • You will get /maincharacters, /gangmembers, /publicservices, /cjgirlfriends, /randomskins, /strippers, /funnyskins
  • In /maincharacters you will get something like | /ryder, /sweet, /bigsmoke, /claude, /catalina, /jizzy, /maddog, /paul, /cesar, /maccer, /tenpenny | etc.
  • In /gang Members you will get something like | /grove1, /grove2, /grove3, /ballas1, /ballas2, /ballas3, /mafia1 | etc
  • In /cjgirlfriends you will get something like | /girl1, /girl2, /girl3, /girl4, /girl5, /girl6 |
  • In /publicservices you will get something like | /guard1, /guard2, /army, /swat, /cop1, /cop2, /cop3, /cop4 | etc.
  • In /randomskins you will get something like | /random1, /random2, /random3, /random4, /random5 | etc.
  • In /strippers you will get something like | /stripper1, /stripper2, /stripper3, /stripper4, /stripper5 | etc
  • In /funnyskins you will get something like | /funnyskin1, /funnyskin2, /funnyskin3, /funnyskin4, /funnyskin5 | etc
Download:

Download 1: .rar file ( .pwn + .amx )



Video:

Xtreme Stunt Server - Clothing System


How to install:

- Download the .rar or download the .pwn and compile (it should compile with no errors)
- After you have unrared/compiled the file, you will get X_Clothing.pwn and X_Clothing.amx
- Place the .pwn and .amx in your Filterscript map ( if you have more FS maps, put in all )
- Open your server.cfg and at Filterscripts, put X_Clothing
- Save, restart the server and voila

Other:
- Don't delete the credits aka my name. Even if you modify or do anything with it, don't delete my name!
- This is my first FS, I've been scripting for like a week.
- This is very good if you're making a video, since you can change skins very fast.

Hope you like it, comments are welcome !
