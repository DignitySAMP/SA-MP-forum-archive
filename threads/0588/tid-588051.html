Just download WinDbg from here
And use this as symbolpath
Code:
SRV*C:\dev\symbols*http://msdl.microsoft.com/download/symbols
Then open crash dumps from "C:\Windows\Minidump"

If you get bsod that complain about ntoskrnl.exe often, most causes is bad ram or with wrong ram timings in bios, horrible usb drivers and bad disk controller. Overclocked your pc? Then remove your oc

Edit: If your computer just restarts without showing BSOD, then your PSU need to be replaced
