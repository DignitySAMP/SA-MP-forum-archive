Vehicle Trampoline

Introduction -
Howdy! This Filterscript, being my first one, is a simple filterscript comprising of a Vehicle Trampoline and an AutoMatic Vehicle Repair System.
The map, which is built by me, is located somewhere near Abandoned airport. Whenever a vehicle falls from the platform (from where you jump) and hits the ground, it bounces back as if it just hit a trampoline. This map/fs was built basically to have fun and enjoy ourselves. Cheers.

Usage -

1. Copy and Paste the Trampoline.amx file to your filterscripts folder
2. Open server.cfg and write 'Trampoline' in front of 'Filterscripts'
3. Goto your server and type '/tramp', and you'll be teleported to the Vehicle Trampoline
4. Get inside a vehicle, drive it into the hole, Enjoy.
