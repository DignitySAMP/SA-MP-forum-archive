Info

What this does is add a clan training facility somewhere in the countryside, their is 3 parts to the facility and the parts are called The Box - a small area where street fighting is practiced, The Warzone - a patch of field that consists of some sandbags and a sniper post to help practice your shooting accuracy and last but not least The King of the hill area - this is a when your aim is to be on top of the car heap and battle to stay on top, enjoy

Commands

/ctftele - This command teleports you to the clan training facility.
/ctfhelp - This command makes this menu appear.
/clearchat - This command clears the chatbox.
/fsinfo - This command gives you the important information about this filterscript.
/weaps - This command displays a list of weapons.
