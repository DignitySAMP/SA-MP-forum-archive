Ok, but streamer is not used to see an unlimited number of objects?
SA:MP has a limit of objects that can be active at a single time. In order to get rid of this limit, the streamer only shows the objects a player is close to. But it's still limited to the SA:MP limits of available Objects. So be aware of that when you're mapping something, that you don't put 2000 objects in the same small area.
