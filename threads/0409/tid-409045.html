Decas City - island for RP/RL servers

This map is a complete new Island with roads, buildings and stuff that are connected to the east side of Los Santos with a bridge. The roads and buildings fit to each other, so they are connected properly and dont float. (Total Objects: 445)

(The video shows an older version of the map, thats because you dont see the docks. And sorry for the quality)
[ame]http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3AXCVDKvBeM[/ame]


It contains
  • 24/7 Shop
  • Truck Terminal
  • City Hall
  • Motel
  • Burger Shot
  • Disco
  • Parking
  • Docks
  • Ship
  • Ammunation
  • Construction Building
  • Gas Station
  • very small hospital
  • opportunity for boats or swimming players to get to the island
