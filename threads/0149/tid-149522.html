Meh, I made this simple script out of total boredom.

What is it?
It's a simple script that let's you chase cows without any results.

Then what should I use it for, if it's useless anyways?
You shouldn't use it, unless:
You're a cat.
You're a total idiot.
You ain't got nothing better to do.

I'm a total idiotic cat and I ain't got anything better to do, where CanIHazDownload?
Download link is totally down. Feel free to mirror it.

BTW, what does it look like?
Pictures are here:

Chasing a cowly cow (in mid-air though)


Use it as a shield!


Any tips?
Don't use it with larger vehicles (Aircraft) and don't use it when you're having high ping/packet loss.

The boring stuff that nobody cares about
Distance from you to cow increases if you speed up, so the chance to crash at high speeds slows down (Only in-vehicle, not on-foot)
The script isn't perfect at all, it looks totally buggy and uses OnPlayerUpdate. There probably is a better way, but I didn't had any ideas what could be faster.


Download: Pastebin

PS: Just cause I'm driving a plane it doesn't means it's meant to do so. Cows don't fly right? So don't teach them to .

PPS: Using high-speed vehicles, like an infernus or a bullet, increases the chance to crash.
