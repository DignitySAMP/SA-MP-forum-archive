MODERN APARTMENT

I have decided to share this interior with the community as I do not plan on using it in any of my projects. The apartment is located on top of a skyscraper in San Fierro, providing you with the stunning view and modern design. The interior does not have any visual bugs (no flickering, no non-solid objects, no texture errors and it's placed on a solid GTA default object what allows spawning in the interior)

This interior is an "inside" interior (you have the ability to look at the street from the windows), however you do not see the actual players or surrounding because the interior is located in a different virtual world.

Notice; when adding the code to your script, change the interior ID to 0 (it must always be 0, if it's not 0 you won't be able to look outside), but the virtual world can be any number bigger than 1. (1+)

EDIT: Regarding the "worldid" problem, all you have to do is add the following:
Code:
new worldid;
