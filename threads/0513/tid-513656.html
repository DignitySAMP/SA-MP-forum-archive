Introduction




Now this filterscript is updated to a newer version (version 2).I have edited and added few commands.Fixed bugs and made it more accurate .





Commands





/groupdive -Only Players present near you ,starts skydiving with you.
/diveall -All players connected to your server will skydives along with you.
/altimeter -Altimeter. Displays the altitude .





Download





GroupDive.amx

GroupDive.pwn





How to Add Filterscripts to your server ?





Just copy both the files and paste it under filterscripts folder in your server directory.
Then open your server.cfg ,there you will see a row of filterscripts ,and then type GroupDive beside that.
