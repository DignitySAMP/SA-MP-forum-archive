If you purchased a server at a host which can be found in the List of Server Hosts, you can leave your review about this host.
Biased reviews are not allowed, so please do not place a review if you're the owner or employee of one of the hosts.

Just to clarify, every host is allowed.

If you have a host from KingJ, x-serverz etc, feel free to post!
It helps other people choose the right host for their server.

More clarification, this topic is not for discussion.
You can place one review, not discuss other reviews or ratings.
If you have a complaint, pm me rather than replying to this topic.


For every review, please use this format:
Code:
Host: Name of the host
Slots: Amount of slots
IP/Port: The IP and port of your server
Purchased: Date when you purchased it

Rate support: (1-10) Rating of their support, 10 = excellent, 1 = horrible
Rate pricing: (1-10) Rating of their pricing, 10 = excellent, 1 = horrible
Rate performance: (1-10) Rating of your server performance, 10 = excellent, 1 = horrible
Rate ordering: (1-10) Rating of their ordering process, 10 = excellent, 1 = horrible
Rate control panel: (1-10) Rating of their control panel, 10 = excellent, 1 = horrible

Things that make this host amazing: A few things that makes this host good.
Things that make this host horrible: A few things that make this host bad.
Will you renew: Will you keep your server?
Moving to another host: If not, which host are you moving to?

Comments:
Any comments regarding your review, the host, etc.
