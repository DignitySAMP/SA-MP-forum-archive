Quote:
Originally Posted by =WoR=Varth

Admin rules and Admin command.
And uuhh.. the admin it self.
What..?

An admin should be:

• Responsible - They should set an example to players on how to behave and follow the rules at all times.
• Mature - they shouldn't act like a 5 year old, kicking people that 'annoy' them for example.
• Experienced - it helps if they have experience. If it's their first time being an admin they may feel 'worried' to kick/ban people, but let them know it's what they do.
• Helpful - They should ALWAYS help new players.
• Committed - They should be committed to the job. They should want to be an admin to help the server, not for the power.
• Respectful - They should respect higher level staff and of course the owner.
• Fluent Language - It's no good having an admin that doesn't understand what 'How do you earn money?' means.
• Informed - No good having an admin that doesn't know all the commands. This one down to you to inform them.

Wow, I never planned on writing that much...
