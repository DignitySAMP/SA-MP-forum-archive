H-Bender System 2.0

AVATAR SYSTEM ON SAMP !! CONTROL YOUR FAVORITE ELEMENT !
What the advatages?
Code:
Fire :
1. You can fly !
2. if you shoot another player and he is not a fire bender, he will get burn !
rock :
1. You can spawn 15 rock ! (y to spawn, N to delete)
2. If you shoot another player and he is not a rock bender, he will hit the rock ! and fall
wind:
1. You can jump very high (Press Y)
2. If you shoot another player and he is not a wind bender, he will fall for 2 minutes
3. Run Faster (More fast if there is some difrent Z cordinate)
CMD:
Version 1.0
Code:
/element = Choose element
/forgetelement = Forget mastered element
