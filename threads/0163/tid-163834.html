I thought about how annoying the construction sites are in real life. It made me think for about 15 minutes before I wanted to create this map. It features one side of the road being blocked and the other side divided into 2 different ways with cones in the middle. It has about 10 vehicles, around 100 objects, and a few detour signs and road blocks.

You can always add this map to your own server as the "Construction Man" spawn. I know RolePlay servers use these kinds of things, so go ahead. Please do not remove the credits on the FS. It is my own creation and I do not want anyone else to take it.

As of now, the map is still WIP (Work In Progress). I will update it soon enough. I want it to look as realistic as possible.

There is a streamed version and a non-streamed version of the map.

Look for my future maps to be added.
