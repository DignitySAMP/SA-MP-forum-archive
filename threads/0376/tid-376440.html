Story

Hay guys, heres dafuq and today
I'm gonna show you guys, how to
put maping to your server.
Filterscript version!

Tutorial

1.) Go and map something in MTA Mapeditor,
then save it and find it from your computer.
Start - Program Files - MTA - Server - Mods - Deathmatch - Resources - Your map name

2.) Now go to ConvertFFS and convert your MAPINGNAME.map.

3. Once that is done goto your SERVER Folder and open
Desktop - Server Folder - Pawno - Pawno.exe
Then paste this to your Pawno, and change my maping codes with yours.

4. Open your Server.cfg and add your Filterscript name to that live. Like this:
filterscripts ls_elevator lights hair radio ********En seatbelt vilkur MAPINGNAME

5. Enjoy.
