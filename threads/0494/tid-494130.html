This is a role-play script which is for beginner's at scripting!

I give you all permission to edit this script as it is not finished yet and this is just a starter for people to continue with,

The features:

Commands for players:
/help
/ooc, /o
/newb, /n
/report
/pm
/am
/me
/do
/s
/b
/credits
/createbusiness - They have to buy the business them selves when created
/approvehouse - they buy the house interior and exterior (PickupModel)
/admins


Commands For Admins:

Level one admin: /mute /kick /ooc /report /pm /ban /getid /approvehouse /createbusiness
Level two Admin: /kick /ban /ooc /mute /report /pm /ban /report /gotoid /am /getid /togglenewb
Level three Admin: /kick /ban /pm /am /gotoid /goto /spec /report /god /mute /ooc /newb /toggleooc /getid /togglenewb
Level four Admin: /kick /ban /am /pm /gotoid /goto /spec /report /god /mute /ooc /newb /toggleooc /kill /getid /togglenewb
Level Five Admin: All of the commands level four has but also you could wish to let them have more!
Level Six Admin: Every command + /makeadmin
Level Seven Admin: Every Command + /makeadmin and Rcon


This is not a finished script as i said it will be finished and released over time!


Please +REP Me i took my time out of this to make this starter for people and it will be finished in a couple of months or so.

Thank you i hope you liked it because i made it on my own except i had a little help from two people - Blademaster680 and my other friend who is called BlackBomb on here they did a bit of adding playerPos and registering with a few commands

Millenium Roleplay!
