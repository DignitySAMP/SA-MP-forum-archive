MJ's GM is created for beginner/old/good

Current Version:

v1.1

Code:
- Total commands (19)
- /getjetpack - You can eject from your JetPack
  by pressing Left Button while flying.
- /me - You can create a fun things with /me but
  don't abuse this command to much
- /chat - Want to chat with your playmate? you
  can use our MJ's Chat!
- /changecolor - Want to change your color? just
  use /changecolor. the server will automatic
  select a color for you. (Random)
- /home - you will teleport on MJ's GM Default
  Spawnpoint
- /teleport - you will see all available teleport
  commands (DIALOG_STYLE_LIST)
- /mjshop - want to buy score/food/drink/cigarrete?
  well use our /mjshop!
- /cheats - includes some of Single Player Cheat of
  GTA SA. You must have 10,000 score to use cheats.
- /aezakmi - You cannot have wanted star anymore.
- /fullclip - Your weapon's ammo will not decrease
  anymore
- /baguvix - Just like in Single Player when players
  shoot you, you will not die. but becareful if they
  shoot you with Minigun/RPG or any powerful weapons
  you will die!
- /hesoyam - Just like in Single Player you will get 100%
  Health and Armour + 2500000 Money
- /rules - the rules of MJ's GM
- /lock - lock's your vehicle except Motor/Bike vehicles
- /reject - eject you from your vehicle/jetpack
- /checkmyid - check's your id
- /suicide - kills your self
- /afk - gonna eat/going out for minutes use AFK no one will
  gonna kill you with your unlimited Health!
- /help - shows all available command
