Regarding BF4 bugs...

DICE suspended development on future DLC's until they hammer out the issues with the core game. There have been a number of patches recently that address the most major game-breaking bugs. Most of the crashes have been solved but there are some other bugs that some people are experiencing such as "netcode" bugs. Some people have these issues, others do not. I suppose it depends on the end-users software and hardware configuration, but the most common issues that affected everybody has been fixed to the best of my knowledge.
