Yeah, the above is right. You would need at least 5MBps "upload" speed (note: not download speeds, they are different.)

You also need to understand, if you ever had such an upload speed, you would need to only dedicate it to the server, and nothing else. Like, useing it for your own use is not acceptable. Going onto MSN, BitTorrent, Porn (lol) won't work becuase it will clog up your internet pipe line with downloading infomation.

So you will need a dedicated PC + a dedicated 3Mbps upstream. That also means you can't connect other PC's to the connection.

Hope this helps.
