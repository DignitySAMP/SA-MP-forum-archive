You can't bind your external IP address to the sa-mp server, its not registered to any interfaces on your computer. Your router uses overloaded NAT (PAT) to make multiple LAN connections across one WAN connection.

You need to bind your server to your local IP (which it will do automatically), and then configure your router to forward traffic to 192.168.0.102 on UDP port 7777. There are plenty of port forwarding guides out there, but you cannot use the bind directive in the server config in that way.
