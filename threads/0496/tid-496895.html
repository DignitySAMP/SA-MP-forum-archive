Shit-Yourself-Rich!

In an attempt to recreate a world-famous well known Dutch/Belgian past-time activity, I present to you my version of 'Shit Yourself Rich' (known in Dutch/Flemish as 'perceelpoepen/schijten'). The rules are simple: You place a bet, then drive your flower pot to where you think/hope the cow will take a dump. After a few minutes, the game commences and the cow is let loose. If you're lucky, it takes a shit next to your marker and you'll win grand prize money. The further away the cow takes its shit, the less amount of money you'll get (or none at all).

..oh and yes it doesn't really take a shit but rather just explodes. Mainly did this because it was easier to script and because it looks more awesome

Location
This minigame can be found at 'The Farm', a region in San Andreas famous for its sales of honey, and incest porn actors.



Download
