Hi guys,

Today is the day. Five years ago SA-MP Team released SA-MP 0.1. Some of you may remeber first beta test back in the 2006, some may not (shame on you that you didn't know about it!). SA-MP 0.1 was realesed on 10th of May 2006. SA-MP itself was announced on 3rd of April 2005. That's six year of development, six year of playing and six years of having fun with San Andreas.

SA-MP has great community, there's about 20 000 people from all over the world playing SA-MP at this moment. That hell of a lot! It only shows how we all appreciate the work you (SA-MP Team) are doing.

Thank you for all of you guys who's still playing GTA San Andreas and for you SA-MP Team for your time spend on this project. I also want to thank you Rockstar Games for releasing a great game which certainly is GTA San Andreas. I want to wish you all good and hope we will see each other next year and so on...

Let's go back to the 2008 and watch this cool video made by Damian - The making of SA-MP 0.2.5 preview (Damian Version).

Feel free to post your thoughts. Cheers
