RMPG.inc
Start and Informations
a simple portal gun script based on Rick & Morty animated TvShow. People who watch this show know what I'm talking about.
basically it has no unique feature comparing to other portal gun scripts , the unique thing about this is the gun and auto rotate the portal to face it player , so you can make portal under your self and jump into it.

Rick And Morty's version :



SAMP Version



Video:

Click On Meh -_-

Functions:

PHP Code:
GivePlayerPortalGun(playerid); 
Things you need:

Streamer - Plugin Thread > https://forum.sa-mp.com/showthread.php?tid=102865

USAGE:
After giving the player portal gun , by firing you can create green portals which is the first/entering portal
then if you hold KEY_WALK (LAlt) then fire , you'll create second/exiting portal wich is blue !
