A few questions to ask first.

What are your system specifications ?
What operating system are you using ?
What bit is the operating system ?

You may need to update your graphics card using "Device Manager". Changing your resolution may help but I don't think that's necessary. Open Grand Theft Auto, press "ESC", Options, Display Setup, Advanced. Turn the Frame Limiter Off, Visual FX Quality to low and leave your resolution as it is. Quit the game and open it up again and try that. Hope this helps.
