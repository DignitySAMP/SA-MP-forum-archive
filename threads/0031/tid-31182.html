Hello Everyone thisis my new GameMode its a Deathmatch based on Las Venturas it has lots of things to do. i will explain
I have built in some of my Favorite FS's and Include's into this script so the 20% of the credits go out to the makers of the FS's

Credits:
Me for making it

Features:
To get money in the server there are a few ways to do it

1. Go to the pirate ship
2. if your a citizen, you can sneak into the PoliceStation and steal packs of cash
3. Win reaction Tests

When you log in it displays this message " ** has joined the server. Name: %s IP: %d ID: %d Ping: %d...

It has 30 levels.. The levels go up by how many people you kill( credits to >=Sandra=< ) and the higher your level is the better stuff you can do for example Better weapons and spawnmoney etc...

When you try to sneak into the PoliceDepartment there are gates surrounding the entrance, you would probebly think it is impossible to get in, but it isn't i have made the gates open every 35 seconds or so and then close again.

Minigames. this Gamemode features 5 DM minigames and they all set you to a virtual world and all have boundries on them.
They are:

UnderwaterDM
Knockout
Rocket
Minigun
DMisland.


106 Random Spawns for Citizens.
6 Random spawns for Police.
when doing the minigames there are random spawms on them aswell.
when trying to sneak into the police station to earn money there will be random money pickups in there.

Advertise messages like for example "try /s and /l, type /s to save your position, then type /l to go back to it!"

it also features Hotkeys which is when you press a button instead of typing a command
i have added hotkeys on vehicles
such as:

press 8 to repair your vehicle. cost $400
press 2 to give your vehicle nos(vehicle must be compatible with transfender) cost $200

a weapons menu(only for the level 20) which features 20 or somthing weapons not including minigun or a rocket launcher. also with alot of other commands for levels.

Installation:
1. Download the thing

2. place the includes into your pawno/include folder

3. Create a folder called PlayerFiles with exact capitals and place it into your scriptfiles folder

4.Place the pwn and amx into your Gamemodes

5.put in your server.CFG lvdm2

6.Go onto your server and play.


Making yourself Level 30:

1. go onto your server. spawn your player

2. disconnect

3. go into your scriptfiles/PlayerFiles folder and click on your name and make the level 29 (30 will not work because where starting from 0)

4. Save settings and go back on and play.
