Hi.
Sorry for my english.

I have been playing in SA-MP since 0.1 version. And I have been sometimes administrator on several stunt servers.

First: thank you very much for such an wonderful multiplayer game. You guys made my life a little better

Second: the problems.
We all know them, they were discussed in many threads (with a lot of posts).
But, since other games have better solutions for them, it means there could be a solution for SA-MP too.

1. The lag.
Unfortunately, 0.3z didn't solve it like other games did. In some cases the results are weird.
I'm not a big fan of the lag shot, on the contrary, I was happy when I read "lag free shot". But now I'm not so happy...
Some servers even decided not to use it.
I know it's a little unusual but how about implementing the option to switch the mode on/off into the client?
It's not impossible.
Yes, you'll have to do some work to implement it...

2. Bigger problem: security.
Many are saying it's (only) up to the scripter and admins to protect the server against hackers.
For the most part they are right. But it's like defending Windows with a security suite, instead of using Linux.
The scripts are getting better, the admins gain experience, but, still, it's like nothing is improved. Every day endless amount of hackers harassing/killing good players and getting banned, changing IP and name, next day again. And again, and again...
And this is the best case scenario. Sometimes it's not so easy to detect them, sometimes the admins can't be online or are too few for a couple hundred players.
How about modifying the SA-MP client so it won't be so easy to access the game memory or the game functions with other applications?
This is just one idea. I'm sure there are other things that can be improved to help servers with security.

Most players in DM areas/servers are hackers and laggers - because they "live" longer.
That's sad

I hope you'll do something about these problems.
Thank you.

Best regards
