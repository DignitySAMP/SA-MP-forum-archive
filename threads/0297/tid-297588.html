[MV]_Christmas
Project on github
Intro:

My first filterscript ... about christmas and new-year. There are usefull stuff init, so enjoy it.

Features:
  • A moving Snowman textdraw by Zh3r0 (edited code)
  • New Year Countdown by Meta (edited code)
  • Snowball minigame by ShOoBy (edited code)
  • Stocks:
    pawn Code:
    native CreateChristmasTree(type,Float:x, Float:y, Float:z)
    native GiveChristmasHat(playerid,type) 
    native CreateChristmasLights(Float:x, Float:y, Float:z);
  • Commands:
    Code:
    /christmas - All the cmds of this filterscript are here listed
  • Christmas tree\'s
  • Christmas lights
  • ZCMD
  • Christmas songs
  • Custom gta3.img (This takes care of the snow all around GTA SA / SAMP)
