Catch The Circle

V.1.1
Description
This FS is allow you to play minigame with clickable textdraw. This FS will spawn clickable random circle each 1 second, but if player already touch the circle before 1 second it automatically spawn new random circle. The score will increase when you touch the circle, but if you don't touch the circle or hit the white background Your live will decrease. If your live is 0 the game is over . And i use Y_ini to save the highest score and the player who made new record. After the player stop the game or game over, player will get bonus money. It depend with player score, with formula score x 100 = bonus money (you can change it).

Command
/start [You can also change it]

How To Install ?
First : Download the circle2.pwn and circle2.amx
Second : Add it to your Filterscript folder
Third : Add circle to your server.cfg beside the filterscript
Fourth : Start your server
Fifth : Enjoy the script !

How To Play ?
First : type /start
Second : choose play (if you want to play)
Third : choose play single player or duel(if you choose duel just follow the dialog)
Fourth : If you are boring and you still have more than 0 live you can choose stop(give up on duel)
Fifth : Choose Exit to close the game and choose play if you want to play again
