Josh's RP BunnyHopStop System



Whats is this?
This is a very simple system that stops bunny hopping, RP'ly!

How does it work?
If they attempt to bunny hop, they will "RP'ly" trip, and fall down. This is very useful for RP server's, as mine has this. This system uses 2 timers, one to set the animation, and one spam timer. The current setting is for 3 presses of the jump key(Left Shift) within 3 seconds. You can change this at any time. If they press the key once or twice in more than three seconds, they will not fall over. Again, you can change how many times they can press the jump key within how ever long you want.

Why is this one better than other ones?
Because, this one makes you "RP'ly" trip, and fall over, not sending you a message saying "dont bunny hop", or something of that nature, it enacts RP.
