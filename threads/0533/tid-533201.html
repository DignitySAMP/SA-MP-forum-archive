pawn Code:
native CreateDynamicObject(modelid, Float:x, Float:y, Float:z, Float:rx, Float:ry, Float:rz, worldid = -1, interiorid = -1, playerid = -1, Float:streamdistance = 200.0, Float:drawdistance = 0.0);
This is definition of CreateDynamicObject. For a computer 0 and 0.0 are two entirely diffrent things (IEEE-754). As PAWN doesn't convert types internally, you have to specify exactly what you want.

So, for example:
pawn Code:
tmpobjid = CreateDynamicObject(3607, 420.546, -965.601, 96.813, 359.858, 0.000, 274.426, 300.000, 300.000);
You are missing worldid and interiorid variables (I assume you want streamdistance and drawdistance). So, just add:
pawn Code:
tmpobjid = CreateDynamicObject(3607, 420.546, -965.601, 96.813, 359.858, 0.000, 274.426, -1, -1, 300.000, 300.000);
(-1 for virtual worldss and interiors)
