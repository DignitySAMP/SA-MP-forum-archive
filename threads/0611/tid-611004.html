The reason SA-MP does nothing against scripts being leaked is quite simple, it is not their business. If a script gets released here, you can report it and it will be removed, but other than that they have nothing to do with it. If you want it to be taken down, you will have to get in touch with whoever is hosting their server.

Why is hosting expensive? Well, you have to rent hardware from companies. Some of the larger companies even have to pay a lot (and I mean A LOT) of money for things such as DDoS protection, as DDoS attacks are very annoying attacks. They are not as easy to block as you may think.

It seems like you know very little of what you talk about. Not only about the DDoS attacks, but also about the hosted tab listings. The limited supply is to keep it somewhat exclusive. This debunks your theory about it all being one big money conspiracy. If that was the case, there would not be a limit.
