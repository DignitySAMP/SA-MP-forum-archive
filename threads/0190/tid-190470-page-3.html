Well you can remove at dcmd_lightfw, the line:
Code:
if(firecrackertime[playerid] == 0)

etc.
You would be able to use multiple firework at a time. >BUT!< Because you will overwrite the value's
of the CreateObject, the previous firework wont explode/get destroyed/get moved. because of that, its not a smart idea to remove those lines.
