Well, I don't know how many of us already know this, ****** has launched its Get Your Business Online (GYBO) Service worldwide, which offers free domain name (Country based) & free hosting for a specified period (Country based). This scheme aims at providing these services to Small & Medium Businesses/Enterprises (SMBs/SMEs).

Hope you find it useful.

Services offered under GYBO:
  • Free Domain Name
  • Free Hosting
  • Adwords Credit
  • Free email addresses
  • Free online tools & resources
  • Additional services like e-commerce, etc
