It's because you don't escape your backslashes, i.e.:
pawn Code:
printf("\.");
Is wrong.

Correct way:
pawn Code:
printf("\\.");
OR, if you don't want to escape all backslashes, you can do:
pawn Code:
printf(\"\.");
Note that you won't be able to use newline characters and such (\n).
