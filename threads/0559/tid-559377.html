Chinatown Gangwar

Story behind it:
Well, After Bullworth and Chinatown. I had nothing to do with the host. So i decided to make a GangWar server. Because in that space between Bullworth Academy and GangWar. I was playing alot of Boarderland 1 and 2. So im got obsessed with it. I said to myself. Why not have a DM server with features like it. Yes. There you have it now.
Its not much but im telling you. Its really fun to play lol Im not doing this for rep or anything. Releasing all these gamemodes (Some kids think that). Im just sharing my joy through out these script that what i like to do. Now i feel even more better sharing this with all of you.


Features:
- Healing system
- Shop System
- Anti Spawn Kill
- Anti Team Kill
- Sound and Damage on hit
- Bullet mode system (Fire, Burning, Explosive, Electric and ooooo Nuke bullet)
- Shield mode system (To avoid bullets modes)
- Amazing MAP
- 8+ Different choices of class.
Much more! play it with your friends and burn them
