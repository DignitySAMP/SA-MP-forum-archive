Custom Quick Poll
What Is It?
It's a poll maker that can be used in-game by an admin, and voted on by the players
What Does It Feature?
Up to 5 customizable poll answers (more with easy modification)
Customizable poll subject
Bug free!
Extremely easy to use
Commands
/StartPoll -- Admin Only, Takes you through the process of creating a poll
/Cancel -- Poll Starter Only, Cancels the poll early
/Vote -- Opens the dialog to vote
/PollHelp -- Tells you the commands
