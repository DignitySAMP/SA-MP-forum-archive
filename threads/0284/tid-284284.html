SA-MP is free to play, all you need is the GTA:SA Game installed on your computer. If you have a Game Controller for your PC, then it'll work for GTA:SA too.

1.) Install GTA:SA Game on your computer
2.) Install SA-MP Client on your computer (Free)
3.) Install/Hookup your Game Controller
4.) Run the SAMP Client, browse the servers in Internet List or Hosted List
5.) Simply connect & start playing, use the 'T' key to chat with the other players

Note: Each server is diffirent, Most Roleplay type servers require a First_Last Name Format for your nickname and also require you to register & play a certain way. But most DM/TDM servers only enforce the basic rules such as no Cheating or Hacking & whatnot. Welcome to the SA-MP Community though

EDIT: The Game Controller you linked in your post would work for GTA:SA
