Its just the way it works in pawn, you cant create the same function twice in one gamemode without getting the "symbol already defined" error.

The only way around it is to either rename the function (if it is a custom one) or merge them together (if it is a pre-defined one such as OnPlayerKeyStateChange()).

The only things I would suggest to you is to merge the functions together and to clean your script up a bit to make it more easier to read.
