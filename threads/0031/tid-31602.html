My First Map/Filterscript ,

Info & Details About the Filterscript
¤ A Basic city just near the docks of Los Santos, (For easy findings, Use the tele command or head forward from the Big ferry boat thing..)
¤ It has 150 Objects exactly,
¤ I Did it to try and make a small city based on Vice City,
¤ Didn't Take too long,
¤ Ive tested it, It works fine, Same with the commands.

Features
¤ Docks / Harbour
¤ A Fully working beach with no excess parts
¤ A Small Pier (With a Tropic Boat at the end)
¤ A Hospital (Taken from All Saints, Los Santos)
¤ A Mini Police Station (Originally a Court house, )
¤ A Fully working waterfall with no excess parts (Except maybe the water comes out too much, as with the rocks)
¤ 4 REAL Houses (In The original game there houses, )
¤ A Stunt Ship (Don't Post about its errors, i know some parts are invisible and climbable, Edit this if you want)
¤ Some Stunt ramps around the city
¤ Recolored Police vehicles and Ambulances
¤ More...

Commands
¤ /sschelp - Brings up the menu of commands
¤ /ssctele - Teleports you to the island's main point, (AKA the point i started making from, )
¤ /sscteleroof - Teleports you to one of the roofs in the city, This one has a white and blue maverick on it,
¤ - I Did experience some problems with the teleport commands, there was commands for each roof, But even though i checked them countless times that they wern't the same coordinates, They all lead to the same roof, Any problems read the Bugs section of this post.
