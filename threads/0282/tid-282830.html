Helloooo. Some of you may remember a few years ago, I did SA:MP Radio (If you don't remember, the original thread is here), it later closed due to low listener count.

However, with SA:MP 0.3d revealed, and now having integrated IceCast streaming I believe now is as good a time as as any to dust off the licence, renew it, and try again.

This time around, SA:MP Radio is planned to be heavily IRC integrated, with requesting and DJs operating out of IRC. Naturally, we're going to need DJs, lots of them.


So this is for you guys. Do you have several GB or higher of purchased completely legal music? Do you orgasm to the sound of your own voice, and think others need to do the same? If you have a microphone, and plenty of legal music you can send down the many tubes of the internet, then private message me for details. You will also need broadcasting software, again, purchased if its not a free program. I use SAM Broadcaster (It's not free).

Remember, SA:MP Radio was, and will again be run as a legal licenced radio station. Everything must be legit and above board. So don't apply if your music is all downloaded. (Ripped from CDs you purchased is fine).

I look forward to hearing from you all, and lets see, maybe this time we'll get going, and become a united voice accross SA:MP.

- Redirect Left.
