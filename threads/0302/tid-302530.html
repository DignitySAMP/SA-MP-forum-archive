This is how you read a hexadecimal color

0xRRGGBBAA

RR = Amount of RED, from 00 to FF
GG = Amount of GREEN, from 00 to FF
BB = Amount of BLUE, from 00 to FF
AA = Amount of opacity, 00 being totally transparent, FF being not transparent at all.

Hope that helped.
