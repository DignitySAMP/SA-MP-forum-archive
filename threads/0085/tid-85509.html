I thought i'd start a caption competition for a bit of fun. Basically, I will post an image and the caption that I judge to be the best will win... something (at this point, probably just a congratulations and a mention in this topic. People who consistently win might win something more, we shall see!). Only one picture will be active at a time and please only post captions, no comments, comments about other's captions etc. Once I have posted a new picture and announced a winner for it, no more captions for that picture can be posted.

If you have a picture you wish to suggest for the competition, please feel free to PM me. The pictures should be related to GTA or SA-MP.

In your reply, state the number of the picture you are replying to (e.g 12) and then your caption. You may post more than 1 caption, but can can make only one post per picture (so put all your captions for a certain picture on the same post)

Finally, if you have no idea what a caption competition is, see this for an example.
