Quote:
Originally Posted by wallee

could somebody explain the difference between the regular streaming and chunk streaming?
In general chunk streaming means that multiple Items in one smaller area will be handled as a Group, which makes streaming more efficient (eg. the streamer doesn't have to check 20 Items that are in one location, but just streams those 20 Items at once if someone is in the vicinity of the whole group (chunk)).
