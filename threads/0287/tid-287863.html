hey its easy to upload it, You can just put a link with the code or put it on a website like speedyshare then copy the download link and put it on here.

You will need to use a converter:

http://www.convertffs.com/

THat converter converts vehicles and objects from mta to samp.

For your map you want to goto your programs file/MTA San Andreas/Server/Mods/Deathmatch/resources/( what you saved the file as)

Then open the .map in pawno

Copy and paste all of it in the conevert bit where it says paste here.

then click convert.

open a new pawno file and copy and paste the new converted file into the pawno you just opened and save it as something.

Then upload it to a websit:

www.speedyshare.com


Once uploaded put the link on your page and then people can use it.

Hope this helps
