Hello everyone i'm back with another release after a very long time, so i decided to keep shit simple and create a speedo meter (KM/H) in 3DTextLabels so lets get started with it.

Introduction:
It detects player state and creates 3DTextLabel when you enter the car and attaches it to your vehicle and uses OnPlayerUpdate to update the label and detect player speed.
By default the color of speedo meter is set to orange.
Command used to change the color of speedo meter: /Speedocolor
There are 12 different colors to choose from in-game to change color of your speedo meter! [Dynamic]
