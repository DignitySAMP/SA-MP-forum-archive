Hello everyone,

Today I will be publishing my first gamemode, I started working on it like 3 days ago, and finally I am done with what I am planning to do.
The script is a TDM shipfight, based on 3 teams, with some small features.
I am still a new scripter so don't judge me much, this script is for other basic scripters too.


FEATURES:

V1.0:

-3 Teams.
-3D Text label.
-Anti Spawn kill protection.
-Briefcase to refill health and armor from.
-RPG pickup.
-Radio chat.
-Money gained/lost upon kill/death(Excluding /kill).

V1.5:

-Added headshot system!


COMMANDS:
-/st || /switchteam
-/r(radio chat)
-/kill
-/updates
-/rules
-/credits

SOME SCREEN SHOTS:
