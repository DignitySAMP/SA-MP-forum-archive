You can use this function to teleport the player there:
pawn Code:
SetPlayerPos(playerid, Xcoords, Ycoords, Zcoords);
SetPlayerInterior(playerid, InteriorID);
Just replace Xcoords Ycoords and Zcoords with your coordinates that you want him teleported to, replace InteriorID if you want to teleport him to an interior, and you just have to make a function to trigger it ofcourse.

As it seems you may be new to scripting, you can do this with a simple command.


ZCMD:

Code:
CMD:gotopos(playerid, params[])
{
	new interiorid, x, y, z;
	if(!sscanf(params, "dddd", interiorid, x, y, z))
	{
	    SetPlayerPos(playerid, x, y, z);
	    SetPlayerInterior(playerid, interior);
	}
	return 1;
}
