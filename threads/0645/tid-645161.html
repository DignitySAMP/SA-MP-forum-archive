There was another thread about this. It should answer all your questions.
https://forum.sa-mp.com/showthread.php?tid=594341

More specifically: here's the official explanation for why it was archived. It's on the thread I mentioned above.
This happened shortly after the Paris attacks, so.... you're kinda late here, buddy.

Quote:
Originally Posted by SA-MP

This is what happened:

First, the Dutch and French forums are not really needed. There have never been any large Dutch or French SA-MP servers. People from France and Netherlands play on English servers.

We have been removing different language sections for a long time because nobody in the beta team speaks these languages and we find it hard to find active moderators. There is usually not enough moderation or sometimes too much moderation.

The Dutch and French forums were left open since they weren't really hurting anything. In the wake of the terrorist attacks in Paris people started posting stuff about the attacks. There were others using the forum "Contact Us" page to complain about posts political points etc. Many posts were also being deleted without any clear reason why.

It was decided to remove these forums since the SA-MP forum isn't really the place for any of this discussion anyway. The posts containing scripts will be archived and made available later.

If you want to create a general French or Dutch chat topic you can create one in Everything and Nothing.
