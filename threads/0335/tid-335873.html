Create some more space between the menu items so that the text with spaces ("wie ben ik") will not be seen as "wie" "ben" and "ik". Also, give the active element another color than the rest of the menu (preferably the color of the little triangle underneath it). Get the first letter of the menu item a capital letter, shows a little better, or get them all caps.

The little links under the blogposts do not show as links. Make them underlined or another color than the text (also align them properly, you can do that with CSS line-height).

"Calender" is written with a "K" in Dutch

Taken from the little triangles in front of the sidebar items you can expand and contract them. If it is expanded (login form) get the whole block the same color as the header.

The little triangle in the menu does not really have a function. Normally it denotes the menuitem and links it with the page. However, your background is not the same as the menu triangle so it does not really have that function and just sits there.

I would also add a tiny picture before your blogname, dunno why.. looks a little nicer imo
