Frog Mode Filterscript


Always wondered if you can jump like a frog in SA:MP? Well, now you can! Using this small and simple filterscript, your dream to be a frog came true xD



How does this works?


It works very simple.After you connect in the server and spawn, you just type /frogon and that's all! You will be able to jump in air by pressing the Jump Key.In order, to jump more better, i have added some angles.To change the current angle, you must press the Secondary Attack Key.To jump forward, you must press the Fire Key.To turn off the frog mode,you just need to type /frogoff.
