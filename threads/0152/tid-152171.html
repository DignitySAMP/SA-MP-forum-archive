[FS]Mastema's Aircraft Passenger Script 1.0

Enter a plane like any other vehicle as passenger. just that easy

Supported Vehicles are:
*Shamal
*Nevada
*AT-400
*Andromada

Installation
Extract the file you downloaded
Place the extracted files in your filterscripts folder
open server.cfg and add after filterscripts passenger
