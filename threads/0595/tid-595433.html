Fort Ember
San Andreas Prison
Features
-400 Objects!
-Approximately 7-8 hours of work.
-Boat Dock off the coast of East Los Santos.
-Offshore Prison located directly in front of the East LS Boat Dock.
-Prison is on top of a large mountain and the only way down is to jump and hopefully land in water, parachute, or take the long, dangerous road back down to the
Island Dock.

Location
-The East LS Boat Dock is located at: 2946.1467, -676.7343, 2.915
-The Offshore Prison Dock is located at: 3965.2092, -878.4566, 6.132
-The Offshore Prison is located at: 4006.7639, -833.8237, 67.3649

Things to Keep in Mind
-This is only an Exterior, I have not yet made an Interior for the mapping. So, I suggest you use your own Prison Interior until I have created one to fit this map.
-When looking at the parking lot of the Prison and the Courtyard in the images, keep in mind that the concrete is all the same shade when seen in-game.
-If you head literally in a straight direction from the East LS Boat Dock, you will land right at the Offshore Prison.
-Yes, it is possible to make it up this mountain to the top. I suggest driving slow up the incline as I did not add barriers for a more sketchy feeling. The start of the incline up the mountain is located right next to the Prison Dock.
-Also, you'll notice as you enter the Prison, there is a space for a gate to be placed. If you are using this mapping for a server, use the gate model: 971.
