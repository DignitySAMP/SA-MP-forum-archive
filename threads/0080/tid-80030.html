Start your engines for...


L.S Speedway


Idea: Blooddrunk & Guedes747
Creator: Guedes747
Worktime: 10 hours
Objects count: 206
Location: Los Santos East Beach (beach near the Docks and the LS stadium)

Info:
The idea of this map comes from a old game i used to play, Gran Turismo 2, it is inspired on a race track called "Super Speedway" and some help from Blooddrunk too.
It has a PIT, Stands arround the circuit (but not on the corners), its a bit empty due his big size, thats why im going to do UPGRADES...
I strongly recomend the use of a STREAMER!

Screenshots:

This time i have a video instead of photos, its a video i made with my friend NoxY about my race track, on his server.
Enjoy the show

Video:

[ame]http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SplENNlwtlE[/ame]


PS: I only use mods so the movie won't turn out boring...

Ok, after watching my video, i think you might not want this map, but incase you like it, and want it on your server, you can download the .MAP file from my small webpage....
