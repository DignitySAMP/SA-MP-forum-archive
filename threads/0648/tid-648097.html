I'll leave gamemode recommendations to other people, I'm not up to date with other people releases.

https://forum.sa-mp.com/showthread.php?tid=318307 - best infodump about enumerators.

Dialogs are no magic, and the dry info you get from wiki is good enough way to learn about them:
Definining and showing dialogs - https://wiki.sa-mp.com/wiki/ShowPlayerDialog
Dialog styles with screens - https://wiki.sa-mp.com/wiki/Dialog_Styles
Handling dialog responses - https://wiki.sa-mp.com/wiki/OnDialogResponse
Bonus - colour embedding - https://wiki.sa-mp.com/wiki/Colour_Embedding
