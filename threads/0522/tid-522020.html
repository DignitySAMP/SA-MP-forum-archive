Paul Walker Memorial
by HeLiOn PrImE

Please give a fair rate, after you check the map in game!
I may seem like a total nerd for Paul, but I bet your players would give you props for mounting this kind of map on the server.
This is something every racing server should have, in my opinion (not particulary this piece). If people relate to racing in sa-mp, they will no doubt relate to Paul Walker.

Credits:
Adri1 and DialUp for their awesome tools
DiaUp - SAMP Arts
Adri1 - Map Mover

CAUTION:
  • This map is coded for the use with Incognito's Streamer Plugin.
  • It might cause lag issues if you are using to many maps and your server is crowded.
Warning:
I do not encourage you to use this particular map. It has far to many objects and it will most likely cause lag issues.
I do encourage the idea of having this kind of monument on a racing server, because players will relate to it. Just don't use this one.
You are allowed to add to this map, but you are NOT allowed to release it under your name.
Pay some respect, and leave the credits as they are!
