Do you mean that when you choose the house, that there is no furniture? (i.e. couches, cabinets etc) If so, you're selecting the 'interior' or 'house' model itself. AFAIK you can't edit or remove items from the 'interiors', only add to them.

You can add objects to these interiors by going to the 'Object' menu, then selecting 'interior objects' from the drop down list at the top. Or, you can edit these preset interiors by going to 'Locations' at the top, then selecting one from the list.

This is more of a question for the MTA forums, so next time try to ask over there.
