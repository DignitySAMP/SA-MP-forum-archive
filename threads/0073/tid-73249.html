Hi. Do you miss underground (metro) in GTA:SA? Then this is for you. My Los Santos to Las Venturas metro FS.

Features:
- 2 underground stations and elevators to them (1 under the Mullholand intersection, LS, and second under the big hangar on LV airport (the hangar where AT-400 is in singleplayer)
- 4 pickups on elevators, about 25 objects (whole FS, including metro, elevators....)
- Commands /buyticket (buying a ticket for $100), /enter (entering a metro)
