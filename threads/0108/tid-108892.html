|| Mo3ґs sugbug system ||



Whatґs this FS about?

Imagine: You have got a great suggestion while playing. You decide to tell it the others later - and then you just forget it.

But now this has a end!

This filterscript, once activated, provides your players the opportunity to make suggestions and report bugs ingame straight away, without even leaving the game.

List of features
  • Making suggestions ingame with /suggestion
  • Reporting bugs ingame with /bug
  • All suggestions/bugs are getting logged & saved in suggestions.txt and bugreports.txt
  • Modern and user-friendly dialog system

Those two are for /suggestion - /bug looks pretty similar.
