Well guys you're Right. I've uploaded this GM when i had only few minutes (i didn't have much time).


GameMode has these things:

- Little Registration system;
- Little admin system (some admin commands);
- 2 Teams (2 skins for each team): Police and Terrorists;
- Player Stats TextDraw (on the left of the screen);
- Simple Weapons menu (command /weapons);
- Little anticheat.

There is a Team Ammount System:
I mean...each teams have their "money ammount".
Every vehicle which respawns, removes some money to the team (if it's a Terrorists car, Terrorists will "pay" the vehicle!!!).
The game ends when a team loses (to lose its Money Ammount must be "0").
