Militia Forest
Creator: Guedes747
Worktime: 1h 30mins
Objects count: 129 Objects [136 with UPgrade(s)]
Location: South of LS Airport (on sea) [Not far]
Info:
This is another of my "Closed map" creations, like my last map "Industrial Jungle"....
But the scenario is diferent, its a forest with 2 military bases, 2 bunkers, watchtowers, buildings and stuff....
The variety objects is a little poor because i invested most of my objects on Trees to create a large forest, and Rocks to fix map bugs, such as holes and "flying rocks"
This idea came some time ago wen i was playing Call Of Duty 2, and i saw the large dense forests in France
I hope you all like the mape, Enjoy!
PS: Unlike COD2 here you have modern Structures, not some Tree-made Bunkers
Sugestions: For a bigger enjoyment use No Radar Blimps, make it a Team Deathmatch and add weapon pickups at the Bases.

Screens:
BEWARE: On Screens a 1 or 2 objects might not appear due the Draw Distance of Objects
