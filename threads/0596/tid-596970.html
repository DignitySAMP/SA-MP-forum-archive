Description of Fs:

Hello, I finally finished this filterscript for you, this it comes to buying convertible vehicles, type "Autobots" or "Decepticons" from the movie "Transformers"... which can be purchased by typing: " /Transformers " in a dialogue that will select the type of transformer to buy and the price of each, which you can change to your liking appear.

Also noteworthy is that it has created a destroyer transformers having created more than 1, to avoid the flood of transformers, which means that there is less need to adapt the id of the transformers with the id of the other vehicles in its gamemode or management system

Besides buying power transformers through dialogue, you can buy them directly using the commands: /optimusprime, /bumblebee, /jazz, /ratchet, /ironhide, /megatron, /starscream, /shockwave, /ss, /skyhammer.

Usage mode: After buying a transformer, to transform, you must use the 2,4,6,8 numpad keys.



Transformations: These 4 keys to transform your car, either in an airplane, a boat, a motorcycle or vehicle mode again.

They can also add more transformers for more keys, which can take from here: https://wiki.sa-mp.com/wiki/Keys since the function "public OnPlayerKeyStateChange (playerid, newkeys, oldkeys)"

Suggestion: They can put on your server, to buy them. instead of money, by score, level, etc.
