*The thing with the Heavy Roleplay,
The server isn't light or medium for two reasons,
1. I HATE Low-Roleplay servers, it's all full of deathmatch and random runarounds.
2. Players have the chance to improve their Roleplay skills, learn from others, try out something new and irregular, Prison keeps you in a certain area and forces you to Role-Play, you can't do much but for Roleplay.
I think that is a very important thing in a Roleplay Server - to keep the Roleplay the most focused objective and/or target.

For the love of all that is 'realistic', NO WEAPONS! Seriously, I don't know alot of prison RP's. But the ones that I DO know, everybody runs around with bats. Like wtf, how did you get a bat in prison!? Did you shove it up your butt?
Second; Allow players to have classes, such as 'outsiders' that smuggle drugs in, this way there can be dealed, and your server can have an economy by allowing players to do typical prisoners jobs. Such as cleaning, cooking, washing clothes etc.
The outsiders could be a side job for players that have done a specific time in prison and are allowed to go outside for a day. You could RP prison guards that will come with you and will follow you everywhere. And allow of every ~20-30 cops to be ~1-2 cops to be corrupt and allow players to smuggle drugs and stuff.

Outside, you could make normal RP for the people that did their time in jail. There could also be ways to escape from prison, but make those VERY high risk and allow the prison guards to have atleast ONE way of busting them, mid-escape.

You can have a knuckler for 500$ , the knuckler does 8 damage each hit,

The Jobs,
there are several jobs, two legal, two illegal.
Garbage-Man [LEGAL]
Table-Cleaner [LEGAL]
Drug-Dealer [ILLEGAL]
Weapon Smuggler AKA the Supplier Caller [ILLEGAL]

----------
Corruption of guards
Guards must Roleplay as a straight, law follower guard until they get an admin's offer to become Corrupt,
They can Roleplay corrupt if their Roleplay Level is high to hardcore and can follow all the rules OOCly, knows how the Prison-System works IC and OOC.
