MMORPG In San Andreas?
Hey there. This is a gamemode I started some time ago but never finished. It's just at it's start. I would say its progress is only like 4% but I think I should release it instead of just throwing it away, because it may help some servers be unique.

So, here are some features (Not so many but I will update it):

Vol. 1.0.0:
*** Login/Register system that I made. It uses y_ini
*** Level up system that I made
*** New and totally different HP system than the original San Andreas - I don't think Health Hackers can hack this one heh. The HP increases with every level up
*** Easily adjustable variables (That's what defines are for, no?)
*** Weapon upgrade station. It supports 10 weapons for now
*** Passive skills (Attack and Defense for now). You can also upgrade them at the Passive skills upgrade station
*** Good graphic interface. The graphic interface includes: Your opponent's HP when you attack him (Gets disabled after 10 seconds if you don't attack again), HP and Level up progress bars and digits
*** Completely new labels on top of your head - One for your level, one for your HP and one for your name. (Which means names can easily be changed by admins)
*** The only commands are the /help, the /yes and the /no ones. Here's what they do:
** /help: It shows some help duh
** /yes: It shows what key your "Conversation Yes" key is bound to. Your "Conversation Yes" key is used to open the Weapon Upgrade Station
** /no: It shows what key your "Conversation No" key is bound to. Your "Conversation No" key is used to open the Passive Skills Upgrade Station

Coming up:
*** Weapon/Armor shops
*** Inventory
*** Achievements
*** The ability for one account to have up to 3 different characters
*** More weapons and skills
