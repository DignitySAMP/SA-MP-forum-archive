Dude, remember that IPs starting with 192.* or 172.* or 10.* are "LOCAL" IP Addresses. Only the people in your network can play. And your default gateway would be 192.168.1.1 I guess. You need to get your internet public IP.
Goto www.whatismyipaddress.com and get yours. Then your port forward configuration should work. Your Server IP will be the one you'll see on the above website, after the PFconfig.
