SIMPLE WEAPONS SHOP (WIP)
I made another filterscript, but it's still in progress of making. But, if you don't want anything special, you can download it as it is now.

NEW UPDATE 0.3:
Finally, a new update, eh?

1. Added a new command - /gunshopsong!
This command will play the new audio stream song from the menu for the player, if they like the song.
The song can be stopped by going into the /gunshop menu, and pressing the 2nd
last thing.
2. Added a TextDraw (Will be improved)
3. Added an audio stream - It's a song that lasts for 1 minute, and plays while in the menu!

This weapons shop, is a very simple FS.

It uses: ZCMD, and dialogs.

Commands: /gunshop - Opens up the Weapons Shop .
/gunshopsong - Plays the audio stream from the /gunshop menu.

Current Features:
A lot of weapons
A simple dialog box, where you can buy them
Audio Stream!
