Scripted airbrake!
This FS works similar to airbrake from cheats. You activate it with /fly command. It is for admins only, but it can be easily changed.
After you have activated it, you can use arrows (or wsad if you use them to move) to fly forward, backward, turn left or right. You can use jump key to move up, and sprint key to move down.
