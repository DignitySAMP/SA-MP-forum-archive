L.S Speedway V2


First of all i would like to thank Neoz for the help he gave me, with server and bug detection, i couldn't do it without him and his server.
Thx mate


Now here's the upgrade list...

Upgrades:
  • Fixed some holes on the map
  • Fixed some problems on the stands
  • Added some Billboards
  • Added start lights like on real races
  • Added a start grid
  • Added lights on rails (to prevent car accidents during the race at night)
Notes and recomendations:
-Since the map is very close to sea level, i strongly recomend the use of weather ID 10, or the water will break inside the tunnels below the viewer's seats.
-I recomend the use of a streamer with a short range of view (regulate according with your preferences and needs).

Download: http://guedesworks.weebly.com/sa-mp-maps.html -> "l.s_speedway_V2.zip" <-
