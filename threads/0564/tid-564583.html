Number one is GTA San Andreas.
KingPlayer and muzammilfreeman said it's GTA V. I don't want to make Kalcor feel good, but GTA V doesn't even come close to what SA-MP has to offer.
Almost anything you have in GTA V can be scripted in SA-MP. The only upper hand I see on it is the engine with it's graphics and mechanics. That's it.
The map may be bigger, but SA-MP has potential for a way bigger map.

Second is DOTA 2. An awesome game that puts everything you have to the test: your logic, your reflexes, your strategies and most of all, your ability to work in a team.
Great graphics provided by the famous Source engine, and a lot of ways to learn it and have fun. Careful, tough, it can cause addiction.

Third are Far Cry 3 and 4. Open world first person shooter, crafting system land, water and air vehicles hunting, lots of missions to play, lots of collectibles and achievements, not to mention great villains, like Vas and Ming, respectively.

Fourth is Tomb Raider. Fine graphics, nice gameplay, and outstanding voice acting by Camilla Luddington. Also great and original soundtrack.
