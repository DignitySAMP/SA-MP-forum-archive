The DETAILS of this filterscript
Starters: I got this idea when i was watching a film [James Bond - Quantum of Solace], during the film i was thinking of what i could release next, then it hit me, a new Police Dept, yeah others have made them but they've used objects from Cityhall etc, so i brought back the amazing LVPD and well Updated it.

The COMMANDS
Cop Commands: This is the list of cop commands included with the release:
  • /NYJAIL [Cell 1, 2, 3, unjail] [Player ID] - Allows an officer to place someone in a certain cell or unjail them.
  • /CCTV - Brings up the CCTV menu, only usable in the office with fax machines. (Credits to =>SANDRA<=)
Sorry about the lack of commands, but because everyone uses different systems at the moment i cant really add more, e.g /cuff many servers already have it same with the others but, i'll try find more

_________________________________________________
Things you Should Know

pawn Code:
if(IsPlayerAdmin(playerid))

pawn Code:
