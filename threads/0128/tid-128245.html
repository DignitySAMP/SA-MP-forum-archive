[INFORMATION]
This Script Has been Updated to v1.5.1 Please Check the Log

Here are the Commands
Level 1+(Moderator): /kick, /slap, /warn, /info, /(un)mute, /spec(off).
Level 2+(Admin): /ban, /explode, /kMenu, /goto, /get, /akill, /(un)jail, /heal, /setscore, /fu*kup.
Level 3(Head Admin): /makeadmin, /nuke, /setweather, /settime, /veh, /sethealth, /setarmour, /restart, /setcashall, /setscoreall,
/setarmourall, /sethealthall.
Admin Chat is /a [text].
Player Commands
/stats, /report, /admins

For /makeadmin you can set your self to anylevel above 3 for now. So for fun you make yourself a level 99999.


*NOTE*You must place all the things in kAdmin(under scriptfiles) in scriptfiles or your information will not save
