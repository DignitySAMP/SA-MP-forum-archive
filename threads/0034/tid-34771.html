Gates In LV
Intro
I have created this small [FS] for mainly RP gamemodes only as it could be good for Public Transport Place or whatever you wish!

Please go easy on me as this is my first release!

Commands
The commands are:

/opengate
/closegate
/lockdown
/lockdownover

/lockdown I thought would be good just incase someone tried to DM or something and /lockdown locks them inside the compound.

You can make it for different teams but you must insert these yourselves.

^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
You can change these commands by opening the .PWN file and editing the names.
