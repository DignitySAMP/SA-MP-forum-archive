Ever since the fall of 2007 I came up with an idea for a single server, which was to conduct weddings. This is the first version, which I did for 2 days. Now, each pair of SA-MP can arrange their relations in the Catholic Church, San Fierro (marked with an icon of a heart). Only in my game, I was present at 3 weddings on the server ... What a pity that the script is ready just now.

Commands:
/marry [id] - to make an offer ID;
/maccept [id] - to accept the proposal from the ID;
/madmit [id] - refuse to ID;
/family [id] - look at a family ID (if no [id], then its) - a team designed to enhance the capacity, namely the emergence of other relatives, except for spouses;
/church - Teleport to the church.
