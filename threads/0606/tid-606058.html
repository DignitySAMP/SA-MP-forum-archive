Government Residence



Beginning

Hello, there SA-MP Community, missed you guys.
So this is my new work of Government Residence, I tried hard to make it look like a IRL building.

About map:
Map used textures that were made by the filterscript of [uL]Pottus, thanks to him for giving me a chance to make something like this happen.
Residence contains:
1. Entrance Hall
2. Meeting Room
3. Private office (x2)
4. Conference Hall
5. Office for government workers
6. Toilet (x2)

Photos:
http://imgur.com/a/gq6Rz

Map is unique by itself, you can use it in your projects. Good luck.
