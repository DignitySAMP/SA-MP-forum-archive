Attach Objects (v1.0)




This is my first filterscript. I hope you like it.


Commands (0.5):
=========


/buyfishingrod

/sellfishingrod

/buywrench

/sellwrench

/buycowboyhat

/sellcowboyhat

Commands (1.0):
=========


/buyfishingrod - To buy a fishing rod (Must be in 24/7)

/sellfishingrod - To sell a fishing rod (Must be in 24/7)

/buywrench - To buy a wrench (Must be in 24/7)

/sellwrench - To sell a wrench (Must be in 24/7)

/buycowboyhat - To buy a cowboy hat (Must be in 24/7)

/sellcowboyhat - To sell a cowboy hat (Must be in 24/7)

/buymobile - To buy a mobile (Must be in 24/7)

/sellmobile - To sell a mobile (Must be in 24/7)

/removeitems - To remove all items

/puthat - To put on your hat. (If you have one)

/takefishingrod - To take out your fishing rod. (If you have one)

/takemobile - To take out your mobile. (If you have one)

/takewrench - To take out your wrench. (If you have one)

/call - Mobile command to call

/hangup - Mobile command to hangup

/answer - Mobile command to answer

/ingnore - Mobile command to ignore
