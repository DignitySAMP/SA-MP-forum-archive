.: Colored Money Script :.
.: By Lavamike :.

>Description<
This is basically a text draw that goes over the money and changes colors based on how much money you have. You can change the values in the script. You can also change the colors with ease and with a little bit of work to the script you can add new colors.

>Bugs<
I didn't find any bugs. If you encounter any bugs, want to ask a question, or want to comment feel free to post here!

>How To Install<
First, download the script. Second, Extract CMoney.pwn to your filterscripts folder. Third, open up the CMoney.pwn, modify it as you wish, and compile it. Fourth, open up your server.cfg and add "CMoney" on the filterscripts line.

>Download<
[Version 1.0 by Lavamike] SendSpace: http://www.sendspace.com/file/zmdv4m
[Version 1.0 by Lavamike] Xendot Pastebin: http://paste.xendot.net/42

>Credits<
Lavamike - Making The Script :P

[b] - Notes -
I only ask that you don't remove the credits at the top from the script or at least don't go running around showing your friends saying you made it by yourself. You may modify it as much as you want except for that. Feel free to mirror, post a link in this topic and i'll add it. You can also edit it/make it better and post a link here and I will post your edited version here too. Please provide a brief description on your changes.
Also note that if people hold down F10 key or whatever it is to remove their hud the textdraw will still be there so if you have a movie making server or something you might want to add a command to turn it off/on. I will add a command in the next release.
