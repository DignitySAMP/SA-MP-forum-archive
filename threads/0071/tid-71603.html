Teleport Command
1. Open your gamemode
2. Under
Code:

Code:
	if(!strcmp("/SpiralFun", cmdtext, true))
{
	SetPlayerPos(playerid, 2512.0779,-1620.2792,231.0349);
	SetPlayerFacingAngle(playerid, 160.0216);
