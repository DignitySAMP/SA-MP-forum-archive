Hey folk,

It's been a while since I've visited the forums and I have this twitching feel again of creating something new. Im a huge fan of space and the new space race and came up with the idea of creating a space/travel-rp server.

Inspired by 'the 100', a great netflix series, it all starts aboard an orbiting spacecraft that is about to send the first 100 back to earth (after there was an apocalypse a 100 years ago) to rebuild civilization. It's up to the players to be valuable and continue expanding the human race. Before heading back to earth, people are stuck on this spaceship until an update comes around, basically stating it's time to go.

Kind of thought of including 'puzzels' to 'discover' new technologies, that could help the human race to move to new planets/get resources etc. Things that are hidden for the players beforehand.

Thinking of including stuff like: oribital mechanics, building your own rockets, spacestations (modular) etc. Really hoping people will group together and create some amazing stuff.

Honestly wondering what you guys think. Obviously there are some challenges, but I think it's viable (e.g. planet to spacecraft size). Looking forward to your comments.

Pictures yeah but none that do the project justice.

There is a sphere object with water that moves around, you can retexture it to lava in order to make a moving sun.

For hyperspace travel I had attached a black box around the player because the singleplayer night sky doesnt look like space at all because of the clouds. Stars were randomly generated. In order to hyperdrive to a planet I used a world to screen method (textdraws) which would start the jump sequence and land you on the planet.

This is pre ww-rp so 2015-2016. It was only a few thousand lines of code, nothing amazing.
