FS:Bazookas
FS:Bazookas is a filterscript of floor bazookas, you can place, delete, see their info and fire them. This filterscript requires MapAndreas, ZCMD and sscanf2, i can say this is a bit dynamic.


Commands
pawn Code:
/removebazooka -RCON ONLY-
/bazookainfo -RCON ONLY-
/placebazooka

Changelog
v1.1
  • Added beta First Person Shooter
  • You can check when a player placed the bazooka. (year, month, day, hours, minutes, seconds)
  • Fixed some bugs in OnPlayerDisconnect and OnFilterScriptExit
Disadvantages
  • The bomb going out of the bazooka isn't 100% accurate but it goes the right direction.
  • The script doesn't detect GTASA objects, so it goes throught them.
Features
-If you spawn a bazooka, but already have a bazooka spawned, the spawned bazooka is deleted and new bazooka is spawned.
-You can check bazooka's info, who placed it and the bazooka id, this may help to find abusers.
-You can move with your bazooka untill you find a place to put it.
