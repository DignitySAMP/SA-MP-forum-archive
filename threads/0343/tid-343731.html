Simplu..

In gamemode,dute la:

pawn Code:
new HouseInfo[34][hInfo];

34 semnifica numarul de case + inca 1 (adica,tu ai in property.cfg 34 case,in joc ai /house 0 - 33)

Editezi in:

pawn Code:
new HouseInfo[35][hInfo];
Si in property.cfg mai adaugi o linie,am sa-ti dau aici 1 exemplu:

pawn Code:
-157.310195,-1703.589965,3.956598,4686.574707,-2095.465820,2557.574951,0,0,0,0,0,0,sergius,Palat BlooDz,50000000,0,0,1,0,1,0,50000,1,750106,182,-1,-1,138,25
Acesta este un exemplu de casa,insemnand:

pawn Code:
Enter X, Enter Y, Enter Z, Exit X, Exit Y, Exit Z,0,0,0,0,0,0,OWNER CASA,DESCRIERE CASA[2camere],PRET CASA,Health[viata],Armour[armura],Interior[1/2/3/4.....],LOCK[1-inchisa,0-deschisa],OWNED[1-Are proprietar,0-de vanzare],ROOMS[nr de camere],RENT[Pret Rent],RENTABIL[0-nu are camere|1-are acmere de inchiriat],TAKINGS,VEC,VCOL1,VCOL2,hDATE,LEVEL[LEVELUL CASEI]
Enter X/Y/Z - coordonatele de la intrare in casa
Exit X/Y/Z - coordonatele de la iesirea din casa(inauntrul casei)
