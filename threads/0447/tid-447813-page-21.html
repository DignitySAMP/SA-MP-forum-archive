Dear SA:MP Community Members,

I am Richard Myers, Owner of Woodridge Roleplay.
We are currently developing our primary SA:MP Server and we are requiring some additional staff to complete our script and get the server up and running. More details about the server and our script will be given to interested applications.

Server Scripter
Scripters are responsible for creating,maintaining, and updating Woodridge Roleplay's scripts. Scripters are the core part of the Development Team, and are very important to the community.
It is preferred that Developers have knowledge with Textdraws and Model Previews, although this is not a requirement.

Server Mapper
Mappers are responsible for creating, maintaining, and updating Woodridge Roleplay's maps.
It is preferred that Mappers have knowledge with object material and object material text, although this is not a requirement.

Web Developer and/or Graphic Artist
Web Developers will be responsible for creating our UCP and our main website up to the standards of the community. Developers are required to know PHP and/or HTML & CSS.
Graphic Artists will be responsible for making several logos and branding pieces for Woodridge Roleplay, must be able to produce high quality pieces.

Moderator
Moderators are tasked with basic moderation of the server, while they are in training to become Administrators. During the Development phase Moderators will be tasked with testing our scripts.
