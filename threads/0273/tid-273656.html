Random Messages
Every minute there will appear an message with the text you can choose!

Why did i make this?

Bored

Installation

* Download the amx and the pwn files
* Place .pwn and .amx in your "filterscripts" folder
* Edit the random messages and compile it
* Put spickupsLS in your server.cfg behind filterscripts
EXAMPLE: filterscripts spickupsLS gl_actions
* Start your server, and it should work!

Download:

AMX:
http://www.gamefront.com/files/20627...omMessages.amx

PWN:
http://www.gamefront.com/files/20627...omMessages.pwn
