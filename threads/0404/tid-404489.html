Introduction

I get idea while I was learning c++ from books and made simple algoritham and simple code.

Video

[ame]http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KBpb-OY-BMA[/ame]

Documentation
  • Spraying - Mechanic make offer to player in a vehicle with colorpick and ID. When he accept that variable "Spray[MAX_PLAYERS]" is setted on 1 (true). And if that variable is true and mechanic with spray is near the car then mechanic can "spray" the car and paint him. And after 5 seconds color is setted on the vehicle. But you must be in the mechanic garage!
  • Repairing - Simple you can repair visual damage or engine damage of a vehicle. But you must be in the mechanic garage!
  • Garage - Simple map of garage in El Corona dump site. It is optional and you can delete it.
