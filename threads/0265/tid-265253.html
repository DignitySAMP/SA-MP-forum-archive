The 32-bit limit is 2 to the power of 32 - 4 294 967 296 bytes - that means 4 GB. With PAE, you can get a 64 GB physical address range...
The 32-bit Windows kernel is the one that limits it to 3.25 GB.
For example, 64-bit Windows 7 Ultimate can only handle about 192 GB of RAM, while the limit for 64-bit is much, much higher.
---
Also, if APB uses 2.5 GB out of 4.0, out of which 3.25 GB are usable, Windows WILL NOT give that error just for the lulz.
3.25 GB - 2.5 GB = 768 MB
Windows 7 can run on a PC with 512 MB of RAM, out of which it will use ~368 MB of RAM, if the processor compensates (higher than 1.0 GHz, which is the absolute minimum - otherwise it will lag like hell)
