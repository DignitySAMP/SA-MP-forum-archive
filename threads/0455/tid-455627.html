You need to get your computer's internal IP address. You can do this by opening a command prompt window and typing in "ipconfig". It will print something about a local area connection and will most likely be something like 192.168.1.XXX where the XXX is another number.

Then you go the page shown in your image and click on "add". The starting and ending port should be 7777 and the server IP address should be the one you found in the command prompt window. You just need to open a UDP port for the SA:MP server.
