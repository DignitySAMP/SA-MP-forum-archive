Realistic Shop [Interior]
What is it:
  • I'd like to point out that this is useful for servers which are not in LV
  • Why? Because unlike the other interiors, I have decided to put this one on the ground - Which means no sliding!
  • It's an interior you can use instead of default shops. It has clothes, electronics, toys, games, tables, literature, sport objects
  • I made it in 1 hour, it has extacly 66 objects

Description
  • It's a shopping interior with many shop items
  • 1. It has a desk for the owner, a coffemaker and magazines
  • 2. TV's, Video consoles, Speakers, VCR players
  • 3. Toys such as planes, cars, boats, trains
  • 4. Jeans, shirts and sweatshirts
  • 5. Hair products such as sprays, creams, fans etc
  • 6. 3 sport objects and 2 rooms for the owner
How to put this map into your server:
  • Copy all of the 'CreateObject(id,x,y,z);'
  • You might need to convert this so it fits your streamer. Use this website: http://maps.serverhex.com/
  • Open your GameMode/Filterscript and find 'OnGameModeInit' or 'OnFilterscriptInit' if you're putting in a FS
  • Simply paste it under and compile. There should be no errors, since it's nothing but objects
