C.S.C.R.
Camera Security Controle Room
Los Santos Police Department

I have recently been working on this FilterScript, but most of the time I am making sure that there are no bugs within the Script. Since I don't like to see any unfinished Filterscripts/Gamemodes released on SAMP Forums, that are also containing bugs. So I have been working on it for ~20 hours (~1200 minutes), and I can assure you that everything has been made from the scratch (No Copy & Pastes).

This is also my Very First FilterScript Published & Released On SAMP Forums! So here I proudly present you the Camera Security Controle Room (version 1.0)!


Featuring:
⇨ Cameras can be Viewed by Entering a Command (/camera, /cameras, /viewcamera, /viewcameras)
⇨ 'next' or 'back' To Switch Cameras
⇨ 'stop' To Stop Viewing Cameras
⇨ 7 Different Camera Views (Currently only inside the Los Santos Police Department)
⇨ 2 Security Doors (1 door can be open at all times)
⇨ 2 Options To Lock/Unlock Security Door (Enter "/cscrdoor" or press "ENTER_VEHICLE_KEY")
⇨ Door Automaticly Closes Within 5 Seconds (Once its open)
⇨ RolePlay Based Messages
(More Updates Will Come!)

NOTE: You Must Login as RCON Administrator!
