Admin House

CreateObjects - 99
AddStaticVehicle - 4


Commands : /entrance , /garage v0.2 /entrance , /garage open , /garage close

ScreenShots:


1. House entrance.
2. House interior.
3. Some funny thing.
4. Automatic doors for plane.

http://www.imagebam.com/image/484fe19355920 go on it and wait a bit , image load , then press Next to view second one.

About:

There is Admin House filterscript , in next version i add checking (just admins can open entrance , garage , and fly with plane).

Some bad thing : /garage doors not close automaticly just open and stay opened.

Download :

v0.1 http://www.sendspace.com/file/2jq9m4

v0.2 http://www.turboupload.com/download/...byparanoja.rar

v0.3 http://up.delux-host.com/1217097327/
How to instal:

Put AdminHouse.amx to filterscript folder , go to server.cfg , and found filterscripts , add AdminHouse , no include requered into GMs.

Where to found:


Everything is by me.

v0.2 - Fixed , now works with GodFather (all commands also) , if you want , make cars working , u need to put those cars in your GM.

And also some commands change , /entrance , /garage open , /garage close , good luck.
