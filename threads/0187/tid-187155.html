*Description*
I made this one for about year ago. It's intended to be some sort of formula1 track.
It has lighters, a garage and ofcourse the track itself. Enjoy!

And please do not claim this as your own work.

*Important notes*
You need a streamer for this map or else the server will crash.
Do not stream the spawnpoint object because that makes the spawn bugged.
Works great with Yagu's Race filterscript

[ame=http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9YwL2fHnYTk]*Video*[/ame]
