Quote:
Originally Posted by Ananisiki

The weapons textdraws shows up, when i try to pick a weapon, it chooses another product in the previous shop textdraw....
also how i can add ~h~in textdraw color?

pawn Code:
TextDrawBoxColor(Shop, 170);
If you want to make blue/red/green/yellow lighter, you just need to do add ~h~ after ~b~/~r~/~y~/~g~;

Example;

pawn Code:
cmds[0] = TextDrawCreate(118.000000, 122.000000, "~b~~h~Lighter blue! ~r~~h~Lighter red");
    TextDrawAlignment(cmds[0], 2);
    TextDrawBackgroundColor(cmds[0], 255);
    TextDrawFont(cmds[0], 1);
    TextDrawLetterSize(cmds[0], 0.500000, 1.000000);
    TextDrawColor(cmds[0], -1);
    TextDrawSetOutline(cmds[0], 0);
    TextDrawSetProportional(cmds[0], 1);
    TextDrawSetShadow(cmds[0], 1);
    TextDrawUseBox(cmds[0], 1);
    TextDrawBoxColor(cmds[0], 120);
    TextDrawTextSize(cmds[0], -1.000000, 212.000000);
    TextDrawSetSelectable(cmds[0], 0);
"~n~" is used to make a new line. Also, for the box (black), it's "TextDrawBoxColor", and it's transparent.

https://wiki.sa-mp.com/wiki/TextDrawBoxColor
