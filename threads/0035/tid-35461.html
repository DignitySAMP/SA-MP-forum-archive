Forumla 1 Circuit
Created by Remcconen, [ProGear]IceMan, [ProGear]Gilian
Don't remove our names!

It's a Formula 1 Circuit cxeated in MTA Map Editor. It's my first release.

Version 1.0
Commands:
  • /ferrariopen , /ferrariclose
  • /mclarenopen , /mclarenclose
  • /renaultopen , /renaultclose
  • /spijkeropen , /spijkerclose
  • /emergencyopen , /energencyclose
Cars:
  • Ferrari: 2 Red Cheetahs
  • McLaren: 2 Grey Cheetahs
  • Renault: 2 Blue Cheetahs
  • Spijker: 2 Orange Cheetahs
  • Emergency: 1 Tow Truck, 1 SWAT Tank, 1 Ambulance
Teleports:
  • /F1

Version 1.1
Now:
  • All things of Version 1.0
  • Yrace FS with 1 F1 race: 7 Laps on the Circuit

Version 1.2
It's comming:
  • All thing of Version 1.1
  • A Safety Car, A Ambulance Heli
  • More gates for safety
  • Tribune
  • /Count command
  • Arrows on the Start Grid for position 1, 2, 3, ..., 8
Bugs
No known bugs. Tell me when you find a bug.
