[b]As you can see i made it Newbie-Friendly, so i wrote the following things for you to understand stuff and change things.

If you want to give +70 ammo everytime he joins the deathmatch,(Ex: He typed /DEDM once anf got 70 ammo, again then he has 140 ammo, and so on.) Comment it! If you want to remove and give again ammo let it uncommented!:
Uncommented:
pawn Code:
#define REMOVE_WEAPONS_AND_REPLACE
[size=8pt][b]Commented:
pawn Code:
//#define REMOVE_WEAPONS_AND_REPLACE
[size=8pt][b]Change following things on your pleasure!
pawn Code:
new DEDMWelcome[80] = "%s entered Desert Eagle Deathmatch, type [/DEDM] to join.";//Message for all players to know|||"%s" Will be players name*/
new DEDMText[40] = "Desert eagle Deathmatch";/*GameTextForPlayer*/
new DEDMCmd1[20] = "/DEDM";/*Command nr.1*/
new DEDMCmd2[20] = "/DesertEagle";/*Command nr.2*/
[size=8pt][b]To change ammo when player joins deathmatch, simple change this line.
Change 70 to number of ammo you want him to receive!
pawn Code:
new DEDMAmmo = 70;
