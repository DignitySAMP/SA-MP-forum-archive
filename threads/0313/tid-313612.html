hey guys,

i have finally finished my gamemode Toretto Racing and i can say that it has became a nice and usefull gamemode for everyone.
it is designed for noobies,normal scripters and pro's becouse even noobies can use this gamemode, they just have to download the server package and then just extract them to somewhere and put on the server and add to their samp: 127.0.0.1:7788
NOTE: THE PORT IS 7788!!! so if you want to use 7777 then change that in the server.cfg

Features:

it has lots of stunts and jumps and pipes and lots more
i have also made some textdraws so when you join the server you can see how to get a car or weapon etc.
there is alos some random messages under the minimap and a clock system.
