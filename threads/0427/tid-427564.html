Thieves can use /rob<id> /robatm


Few Unique Features

/help [MESSAGE]- Sends admin a message if player needs they can use /help

/myinfo - Shows player his stats like
His wanted level
His Player level
Does player have a contract on him
Is he carrying C4


/robskill- Show players his robbing skills, they can increase it every time they rob.

/terskill - If player is a terrorist he can use this to see his terrorism skills

/ccommands- Cops can use this to see criminal records and bang theives

/Cureme- you can use this to heal yourself if you are near a hospital LV/SF/LS CP's in-game added.
