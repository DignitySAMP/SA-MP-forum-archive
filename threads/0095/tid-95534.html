Department of Motor Vehicles

Welcome to the Department of Motor Vehicles! Here, one of our esteemed licensors will test your driving skills. Then, they will either award a B- or A-license! The A-license of course, being better! This is also my first script. I'm looking for some constructive criticism to improve it, .

Features
  • Over 600 lines of PWN
  • Two-tiered license system
  • Faction system
  • Invitation/Firing/Promoting/Demoting functionality
  • /givelicense for all ranks; /takelicense for rank 4+
  • 4 Sentinels and 2 Sabres for testing
  • Working radio
  • Dini saving
Use

The script is use-able as an FS, but I highly recommend for it to be integrated right into your GM. There are also some things you might to change for your own use.

Line 530: This is a switch to determine who can /takelicense. You might want to change this to another faction(s) like LSPD, FBI, etc.
Line 599 .. 605: Here you find the GetPlayerRankName function. If you want to change the rank names, here's the place to do it.
