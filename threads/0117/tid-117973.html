Download & Apply of custom models, used for custom, global skins, vehicles or objects (!)

Requirements:
~1 GB local space for a backup of the gta3.img

Compatible to:
Windows 98,2000,XP,Vista,Seven

Tested under:
Windows XP, Seven

How to use ?
Download, extract, run "samp.exe", connect to a server.

How to register your own server ?
Watch the Tutorial in my *******channel (www.*******.com/eastermaniv)

What if someone else registered my server ?
Write an email to and give a proof, that YOU are the real owner.

I registered my server wrong ? What now ?
Write an email and wait. I wont hurry for people, who are unable to watch a video-tutorial.
