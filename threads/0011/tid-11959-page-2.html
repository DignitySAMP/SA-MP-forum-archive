Car dancing FilterScript
Woudn\'t be nice to chalenge your hommies into a car dance duel? Yeah I BET!
Well I was trying to do that, and I came out with this... THING Sorry guys this is the best I was able to do, I\'m still trying to make it a little more "NICE LOOKING" because it sucks! So if anyone can help me making it look nicer, I WILL REALLY APRECIATE THE HELP!

This is a FIlterScript, to make it work, just add it into your FilterScript Folder then add "CarD" at the end of the line that start with the word "FilterScripts"

Once in your server, you can use "/cardance [BET]" to set up a challenge, all the ppl interested in participate, must get near your car and have each one their own car.
Then use /start to start th competition.
The winner gets all the other ppl money, if there is a TIE, it will untie ONLY with the players that are tie, all the other player loose their money. this untie will continue until there is ONLY ONE TRUE winner.
Your car dosn\'t need to have Hydralics(as there is no simple way to check this), you can participate just by pressing the NUMPAD keys.
If you get off your car, you will automatically LOOSE, so be carefull!

The letters mean:
U = UP (or 8 in your numpad)
D = DOWN (or 2 in your numpad)
L = LEFT (or 4 in your numpad)
R = RIGHT (or 6 in your numpad)

You have to wait till some "[" "]" apears around the middle letter, if you hit to early or to late, you will recibe a message.
Message and dancing can be changing, just follow the example
FOR DANCING EDIT LINE 38

For GOOD messages edit lines 48 to 56
For EARLY messages edit lines 61 to 69
For GOOD messages edit lines 74 to 82
For WRONG messages edit lines 87 to 95

Messages are shown random, this files came with 9 messages of each kind for a nice play.

Try it and tell me your opinion. If you thing I have made something wrong or something can be done in a better way, just post here! I will apreciate ALL OPINIONS! even BAD ONES! (feel free to say that is a crap, it won\'t hurt my feelings, cause I already know that :/ )
