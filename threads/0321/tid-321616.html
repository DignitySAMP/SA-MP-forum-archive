Introductions
Hello guys. Well this is my first Filter Script that I am releasing. It's a basic FilterScript with many useful stuff that can be used on your server. It took me just a small amount of time to make this, around one hour or even less. Bellow there will be a list of things added in the FilterScript and all upcoming updates.



Things in it
There are multiple commands that can be used in game
  • v.1.0
  • /guns (Dialog with prices)
  • /melee (All melee weapons with prices)
  • /misc (Bombs etc.)
  • /radio (Online radio with 15+ stations)
  • /camera (Gives the player camera)
  • /fastcars (Admin command for fast vehicles)
  • /stats (Your in game stats (line: 95 -101)
  • /time (Shows the current server time)
  • /nitro (Adds 10x nitro to your vehicle)
  • /heal (Heals you to 100)
  • /armor (Gives you 100 armor)
  • /clearchat (Clears your chat)
Other things that are added [v.1.0]
  • Anti Money cheat
  • Random Messages
  • Anti Swear system (replaces the swear with ****)
  • Giving back player weapons after he dies


Bugs

No known bugs currently


Upcoming [v1.1] [30% finished]
  • Anti Weapon cheat [Finished]
  • Random messages as text draw [Finished]
  • Achievements
  • Register and login [Finished]
  • Admin commands [Finished]
  • Caps for all skins
  • More vehicles like /fastcars etc.. [Finished]
  • Lots of animations
  • Anti spam system [Finished]
