Starting a server;
- Ensure you know what you are doing, you don't want to start something you know nothing about!
What do you need;
- Script, Normally unique ones get the players
- Good name, Using a name like [REFUNDING ADMEN] etc puts off many players.
- A good host, Someone who can host your server and give you support if you go down.
Advise;
- Just get to know the script and everything in it if you are using an edit, ensure that when you do get players you carry out everything, for example unbanning someone after 1 day on a 5 day ban etc.. Just be truthful etc.
- Hash Passwords, Self explanatory, most scripts do this anyway..
Ideas;
- Use your players opinions on what to add
Host;
- You upload your files onto a host; List here
- They usually deal with the most of the running etc, meaning you just pay and it pretty much will run without a hitch.

Other Notes;
- Hosted tab, this can be good attracting players as there is only a few hosted tab listings.
- Don't use NGG edit if you are going to do it, This is bad..
- Enjoy it, If you ever start to feel bored, try not to give up, it can be fun & annoying, just persevere.

Hope this helps.
