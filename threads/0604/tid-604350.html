The limit of global textdraws is 2048 (https://wiki.sa-mp.com/wiki/Limits), and you have 68 textdraws for every player, so 2048/68 = 30.

In this case you need to use player-textdraws instead global (https://wiki.sa-mp.com/wiki/CreatePlayerTextDraw).
