Weed planting.

This system is simple, easy to edit and uses ZCMD and sscanf as two main features.
  • Picking weed after a limited amount of time
  • Planting weed with a simple command.
  • Streamed dynamic object for the weed planting.
  • Growth of the plant depending on whether it's ready to be picked.
