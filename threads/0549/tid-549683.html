If the list below was on a RP server, would you play on it?

— Set in a fictional country, with it's own history, laws and society.
— An application system similar to LS-RP's but with harder questions and required a bigger character story.
— A small staff team (2-4 admins, 4-5 helpers/testers/supporters)
— Every rule is to be 1) learned, 2) followed, 3) strictly enforced by the staff.
— Custom mapping (not an insane amount of objects that cause players on low-end PCs to lag terribly)
— New players won't get refunds. Instead, a realistic currency will be set and you'll have to work your way up to the millions.

I'm currently trying to build a RP community from the ground up and am wanting as many people's input as I can get on my ideas. If you've got any ideas to suggest, please let me hear them. Serious ideas and comments will be taken into consideration.
