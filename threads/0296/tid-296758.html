But since good and bad music may not be the same for two different person, I wondered, how would one actually define good and bad music?

Criterias:
  • Lyrics
  • Instruments
  • Singers/No singers
  • Ambience (Emo music may make you sad, Dance music happy? Etc....)
  • Style in general
  • Emotions when listening to a song
  • Etc.
How do you define good and bad music?
What is important to you?
You may post one or two examples...

Please do not start a war like Hip Hoppers vs. Metal Heads or something like this.

I like bands like Nightwish or Within Temptation.
I really like the combination of classical elements and big orchestras combined with heavy tones and a beautiful voice of a woman.
Within Temptation's songs' lyrics are about serious topics. Well, I can't quite describe it. Nightwish's ones I often don't quite get.
And their songs are also varied, no song is really the other.
Another important point is that their music is not electronic, they play real instruments and don't use autotune.
I do really like artificial music where the beats are computer generated by up to 100% and the singers voice has been extremely modified.
Nightwish - Meadows of Heaven: [ame]http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JmXk1UhlIgg[/ame]

I like for example Taylor Swift.
She's got a beautiful voice and the lyrics of her songs.
She's got some kind of humour I really like and how she writes songs, they are often quite personal.
From what I've seen on *******, I also like her as a person. She isn't the "Sex sells" kind of woman.

I like Demi Lovato's song "Skyscraper" because it's a pretty emotional song. You can also see that the song mirrors her real emotions when watching her music video.

On-topic: depends on the mood. I just LOVE music, almost listening to it 24/7 and I'm quite of an emotional guy (*prepares for the trolls*) so meh. If I'll go to a party, I like listening to some low-IQ David Guetta things, to get in the party-mood. Or if I've got a hangover, just playing "David Guetta - Memories". I know, he's not the most deep-going artist, but I ain't deep-going either when I go to a party lol.

If I'm not in any particular mood, I mainly listen do DnB or Dubstep. And no, it's not filthier than Hitler ratio in WWII. I neither got an eargasm nor my neighbours complained about it. I just like it, that's it.

I also used to love grunge, I grew up with it, my brother continuously played Nirvana, and after a while I just liked it.

And I like the old Eminem music, we listened to it all time when I was in primary school. Gangsta, I know. Not liking his new things, but doesn't matter, if you like it I'll meh.

Anyhow, if I'm in a sad mood, I can really listen to some..sad songs. You know, those songs which exactly describe what you're feeling at that moment. Just beautiful as that, knowing someone else feels the same way like you. If you then put it on really hard, it's like your environment shouts out your pains. Sounds quite emo tbh. An example with a lol-title would be "Oasis - Stop Crying Your Heart Out", or the Rock Werchter live version of "Editors - No Sound But The Wind".

I like a lot of music genres, from classical to DnB, from Hard Rock to Rihanna, from Adele to The Prodigy. But the one genre I do not like is metal. As stated before, that's my problem, not yours.
