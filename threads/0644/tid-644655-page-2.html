18.12.2017, 10:49
There was not much going on the last 4 weeks. I've been busy with other stuff, sorry. The promised features will be implemented soon. But anyways, we added a new feature to our site:

Server owners can now upload their models/texture (which are being used by their SA:MP servers) on our servers, in order to make them reachable via an http(s) link. That link can be used to redirect download requests (in PAWN) from clients to our servers. We compress those files before we send them back to the clients to improve download speed.

If you don't know how to redirect downloads in your SA:MP script, have a look at the filterscript dl_redirect.pwn (made by Kalcor)

You can test that feature by clicking 'My Files' in the user-navigation bar. (you need to be logged in)

If you have any questions, just leave a post here.
