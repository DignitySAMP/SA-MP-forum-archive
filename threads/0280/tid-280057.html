Instead of
Code:
wget -c http://mirrors.kernel.org/ubuntu/pool/un...03504mkdir mysql32
dpkg --extract libmysqlclient*deb mysql32
sudo mv mysql32/usr/lib/* /usr/lib32/

Code:

dpkg --extract libmysqlclient*deb mysql32
sudo mv mysql32/usr/lib/* /usr/lib32/
Pretty sure this works... You get the 64bit version with aptitude
