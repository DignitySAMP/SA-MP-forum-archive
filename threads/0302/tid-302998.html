Description

Hey guys. This is my newest FilterScript . It's a SnowBall minigame , that anyone can join. I updated it a little bit so now it doesn't have any bugs and it works perfectly. Also it's verry small and simple. When you type /snow you will be teleported to a place where when you will press AIM Key first you will take some snow from the ground , and the second time you'll press it you will throw the SnowBall . If someone it's in front of you on a distance of 25.0 you will shoot him and he will die. As a reward you will get 500 $.

Video

Of course that I have a Video , but this Video is very small and it has been made with the 1.0 version (Now the script it's 1.2) so it may differ.

[ame]http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3VklUcSienw[/ame]
Download

You can download the script from here:
No mirrors please!

Instalation

To install the script make the following things:
  • Download the Script and unZip it!
  • Put snow.pwn and snow.amx into your FilterScripts folder.
  • Into 'server.cfg' add 'snow' at the filterscripts line.
  • Start your server and ingame write /snow!
