This game is a combination of freeroam, team deathmatch and zombie mode, but its main theme is zombie mode based on Resident Evil series. It also includes things from The Walking Dead series, famous zombie movies and our own creativity.

You can participate in special events made by administrators to earn more EXP, cash or you can choose a special award like war vehicles (Rhino), special zombie class (UMB, mutant, tyrant, etc) or even be transformed into a Nemesis with 4000 HP or be part of the "Stars" team (includes RPG, more health and armor).

Photo: Woodbury town, from The Walking Dead series.


HOW TO PLAY?

This game is about surviving and attack every enemy you find (zombie, umbrella or human).

Depending on your team you will have various ways to defend yourself or attack; there are 3 teams: Infected, Humans and Umbrella.

As you progress through the game you will gain advantages that you will be able to use in anything you want. From being transformed in the Nemesis proyect to be part of the Umbrella Corporation.

Photos: humans attacking Nemesis proyect.



BASICS

Teams and their features:

Infected: This team is light gray on radar and attacks with right mouse button; it's objective is to infect every single human or Umbrella. The advantage of this team is that they kill fast, never get tired and gain more EXP than the other teams. They also have hives where they can eat fresh meat to recover health.

Humans: This is green and they attack with weapons and can drive vehicles. Their objective is to survive and defend themselves from the infected or the Umbrella Corporation. After the incident they settled survival campsites around the cities and can receive help from campsites.

Umbrella: This team is orange, attacks with weapons and can drive vehicles. Their objective is to kill everything, even humans; they have bases where they can get equipment and get healed. To join the Umbrella team you require 140,000 EXP.

Photo: Apocalypse Mode event.


Zombies: how to get antivirus, bite and advantages

There are two ways to be disinfected; one is to use /Items and buy the antidote and the other way is to go to the red cross in the radar. The cheapest way (free) is to go to the red cross on the radar. This is located near Los Santos hospital, east side of the Skate Park and south side of Glen Park.

The advantage of being zombie is that you earn much more EXP and cash than being human (10 or 15 times), and also when being zombie it is easier to kill humans that are alone. Zombies have advantage over single or small groups of humans.

You can bite pressing right mouse button (aim key) near a human. Use space bar (sprint key) if you are inside a car.

Zombies can't drive but can use /Dig and /Places.

Photo: surrounded by zombies in the sea.


How to recover health?

A control point is a place where each team can be healed or gain exclusive advantages of their team.

Infected control points: they use purple color in the radar, if you are infected you can recover health there.

Human control points: they use green color in the radar; humans can recover health there. It is lethal for other teams to go to a human control point.

Umbrella control points: they use orange color in the radar; umbrellas can recover health there. It is lethal for humans to go to an Umbrella control point. It can be used by infected umbrellas to recover health.

Basic commands

/Help - How to play and answers to common questions.
/Cmds - Command list
/Weapons - Buy weapons.
/Cars - Buy cars.
/Dig - Go to a zombie dig point in the city.
/Options - Change time and weather.
/Places - Places where you can teleport.
/Products - Buy paid products (you can get free VIP now in events as we are just opening!)
