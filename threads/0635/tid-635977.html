Bone County Roleplay aims to enhance the quality of roleplay in the SAMP community. The server first opened it's doors on the 13th of August, scoring in 40 players on the grand opening. Due to complications the server shut down but is now returning with a brand new start. Down below you can take a look at the new and improved features, mappings that we have to offer:









Bone County is littered with businesses, all of which are enter able and have a variety of items. Business types include: Furniture Stores, General Stores, Gas Stations, Bars/Clubs, Pawn shops, Clothes stores etc. Businesses are available for purchase, of which there is no limit to the amount you can own. Tax is set by the government, currently the rate is (default) $150 per business, all optionally payable at the county hall.


Bone County has a wide range of houses available for purchase (or breaking into if that's your thing). There is no limit to the amount of houses you own, but houses are taxed based upon their value. The amounts vary. As with everything else, you can sell your house to other players. Houses can also be broken into. Security is purchasable. You cannot store items in houses per-say, but you can store items in furniture, just make sure your house is secure.


Bone County is served by several emergency departments, in cases of emergency: Call 911. San Andreas State Police currently serve Bone County, they have a vast range of functionality. San Andreas Medical/Fire Department currently serve Bone County. Dial 911. Comparatively to other servers, medical staff have the ability to perform CPR if you have died. Police can trace weapons, use DNA analysis for murders, find crimes by location and crime type and even find patterns of crime within different areas.


There are various jobs available around Bone County. You can fish at various locations around Bone County, if you're lucky you may catch something else. You must pass your driving test to become a taxi driver, the system is extremely detailed. You can deliver pizzas to addresses throughout Bone County, it is available near the spawn. Mechanics have the ability to repair, jumpstart, re-spray and tow vehicles. There are many more jobs that aren't listed here, using /map in game will list them all.


Vehicles are available for purchase around Bone County, at different prices and there are no limits to the amount you can own. All motorized vehicles have spark plugs, oil levels, water levels, radiators, gearboxes, batteries etc. Being involved in accidents will damage these vital parts and they must be maintained/repaired. All parts and levels eventually wear down through general usage. Vehicles will behave differently when this happens. Vehicles can be bought, sold, stolen and exported and there are no restrictions. Vehicles have space for storage for weapons/drugs/money etc and have upgrade-able security systems. The vehicle system is very detailed and is designed for immersion, there are many variables.


Weapon System
Weapons are entirely unique in Bone County Roleplay, every weapon completely unique with a serial number (if legal) and completely traceable. All weapons can be tracked to an owner if they are purchased legally, providing lots of information on the weapon. All weapons can be tracked by Admins and can never be lost, Admins can teleport to any weapon and locate any weapon. Weapons have custom damage and hit markers are shown to the person who has been shot to assist with roleplay. Weapons are created by factions using the correct parts or can be purchased legally with a weapon license.
Drug System
There are several types of drugs available in Bone County Roleplay. Marijuana, Herion, LSD, Cocaine, Steroids and PCP. All drugs have various effects, without giving too much away there are several benefits. Drugs go hand-in-hand with addiction and it wouldn't be realistic to miss that out. Anyone can order drugs and sell them, official factions are supplied them for a cheaper rate.


There are many types of furniture available in Bone County Roleplay, some storable, some not. Sofas (storable), tables, chairs, beds (storable), electronics etc. Many items of furniture can have items stored in them including money, weapons and drugs. Furniture can be stolen, moved and transported in any vehicle. Vehicles have 3 slots (default). Most furniture can be stolen and sold in pawn stores for a percentage of the original value. You must physically carry furniture between locations and including storing into vehicles.
