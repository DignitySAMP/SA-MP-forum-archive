Non - Genuine Windows

Well Microsoft, in a rather brilliant move, has released an update that checks your OS serial against a list of known leaked serials (corporate edition keys, etc). If your serial matches one of them then it puts a darling little message on the bottom right hand of your login screen, and also a system tray icon that nags you to buy a genuine copy of windows.
It will also pop up a dialog that says “This copy of Windows is not Genuine”
“This copy of Windows is not genuine and you have not yet resolved this issue. This computer is no longer eligible to recieve select security upgrades from Microsoft.

To protect your copy of Windows, you must click Get Genuine now.”
Every so often a little balloon will pop up that says “You may be a victim of software counterfeiting.” Below that it will say “This copy of Windows is not genuine. Click this balloon to resolve now.” If you’re working on a system that has this running it gets annoying in a hurry. What the time interval between pop-ups is, I don’t know, but I imagine it’ll drive most people to buy a legit key after a while just to get the thing to shut up. Instead pay $400 or cheap & expencive moneys , just send a message to Microsoft a message about your problem and your Windows will activated after 3 or less months . Better Uninstall and Delete your unnecessary things that you don't need them .

Extra : Your PC Turns off ?

The main problem is your PC Motherboard and not the Graphic Card . Graphic Card have nothing to do with this problem , Graphic Card is for your PC , Gaming and other things , it's PERFORMANCE Driver like ATI Radeon , AMD Athlon X2 , Intel , NVIDIA . The motherboard's main problem when your PC Turns off .
But there are motherboards Intel , ATI , nVidia and other motherboards , even it can happens due the CPU high usage and other unknown problesm , better get the PC to the mechanics .
So don't get confused with Performance driver with MotherBoard , there's a difference .

How to fix that ?

Take your PC to PC Mechanincs or take it to the PC Shop where you bought it .
Do not do actions on your PC or on your PC Motherboard , may you destroy the PC .
PC Mechanics can fix your PC Motherboard ASAP .

You can also ask the Pro PC Peoples for your problem , they can help you .

SOLVED

Can't open or connect gta_sa.exe or samp.exe ?

First go to Firewall , select allow a program through to windows firewall and select samp.exe and gta_sa.exe .
When you do this , you have to reinstall both SA:MP and GTA sa correctly .
Before reinstall make sure you have deleted SA:MP & GTA Sa components in Documents and in Programs files .
If it doesn't allows you to delete them , you have to restart your PC .
You can also reinstall GTA SA by > Control panel > Programs and features > Uninstall a program and choose gta or samp if it's ever available there .
