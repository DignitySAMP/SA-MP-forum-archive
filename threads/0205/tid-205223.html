Afterlife & Accept Death by Admantis

What is this?

This is a 20 - 30 minutes work made by Admantis based on something I saw in newest RP scripts. When you die you will return to your last position and you can wait for a medic or something or accept death. If your laying death for some (adjustable) time, you will bleed out and die.
When you accept death or bleed out, you will be in afterlife for an (adjustable) time.
Default auto-bleed-out time is 5 minutes, and afterlife time is 2 minutes and you can accept death with /acceptdeath

How I should use this?

You should take the pastebin code and use it as a filterscript. It compiles PERFECTLY, so if you have errors its your fault. It uses no special includes so you shouldn't wory about it. I don't suggest to use this for RP scripts that already have that system.

Where to download?

http://pastebin.com/cqUAbar8

P.S: This script is semi-old, some lines are recommended / should to be changed according to your gamemode.
