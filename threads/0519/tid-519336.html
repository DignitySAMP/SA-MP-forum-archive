As someone else said, it depends whether you are an already well-established community or not. With my team's servers (when they were online) we definitely noticed an increase in players when we put our servers on the hosted tab, but that's because they were small servers which weren't really advertised or anything. SACNR is a big server, they do not use the hosted tab (last time I checked they weren't on it anyway) and they still get lots of players because of their popularity.

So if your community is new then yes, get hosted tab for a few months while you start up.
