I can't really say i've had a very populated server in the past but some things you should do:

- Don't Rush scripting, Doing that can lead to bugs. Bugs are horrible and nobody wants to play a bugged server...
- Publicity, Get some good Publicity going, Advertisement Page On SA-MP Forums, ******* Videos a nice signiture etc.
And remember don't rush publicity, the More professional it looks, the more better the server comes across to me to be honest.
- Ingame Spelling (ClientMessages etc..), Seeing Clientmessages in a server that have poor grammar, spelling and certain words makes it look very unprofessional and makes it look like a 9 year old wrote it... Example of bad Messages: hey welcome 2 my server hav fun!!!!!!!!!
- Admins, A nice, active admin team is good. I had a server for a month once, started with just 4 of my friends and me, We was all admins, Just because of the admin team we must have got around 4 more active players. Even thou' that is only 4 players it makes everything up in the end.
