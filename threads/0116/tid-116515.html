As you might have noticed, the servers in the 217.18.70.* range have had a lot of downtime and even data loss over the past few weeks.

ASUS
ASUS is still examining the servers, which they have been doing since the first day the problem occured.
We've set a deadline on the 21th of December, if they did not find the cause by then we'll be returning the servers and purchasing HP servers instead.

Backups
Backups are created every night at 04:00 to our remote backup server, and kept for at least 2 weeks.
Due to the problems, the server managed to corrupt the backup files while they were being created.
We've changed a few routines which should resolve this issue and prevent the backup files from getting damaged.

RAID
We expect the problem is with the RAID controller, but ASUS did not deny or confirm this.
Just to be sure, we've disabled RAID on all servers and hope this solves the problem.

Compensation
We've already compensated people who opened a ticket about it by adding several days to their server, double the time the server was offline.
ASUS will have to compensate serverFFS as well, which will be shared among the active customers at that time.

Future
Like mentioned earlier, ASUS only has a few days left to find and resolve the issues.
If they fail to do so, we're allowed to return all their hardware and will purchase HP servers instead (with the same CPU).

We apologize for the problems caused and hope you can understand our course of action.
If you have any other questions, feel free to reply to this e-mail or contact our live support.
