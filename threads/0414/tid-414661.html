You can't just import a command like that to another admin system. You need to adjust it.

Take this for example:
pawn Code:
if(GetPlayerAdminLevel(playerid) >= 5)
From your old one

(And since your new one doesn't have that variable, you need to find the variable of your player info. Then change it to this or similar)
to

pawn Code:
if(GetAdminLevel(playerid) >= 5)
As you can see, it uses different variables.
