» MAP: Angel Pine Improvements
_______________________________________________


» Description

I have added all sorts of general improvements to make Angel Pine more attractive. This includes trees on the streets, benches, trashcans, newsstands, phonebooths, and a couple of other things such as fences and some vehicles.

This map would be very suitable for any servers who wants to have Angel Pine as one of their locations, the map makes Angel Pine much more enjoyable to look at.

I originally made this for another server, but decided to release it publicly. There are 155 objects and 9 vehicles.
