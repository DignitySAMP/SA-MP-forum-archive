This is my 1st release in the FS, so I hope you guys like it


What is it??
It is just a Weapon Shop Menu filterscript that you can set the
price and amount of ammo will be given to the players who buys on the weapon shop. It has 12 weapons in it. It Saves Weapons On Player Dis-Connect and Load them On Player Connect.


Screen Shot:









COMMANDS
This filterscript is extremely efficient, and uses only one Command for Shop and One Command for Skills.
• /shopweaps
• /showskills

Version 4.0: Killing Sphree, Modified Weapon Skill System and Rewards On Killing.
