Vuki_Transporter


With the help of DFT-30, you can easily transport vehicles adapted to the size of the DFT. (don't hope you'll transport an AT-400 )

Here a video test that proof you can drive and carry the vehicle:
[ame]http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eRmYsrvekfo[/ame]

Commande:
/ramp: Open/Close the ramp of the DFT-30.

Bug:
- According to syncro, it's better to enter to the car once you drop the vehicle (never tested with others players).
- To get out the vehicle of the DFT, you must push it out.
- If you found a bug or a mistake please report it here.
