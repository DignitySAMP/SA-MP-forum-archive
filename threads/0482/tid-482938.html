Now I realise you could just make anything up here, but equally I'm sure there have been many 'WTF moments' during your SA-MP sessions that stick in the mind.

I was on PartyServer (as always) wandering across the road above SF Swat compound, having just come from the Cluckin Bell around the corner and up the hill to replenish my health after some hectic combat. I glanced at the radar and saw a Mechanic blip approaching fast from the south, above me. I switched to M4 and looked skywards. A Hydra appeared in the distance and as it came closer it was clear the pilot was at minimal health. I aimed up and ahead of its path. Fired 3 shots. To my amazement the pilot died, I'd hit him! And the now empty Hydra came tumbling out of the sky, bouncing off the buildings ahead of me and sliding to a halt on its roof some way into the distance.

I glanced around, grinning from ear to ear, ready to say 'omfg did you SEE that?' but of course, nobody at all was around to witness my glory.

I wish I'd Frapsed that.
