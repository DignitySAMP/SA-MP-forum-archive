ZOMBIE MOD
Probabily you've never seen a gamemode like this one... 4 different maps (interiors), where zombies have to use their knives on humans to infect them.

If you're a human and get infected, than you'll become a Zombie, getting knife, 150 HP, other skin etc.
Humans spawn with PumpShot., M4 and Desert Eagle.

When a infected human die, he becomes a human again.

At the end of the round (6 minutes each round), the team with more kills will be announced, a wait screen will appear for 10 seconds, than a new round will start.

Download .pwn - http://www.4shared.com/file/97953463...zm_online.html

Hope you like it, enjoy
