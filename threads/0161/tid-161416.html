I thought this would be a cool idea to make, but a drilling rigg outside of Los santos, near the beach.

I made this rig completly out of crane parts and other missmatched stuff.

it has:
Realistic smoke from the machinery
broadcasting antenia
Working drill (Moves up and down)
Oil cap like BP has.
Oil pump
Methane filter
Mechanic deck
drill engine deck

and a kick ass looking design.
