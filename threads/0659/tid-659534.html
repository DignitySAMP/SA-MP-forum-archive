Speaking from experience here, as I myself have founded and run my own very successful Web and Game Hosting Company for a number of years.

It's unfair to judge a relatively new Company only on the website they have, yes it's there to provide an initial representation of the Company, and yes having a custom site is ideal and arguably more professional, but let's be realistic here guys - Prestige Steve's website does what a Hosting Companies should, it gives you the USP', Prices, Services on offer and the like at a glance. You'll find that arguably the long-term market leader for 'Premium' Game Server Hosting also uses a very common template for their Website; Crident.

I have myself used Prestige Steve's services, due to incredible demand I worked with the founder on a more personal and direct level reselling his SAMP services to my Customers, and I can honestly say we didn't have any issues - Although should we have I have no doubt in my mind that he'd have been more than happy and able to assist.

As long as the Company continues to grow, expand and develop it's services it's harsh of you to criticise it, the long and short of it are can you trust them, is it good value for money, and are they the right company for you - Only you as the potential customer can answer those questions for yourself by giving them a chance, don't base your decision on well theoretical bullshit. (No one here's even mentioned the benefits of dockerisation, containers, sso, nor have they seemingly considered the work and overhead that goes into providing such a service - Comparing a Hosting Companies services to running a server on a VPS is simply ridiculous.)
