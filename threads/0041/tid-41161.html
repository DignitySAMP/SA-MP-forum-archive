RPG commands
PART 1:
/rob
by bogeyman_EST


Features:
  • Robbing amount depending on the player's cash
  • Anti-spam system to prevent /rob spam
  • Player needs to be near the player he/she wants to rob to be able to rob
  • Players can't rob when they aren't in the same vehicle as the player they are trying to rob
  • Players cannot rob themselves
  • Highly customisable colors and anti-spam time between /rob commands

Installing:
  • Download the /rob command
  • Place the rob.pwn and rob.amx into your filterscripts folder
  • Open rob.pwn and edit the defines to your wish
  • Add "filterscripts rob" in your server.cfg

Usage:
  • Type /rob [ID] in the game and you will rob that player!

Download:
  • Mirror no. 1
  • Pastebin


RPG commands
PART 2:
/hit
by bogeyman_EST


Features:
  • Highly customisable colors and antispam time
  • Hit is cancelled if the player dies not from weapons(splat, driven over...)
  • Hit is cancelled if the player disconnects
  • Antispam to prevent command spam

Installing:
  • Download the /rob command
  • Place the hit.pwn and hit.amx into your filterscripts folder
  • Open hit.pwn and edit the defines to your wish
  • Add "filterscripts hit" in your server.cfg

Usage:
  • Type /hit [ID] [amount] to place a hit!
  • Kill a player who has a hit to recieve the money
  • Use /hits to check the current hits

  • Mirror no. 1
  • Pastebin
