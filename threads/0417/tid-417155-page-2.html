Oh, where do I start?

Back then when I first started to play this game(early 2007s-late 2006s i suppose) my English was next to none, I was simply playing on Turkish servers as I had no clue whatsoever as to how to communicate with foreigners. I had no foreign language knowledge whatsoever except for a few simple words. Then I found about a roleplay server, I tried to learn English there. I failed but people were always positive to me so I never stopped trying. As time passed by, I realized my English was getting better and better. It was the early 2008s when I discovered about PAWN. I decided to learn PAWN, I looked at lots of documentations, tutorials despite my half-broken English. I kept on scripting, I never gave up. I spammed skypes of people I know of as to how to script on PAWN, quite a few of them helped me and for this, I'd like to thank them once again. As years passed by, I realized my English wasn't anything to be laughing at nor was my PAWN knowledge. So I decided to move on to other scripting languages. Now, I can safely say that my current profession about scripting and my passion for English are all SAMP's doings and for this, I'd like to thank every single one of you guys. Let's keep our great work up!
