Digital Ocean is good if you're looking for cloud hosting. Which is what their nodes are designed to run. I personally use it if I need to use a vps for an hour or so to test it, being that they charge by the hour.

Anyhow, go with nfoservers.com. I personally don't use them anymore for my own reasons, however, they have amazing support, their nodes are configured for running gameservers and it tells you how much you can run when you sign up. The only downfall is that its a little bit more than other hosts (personally why I switched, though I do regret it)
