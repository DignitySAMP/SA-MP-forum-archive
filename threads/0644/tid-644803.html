Dear Community,

I've always held this project kind of 'secret' and only showed it to a limited amount of people, however since the upcoming version (0.3.8 ) I got the feeling this creation will have less uniqueness due to the possibility of importing large amount of codes and implementing for example the Vice City map!

The idea behind this project is/was to push SAMP to its final limits by implementing as many objects as possible within a small range, trying to figure out how to get it working smoothly - which I can conclude I succeeded in besides some limitations. Please don't get me wrong here: the project is not finished nor is its' limit reached! Basically I mapped step by step, first initializing the basis and slowly adding more details, trying to create a realistic environment while avoiding and fixing crashes, crashes.. aaaand crashes.

The lack of interest of team members / specific people not willing to test it made me stop the development of this project and with the release of 0.3.8 upcoming I decided to show it towards the community.

I listed some information regarding the map for the people interested:

- Custom mapped island heavily inspired on GTA:Vice City.
- BETA stage, it is in no ways finished.
- ±3000 objects (containing ±2000 re-textured objects)
- Used a custom streamer however applied standard draw distances
- Had an second island connected by bridges (like VC) however limitations of SAMP made it impossible.
- Realistic island: All buildings categories and infrastructures are added, there is loads of variety like the set up of LS, SF and LV. (Only a fire department is missing if I can recall correctly)
- Video recorded on a low-end wooden laptop using Fraps.

NOTE: In case people are actually interested I will upload more parts featuring the whole island.


PART 1 - Featuring the Airport, Industrial Area and Middle-class neighborhood.




NEW! PART 2 - Featuring the lower-end neighborhoods.


Please take into consideration the amount of time and effort I have spend into this project (you seriously don't want to know), so please try to avoid idiotic replies such as "You could have added more details" and "I don't see any bushes", please. thank you in advance.

Enjoy watching, please don't hesitate to ask any questions with regards to the project & have a nice day.

Yours Sincerely,
Delfino
