In DM terms: Carkill - parking ontop of someone to kill them (also called carparking) or using someone's vehicle to kill them (e.g. shooting a car so it will blow up)

In RP terms: Character Kill - When your character is dead. In RP, your character "passes out" when he dies, but he has amnesia to who the person who caused it was. That way he can't revenge kill. However, when an admin of the RP server puts a character kill in, the next time he "passes out" he will actually be killed from the incident. It's like a fully-RP way of banning someone by character
