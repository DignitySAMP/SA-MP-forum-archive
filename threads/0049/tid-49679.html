[GM] Area 51 DM
About: This is a Area 51 gamemode some people also known as area 69. There are 2 teams gangstas and cops. There is a scoring system like my [FS]Levels and you will get weapons when you get to a certain level or be able to fly certain planes. You can get score by killing people, After your team wins you will be awarded 5 points (Not much so go for the kills )! Your scores will be reset when you leave because it's much more fun !

Gameplay: You have firstly have to get the other teams deaths to 0!

Then you will be sent to area51 and you either need to capture the checkpoint for 2 minutes or get the score of the other team back down to 0. Then this will restart again!

Scripting: It's noob scripting i did it a while ago but it still is fun ! It uses mostly variables to store data. This script is about 1400 lines.

Commands:
  • /Level [ID] -Shows the players level!
  • /Levels -Is a list of the scores for leveling!
  • /Commands - Its a list of commands!
  • /Kill - Kills yourself!
Admin commands (Rcon):
  • /LevelSet [playerid] [Level] -Sets a playes level!
  • /Area51 - Starts area51! /Renegade - Starts renegade!
  • /CopsScore [Score]- Changes the cops score on the score board!
  • /GangScore [Score]- Changes the Gang's score on the score board!
  • /TakeScore [playerid] [Score]- Takes the score off the player!
  • /GiveScore [playerid] [Score]- Gives the player the score!
  • /SetScore [playerid] [Score]- Sets the players score!
  • /SpecialSpawn [playerid]- Gives the player special spawn privlages!
