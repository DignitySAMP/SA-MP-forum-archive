It's case to say that once again, the efforts and sweat spent on SA-MP were recognized. This time by one of the biggest (if not the biggest) multigaming corporation, Electronic Sports League (ESL). http://www.esl.eu/eu
After many votes from the SA-MP Deathmatch community, SA-MP is finally going to have what Deathmatchers dreamed since a long time:

- A professional anticheat.
- Official Leagues & Ladders.
- Official Cups & Tournaments.

http://www.esl.eu/eu/gtasa/
http://www.esl.eu/eu/wire/anti-cheat...atch/21338330/

For now, SA-MP is on a test phase, to get familiar with the EWAC (ESL Wire Anti-Cheat) in which players can test it by simply playing at any SA-MP server, starting the game from ESL Wire client.
If the tests go well, shall the serious business start


Congratulations to all the responsible members of the SA-MP Development and congratulations to the active community that made this event possible. Having over 20.000 daily players playing a multiplayer mod for a game that is almost 6 years old is really a deed.
