It's better to come up with your own RP lines, unless English is not your first language and you're having trouble finding the corrects words to create a proper RP line. If so, I'd recommend a dictionary and such. If roleplaying is your issue then think of roleplaying as you're a character living a life in the world the RP server is set on.

As KurtBag said, /me is for actions, and the more details you add in, the clearer it is to the player you're making an action on. When making an action you have to think of not metagaming(Using OOC info for IC info) or powergame(Doing an action that would be unrealistic in the setting the RP world is set in) and try to make your action clear to the person. No need to add unnecessary information, it's better for it to be simple rather than it to look complicated but hard to understand.

And /do is usually when you want to describe a RP situation or add more RP info into a situation that you're on, or on how you look and such.
