On some other forum, some user said the he he saw the "Hat man" which is a ghostly shadow figure. I did some research, and the Hat man is actually really popular, but rare to see. It's a random shadow that can be seen anytime, it is sometimes on the wall, or floating in mid air. It makes unearthly movements as if it isn't from this dimension.

http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shadow_person

From this research, I have dug deeper into other weird stuff. It seems that there is some dark spirit side that many people experience, but don't talk about it. One is called sleep paralysis. It's about people experiencing some kind of demons or shadow people contact right when they fall asleep or are about to fall asleep. They say when they fall asleep, some shadow stand near their bed and attack them by choking them. They said it mostly happens to them when they sleep on their back.

http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleep_paralysis

I am not a strong believer of this stuff, because to tell you the truth, I am a Christian but I have never ever seen any kind of paranormal activity stuff. But I still wouldn't say it doesn't exist. My cousin, a 24 year old girl, she's really smart and a happy person to be around. One day she told my mom and I that she were sleeping over at her friend's house one day, she said she was in the bed trying to sleep and her friend went to the bathroom to brush her teeth. My cousin said when she was on her bed, something came onto her and started choking her while pressing on her chest. Then she screamed out and her friend came back and the thing leave her alone. My cousin said til that day she never sleeps on her back again. In my opinion this sounds pretty similar to some sleep paralysis experiences. When my cousin told me about this I just thought it was some kind of ghosts or something, but ye it sounds like that sleep paralysis stuff.

If you're interested, read some of the comments here, I swear it's scary as hell.

http://www.end-your-sleep-deprivatio...paralysis.html
