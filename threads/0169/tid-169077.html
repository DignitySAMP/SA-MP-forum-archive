After a long time (8 months) ive decided to come back to the sa-mp scripting community and re-release my scripts.

SCRIPT:
LookinsEngine
DESCRIPTION:
LookinsEngine is a very simple system that allows you to start your engine every time you enter a vehicle (unless it has already been started) and start your engine as if you were in real life.
When you enter a vehicle you will be prompted to press "C" on your keyboard to start your engine now there is a catch there is a 1 in 4 chance the engine can end up stalling on you then you can then try again.
This is very useful for RP servers and me personally almost any server as it can add a sort of rush feeling if your trying to get away from someone trying to kill you or if your trying to kill someone yourself.

This script can benefit you and your server more than you realize people who have used this script or seen it first hand love it!
