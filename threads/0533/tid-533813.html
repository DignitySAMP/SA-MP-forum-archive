Extreme Dialog Creator
Extreme Dialog Creator is a an advanced dialog creator specialy made for PAWN(SAMP).
This dialog creator was developed to be one of ExtremePAWN's tools.(Click here to visit Extreme Pawn's official page)

However I want to share this tool to anyone who would not like to use Extreme Pawn.

Features :
  • You don't have to care about those "\n" anymore... Enter your text normally in a multiline textbox.(Which means you will use ENTER to make a new line)
  • Advanced list dialog creator. (You don't have to care about those \n, As it has a listbox where you can add/delete or remove items as in GIF 3...)
