ENB Series
-Add HD effects to GTA SA and slightly upgrades graphics
SRT 3
-Completely retextures GTA SA with high quality, and somewhat stunning textures.
-I think you were looking for something like this.
-Created in 2006, can you believe it is that old and not very popular?
Project Reality
-Not necessary, but helps ENB with improving some of the graphics, as well as other smaller details in the game
Xtreme Graphics Pack 2
-Does the same as Project Reality, but it is less extreme. Just an alternative
Project Oblivion
-Re-creates the vegetation / trees on SA, and adds a whole bunch more everywhere you look.

That's basically all you need to get great visuals.
