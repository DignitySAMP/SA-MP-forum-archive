Rocky Island


Introduction:
Hello, this is my first map I made since a year so I hope you guys like it.
I used MTA to make this map, version 2.0.
The map has exactly 699 objects so I suggest you use a streamer, I used Incognito's streamer.
The map is near Los Santos in the middle of the sea, there is a teleport to the army base: /teleportme.
It was supposed to be a Deathmatch map for my server, but I don't have the money to host one.
You are free to edit and use this map in servers, just leave the credits to me.
