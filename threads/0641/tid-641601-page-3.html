I've been around SA-MP since about mid 2013, I believe sometime after the 0.3x release. I found out about the modification originally through a video that showed someone walking around inside a journey using a chat-box to type in "/sit" (or something similiar). I was intrigued and at first I thought this was just a single player modification, I thought being able to use a chat-box to do actions in GTA:SA was the coolest thing ever. I soon after found out it was actually a multi-player modification and started watching SmokezHerbs LS-RP videos. I remember thinking to myself that it was amazing the guy could remember all the different commands there was. I eventually submitted, after multiple attempts, an application to LS:RP. Sadly I soon had an encounter with a trucker (I think) who we for some reason or another got in a dispute against. I had done the application completely on my own but admittedly didn't really understand it, I just strewed a response together enough to get accepted in. I called 911 and told them the guys name and when an admin came, I told them I knew their name because it was, well, above where their head was. I was pretty new to this type of game and I legimiately didn't know any better, I was unfournately banned at this point which I was pretty sad about at first.

Eventually for some reason or another I also found out about NGG. I eagerly rose through to Level 2 and around this time, rimkits and giftboxes were the hype. I found a guy and asked him for $50,000 so I could level up to level 3 and have a chance at the giftbox (which was level restricted). The guy kindly gave me that amount and I went to go use the giftbox, and guess what? I got a rare rimkit, I had people all around me spamming me jealous wanting to buy it. Unfortunately, I felt I had somewhat been in debt to the guy who helped me level up, so I sold him it for $15,000,000, which I soon found out was much less than I could have gotten. I eventually stayed on the server for a while and joined different gangs & later a faction (FDSA).

Anyway, I spent a good amount of time on the server before eventually leaving and moving on to other things. I really want to thank NGG for teaching me so much about SA-MP, many life lessons and how to begin roleplaying. I made a lot of friends there and although I haven't really approved of the direction NGG started taking in late 2014 - 2015, I have to admit that without it, I might have grown out of SA-MP really quickly and I am thankful as I'm still continuing to have fun with the game nearly four years later.
