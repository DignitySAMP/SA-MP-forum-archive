"The gamemode is unfinished and doesn't have the basic commands."
O.K.I did some (maybe even more) crazy facts in the past but this I think is the most biggest.As far the title says (and logo), I post my gamemode been working for some time.The gamemode isn't finished (doesn't have commands like /teles, /cmds, /credits etc.).He have over 35 commands (I'm not sure how many are) and many systems (e.g: gang system).Much of it is based on the save system called SQLite and on Y_ini.The gamemode is of type RPG/Freeroam (is a combination) and does not recommend it for beginners.


REGISTER SYSTEM:
Register/Login system is quite complex and uses SQLite.Everything in this script is linked to SQLite so all accounts and details are saved in Accounts.db.




AFK/BRB SYSTEM:
The AFK/BRB system (as I said) isn't advanced.




HOLD SYSTEM:
Most of gamemodes have a system called "hold" so I did one using mSelection.




VEHICLE PANEL SYSTEM:
From the vehicle panel (/vpanel) you can activate nitro, flip and fix modes.




SPAWN SYSTEM:
This is the "guns" and "cars" spawner.




BANK SYSTEM:
Banking system (which gave me some trouble because of "bugs") is on Y_ini and require a bank account (to save money).Once you have used the command for bank (/ bank) will ask you to create your account and once you have created the account, a file will appear in scriptfiles/Bank with your name.




ADMINISTRATION SYSTEM:
The administration system is unique and scripted 80% by me (obviously I was inspired by other scripts).It also includes systems like cookies and coins (embedded) and obviously a donor system.




PHONE SYSTEM:
The phone is a simple enough that you can talk to a player in private.




PROPERTY SYSTEM:
It contains two "branches."On one hand it is an apartment system and on the other hand, is a house system.The house/apartment can be bought with money and it is in all of San Andreas.




GANGS SYSTEM:
This system is quite important (known as the "/ createclan" for romanians).It is based on the structure of this script.




The gamemode also contains many other things like this.I didn't wrote them because it is too much writing and I will let you to discover.I posted this gamemode because I'm tired to script him, simply I'm tired of PAWN.
