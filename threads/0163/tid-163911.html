This is Jarred here, bringing you my fourth map, and my second Roadblock map! I would consider this map to be version 2.0 of my previous roadblock map! This map has around 16 objects, with four vehicles included. Two firetrucks, an FBI Rancher and an LSPD Cruiser. This features a murder crime scene, where a truck has blown up in the Intersection. They were obviously kidnapping a cop, and you can find both bodies in and around the crime scene.
