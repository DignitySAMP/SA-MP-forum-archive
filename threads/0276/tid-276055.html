Neon System
Today I bring a filterscript for roleplay servers (you can use for everyone, but I did think to put in a job or faction, you must edit it depending on what your variables.), Used to put new lights and neon lights for vehicles .

Commands:
- /tablero. To turn on/off the lights/neon.
- /colocar [luz-neon] [color]. To place a neon/lights to a vehicle.
- /luces. To turn on the lights.
- /neon. To turn on the neons.
- /quitar [luz-neon]. To completely remove the vehicle.
- /show-neon. To turn on rand ( video 2 )
