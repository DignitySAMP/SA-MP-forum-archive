I can give a lot of tips...
1. Encrypt as much data as possible, also when encrypting passwords, double encrypt or encrypt it with diffrent types of encryptions. Eg. Encrypting MD5 hash with whirlpool...
2. In the start, don't add adds, not a single ad, if your website isn't big, and there is ads, people wouldn't use it (i mean, then i would just stick to ebay and shit..)
3. You name and domain name must be simple to remember.
4. You logo, it has to be professional but more important, unique, think about ebay's logo, you remember it... my point exactly.
5. Selling fees, are you fucking kidding me? This is a big no, no.... instead of this you could a premium account system and ads (but only after your website is a little big.
7. Get a good lawyer to draw up a contract protecting you from get sued and shit.
