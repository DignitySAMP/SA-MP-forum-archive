LS Bridge Restoran

Information:
This map has been created by Plugy.
This map has been created for Fun and Roleplay.
This map has been edited to [FS] - Filescript to test it.
PS! It includes two floors Restoran, up store is VIP's
and down us regular Restoran with Kitchen.
Added 3 teleport commands.

How Install?:
Open your Gamemode PAWN file. Add CreateObjects under OnGameModeInit.
If you want add teleport command in gamemode, just copy/paste under OnPlayerCommand.
After updating your gamemode PAWN file, upload it and restart server. After restart check it.

Commands:
/view[1-3] - To teleport on map locations.
