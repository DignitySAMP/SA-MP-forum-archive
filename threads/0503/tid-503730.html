[Mulholland's Modern House]
By Erwin.

Hello there! Today, i've just finished my another new map, which i've started working on it, few days ago. So bassicaly, i've mapped a house, with a custom interior and exterior. For that, i've used alot of wall objects, for floors and ofcourse walls itself. The house is called modern, because there are included some of the modern objects with an also nice terrace. The house has two floors. Overall, the house has 7 rooms, like: Guest room, kitchen, bath room, resting room and bar.
The map has over 1132 objetcs at one place.

For map creation was used JernejL's Map Editor.
