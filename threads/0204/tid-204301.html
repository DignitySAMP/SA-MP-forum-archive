Installation:
-----------------

1. First put "crimelife_all_in_one.pwn" & "crimelife_all_in_one.pwn" in your filterscript folder

2. Then add "topazicon.txt & topazpickup.txt & topazcars.txt" in your scriptfiles folder

3. Then add "streamer.dll" in your plugins folder

4. Then at last write "crimelife_all_in_one" in your filterscript line

5. And "streamer" in your plugins line. "NOTE" : If you don't have plugins line then create a line
