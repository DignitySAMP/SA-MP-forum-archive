Doherty Break-In is almost just the same as Area51 break-in

Exept i'm trying to make it more .. difficult for the attackers. in 0.1B (wich is this) it'll just be plain simple..
But in more versions, i'll try and make it more difficult.

Download:
Rapidshare (version 0.1)
Pastebin (version 0.2)
SendSpace (version 0.2)
SendSpace (version 0.5) <- .zip -> .pwn and .amx
Mirrors allowed

Objective

Attackers:
They have to parachute to the drug place in SF (Doherty). There's an parachute on the end of the ranch (behind the certuns).
Your suppose to parachute near the red marker ...
When your landed .. run to the checkpoint to win the game. The server will restart and you'd have to do it all over again ...

Defenders:
As Defender you have to defend the Checkpoint (red marker on the map) at all cost. You've got 3 guns to stop them ... (Spas, deagle & M4 / Sniper)
When the 10 minutes have passed, the defenders win.

Installation:
1. Download it.
2. Put the .amx and .pwn in the 'Gamemodes' file.
3. Open server.cfg and change gamemode0 to
Code:
gamemode0 doherty
4. Run the server
5 You're done!
