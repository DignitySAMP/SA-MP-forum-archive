There's only one response to that. Don't even waste your time. You're probably wondering, why? I want to have my own community. Well, there's a few reasons. There are many stable servers that are already out there, that many people have outstanding stats and positions on, so ask yourself this question... "Why would someone leave a popular server that they have everything on, for a server that's dead?".

New servers might peak to 20-30 players one time, then slowly, die out. The only reason players join new servers, is to see if they can get a free admin position, or lead a faction, an feel like they are something on a server for a bit. But ultimately, they go back to the popular server, where the actual RP and stability is. All new players on your server want to do is try and take advantage of you, and get free stuff.

Bottom line, there's no reason to start up a new server, when there are plenty of stable ones already out there. So, your best option is to try and become a well-known member on a popular server, or a member of the development team there, not starting up your own server, because you are only wasting your time.

Hope this gets a point across to some people, and please note, I am saying this to be mean, I am only just trying to help, and keep people from wasting time and money, to only fail.

It depends on the server staff how the server will be. If owner just accepts everyone as admin, 'because they like the person' who they don't even know in real, then yes, everything will most likely be a fail.
