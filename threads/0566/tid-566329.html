Body system
I made this for my server, but im going to release it because i need reputation to advertise my server. It's not ready and wait for new updates. Also suggest your ideas for this filterscript, i was in hurry so i couldn't do any big intro.


Commands
/liftbody - lift body up
/putdown - put body down
/dighole - You need spade and then you are digging a hole for 10 seconds
/puthole - You need /liftup the body and near your digged hole use this command
/examine [body/hole] - examine body, who is it and who killed him
/closehole - You can close hole and open hole with that
