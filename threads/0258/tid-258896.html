Hey, I was bored so I decided to script, I came up with this. It is my first GM that I have ever made. It only has 4 commands inside it. /kill,/heal,/gun,/spawn

/kill Kills you
/heal heals you
/gun Teleports you to the Ammu-Nation in LS
/spawn Teleports you back to spawn

You can earn money for guns by doing stunts.
As this is my first GM, And I am kinda new to this, The script has 38 warnings, But it doesn't effect it at all. You can fix it and I bet you guys can fix it in like a minute :P.

You will spawn with a Kantina and a Deagle.
