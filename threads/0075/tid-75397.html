--== Los Santos Fair Fun ==--
Created by: Toniu_

Introduction:
Yo again, today I am realising a Los Santos Fair Fun filterscript, Its a small Fair Fun placed in Los Santos, near the bank. This Filterscript contains 33 objects and 7 commands. This filterscript is not any kind of edit of something, it has been made from a scratch, as it is fairly easy stuff I give you all credits to edit it and use it, but never ever RE-REALISE it. I know it is very easy, it took me about 1 hour to make it, it is pretty Okay for some RP Servs Enjoy!

Features:
1. This small FS contains 33 objects, 8 pickups and 7 commands
2. There's a moving gondola, when you type /gondolaup it will move you and an object high in the sky so you can see beatiful views of LS
3. You can get some ice creams from ice cream parlour, get drunked, get stoned. Everything is there !
4. You can try fighting with others by using Box Arena
5. You can also try skydiving, you can register yourself near an old plane, by typing a simple command /skydiving
As I spent only 1 hour making it there's not much things to do , but you can add them easily !

Commands:
1.-/gondolaup and then /gondoladown
2.-/icecream
3.-/weed
4.-/drink
5.-/skydiving
6.-/box

Screenshots:
Overview of the entrance

Credits:
All Credits go to : Toniu_ >aKa< Tom_Johnson, you can edit it in any way you like but please leave me some credits, and do not re realise it

Instalation:
1. Download the .pwn and .amx file.
2. Place them in your filterscripts folder/
3. Add in your server.cfg on filterscripts FairFun
