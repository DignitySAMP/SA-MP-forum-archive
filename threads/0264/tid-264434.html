Today I would like to share an experience with you that I had on a server. Now, before I start with any details, I know this information is completely random and many of you may find this useless. However, I found it entertaining to witness and experience, so you may find it enjoyable to read.

So, I was on a server (whose name or IP I will not mention).   • Understand that libraries and plugins are not used to create the Pawno 'engine'...whatever that is

  • Understand that Pawno is not the compiler, but merely a text editor with syntax highlighting and in-built compiler
  • Understand what includes/libraries are
  • Understand what plugins are

They claimed that I had been asking him super complex, in-depth questions that not everyone that knows the basics of scripting in SA:MP would know. The fact is, they were EXTREMELY simple questions.
