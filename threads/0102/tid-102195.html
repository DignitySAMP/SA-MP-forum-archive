Story
If you know me well, I like to do my scripting somehow in-game and go the easiest way, and some people are like me and like to do that. Some may find this useless and some may find it useful
How To use
Simply Create a map icon using /createmapicon and then once you created yours look in your scriptfiles folder and open up "Mapicons.txt" NOTE: If your going to make more than 32 icons, I suggest using a Mapicon streamer.
Bug Reports
For Vista Sometimes the "Mapicons.txt" is not there, but if you click "Compatibility files" you see it.
