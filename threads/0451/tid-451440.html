Alright, so I've been working on a filterscript/small interior of a Pawn Shop. I am about 20% progress in the project, but I have been leaning away from SAMP more and more lately. I have about 20-25% of the map done, and 15-20% of the script done. The map is just a box with floor, wall, and ceiling textures. The script is a bit functional, being able to donate a weapon to the pawn shop, browse and buy a weapon (not like a store, it only has about 4 menu items at a time, and the items change every night at 10 (22:00, not tested yet, but seems to be working) and theres 5 shop inventory possibilities, and 1 is an empty inventory.) And there's also an informational menu. I have several things to do in the script that I haven't touched yet: 1) Selling your item and being able to work out your own deal with the seller (similar to the show Pawn Stars). 2) Being able to Pawn an item, and work out a deal for a lesser value than selling it (if you don't know what Pawning is, look it up), then have a new menu item that lets you pay for your pawned item to get it back. And finally 3) Trading your item for an item of equal value (same type of item: pistols=pistols, rifles=rifles, etc.). Don't expect it to be out for a while. I have no idea when I will start work on it again. Also, there are 2 ideas for it, and haven't decided on it yet, one with the interior (you walk to a pawn shop and use it like you would any 24/7 or ammunation), or just open it with a command (removing the interior entirely, and just having a command like '/pawn' to open the pawn shop). If anyone is interested in a short video, I will create one, and if anyone likes the idea, I will spend more time on it.
