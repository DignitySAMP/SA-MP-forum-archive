Lawedan Roleplay
Lawedan Roleplay is an open world Roleplay Server currently under development by Lawedan Computers, Inc, a gaming professional enterpise.

Our staff is divided in groups of elite handpicked professionals called 'departments', each department has members that are trained for the specific position they will work as. Thanks to our project managment systems our staff works based on due dates.

We have paid members and volunteers, both of them having the same responsabilities.
Thanks to our wiki and the forums we update our members on a daily basis.



Our main goal is to the most realistic roleplaying experience in San Andreas Multiplayer, for this we are actively looking for the most experienced scripters and mappers.

So what will Lawedan Roleplay have?

The latest Pre-Alpha meeting showed the following task lists:
  • Development of a country system, there will be a country governing each piece of land.
  • Use on Artificial Intelligence on NPCs to create the idea of a populated city, airport securities, planes and NPC helicopters are in our expectations
  • Realistic legal system, each criminal must go to the court before proceeding to jail, court will possibility be leaded by a NPC judge thanks to our evidence system
  • Realistic evidence system, where the player may leave hair while walking, entering a house or car - Blood when shooting someone, bullets if a weapon was fired and the list goes on (we usually contact specialists before proceeding to script the system)
  • Advanced faction system, no more requesting a faction, you just register your company or form your gang, patent your products and get an employer id.
  • Realistic vehicle system, includes signing papers, insurances and loosing your car if it explodes with no insurance
  • Realistic housing system using legal systems
  • realistic crashing system thanks to MapAndreas.
  • Realistic injury system, detecting the weapon that lowered your health to decide if you are injured or not and if yes, you will start loosing blood (which is stored in a variable).
And that's just a small list of coming soon features!
