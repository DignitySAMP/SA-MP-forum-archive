Laptops are plenty fine for gaming if you choose the right one.

I play Planetside 2 on my laptop all the time. Feel free to look up the specs for that game haha

You NEED more memory, especially since that video chipset shares system memory. Its lagging because Windows is relying on your disk (pagefile/swap) a lot for loading/unloading textures. Drop 4GB in there, don't try and run a full HD resolution, turn quality down to medium and use 16-bit textures. You'll be good at that point I think.
