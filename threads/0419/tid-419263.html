Former head scripter over at NGG (NG-RP), made a enemy of me by trying to censor legitimate posts of mine (it's a common thing over there who challenges the status quo). I didn't sign any exclusive agreement when I sold it and I don't see why it has to remain exclusive to NG-RP forever, so I'm giving it to those in the scripting community that are brave enough to pry it open, modify, compile and release it, so here it is:

g_casinogames.pwn - http://pastebin.com/3FtF3MAc

I originally made this script as an intention of adding more games toward it, but honestly I got a little too ahead of myself and only managed to finish Texas Holdem Poker.

Now there is a few bugs in the hand analyzer and a few minor ones in other places and I would fix them if only I had some spare time so I'm releasing it as is with no intention of releasing bug fixes.

Disclaimer: I'm giving you FULL permission to modify, rename, gut and pillage to your heat's content. You may even re-release it to these forums if you happen to make game alterations or bug fixes. You can even remove my name and say you came up with the whole thing, just remember to think of me when you do it.

This is pretty much my LAST contribution script-wise toward my 6-7 year journey here on sa-mp, it's time I move on to learn and do other things, It's been fun guys.

Quote:
Originally Posted by GsT

Great job putting your time into this, not many would have had the patience to pull this out. Do you happen to have a list with those bugs that you found out, in order to check them?
Thanks.

I don't really have a official list of all the bugs but the ones I do know:

1) Three of a Kind gets mixed up with a Four of a Kind.
2) Raising the pot (dialog) has a funky bug when the active bet is more than the pot.
3) Leaving the table (quitting/timing out/kicked/banned) while being an active player causes the game to get stuck in a endless loop, this is known to happen when there is more than 2 players.
4) Players don't always go to where their suppose too in respects of the table offsets, I don't know if this is a sa-mp sync issue or an error in my coding. I tried to fix this by "re-seating" players every so often via the table pulse timer, but it seems to not want to work. I experienced this issue more often if 3+ players are at the table.
5) Blinds and dealer rotations have a mind of their own and will vary between working and doing something completely unexpected.

Like I said, I would go around a fix all of them, I just don't personally have time for it, but everyone is welcome to post fixes for them, I'll even update the opening post and give them credit.
