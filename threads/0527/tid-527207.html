Today I bring you a simple filterscript, or you can say a filterscript which was requested by many players. I know many of you would say that it is a simple script, and easy-to-make, but who knows it could be helpful to someone?. Well, this filterscript will be more like a collection of modded cars. I would be making modded cars and mod almost every car in SA. Well, I don't really mean mods, it is just attaching of objects . I will be updating this thread frequently. This idea came to my mind after I finished a custom attach object to vehicle filterscript for personal use. If you would like to have a car added please be not afraid of posting it's name and type of objects in the comments section!

So lets go!
