Last Car Standing Platform
By DiGiTaL_AnGeL


Hi! This is my 2nd map, and it's represented by a Last Car Standing Platform. The LCS is simple: the last car remaining on the platform wins. The admin give a prise to the winner, and teleports the player(or you can do a tp cmd. It has 5 Bullets and 5 Cheetahs, but you can replace/add more cars.
I made this map in 30 minutes, because I was bored If you like it, don't forget to REP+ me and comment.
