Hello SAMP community,

Well our server Las Venturas Gang Wars has had to close down due to our personal lives so we have decided as a team to release our script for you to enjoy!

INTRO

Las Venturas Gangwars was a server started by three TDM lovers. The server's main aim was to provide all it's players with the greatest TDM experience ever. The server is based in Las Venturas just as the title says. When you join the server you get the chance to select from 4 teams which inlcude:
  • Leone Family
  • Triads
  • Sindacco Family
  • LVPD
FEATURES

-Zone Captures
-Gun Shops
-Health and Armor Shops
-Ammo Shops
-Special zones for free health refill and armour.
-Team Radio
-Team bases scattered throughout Las Venturas
-Spawn Protection
-Cookies system
-Custom Time and Weather (you can change your own time and weather)
-Dynamic Sync system
-Easy ChangeTeam system
-Live streaming Music system
-Hot Air Balloon flying all around LV
-Custom mapped army bases.
-Weapon, Jetpack, Money Anticheat.
-Lasers for AK47 and M4

TEAMS

-Leone Family: Based in 'The Strip'.
-Triads: Based in the 4 Dragons Casino.
-Sindacco Family: Based near the LV Stadium.
-LVPD: Based in the LV-PD.

CREDITS

-Surferdude AKA Tactical (Coding)
-DirtyLilFreak (Mapping)
-XFlawless (Helping)
-SAMP Team (Plugins and Includes)
-****** (Plugins & Includes)
-[HiC]TheKiller (MySQL)


IMPORTANT INFORMATION

-DO NOT RE-RELEASE THE SCRIPT
-DO NOT RE-RELEASE THE SCRIPT AS A BETA SCRIPT
-MIRRORS NOT ALLOWED
