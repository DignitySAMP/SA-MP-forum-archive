Problems
-Mods could be corrupting your game.
-Your gta_sa.set (located in the documents) could be affecting your game.
-Problem with the server (rarely occurs)

Solutions
-Uninstall all the mods, hopefully it will fix it.
-Delete your gta_sa.set
-Don't play on the server (as I said, that only happens on a rare occasion.)
