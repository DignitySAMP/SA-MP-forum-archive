* Introduction
Its early July, Private Ryan Miller is on the job for delivering a truck load of Military weapons to Los Santos International to be shipped off to Iraq to help the soldiers. But Private Miller has lied about knowing his way around Los Santos and gets lost! Criminals of San Andreas come to Los Santos to get a chance to rob the armored truck and take some weapons! Private Miller is aware that he may get robbed so he is on the look out for suspicious people around him. Can you get inside?

So basically, a NPC drives around Los Santos in a Barracks Alone and unarmed and if your close enough, and lucky enough you can break into the back of the truck and steal some weapons. You will get one weapon from each class (Pistol, Shotgun, SMG, Heavy) with 750 ammo then launched out behind the Barracks. There is a three minute spam timer. You cannot attempt to break into the Barracks if your in the driver seat of any car.

* How To Use

1. Download the file (At bottom)
2. Put all the files where the need to be (In your server directory, same folder as they are in the .rar file)
3. Open your server.cfg file and put 'maxnpc' as 1 or 1 higher than your current maxnpc
4. Go ingame, get near the NPC and type /breakin
