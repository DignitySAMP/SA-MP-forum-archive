Cops vs Terrorists
v1.0

Reason for releasing :
I made this script for a guy called Joy_Freed (SA:MP forum nick), later we had some problems, so i quitted his community. Then i released the script over here.


Features :
Its a simple GM, the features are as follows :
- Two teams (cops vs terrorists)
- 1 capture zone (desert airport)
- Base briefcase to buy weapons.
- Map icons
- Mapped bases
- Gang zones
- Kills, Deaths, Scores, Ratio textdraws
- Spree system
- Random messages
And much more..

- And all others.
