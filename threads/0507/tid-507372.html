HanuSpeedo
v2.0
Introduction:
Well, my first speedometer didn't get any good response so, I had decided to make the second version of this Speedometer. And today I am releasing it. Hope U all will like it. And one more thing, I want to say THANKS to Daddy Yankee for object id's.

Features:

Clock Speedometer.
Gear System.
Digital Speedometer ALSO!!
Measure the speed in Km/h.
Black in color.
Perfect Speed calculation.


Installation:

Download the .rar file from the link.
Move HspeedoV2.pwn and HspeedoV2.amx in filterscripts folder
Open server.cfg and add Hspeedov2 at the side of filterscript text.
Save the file.
Open your server and enjoy it.
