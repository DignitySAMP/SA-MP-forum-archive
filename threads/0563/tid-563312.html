All right, there are a few things you could do.

1. It is most likely your gta_sa.exe. Make sure it is v1.0 US, and make sure that the rest of your game is v1.0 US, use downgrade patch if necessary. Identical issue was reported here and the solution lies here.

2. If none of that is the case, try renaming your GTA San Andreas folder, to, let's say, GTA San Andreas2.

3. Try running as administrator and/or in compatibility mode as an earlier Windows version.

And just a few question: is it only this specific server? And have you tried simply restarting your PC? What is your OS?
