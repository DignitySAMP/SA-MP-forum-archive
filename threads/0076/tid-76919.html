No new software?
Bluetooth running in the background?
anti-viruses that are scheduled for an update

Also try:

"ping -t www.sa-mp.com" (in your cmd of course)
and check the constant pinging, as soon as it goes high, do a ping to ******.com (don't include the -t)
and check if they are the same, if they aren't then and you have too many NAT and firewall rules configured (on your router), the problem could potentially be a bogged down router due to too many operations being processed.

My advice to you would as soon as it lags, open your Task Manager and goto Processes and end all your programs that need to update and do update automatically, such as anti-viruses, bluetooth and such.
