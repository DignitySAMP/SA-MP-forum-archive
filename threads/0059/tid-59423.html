[GM]Protect The Prime Minister

Introduction:
When i started playing sa-mp (2,5 years ago) my favourite gamemode was Protect The Prime Minister, i was wondered there was no such gamemode released yet... So, i decided it was about time! I had not much time, so it's a very basic mode. Feel free to edit yourself.


Teams:

Team 1:
  • Name: Prime Minister
  • Color: Yellow
  • Duty: Survive for (default) 10 minutes
  • Spawnlocation: Front of hotel near tenniscourt in San Fiero
  • Spawnweapons: Cane
  • Enemies: Terrorists & Psycho's
Team 2:
  • Name: Bodyguards
  • Color: Light-Blue
  • Duty: Protect the Prime Minister
  • Spawnlocation:Parkinglot near Tenniscourt in San Fiero
  • Spawnweapons: Silenced 9mm, Micro SMG (Uzi), AK47
  • Enemies: Terrorists & Psycho's
Team 3:
  • Name: Police
  • Color: Dark-Blue
  • Duty: Hunt for the Terrorists and Psycho's
  • Spawnlocation:At policestation in San Fiero
  • Spawnweapons: 9mm, Shotgun, Country Rifle
  • Enemies: Terrorists & Psycho's
Team 4:
  • Name: Terrorists
  • Color: Red
  • Duty: Kill the Prime Minister
  • Spawnlocation:Las Venturas Airport
  • Spawnweapons: Knife, Shotgun
  • Enemies: PM, Bodyguards, Police & Psycho's
Team 5:
  • Name: Psycho's
  • Color: Green
  • Duty: Kill everybody
  • Spawnlocation:Grove Street Los Santos
  • Spawnweapons: Shovel, Micro SMG (Uzi)
  • Enemies: PM, Bodyguards, Police & Terrorists

Gameplay:
There are 5 teams, each with a duty. It's bases on a simple TDM.
The game can end in 2 ways:
  • The Prime Minister survived for a given time (default 10 minutes)
  • The Prime Minister got killed
In case the PM survives, The PM will gain 1 point, when the PM got killed, the Terrorists will gain 1 point and the gamemode will restart.
The score will get saved in a file (scores.txt).

If the player who is the PM leaves the server, the mode will not end, but the timer will stop and the PM-skin will be available again. (Use F4 and /kill to choose a new skin). When a new player starts as a PM, the timer will get set back to (default) 10 minutes.


Commands:

Normal commands:
/help, /kill, /score, /duty

Rcon Admincommands:
/kick, /ban, /mute, /unmute, /freeze, /unfreeze (All with a 'playerid' param!)
/resetscores


Vehicles:
For this gamemode (i use it for more gamemodes) i made a vehicle-from-file-loader.
All "AddStaticVehicle"-lines are in a file called "vehicles.txt".
I only placed some vehicles on all spawnlocations and some at WangCars, a total of 56 vehicles. You'll have to add more cars yourself.
