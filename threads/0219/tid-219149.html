( Minigame ) Dшn't Get Wet
Last man dry wins!

Without further parley:

[ame]http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2Ex7D2xE-9A[/ame]
Note: This is not a one player game, like in the video. It's multiplayer, up to 54 players can join!

Well basically objects drop down one by one in random order, and your job is to be last man dry. (Minimum players to join 2)
_ _ _ _ _

How to play?
1) Download Don't Get Wet minigame by clicking download link below

2) Open WinRar archiver and move the .amx file to your filterscripts folder

Code:
You will also need foreach and zcmd includes.
Once downloaded, move them to your pawno/includes folder.

foreach (Download New Version - Without "Iter_Func2" functions)
zcmd
3) Add appropriate lines to your server.cfg:
Code:
filterscripts DontGetWet
Code:
Minigame requirements: You will need to have room for 108 objects to be created.
4) Start sa-mp server client and connect to your server
5) To play Don't Get Wet, all you need to do, is type /getwet!
In later releases I will be adding some more fun ( weapons, pickups, everything and anything! )
