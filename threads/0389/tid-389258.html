I think people should look to zombie mod in CSS for inspiration, there are some great zombie mod servers there, it's the only game I play nowadays.

The key points a scripter would need to focus on for it is instant respawn (even if it's as a zombie after you're killed), making it harder for humans to shoot & kill zombies (not saying you should give zombies 4000+ HP like in CSS, but something more than 100 would probably be appropriate - maybe 200-300 max?), prop spawning (like a range of items you can spawn for credits you get when you kill someone (this server I play gives you 3 credits when you get a kill - and a basic prop is about 2-3 credits), custom scoreboard with teams (zombies/humans) to see who's dead or alive, and knife-to-kill for zombies or something along those lines, just a hit from a zombie infects you, or 2 hits.

These are just a few suggestions from the server I play, but these things are the main reason why I play.
