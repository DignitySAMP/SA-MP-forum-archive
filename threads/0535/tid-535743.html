For those who don't know yet, ArcheAge is open beta until the 8th of September and is scheduled to open on the 16th. If you're looking for a new game to play this weekend, I highly recommend giving this a try. I've been part of the closed beta and this one (open beta) so far and I've been having a blast.

If you don't know what ArcheAge is, it's basically a pirate/naval themed MMORPG which has limitless features and has some of the most epic game play experiences I've seen in a long time.

Here's the trailer:

[ame]http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1pSQPhsys6M[/ame]

And a PvP gameplay video: [ame]http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AYbFo7UNj58[/ame]

Sign up here in case you're interested.

Also, while it offers advantages for those who invest real money in kits, all of mentioned "advantages" are available in the auction house for in-game gold so please keep P2W arguments/rants away as it's not P2W at all.
