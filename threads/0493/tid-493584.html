Tired of using your head to think of a name yourself?

Heres the solution: the ultimate name generator that does all the creativity jobs for you!

Simply use www.mauzen.org/wt2.php?p=*%20RolePlay

or
www.mauzen.org/wt2.php?p=your_name_pattern
and it will return your new name in no time. In the pattern, for every * the awesome AI algorithm kicks in, and the * are replaced by a selected term to form a name that perfectly fits your needs.

Some more examples:
www.mauzen.org/wt2.php?p=*%20Hosting
www.mauzen.org/wt2.php?p=*%20Gaming
www.mauzen.org/wt2.php?p=SA-MP:%20*
www.mauzen.org/wt2.php?p=Arnold%20%22*%22%20Schwarzenegger


Try it and youll be impressed. As youll see, the generated names are much better than pretty much all the names that real persons invented.
