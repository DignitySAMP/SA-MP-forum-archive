SA:MP Players look for more than doing tricks all day, killing players, and freeroam.. They want to see more activity so that is when Roleplay came in. There are so many situations in Roleplay, in my opinion, it doesn't get bored. That's why you see so many threads "Any Roleplay Severs?!" It's because there are Roleplay servers, but they aren't strict about it. People want to see a script they never saw, a map they never even thought could be made. Roleplay is just basically fun overall, seeing other peoples Roleplay skills, and imrove yourself. Simply, you pick a character and Roleplay the way you choose to. Some people have different opinions, don't get me wrong... There are a LOT of Stunts/DM/Freeroam servers.. Just check the Server Advertisement section!!!

If you're looking foward to making a server, it's your choice what to make. Just some advice, if you are making a server and you acually want playerbase growth, do something creative, don't be the same. Something that sticks out will never get back in, if you just be creative, you can acually get somewhere!
