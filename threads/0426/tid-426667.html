Hello guys,

I've been working today on a filterscript, that's based on drugger job. IT HAS NOT SAVING SYSTEM, because I would need to make a login/register system with that, but since it's just a job, I made it simply.

What is this?

This is a simply drugger job, it has many features for your drugs. It only doesen't have a selling price, I mean, you can put a fee on what you sell, but you'll have to do something like "1000$ for drugs sir." and wait for him to give you the money, then, when he's near you, give him the drugs. Let me show you the commands/features.

► /takedruggerjob -> In this way you'll take the drugger job, but you have to be in a place witch is shown in the video below.
► /quitdruggerjob -> If you're already an drugger, you can leave the job by doing this.
► /pickupmaterials -> You'll pickup the materials, witch will be needed for the creation of the drugs.
► /givedrugs -> Gives the drugs to someone that's near you
► /createdrugs -> Creates the drugs if you got the materials
► /mydrugs -> Checks if you got drugs or not
► /usedrugs -> Uses the drugs if you got them. ( Has drunk camera function )
► /druggerhelp -> Will take up the drugger help menu. PS: Wont be shown if you're not an drugger.

Those are all for the moment.
