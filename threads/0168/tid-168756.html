After a long time (8 months) ive decided to come back to the sa-mp scripting community and re-release my scripts.

SCRIPT:Lookinsamaw
DESCRIPTION: Lookinsamaw is a script that helps in more ways than one with a 5 msg system that allows You to post important things on your server every 10 minutes (could be 5 i cant remember its been awhile but you can edit it to whatever time you like) this is the old Version 2 that i released a long time ago and will be updated very shortly.
To edit the messages open up pawno as you would usually open the script and change the lines
Code:
SendClientMessageToAll(GREEN,"Lookinsamaw by Lookin!");
To what ever message you like for example
Code:
SendClientMessageToAll(GREEN,"Welcome to my server!");
then compile and your away =D

This script also includes a random weather system that changes the weather on your server randomly to give the server more of a realistic feel to it one minute it could be sunny as a lamp on fire the next a storm could hit like a bum on the street flashing the public his a$$.

This script can benefit you and your server more than you realize people who have used this script or seen it first hand love it!

So why not try it

Version 2 http://www.mediafire.com/?cua6rfo95vq7nd3

BUGS: No bugs so far but feel free to let me know if u see any

no mirrors please!
