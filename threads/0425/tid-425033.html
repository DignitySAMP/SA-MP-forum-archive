Hello. I recently finished roleplaying my female character.. It was long and disturbing story but the server itself is great I guess..

Ok so heres a short story for my first week:
  • Raped twice.
  • Forced to be a prostitute or threatened to die.
  • I first moved in @ Fort Carson but then had to get away and moved to a small town called Las Monicas
  • Got a job as a full time cab driver at Bone County Cabs
  • Lost my job after sleeping with another taxi driver in a cab.
The first week wasn't it's best but I still enjoyed myself it was a fun and very adventurous, in the next few weeks this had started to calm down as this family that had been threatening me had left me alone after I had made the money to pay them. I was then confronted by them again and spoke with the leader of their mob in their family owned business in the center of Fort Carson.

Ok so here's briefly what happened in the next few weeks excluding the above.
  • Moved into a new house in Las Payasadas
  • Got a new job in the BCFD
  • Went to a freinds pool party at the lesiure center
  • Met someone special :3

I had then got bored of the FD and decided to move on to something a little less practical after the constant fire missions etc, it became too much for me. I wanted to focus on my private life.

A little later on I had been getting into the drug system of the server and using the drugs to get free armour (drugs give you armour) and then decided to start that for my new life in Las Payasadas.. after a few jobs in
this style of work.. I had started to become rich, the economy of the server is very realistic so the majority of players are not rich really.. I considered myself quite lucky to be at this wealth standard.. I then became addicted to this lifestyle, the health of my character was quite low and I had less than 3 points left of my drivers license.. I ignored the fact of the risks and just continued.. Further on I had got myself into some trouble with another criminal faction "The Vivas" a well known faction run by the owner of the server.. not did I know that a few days later I would be CK'd. The following is in a timeline style.
  1. Constantly stalked by them.
  2. I kept recieving out of the ordinary phone calls from Payphones
  3. They offered my money and a new house.. I rejected..
  4. I was once again raped behind the cinema, which got me thinking it was the same gang from the beggining
  5. I logged off for the day.
  6. Logged in the next day and we're immediately kidnapped by 4 men.
  7. Tortured and gangraped..
  8. Killed by the owner.. Jesse Viva AKA Cody Drew
After that day my IC life was over and I had to start another.. the BCMD we're not online that day to save me neither did the BCSD manage to get to me intime, overall.. I think it was just a laugh playing that character.. NOT A SERRRIOUS CHARACTER :P

Well that's all I guess.. I really recommend that you guys check this out, I had alot of fun in such a short amount of time!
