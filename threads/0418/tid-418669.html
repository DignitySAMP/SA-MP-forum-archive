INTRODUCTION

I made this simple filterscript because I was bored. So basically you can become a pilot and transport stuff to other airports for money.

FEATURES

Commands:

Code:
/pilothelp - Displays a dialog with all the CMDS I added.
/work - When in an airplane (and when you're a pilot) it'll give you work, transport stuff to another airport,...
/join - Use this when near the pilot job spot
/quitjob - To quit your pilot job.
/accept [join/quit] - Checks if you really really wanna quit/join, if you type this you'll join/quit your pilot job.
Players can't join an aircraft when they're not a pilot, also when you're a pilot and you exit an aircraft before you did your job you lose 1000$.

NOTE: This filterscript doesn't use any saving system, which means everytime you connect your pilot job will be RESETED, so you'll need to join the job again.
