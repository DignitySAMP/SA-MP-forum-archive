How to change www.******.com.
  1. Open the server directory.
  2. Open ServerStocks folder, and open Defines.pwn.
  3. You will see the following line somewhere at the start.
    pawn Code:
    #define SITE "~w~www.~b~~h~******~w~.net"
  4. Change that to your likes.
  5. Press CTRL + S and save the file.
  6. Open the gamemodes folder and open TE.pwn
