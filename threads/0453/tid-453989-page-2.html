To allow outbound connections, you must "portforward" the ports UDP/TCP depending which the server uses which i forget

for localhost your IP is not the "Remote IP" i.e 127.0.0.1, 192.168 etc.. these are all local IPs which only you or someone on your network can access (example: 192.168.1.71) if your friend is using your router they can access your server from their computer/laptop.

For someone who is not on your network you must open the ports to tell the router the outbound connections can pass through the port 7777 on UDP/TCP you can configure these in your router settings which can be found on the router which most router have on the back.

but then again it's all down to your ISP if they allow you to edit such setting which Ash. suggested :P

If you do have access to your router head over to http://portforward.com/ they give some guides on how to portforward for your router if it's listed.

Not exactly sure if this post will help in anyway, but it gives some details on the issues some people don't know how it works fully etc.. :P
