I know many will start copying the system and exhibiting it everywhere and exhibiting their copyrights, but please rate our work and expose our names if you will embed in other forums.
Now let's start with the system.

The system is used with ordinary functions.[BETA v0.7]

To begin with, when you put in a mod, you will need:
1) Go to the server.
2) To write command /horse
3) The horse controls "ALT, Space, Shift"

SHIFT = Left
ALT = Right
Space = Sprint

I came back again and decided to finish my script for those who wanted to open a project from the wild west, survival, role play, or at least with horses
