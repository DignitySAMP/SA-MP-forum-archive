NaTi's Anti- Bunny Hop
What the hell is Bunny-Hopping?
Bunny Hopping is called when player presses the Jump button in order to get faster and reach his destination. Which is usually prohibited in RP Servers.

What this Filterscript does?
This filterscript lowers the chance of Bunny Hopping and avoids players to bunny hop. If it finds a player bunny hopping it Slaps the player (You can make your own way of punishment on the player btw).

Note: It's Recommended to not be used as a Filterscript. Must be directly use in your GameMode.
I know no one gives a shit to keep the credits, but atleast don't claim it as yours.

This filterscript uses a timer to detect Bunny-Hop for each player. The default time is set to 3 which is good. if you're willing to change the timer length:
pawn Code:
SetTimer("Bunny_Hop", 3000, false); //Change 3000 to the time you want.
Note: Timer Length is as Milli-Seconds, 1000 MSec = 1 Sec.
