Library

Map: Library
Creator: Erwin.
Date: 05.04.2016 - 12.04.2016
Editor: SA-MP: Map Construction.
Amount of objects: 387.
Converted as: CreateObject.
Features: Re-textured interior and exterior objects.

Hello SA-MP community members. This is my first Library map, which is located in Los Santos, beside the City Hall. It is not very big, nor small, but medium sized. This map is good for any kind of gamemode, but more likely it would suit Role-play. If you plan to use this map on your server, then please kindly leave the credits to it's respective owner - SjuteL (Justice).

Screenshots

Download

Just simply copy and paste it in your pawno, compile it and use it as a filterscript for your gamemode.

I know how does the libraries look like. This library, is of course, far away from an ordinary library, where you see books everywhere. In my opinion, it is not always about the books, it is also about the comfy couches and chairs, where people can sitdown and read the book, or nap. It is also about cafe / kitchen, where people can come and eat their food and also make their food. I have choosen these type of textures, because it would make me to feel like, that I am home or something, that it has pretty cosiness atmosphere. I have mentioned, that it is not a big, nor small library, but medium sized, so I couldn't place more tables with computers. Of course, "Libraries are meant to be QUIET", that is why they would have listen, or watch something very quiet, to not disturb others.
