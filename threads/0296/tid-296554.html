I've had it since release date for Xbox. I must say, the campaign was amazing (and very emotional if you really got into MW and MW2). It was very entertaining and high-action paced. I also enjoyed the Spec-Ops mode. I'm glad they brought back the missions for Spec-Ops AND added the survival map (on all 16 maps). Those two aspects are also epic.

Then we get to the multiplayer, which I'm disappointed with. The MW2 multiplayer was MUCH better, in my opinion. Here is a small list of why I only played it til I got to lv 15 and probably will never visit it again:
  • The maps are small and very cramped
  • The spawning system is TERRIBLE. I've had multiple occasions where enemies spawn, literally, right behind me
  • The movements are slow. When I'm sprinting, I feel like I'm on a sluggish job. The action is high-paced, as the maps are small, but the movement counters it
  • The kill streaks are overpowered (Juggernaut)
  • Death streaks, including pain killer, are back. You shouldn't reward someone for not knowing how to play the game
  • Perks (Last Stand)
I'm honestly MUCH more content just playing MW2. Despite some of those flaws are in it, regarding the death/kill streaks and perks, the overall gameplay is more enjoyable from my perspective. If you're buying the game just for multiplayer, I wouldn't suggest you not buy it. However, many other people like it better than previous, so you may, too.


EDIT: At everyone bitching about it being MW2.5, or a base off of other games; shut the fuck up already! Who cares? Why would you fix something when it's not broken? MW3 just broke records with the number of copies it sold within 24 hours, so obviously they don't need to fix something based on a few opinions of Battlefield fans or overall trolls. If you are a battlefield fan, tell me, what's so different in BF3 than BC/BC2? The overall gameplay still feels the same. There are many neat aspects, such as the realism in lighting and animations, but most average gamers don't pay attention to them. You don't even notice most of the differences that people point out at making BF3 be such better game than MW3.

Seriously, if you're just going to bitch about it being the same thing, shut the hell up. If it ain't broke, don't fix it.
