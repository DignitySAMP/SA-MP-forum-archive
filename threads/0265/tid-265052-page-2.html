Ok so hosting, you don't have to worry about server/network problems the server providers take care of that for you. You get a control panel and a support team to help you along the way.
Downsides to hosting: You pay more sometimes depending on slot amount.

VPS: You get to control everything, can be a bit more challenging, you can host more slots for a cheaper price, no control panel unless you pay for or make one, no game server related tech support and you can generally do more with a vps.
