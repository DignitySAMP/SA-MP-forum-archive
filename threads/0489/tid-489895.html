By Erwin.

Hello there sa-mp community.Today i want to release my map called "Angel Pine's Modern Gym". I started working on it 2 days ago and 100% finished it today. I'll explain why it's located in Angel Pine and why it's called Modern Gym, why exactly the gym. I actually don't know why exactly the gym came in my mind, because i myself, going to gym 4 times in a week and i like to keep my self in a good form. Also i've never mapped a custom gym, so here it is, it's done. I've choosed the Angel Pine, because it's a small village tho, and in other 3 city's like LS, SF, LV they have already gym's, but not those small villages, like Angel Pine, or for example: Las Barancas, Fort Carson etc. And all of them i choosed Angel Pine, immediately. Why Modern? Because it has modern obj's. Ofcourse it's not just a gym's building, with it's interior etc. I've made my own thing, used my own imagination and creativity. I've made an exterior and interior for the gym and It has 2 floors. On the 1st floor, there's a management room, a small cofee bar and the wardrobe. On the 2nd floor, it has the biggest room tho, with the all exercisers and stuff, like in the real life. And also there's a office room. More if you want to see, check by your self, i've posted screens and even a video of the map,so you could see everything. This map will be available ofcourse, you will see the download button below.
