Description:

This is a script i have been working on.
The admin(s) can turn [on/off] the [1-5] Customizable Arenas inside the script.
When an arena is turned on/off players recieve notification.
Once the arena(s) are opened players can type /derby join in order to be teleported to the active arena, or /derby leave in order to leave the curent arena.

Commands:

/derby join - teleports the player to the active arena
/derby leave - teleports the player to its last known position (saved into special variables)
/derby 1 on - turns arena 1 on (same for /derby 2,3,4,5 on)
/derby 1 off - turns arena 1 off (same for /derby 2,3,4,5 off)
