єOther Systemsє

єHouse - We have over 300 Houses across LV, FC and SF. House owners are able to edit their renting price, set the house sell price and enable/disable Renting.
/robhouse - /buyhouse - /sellhouse - /unsellhouse - /rentprice - /open - /close - /unrent - /house garage - /house upgrade - /till - /tenants - /evict

єBusiness - We have over 200 Business across LV and LS. Business owners are able to edit their entrance price, set the business sell price.
/robbiz - /buybiz - /sellbiz - /unsellbiz - /bizstats - /setfee - /open - /close - /ordercomps - /setprice

єFeatured Legal Jobs: Medic, Taxi Driver, Detective, Lawyer, Bodyguard, CarSeller, Trucker, Farmer.

єFeatured Illegal Jobs and Side-jobs: Gun Dealer, Drug Dealer, Thief, Car Jacker

єCar Ownership: You are now able to buy/sell cars also factionize it.
/car buy - Ability to buy a vehicle, if it is on sale.
/car sell [price] - Set your vehicle on sale. Other people may buy it.
/car unsell - Stop your vehicle from being sold.
/car lock - Toggle the lock of your vehicle from in/outside.
/car find [ID] - Find your vehicle, if it isn't occupied.
/car respray - Respray your vehicle at the PNS business.
/car tow [ID] - Tow your vehicle ($15000), if it isn't occupied.
/car call [ID] - Call your vehicle to you ($7000), if it isn't occupied.
/car savemod - Save the tuning of your vehicle.
/car unmod - Remove the tuning of your vehicle.
/car factionize - Make your car belong to your faction.
/car scrap - Delete your vehicle - no refund!

єBest Mapping ever: Our has one of the best mapping ever. San Franciscos is not what is was before!

єBank: We have a very simple banking systems with commands such as:
/(f)withdraw /(f)deposit /(f)balance /transfer /loan (Max $25.000)

єRadio: Radio can be bought at any local 24/7's and it makes you talk with your faction members/friends more easier.
/r [Text] /freq [Slot] [Frequency] /radio (Shows your frequencies) /getfreq (Factions) /setfreq [Frequency] (Faction Leader) /syncradio [Slot]

єLaptop: The laptop system is to keep everything in a faction organized and up to date. The Laptop has a news feature where only the leaders of the faction can set news for other members to see. You can also check out the current members in the faction and the factions stocks.

єOne shot One kill System: Hitmans can use sniper to do a one shot kill assasination.

єName on/off: This is a command only for Donators or people with the minimum level of 20. This command will hide your IG name. (prevent Metagame)

єQuary: The Quary is one of the most important place in our . The Quary is the place where we keep all stocks such as weapons, alcohol and stuff. These stocks are needed to restock the businesses by the Delivery Company. The Company workers take the stocks from the quary and stock them in the businesses. In order to take the stocks, the players will need to deliver them with a unique way. For more info, contact a high leveled player IG or an admin.

єPhonebook: A phonebook can be bought at any local 24-7's. With a phonebook, you can find anyone's number by simply doing /number [Name/ID]

єElections/Vote: Each player can vote one of the two candidates for Mayor of San Andreas;

єIRC: If there are no administrators , you may use /irc to get help from (IRC) Administrators.

єHelper Team: New to Roleplay? Use /irc and get help in few seconds by an Helper!

єGuide Book: Get the most out of BG:RP with finding the most important parts of the city. (/helpme)

єMafia Wars: Wars between Families may occur and admins will manage them with commands. (/makewar | /cancelwar) with additional info about it.

єClothing:We have several types of clothes shops where you can buy your favorite clothes.
