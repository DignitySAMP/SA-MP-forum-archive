Should look something like this.
In the first line,
Key=stinger952
Level=1337
AdminLevel=1338 <------ Change it to the highest admin lvl you could get, (normaly 1338,)
DonateRank=15
UpgradePoints=0
ConnectedTime=57
Registered=1
Sex=1
Then log on and you got all admin cmds.
